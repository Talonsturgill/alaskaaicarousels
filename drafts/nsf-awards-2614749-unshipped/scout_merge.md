### 2. Enstar and HEX in emergency arbitration (Beat A)

Enstar and Alaska Pipeline Company filed an emergency arbitration demand with
the American Arbitration Association against HEX Operating on July 30th and
disclosed it to the RCA on August 7th. Enstar says HEX halted discretionary
deliveries on July 26th and is offering gas at $17.50 per Mcf against a $13.50
rate it says was locked in, and quantifies a potential 3 Bcf winter shortfall,
which its CEO translates as 18 midwinter days.

Strong material, and it sits directly beside this repo's own Cook Inlet Gas
Watch. Held back for two reasons. `dedupe_check` returns LIKELY DUPLICATE
against No.27 (2026-08-06, eight days ago), which was also a Southcentral power
supply deck and also cited Enstar and Cook Inlet storage. And the AI link is
analytic rather than documented, which the scout said plainly.

### 4. Alaska DOT&PF flew Juneau's glacial outburst flood (Beat C)

Suicide Basin released August 11th; the FAA cleared a temporary flight
restriction at 8 a.m. on August 12th so state drone crews could work the levee,
bridges and the wastewater plant, livestreamed into the emergency operations
centre. The crest came in at 14.7 feet at 2:15 p.m. on August 13th, against a
refined forecast band of 15.8 to 16.3 feet.

Genuinely in window, vivid, and the forecast overshoot is the honest heart of
it. Held back because the scout is explicit that no agency described machine
learning in the flood forecast, so the AI content is uncrewed aircraft rather
than AI, and the deck would have to be careful not to imply otherwise.

  as tbrown46@uwyo.edu, so he is University of Wyoming. The UAA award lists PI
