## Thesis

NSF has already paid Alaska in full for a refinery that exists only as a
specification, and the deck is a photograph of the one object that is real
standing beside the one that is not.

## Slide count rationale

**Ten.** Two directors independently argued ten with non-overlapping beat lists,
and the third offered nine or ten. Today's craft refresh found every independent
line of evidence pointing at the top of the 8 to 10 band rather than the bottom.
The specific test applied to each candidate cut: the mechanism quote (03) and the
money split (04) cannot share a frame without one shrinking to a footnote, and
the honesty beat (09) cannot fold into the close without turning a single ask
into a stack. Nothing here is padding. If a slide fails review and cannot be
repaired, the deck ships nine by cutting 06 and folding its energy ceiling into
05, and it does not pad to hold ten.

## Continuity system

**(A) THE TENSE GRAMMAR — the primary motif, and the deck's whole discipline.**

    SOLID   it exists, or it happened.  Casts a real shadow. May carry gold.
    DASHED  it is promised.             Casts nothing, or a dashed shadow.
            phantom dash 30 5 6 5 6 5 at 1.25 px, never gold.

This governs every mark at every scale, from a 1.25 px dash to a 2048 px shadow
map. The hero is this rule in three dimensions: a real vessel throwing a two-part
contact shadow into a lit pool, beside a wireframe twin throwing nothing. The
legend is printed ONCE, on slide 03, and never explained again.

It exists because the fact-checker's hardest constraint is tense. Nothing is
built, the grant starts September 1st, and every sentence must be future or
attributed. A rule the artwork enforces beats a caveat the copy restates.

**(B) CAMERA MOVE across one continuous bench.** Every slide is a station on one
camera path in one room. The flat slides are not flat slides, they are objects
lying on that same bench under the same amber falloff. The contact sheet reads as
one take. Declared per slide in a mono camera log merged with the counter.

**(C) EDGE-TEASE, the cold spill.** A cyan bleed enters from the right margin on
02 (12 px), 03 (26 px) and 04 (48 px), from a source the reader cannot see. Slide
05 pans right and reveals it was the model. The open loop is lit, not written.

**THE GOLD RULE, and it is checkable in one pass.** Rendered light is AMBER,
vector marks are GOLD. `#FFC72C` never appears as emitted light inside a GL
frame; it appears only as flat DOM or SVG. Amber sits near 32 degrees hue and
gold near 45, so they separate under the grade.

**Gold means money that is already obligated, and nothing else.** Exhaustive
placement: the Polaris glyph beside the counter on all ten slides; the figure on
01; the obligated figure on 02; the cap of the UAA bar on 04; the lit segment
terminal on 08; the sealed ring and Polaris on 10. Gold area never exceeds 1.6
percent of any frame. Gold never touches anything the abstract merely proposes.

## THE SHARED GL SCENE (built once, three cameras)

All three rendered slides use ONE scene built by one function, with only the
camera and the twin's presence changing. This is the bespoke-check-safe way to
pay the GPU cost three times.

```
AKT.setup(canvas, { w:1080, h:1350, bg:0x081420,
                    fog:[0x0B1A2A, 6.5, 22], exposure:1.12 })   // MULTIPLIER
AKT.environment(R, { intensity: 0.30 })

KEY   PointLight(0xFFB25E, 28, 9, 2)  pos [-0.72, 0.72, 0.30]   // INSIDE the vessel
      castShadow, mapSize 2048, radius 9, bias -0.0004
FILL  DirectionalLight(0x5AC8F0, 0.55) pos [7, 2.2, 3]          // the model's glow, no shadow
AMB   AmbientLight(0x16243A, 0.9)
```

**Verified camera arithmetic.** The directors room supplied camera stations with
one arithmetic error, which was recomputed here rather than trusted. The hero's
pitch was pitched at 7.09 degrees with a horizon at y 477; the correct values are
below. Never eyeball, and never accept a number a director computed by hand.

| 05 | [2.05, 1.55, 4.20] | [0.10, 0.78, 0.05] | 46 | **9.53 deg** | 1590.2 | **408** |
| 10 | [0.60, 1.10, 2.35] | [-0.66, 0.86, 0.28] | 38 | 5.66 deg | 1960.3 | 481 |

**Verified projection, slide 05.** Real vessel at world x -0.72, z +0.30; model
twin at x +0.95, z -0.25; both 1.28 tall, rmax 0.40.

| Feature | Real vessel | Model twin |
|---|---|---|

| Top y | 501 | 505 |

The tops land **4 px apart**, which is the entire reason the 46 degree lens is
load bearing. At a wider lens the nearer object wins on size and the reader reads
"big jar, small jar" instead of "the thing and its model". The label corridor
between them measures **346 px**. The bench occupies y 918 to 1350, the bottom 32
percent, so the lower third is modeled lit steel on every rendered frame.

**Materials.** Vessel wall `MeshPhysicalMaterial {color 0xDCEAF2, metalness 0,
roughness 0.08, transmission 0.55, thickness 0.9, ior 1.47, attenuationColor
0x9FBFD0, attenuationDistance 3.4, clearcoat 0.6}`. Culture medium a SOLID
emissive lathe, `{color 0x6A2E0C, emissive 0xFF9A3C, emissiveIntensity 2.6,
roughness 0.55}`, never a second transmissive volume. Bench `{color 0x2A3A4C,
metalness 0.55, roughness 0.42}`. Ash an InstancedMesh of 900 icosahedra, scale
0.012 to 0.05, seeded `AK.rng(20260814)`, `{color 0x14171A, roughness 0.94}`, of
which exactly 6 are swapped to `{color 0xD6E2EE, roughness 0.18}` and DECLARED
NOT A QUANTITY. Twin from the same lathe profile, `{wireframe:true, emissive
0x5AC8F0, emissiveIntensity 1.6, transparent, opacity 0.55}` plus a 6 percent
cyan skin lathe so it reads as volume rather than a net.

**Order of operations, fixed, because it is a 34 second trap.** Draw the 2D room
(wall gradient, fog, bench pool, grain base), then `AKPOST.grade(cx, ...)`, THEN
`drawImage` the offscreen GL frame on top, then only cheap local overlays. Never
grade after GL pixels land.

**The exposure units trap, written twice on purpose.**
`AKT.setup({ exposure: 1.12 })` is a MULTIPLIER.
`AKPOST.grade({ exposure: -0.10 })` is STOPS, and -0.10 sits inside the house
band of -0.15 to +0.06.

**Grade block.** `{exposure:-0.10, saturation:1.05, contrast:1.12, filmic:true,
lift:[0.008,0.012,0.026], gain:[1.020,1.000,0.968], vignette:0.24,
bloom:{threshold:0.68,strength:0.40,radius:10},
grain:{amount:0.05,size:2,seed:20260814}, aberration:0.6, dither:true,
sharpen:0.32}`. On the flat slides 02, 03, 04, 06, 08, 09: `aberration:0,
sharpen:0.22, bloom.strength:0.22`. Never aberration on data or type. Dither
always on, because this deck is one large soft gradient.

**Fallback, designed and mandatory on 01, 05, 10** for `AKT.webglOK() === false`
or `shot.ok === false`. Vessel body from Gradient Solids (#43) as a symmetric
linear band with a warm core at 33 percent; bench from akengrave (#93) with
`light {azDeg 248, elDeg 22}` so the low raking key reproduces the same
near-source read; tray shadow from `AKSNOW.contactShadow()` fed `#0B1524` and
never pure black; twin as a phantom-dash outline (#67) with a neon triple layer
(#80) in `#5AC8F0`; ash from stipple (#66) over an engraved plateau. The fallback
loses the caustic and the transmission and keeps the entire argument.
