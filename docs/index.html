What Alaska AI covers


Seven standing beats, tracked every day. Each one keeps its
own page whether or not it made news this week, with the decisions on it, who decides
them, and whether the public still has a way in.



  
Alaska data centers

  
Every proposed AI and cloud campus in Alaska, the land it wants, the power it needs, and who gets to say yes.


  
Alaska power and the grid

  
The Railbelt, Cook Inlet gas, the turbines and the interties. Who pays when a new load the size of a city plugs in.


  
Alaska land and permitting for AI

  
State land leases, gravel, permafrost and the public comment windows that are the only door most Alaskans get.


  
Defense and federal AI in Alaska

  
JBER, Eielson, Clear and the federal decisions that land in Alaska without an Alaska vote.


  
Alaska AI research and science

  
The university labs, the agencies and the field science using machine learning on Alaska problems.


  
Alaska data sovereignty and AI

  
Who owns Alaska data, who trains on it, and the Native corporations and tribes setting terms.


  
Alaska AI policy and legislation

  
Bills, the governor's desk, the regulatory commission and everything that decides the rules before the concrete pours.




What this is


Alaska AI is a daily publication on Alaska and artificial
intelligence, and an AI studio. The reporting side writes one verified
story a day and keeps the Alaska AI Docket, a public record of
21 AI infrastructure decisions in the state, 93 source
documents on file, and for each one the plain answer to who decides it and whether an
Alaskan still gets a say. It is published as
open data under CC BY 4.0 so anyone can check the
work or build on it. The studio side builds
Alaska businesses an agentic operating system,
its flagship product, a package of 1 to 1000 AI agents working together to automate every
possible aspect of the business.


Deadline alerts, in your inbox


One email when a public comment window opens or a decision is about
to land. Nothing else, ever. Unsubscribe any time.
