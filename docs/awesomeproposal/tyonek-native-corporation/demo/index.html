Affiliate past performance completeness check

Tyonek Native Corporation  /  Prepared by Alaska AI  /  August 2026

This is a scripted demonstration of the build the field study proposes. It is not a live system, it is connected to nothing, and it holds none of Tyonek's documents. Every entity name on this screen comes from public records. Every registration number in these scenarios is an obvious placeholder written as DEMO, never a real UEI or CAGE, because inventing one that looked real would be the exact error this build exists to prevent. The one real registration number on this page is your parent UEI, in the sources at the foot, which is public on USASpending. The solicitation number is real too, FA8773-25-R-0002, quoted from the GAO decision.

Pick a bid that names affiliate past performance

Three scenarios, all built on your own registrations. One uses a real solicitation. Two are illustrative, and they say so, because we would have to invent a solicitation number to pretend otherwise.

Entity names come from USASpending and from tyonek.com. Nothing else on this screen is drawn from a real proposal, because we have never seen one.

"Affiliate companies, sister companies, teaming arrangements, joint venture agreement, etc., will be considered, provided that sufficient documentation is included in the proposal. The prime contractor must demonstrate that the affiliate will perform significant and critical aspects of the contract if awarded. Proposal documentation should include a copy of the signed arrangement such as documented affiliation, a copy of the teaming agreement, a copy of the joint venture agreement, etc. If the required information is not included in the proposal, the proposed affiliate companies, sister companies, joint venture companies, etc. shall not be evaluated or considered." Quoted in full from RFP FA8773-25-R-0002, recorded in GAO decision B-424364. The five checks below are that paragraph turned into a rule.

Verdict source

A fixed rule, version 1.4. No model runs in this step. The rule takes the entities the bid intends to cite, compares what the solicitation demands against what is on file, and returns the same answer every time it is given the same inputs.

present the only pass missing expired ambiguous

One colour covers everything that is not present, because the build treats them the same. Expired is not present. Ambiguous is treated exactly like missing. Anything not affirmatively verified against a page in a document reports as missing, so a gap can never hide behind a blank.

The one page a person signs before the proposal goes out

This is the handoff. It is the whole product of the check, and it is what lands in front of a human.

Roles named on this page come from the cost drivers in the field study. Roles on this page are roles, not people. We did not put any individual's name on a demonstration gap list.

Two places use a model, and neither one can move a verdict

Both sit at the outer edges. The completeness decision in the middle is one list compared against another, so a rule does it, every case can be tested, and it gives the same answer every time.

Model step one, before the rule

Reads the scanned page as a document is filed

A signed agreement arrives from wherever it is held today. The model proposes what the document is, who signed it and when it expires, copying the text it found and pointing at the page. It refuses to answer without a page reference.

document  executed affiliation, proposed
found on  page 7 of 9
quoted  "executed this 12th day of March 2026"
expires  no answer, no expiry appears on any page

A person confirms this once, at filing, never once per bid. The confirmed fact is what the rule reads later, so the model sits upstream of the rule and never inside it.

Model step two, after the human gate

Drafts prose from facts a person already approved

Once the gap list is signed, the affiliate narrative is drafted from the approved facts only. It stops where the judgement starts.

Tyonek Technical Services is an affiliate of the offeror. The affiliation is documented in an executed agreement filed under UEI DEMO-S1 and CAGE DEMO-S1, signature page 7 of 9, confirmed at filing on 12 March 2026.

[Work share statement, left blank. Stating that this affiliate will perform significant and critical aspects of the contract is a representation about future performance, so a person at Tyonek writes it and owns it.]

A person rewrites and owns every line of this. Nothing here is a verdict, and no draft changes a go or a no go.

A third use is possible, a second reading of the solicitation to flag where it disagrees with yours. The study leaves it out, because reading the provision takes a capture manager about ten minutes, which we are assuming rather than measuring, and a redundant read does not earn its keep at your bid volume.

How it sits in your operation

Three steps. The work does not disappear, it moves from remembering to verifying.

01
At filing, once per document
A signed affiliation, teaming or joint venture agreement is indexed once, wherever it came from. The scanned page is read, a person confirms what it is and when it expires, and that confirmed fact serves every bid afterwards. This happens once, never once per bid.
02
At bid time, when the check runs
The rule runs against the entities you intend to cite, resolved on UEI and CAGE only. Names are never matched, so two registrations beginning the same way can't be confused with each other. Every verdict keeps the rule version, the source document and the page it came from, so a debrief question months later has a record to answer it rather than a memory.
03
Before submission, a named person signs
Every open item is closed or the affiliate comes out of the proposal, and both of those are a person's call. There is no override in the build, so nothing goes out on a system's say so. The signature is required even on a clean run, because a clean run is a claim someone has to own.

One row of the work is deliberately untouched. Judging and stating that an affiliate will perform significant and critical aspects of the contract is a contractual representation about future work share, and it belongs to a person at Tyonek. A system that made that call for you would be a liability rather than a feature.

What this demonstration deliberately does not do. It makes no maintenance sign off and no return to service call, because 14 CFR 145.213(d) reserves that to a certificated mechanic or repairman and 14 CFR 43.9 makes the signature itself the approval. It carries no AS9102 first article content and no quality records, because if that material is export controlled a hosted model has to decrypt it to read it, and we read 22 CFR 120.54 as putting that outside the carve out, which your export control people would rule on before anyone wrote code. It touches no government property record. It predicts nothing and it forecasts no maintenance. Each of those is scoped out in the study, with the reason written down.
