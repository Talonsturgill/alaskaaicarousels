A product idea for Within the Wild Adventure Company (Tutka Bay Lodge)

Every Tutka Bay arrival question gets a staff-ready reply draft

A staff-reviewed workspace where a guest's pre-arrival question becomes a draft reply for the route they plan to take to the lodge.

Tutka Bay Lodge is reachable only by water taxi from Homer or floatplane from Anchorage, and the team already helps arrange travel, including for cruise arrivals. That makes route questions a natural first job for a reviewed reply draft. See the research

    The research behind the offer

    Build plan, alternatives, and dependencies

    Where AI earns its place

    Reads the pasted guest message, picks out the arrival route, and drafts a reply from the approved route wording, leaving unknowns as questions.

    The systems it needs

    Starts as a paste-in workspace the team opens in a browser. No connection to the lodge's reservation system or inbox is assumed or proposed for the first slice.

    The human decision stays here

    Nothing is sent by the tool. A team member edits and sends from the lodge's own email. Cruise and unclear requests are held for staff.

    Buying, configuring, or building

    The first slice is a configured workspace using the lodge's own route wording. A hosted guest-messaging product is an alternative the lodge can compare after the pilot.

    Website FAQ page on routes to the lodge

    Guests find the basics without writing. Static text does not answer one guest's party, dates or cruise connection. Keep it; the desk drafts replies for the questions the page can't settle.

    Saved email templates

    Free and already familiar to the team. Staff still pick, merge and edit each template by hand for every guest. Use the templates as the starting wording inside the desk.

    Hosted guest-messaging chatbot such as HiJiffy

    Answers across chat channels at any hour. Subscription, setup, and reply content the lodge must still write and keep current. Compare after the pilot if guest chat volume is high.

    Dependencies

    Lodge staff supply current route wording, transfer partners and any rules about timing.
