    The workflow
    01

    The desk drafts a reply from published facts and asks for the ship's all-aboard time.

    Unknown today: A guest asks by email or at the terminal whether a tour fits their ship's schedule.

    A Welcome Agent approves any reply that confirms a time.
    02

    The desk quotes the published window-seat and 300 pound policy, then routes the case to an agent with the guest's words.

    Unknown today: Seating, weight and mobility questions reach staff as free text.

    Agents decide every accommodation and every boarding exception.
    03

    The desk compares the Taku lodge flight with the glacier-only flight using published facts only.

    Unknown today: The same tour-comparison question is answered one message at a time.

    Staff review the answer card weekly and edit wording.
    Build plan, alternatives, and dependencies

    Where AI earns its place

    Reads the guest's question and the published tour facts, then drafts a short reply and a staff note. It never confirms availability, weather or boarding.

    The systems it needs

    Runs as a small page on the Wings website. Reservation availability stays in the existing booking system, which this pilot does not connect to.

    The human decision stays here

    Every reply that confirms a time, seat, accommodation or price is approved by a Welcome Agent before the guest sees it.

    Buying, configuring, or building

    The pilot is a configured workspace on the published tour facts, so Wings owns the answer card and wording. A bought chat product could replace the page later.

    Static FAQ page

    Free and already published. It can't combine a guest's ship, tour and mobility question into one reply. Keep it as the source the answer card quotes.

    Hosted tour chatbot

    Ready-made website chat; vendors report strong results for tour operators. Fit with Wings' booking system and its approval rules is unknown. Compare after the pilot's staff-approved answers show which questions repeat.

    Dependencies

    A Welcome Agent reviews the first two weeks of drafted replies.

    Wings supplies current tour lengths and all-aboard rules for the answer card.

    Research

    Why Wings Airways

    A website answer desk that turns a guest's ship-day question into a short reply drafted from Wings' published tour facts, with seating, weight and mobility questions held for a Welcome Agent.

    Wings flies from downtown Juneau steps from the cruise docks and schedules tours around ship departures, which is the question this desk answers first. Similar tour operators run catalogue-trained assistants for repeated questions.
