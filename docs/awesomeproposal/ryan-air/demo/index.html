How it sits in your operation

STEP 1

Your agent counts what's on hand, exactly as the Bethel Lead Cargo Agent posting already describes. Nothing about the physical work changes, because that count is the ground truth everything else rests on.

STEP 2

The desk compares and drafts. New lines against yesterday, the storage date off your published three day rule, the template filled. Nothing leaves the building at this point.

STEP 3

Your agent releases or holds. A notice exists only after that click, and every released one is stored with the time it went. That stored record is what your storage clause rests on.

For the first two weeks the desk would run alongside the counter and send nothing at all, so you can read what it would have said before anyone receives a word of it.

What this demonstration does not do, on purpose

  • No voice agent on the station phone and no chatbot answering shippers. Every question they would field is a money statement, and a tribunal has already held that a carrier owns what its own website tells a customer.
  • No rate quoting to anyone. Your tariff runs four weight brackets, a $30.00 minimum and 6.25 percent federal excise tax, so a quote is arithmetic with modifiers riding on it. That belongs in a calculator you can test, not in an arrival notice.
  • No writes to Takeflite. The desk reads one daily list, and we would hold no Takeflite credentials.
  • Nothing in dispatch or weather. The cost of an error there is a human life and the human relay is the safety control.
  • No autonomous sending, ever. Nothing reaches a consignee without a click from a person standing at Bethel.
