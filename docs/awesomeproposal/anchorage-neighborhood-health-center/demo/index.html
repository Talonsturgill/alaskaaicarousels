03  /  THE DATE CALCULATOR

Your sliding fee rules, computed instead of remembered

The expiry trigger in your published sliding fee policy is arithmetic on a date. That makes it a rules problem, so it gets a rules fix, with tests. Change the inputs and watch it work.

Today for this demonstration is August 6th 2026, the day the study was prepared.

    One year means the same date the following year. Six months means the same day of the month six months on, moved back to the last day of that month when the day does not exist. Thirty days means thirty days. That convention is a decision rather than a fact, so it lives in the tests below where you can see it and change it.

    04  /  HOW IT SITS IN YOUR OPERATION

    Three steps, and a person on every one that matters

    STEP 01

    It listens, it does not replace

    It watches for one event, a completed online registration. Nothing changes about how someone registers, what your form asks, or where the answers go. On any failure the patient gets the same links and the same callback they get today.

    STEP 02

    It sends what you already send

    The same intake links, in the languages you already publish each form in, at the moment the registration lands instead of somewhere inside your published one to two business day window. Two reminders follow on a fixed clock, then the ladder stops and hands the record to a person. Every send is logged with a timestamp and a result.

    STEP 03

    A person decides, every time

    Your staff review the packet, make the sliding fee determination against the documents, and call to book the appointment exactly as they do now. Software computes dates. It never makes a determination, never books, and never answers a clinical question.

    The later phase, switched off and not part of this ask

    Two supervised model steps sit in a later phase of the study, reading what a patient types into candidate values on a form, and answering the narrow set of administrative questions you already publish. Neither is in phase one. Neither gets funded unless your own measurements say it should.

    Off, and it stays off. This demonstration performs nothing from the later phase. Three things stay out at every phase, your ambient scribe which is working and is not ours to disturb, live spoken interpretation, and clinical triage.
