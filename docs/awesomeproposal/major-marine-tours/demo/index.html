Desk Answer

The reservations desk picks a branch, enters what the booking contains, and gets back a date, a fee or an eligibility, with the sentence off your own page to read out loud.

This is a scripted demonstration of the build the Field Study scoped and did not recommend funding yet. It runs entirely inside this page, it is built from Major Marine's own public pages, and it is not connected to your booking system, your reservations desk or any guest record. Nothing here is fetched live and nothing here is sent anywhere.

There is no model in this

No AI runs here. Every answer below comes out of a decision table. The same inputs give the same answer every time, the input space is small enough to test end to end, and there is nothing to train, nothing to tune, and no score anyone has to take on trust.

It never guesses. Where your pages carry no rule for a combination, the table refuses to answer and routes the call to a named owner. That refusal is the correct output rather than a failure, and this demonstration shows several of them on purpose.

It never talks to a guest. Desk Answer is a lookup. It has no voice, it writes nothing in your company's voice, and it never pretends to be a person. It hands one published sentence to your agent and your agent reads it.

The acceptance bar, run in your browser

04

How it sits in your operation

Three steps, and the third one is the reason we scoped it this way.

STEP ONE

The guest asks, the agent enters the booking

The agent picks a branch and fills the two to four fields that branch asks for. No name, no card, no booking reference. The table sees the shape of the booking and nothing about the person.

STEP TWO

It answers, or it hands the call to a person

A published answer comes back with the sentence and the link. Where your pages are silent it returns nothing, names the owner of that branch, and writes one log line so the gap gets counted instead of guessed at.

STEP THREE

A person still decides

Whether to make an exception is a judgement about a guest and about your business. The table has no authority to grant one and it never offers. That row of the workflow is deliberately untouched.
