    The refusal

    A score is a property of a candidate. It is never a property of a person.

    This is the part of the build worth arguing about, so it is written down here and not only hidden behind a button.

    • Nothing resolves on a threshold. Every candidate lands in a state called matched, unconfirmed and stays there. There is no score at which the software acts, and we would not add one at any number you named.
    • There is no bulk resolve. Try the two controls at the top of the work queue. They are in the demonstration because we want you to press them and see what comes back.
    • Confirming requires a reason. Not a checkbox. A sentence the next person can read in six months when they are asking why this record changed.
    • The matcher has no write permission on a Shareholder record. It reads records and writes candidates. It can't record a confirmation at all, so the guarantee is not a policy somebody could relax later under a deadline.

    Here is the failure the whole build is shaped around. A wrong match takes the name off the Address Unknown List while the Shareholder still receives nothing. The roster gets shorter, the reporting looks better, and the only person who would notice is the one you already can't reach.

      The invite

      What a confirmed Shareholder gets, on a phone, with no signal

      Confirm a record in the work queue, then release the invite by hand. Flip the signal off before you answer and watch what the phone does with it.

      Offline is a requirement here, not a nicety

      The AIRRAQ fiber network covers 13 Western Alaska communities. The Alaska FiberOptic Project, reported on Calista's own news page, adds seven more Y-K villages, with construction expected to be completed by December 31, 2027. Much of the region runs on satellite in the meantime. An invite that needs a live connection to accept is an invite a large part of the region can't accept, so the answer is stored on the phone and sent when the connection comes back.

      It starts a request. It does not move money.

      Tapping a button sends a request to Shareholder Services. No bank detail is entered on this screen, no payment instruction is created, and no distribution changes until a person at Calista finishes it through the process you already run. Payment movement was not scoped and this demonstration performs none.

      This screen is in English, deliberately

      We would not build a Yugtun version of it and we did not generate one for this demonstration. Machine and outsourced translation of Alaska Native languages has a poor documented record in this exact region. Calista already staffs that ability inside Shareholder Services, where the receptionist posting says ability to speak Yupik is strongly preferred, and we will not sell anything that quietly displaces that person.

      How it sits in your operation

      Three steps, and a person is standing on the second one

      01

      The queue fills itself

      The published roster and the returned mail become the work list the day they exist. Nobody retypes a name into a spreadsheet. Nothing about a Shareholder changes at this step, and nothing can.

      02

      A person decides, every time

      Candidates wait at matched, unconfirmed. Someone in Shareholder Services opens the evidence, then confirms or rejects with a reason in their own words. There is no bulk action, and no threshold anywhere that resolves a record.

      03

      The record carries its own history

      Who decided, when, and the exact evidence in front of them, on every line. If a decision turns out wrong you can find it, and you can find it by name rather than by guessing.

      Scope

      What this demonstration will not do, because the study did not scope it

      • No model runs anywhere in this page. Phase one of the build contains none either. The matching you see is rules with points, which is exactly why every one of them prints with its evidence beside it.
      • No translation of any kind. Not here, not in phase one, not in phase two, not at any price.
      • No heir determination. The build can assemble evidence and link every line to its source image. Deciding who an heir is stays with the people who do it now.
      • No payment movement. The invite starts a request and a person finishes it.
      • No voice agent and no phone answering. It was not scoped and we are not going to demonstrate something we did not propose.
      • Nothing is sent to anyone. Not from this page and not from the build. Every outgoing message waits for a person to release it.
