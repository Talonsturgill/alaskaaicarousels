02  How it sits in your operation

Three steps, with a person on every one of them

You more than double hotel staffing for summer to make sure guests have caring, personal experiences at your properties, and the roster starts with Seasonal Front Desk Clerks. Those are the people this is for.

STEP ONE

One named person changes one card

A front desk supervisor owns the cards day to day. They edit one card, the change is dated and signed, and all three desks are saying the same thing that afternoon. Nothing edits itself, nothing is generated.

STEP TWO

The calendar decides, not memory

Every window above comes from a window you already publish. 21 August is past or it is not. Nobody has to hold three calendars in their head, and every case can be tested before anyone relies on it.

STEP THREE

The clerk answers the guest

A clerk reads a card and says it in their own words. Anything genuinely new still goes to a supervisor, which is the right answer, and your desks are staffed 24/7 anyway.

A guest never talks to this. It sends nothing, books nothing, prices nothing, decides nothing. It holds answers your people agreed on, shows the ones that are true today, and prints them. Every answer that reaches a guest passes through a person first, every time, because there is no other path through it.
