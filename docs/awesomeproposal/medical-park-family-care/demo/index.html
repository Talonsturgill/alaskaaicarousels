A patient opens this on a phone at 8:40 on a Monday. It answers five things Medical Park has already written down, in Medical Park's own words, and it hands everything else to a person.

Every answer inside it was copied by hand from a page on mpfcak.com that we read on August 1, 2026, and the source address is printed under each one. There is no AI anywhere in it, because phase one of the study has no AI in it.

Why it behaves that way

Every answer on that screen is a row in a file your staff edit

Nothing on the patient screen was written by us and nothing was written by a model. Each answer is your own sentence, copied, with a source address, a last reviewed date and an owner attached to it. Correcting a line is an edit to that file, never a call to us and never a deploy.

The badge on every card says the same thing on purpose. A patient who can see it is a lookup trusts it differently from one who suspects a chatbot, and your staff can audit the whole thing in an afternoon.

The scope, stated as refusals

What it will not do, in any version we would build

  • No AI. Phase one is a fixed list of answers, a form and a counter.
  • No booking. It holds no schedule and it writes to no system.
  • No triage, no symptom questions, no advice. A clinical word stops it.
  • No after-hours contact. It points at the pediatric triage line and the adult routes you already run, then stops.
  • Nothing about a specific person. It holds no records, so it can't check a policy, a prescription or a balance.
The refusal is the feature

A symptom word beats a topic word, always

Type "my knee hurts" into the box on that screen. The page has a records answer and an appointment answer and it uses neither, because the clinical list is checked before the topic list. Your contact page already says where a question like that goes, so the refusal quotes you rather than improvising.

A page that answers a clinical question badly is worse than no page at all. This one is built so that outcome is unreachable rather than unlikely.

    How it sits in your operation

    Three steps, and a person is still on every one that matters

    Step one

    Your words go into a file you own

    Each answer is copied from your pages, given an owner and a review date, and checked by whoever owns it before the page goes live. Your staff edit that file without calling us. The page has no other source, so it can't say anything you have not approved.

    Step two

    The page answers only what has one fixed answer

    Insurance list, same day, refills, records, after hours. Anything clinical, anything about one specific person, anything it does not hold, it stops and hands the patient to your phone or your portal. It never guesses and it never reassures.

    Step three

    Your front desk works the list, and nothing else moves

    What the page can't answer lands as a filled-in row with a name, a number, a reason and a window. A person reads it and a person calls. Nothing books, nothing writes to your record system, nothing goes out without a human doing it.
