    Staff confirm each brief before it informs a patient.
    02

    Build plan, alternatives, and dependencies

    Where AI earns its place

    Reads the pasted eligibility and benefits text, extracts percentages, frequency limits and remaining annual maximum, compares them with the planned procedure and drafts the brief, patient note and flags.

    The systems it needs

    First slice is a paste-in workspace with no connection to the practice management system or payers. Direct payer and system connections are a proposed later step and would need Nizich's system and payer list.

    The human decision stays here

    Staff paste the data, confirm each brief, approve patient notes and make every payer call. Nothing is sent to a patient automatically.

    Buying, configuring, or building

    If Nizich's practice management system and payers are supported, a bought verification tool may beat a custom brief. This slice fits when staff want a lighter brief in their own wording; compare both before committing.

    Overjet AI insurance verification

    Automatic checks across 1,000+ payers and practice systems, per its case studies. Subscription pricing and Nizich system compatibility are unknown. Check first; choose it if it covers Nizich's payers and system.

    Payer portals and phone calls

    No new cost and authoritative answers. Staff time per patient, with hold times and incomplete history. Remains the fallback for every flagged plan.

    Features in the existing practice software

    No extra tool to learn. Eligibility features are unknown and may show partial detail. Ask the vendor before building.

    Dependencies

    Staff paste eligibility text from the portal or payer response into the workspace.

    Nizich agrees which visit types and plan details the brief should cover.

    Ten to twenty de-identified past plans are available for testing.

    Nizich confirms who retrieves the payer text and how long that takes.

    Limits and our response

    Sample records are made up; no live AI or Nizich systems are used in the demo.

    Estimates depend on payer data and are not a guarantee of payment.

    Nizich's verification volume, current tools and payer mix are unknown.

    Live use means pasting patient eligibility data into an AI service, so a HIPAA-appropriate vendor arrangement and Nizich approval are needed before any real patient text is pasted.

    Both industry offices used connected, automated verification tools, not a paste-in workspace; their hours and completeness figures are vendor-reported and don't transfer to this slice.
