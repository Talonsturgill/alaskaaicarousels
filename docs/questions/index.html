Which Alaska AI infrastructure decisions can the public comment on right now?

  1. AIDEA's free state land for a Mat-Su data center park by AUG 19

    Anyone can comment on the DNR preliminary decision to transfer the land to AIDEA through 5 p.m. August 19, 2026, on the DNR website. Commenting also buys standing, because the notice says only people from whom the division receives timely written comment during this window will be eligible to appeal the Final Finding and Decision. This is the one open comment room among the tracked decisions.

    Comment on the record

  2. The Dixon Glacier diversion into Bradley Lake by AUG 28

    Anyone may file a comment, a motion to intervene or a protest with FERC in docket P-8221-124 until 5:00 p.m. Eastern on August 28, which is 1:00 p.m. Alaska time. FERC's eFiling system takes filings online and the notice also gives a mailing address.

    Comment on the record

How reliable is the Alaska AI Docket, and can I reuse it?

Every fact carries the document it was checked against. 20 decisions rest on 82 source documents, with 133 dated change notes recording how each one moved, and a daily routine re-fetches a primary source whenever a date comes near. Nothing enters on rumour, and what can't be verified is dropped rather than published softly. It is open data under CC BY 4.0. See the data for the schema and the license, and the source archive for every document.
