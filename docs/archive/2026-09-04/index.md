# The Half That Lasts. Alaska's quiet land decision.

> The state held two public meetings on a proposed 19,950 acre transfer to AIDEA near Houston and took no testimony at either.

Published 2026-09-04 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-04/

## The story

19,950 acres. Three state notices. None says data center.

The state's own words go this far and stop.

Three notices govern the conveyance. July 10th, August 19th and August 24th, 2026. The furthest any of them goes on use is "to create a multi-use industrial and energy development district". The July notice states no intended use at all.

One window is carrying two decisions.

About 19,950 acres would go to AIDEA under a noncompetitive public and charitable use conveyance, case. A Draft Land Classification Order,, rides the same window. The land is reported to transfer at no cost. Both close on the same date.

The ground was here before the file number.

Most of it changes what it is for.

About 15,100 acres now designated Forestry, Habitat and Water Resources would be reclassified as Settlement. A tenant can arrive or never arrive. A classification stays until somebody changes it back.

Three named uses, and one with nothing in it.

Forestry, Habitat and Water Resources are what this ground is classified for today. Settlement is what the order proposes. AIDEA's plan lists possibilities and not commitments, data centers among them. No tenant, job number, energy need or water use has been named.

More than 2,000 people have already written in.

Scoping opened July 10th, 2026 and the original close was August 19th. More than 2,000 written comments had arrived by August 24th, by the department's own count. Two informational meetings were held and neither took testimony. Houston's council voted unanimously against the process.

The acres are known. The place is not.

A survey township is 36 sections of one square mile. The conveyance is about 19,950 acres, which is 31.2 sections of the 72 in Township 18 North and Township 19 North. Where those acres sit has not been published.

Comment closes 5:00 p.m. Monday, September 14th, 2026.
