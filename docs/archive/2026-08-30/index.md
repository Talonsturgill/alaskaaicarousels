# Eight Machines, One Sheet

> AI in Alaska usually means data centers and gigawatts. In the state's rural health awards it means a note taker for about $25,500.

Published 2026-08-30 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-08-30/

## The story

The robot is in Ketchikan. The stroke reader is everywhere.

TWELVE BOROUGHS CUT OUT OF ONE STATE SHEET

185 awards. Eight machines.

Announced on four Fridays since August 7th, across all 30 boroughs and census areas. Eight of them buy a machine that reads, decides or flies.

The biggest machine is a handle.

PeaceHealth Ketchikan Medical Center takes it for the first robotic assisted minimally invasive surgery in Southern Southeast, serving four boroughs across water. A surgeon drives it.

Statewide means nobody can point.

The Alaska Stroke Coalition takes $3,156,626 for an AI imaging network across 21 acute care hospitals, running on existing systems with no new imaging hardware. The state publishes no list of which 21. MapHabit and the Statewide Independent Living Council are statewide too.

A planning year buys a map.

Tanana Chiefs Conference takes $250,000 to plan drone pharmacy from a Fairbanks hub to 42 Interior villages across 94,000 square miles. It is one of 19 planning awards. No aircraft is bought.

The count of eight is a keyword search of the state's own award titles and summaries. It is not a state category. Every dollar here is preliminary and the state says minor adjustments may occur.

The first Friday had none of them.

August 7th moved $4.5 million across 19 projects and not one of the eight was in it. August 21st moved $130 million across 105 projects and five of the eight were. The machines arrived in the middle of the month, not at the start.

Which of the eight would you fund at scale next year?
