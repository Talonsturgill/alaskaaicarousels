# Governed First, Alaska Native Health and AI

> Data sovereignty is not a privacy setting.

Published 2026-07-23 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-07-23/

## The story

In Alaska Native health, the Tribe decides what AI runs.

Sovereignty means the Tribe governs its own data.

In Alaska, that record is life and death.

One is live. One is proposed.

Two rule sets, one authority.

Alaska Native institutions convened this.

In 2022, a third had never heard of AI.

This rests on one paper.

Adopt fast. Govern first.

## What we verified

- A symposium-perspectives paper on AI/ML in Alaska Native healthcare was published May 23, 2026 in the International Journal of Circumpolar Health (doi 10.1080/22423982.2026.2677426). [International Journal of Circumpolar Health (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- The 2025 Anchorage symposium had 31 participants. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- Symposium participants by organization, ANTHC 38%, Southcentral Foundation 27%, Maniilaq Association 15%, Stanford 8%, CDC Arctic Investigation Program 8%, Other 4%. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- Symposium participants by role, researchers 27%, IT/programming 24%, leadership 17%, clinicians 12%, compliance 5%, other 15%. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- Alaska's air-evacuation rates are cited as at least 40 times higher than anywhere else in the US. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- Over 80% of Alaska communities are not connected to the road system. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- In the 2022 baseline, 35% of respondents said they had never heard the term AI/ML. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- The paper reports that overall knowledge, use, and awareness of AI/ML increased substantially between the 2022 and 2025 symposia. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- The governance approach references the CARE principles (Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics) and the FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- The system operates under Indigenous data sovereignty, the right of a federally recognized Tribe to govern the collection, ownership, and use of its own data. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- A rural tribal health organization has implemented a Cerner AI Scribe in primary care visits. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- ANTHC shared a statewide organizational AI use policy. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- A medevac decision-support model is under development (proposed, not yet deployed). [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- The paper's authors span Southcentral Foundation, Stanford University, the Alaska Native Tribal Health Consortium, and Maniilaq Association. [IJCH (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13202658/), primary document.
- A companion community-engaged AI framework paper for the Alaska Tribal Health System was published April 7, 2025 in Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence. [Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence (via PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12009764/), primary document.
