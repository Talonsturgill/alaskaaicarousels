# A Year After Halong, the Map Under the AI Forecast

> NOAA's 16-day AI forecast now finishes in about 40 minutes. Y-K Delta airport stations have gone days or weeks with no observations.

Published 2026-10-17 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-10-17/

## The story

Forecasts now run on AI. The map under them has holes.

An AI forecast starts from a map of measurements.

NOAA fine-tuned its AI model, AIGFS, on its own Global Data Assimilation System analyses. That system puts surface, balloon, aircraft, buoy, radar and satellite observations onto a three-dimensional grid.

Days or weeks with no observations.

Since April, every airport weather station in the Y-K Delta region has gone days or weeks without observations at times, climatologist Rick Thoman told KYUK.

The models had the track. They missed the shift east.

Global models forecast Halong's track into the Bering Sea well but failed to forecast its eastward shift, Thoman said. A lack of balloon launches across Western Alaska may have played a role, KYUK reported.

Saltwater ran more than 15 miles onto the tundra.

More than 1,500 people were forced from their homes. In Kipnuk, 90% of buildings were damaged or destroyed, KYUK reported.

What got faster. What went missing.

"We need ocean observations."

"Speed and skill are not the same as trustworthiness."

AI sea ice forecast systems barely existed a decade ago and now number in the dozens, researchers wrote in Eos on October 9th. The observations needed to build and test them remain sparse across both poles.

Where should Alaska's next observation go?

"I'm not just talking more [FAA] weather stations."

- NOAA says AIGFS shows a significant reduction in tropical cyclone track errors at longer lead times. [NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-deploys-new-generation-of-ai-driven-global-weather-models), primary document, 2025-12-17.
