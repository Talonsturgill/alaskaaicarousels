# Alaska's AI budget order changes hands December 7th.

> Alaska's order telling every agency to review spending with AI has no end date. A new governor inherits it on December 7th.

Published 2026-11-22 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-22/

## The story

Alaska's AI budget order changes hands December 7th.

Every agency is told to follow the money with AI.

Signed August 4th, 2025. The Office of Management and Budget runs the review every year.

One office runs it. It reaches every agency.

The order makes the Office of Management and Budget responsible. It applies to the executive branch, including departments, boards, commissions and public corporations.

Seven duties. Two of them are AI.

It said reserves would cover seven months.

The order said state savings could fund only seven months of government operations, and that no process existed to review zero base budget spending. Dunleavy said it would "eliminate waste."

It took effect immediately. It runs until revoked.

The office it names gets a new head.

Governor-elect Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins named Alexei Painter to lead the Office of Management and Budget. Painter is leaving the Legislature, where he helped guide lawmakers' budget process. He once worked as an OMB analyst.

On December 7th, the order passes on.

Kreiss-Tomkins takes office December 7th. The order stays in effect until revoked, so keeping it, rewriting it or revoking it becomes his call.

Should every agency keep reading the budget with AI?
