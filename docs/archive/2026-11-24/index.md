# Petersburg's Three Data Center Tests, and the Wider Freeze

> On November 2nd, Petersburg's draft data center freeze had three tests. On November 16th, a 5 to 2 vote widened it to every data center.

Published 2026-11-24 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-24/

## The story

Petersburg drafted three data center tests. Then it widened the freeze.

5 to 2 to cover all data centers. Then 7 to 0.

The amendment would widen the freeze to every data center, on or off the grid, whatever its cooling. The Petersburg Pilot says it closes the opening left for Mazzella's 2-megawatt project. The final reading is scheduled for November 30th at 6 p.m.

The grid, in writing.

No permit unless Petersburg Municipal Power & Light found, in writing, that today's grid could fully supply the load.

No generators for everyday power.

Diesel, gas or any generator was barred as everyday power. Backup during a real outage, or a curtailment the utility directs, was allowed.

Every drop of coolant stays in the loop.

100 percent of coolant and refrigerant circuits had to stay contained and keep recirculating. An Alaska-licensed engineer had to certify the cooling plans.

As of mid-November, Petersburg had no data centers.

The same amendment tied, then passed.

Mayor Bob Lynn and Stanton Gregor voted no on the amendment.
