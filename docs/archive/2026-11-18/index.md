# 261 Minutes When No Flight Left Alaska

> Flights within Alaska kept flying on November 13th while every flight leaving the state was grounded.

Published 2026-11-18 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-18/

## The story

For 261 minutes, no flight left Alaska.

At 11:54 a.m., the FAA stopped departures nationwide.

Every flight leaving the state sat, an Anchorage airport official said. Flights inside Alaska kept flying. The FAA lifted the stop at 4:15 p.m. Alaska time, on Friday, November 13th.

By mid-afternoon, 827 flights had turned back or diverted.

Cirium counted 670 flights sent back to where they took off and 157 redirected to another airport, nationwide. Reportedly only the second all-flight grounding since 9/11.

Three days earlier, the FAA's AI went national.

SMART, an AI traffic advisor built by Air Space Intelligence, launched around Washington on September 21st. On November 10th it went live in the airspace of all 22 air route traffic control centers. House Democrats had asked GAO for an audit on October 13th.

The FAA blamed a router. The AI's maker saw no link.

"A router sent too much data, overloading older air traffic control equipment." "Not based on or a result of the SMART system." "No indication" of a link to the new computer system.

The new AI is counted in minutes saved. The stop took hours.

Inside Alaska, the clock kept running.

2,427,971 square miles of airspace.

The FAA's most northern and western en route center. Its own automation, ATOP and MEARTS. Six foreign neighbor centers, in Japan, Russia and Canada.

The FAA counts its new AI in minutes. Should Alaska ask about the hours?
