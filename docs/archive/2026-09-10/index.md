# One Agenda. Two Items. One Vote.

> A hearing on Anchorage's police AI rule was set for September 1st, 2026. The camera money passed 8 to 4 that night. The rule got no vote.

Published 2026-09-10 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-10/

## The story

UPDATE. $598,998 passed 8 to 4. The rule got no vote.

Three of the four no votes wrote the rule.

Keith McCormick, Daniel Volland and Sydney Scout proposed AO 2026-108. All three voted against the money. They lost 8 to 4 on the night of September 1st, 2026.

What the money is, and what it is not.

The $598,998 comes from federal asset forfeitures, per a memorandum submitted to the Assembly. Chief Sean Case says it moves the crime center to its final location. The Assembly approved $11.8 million last year for the underlying system. ADN reported the Assembly considered a $600,000 ThunderCat contract.

750 is the ceiling, not the count.

The gap from 11 to 238 is wider than the gap from 238 to 750. Registered means APD knows the camera exists. Integrated means APD can pull the feed, through a CORE device from Axon.

Three gates. None of them struck.

ADN reports the ordinance would require Assembly permission before AI with human feature recognition is added to the network. Alaska Public Media reports it would require a warrant before data goes to federal agencies. ADN reports the technology would face a period of temporary approval and annual reporting.

CODE.

"Let's put this into code now, so that when power changes, when the political winds shift, as they always do, we have protections for the people, no matter who is overseeing and implementing the system."

What is already in code. And what is only practice.

Municipal use of facial recognition technology was officially banned by the Anchorage Assembly in 2023, per APD's own page. All scanned license plate data is automatically deleted after 14 days. Traffic camera footage is overwritten every 3 to 5 days. The camera registry is voluntary, the owner keeps control at all times, and APD must obtain permission to access video. APD does not use Flock cameras.

Fourteen days, read twice.

How long a scanned plate lives. Deleted after 14 days, per APD's own page. How long the rule waited. September 1st, 2026 to September 15th, 2026.

The rule is expected back on September 15th, 2026.

Sponsors plan to bring AO 2026-108 back to the Assembly. Public testimony is how a resident gets read into the record.
