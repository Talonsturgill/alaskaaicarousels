## What we verified

- Alaska DNR's Division of Oil and Gas issued a preliminary decision dated May 12, 2026 regarding the Stak Energy lease (ADL 422741). [Alaska DNR Online Public Notices (ADL 422741)](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=223817), report, 2026-05-12.
- The decision would grant STAK Energy Corporation a 50-year lease of 715.4 acres of state land. [Alaska DNR Online Public Notices](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=223817), report, 2026-05-12.
- The site is located adjacent to the Dalton Highway approximately 26 miles south of Deadhorse. [Alaska DNR Online Public Notices](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=223817), report, 2026-05-12.
- The site is near Dalton Highway Milepost 390. [Northern Alaska Environmental Center](https://northern.org/news/stak-energy-campus-what-the-states-lease-documents-reveal-about-scale-risk-and-unanswered-questions/), report, 2026-05-28.
- The proposed use is a commercial natural-gas-powered energy facility including modular high-performance computing (AI data center) units. [Alaska DNR Online Public Notices](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=223817), report, 2026-05-12.
- The project proposes 1 to 3 gigawatts of on-site natural-gas-fired generation. [Northern Alaska Environmental Center](https://northern.org/news/stak-energy-campus-what-the-states-lease-documents-reveal-about-scale-risk-and-unanswered-questions/), report, 2026-05-28.
- The facility would consume 350 to 500 million standard cubic feet of gas per day, roughly 128 to 183 billion cubic feet per year. [Northern Alaska Environmental Center](https://northern.org/news/stak-energy-campus-what-the-states-lease-documents-reveal-about-scale-risk-and-unanswered-questions/), report, 2026-05-28.
- Reported cost is about $500 million for initial site development and more than $10 billion for the total project. [Northern Alaska Environmental Center](https://northern.org/news/stak-energy-campus-what-the-states-lease-documents-reveal-about-scale-risk-and-unanswered-questions/), report, 2026-05-28.
- Site work includes a principal gravel pad of approximately 640 acres, a 1.8-mile gravel access road, and roughly 7.1 million cubic yards of gravel fill. [Northern Alaska Environmental Center](https://northern.org/news/stak-energy-campus-what-the-states-lease-documents-reveal-about-scale-risk-and-unanswered-questions/), report, 2026-05-28.
- The public comment window originally closed at 4:30 p.m. Alaska Daylight Time on June 15, 2026, and was extended to July 17, 2026 due to comment volume. [Alaska DNR Online Public Notices](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=223817), report, 2026-05-12.
- More than 500 public comments were received, with fewer than a dozen in support of the project. [Anchorage Daily News](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/07/07/data-center-critics-flood-alaska-land-managers-with-opposition-to-north-slope-project/), report, 2026-07-07.
- Stak Chief Strategy Officer John Boyle, a former Alaska DNR commissioner, said the vast majority of comments are form letters lacking substance. [Northern Journal](https://www.northernjournal.com/data-center-critics-flood-alaska-land-managers-with-opposition-to-north-slope-project/), report, 2026-07-03.
- The generators could produce a gigawatt or more, roughly 30% more than the peak demand of urban Alaska's entire grid. [Anchorage Daily News](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/07/07/data-center-critics-flood-alaska-land-managers-with-opposition-to-north-slope-project/), report, 2026-07-07.
