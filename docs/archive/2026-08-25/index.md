# 750 Funded. About 15 Running. One Hearing.

> 750 camera feeds is the ceiling Anchorage funded for its police crime center.

Published 2026-08-25 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-08-25/

## The story

750 feeds funded. About 15 running. Hearing September 1st.

One employee. About 15 cameras.

The Real Time Crime Center opened at police properties and Town Square Park. The Assembly had already approved $11.8 million for a system designed to stream up to 750 feeds. The room was one person.

Most of the cameras already exist.

The center is separate from dispatch. It is used once a call comes in, to help officers respond, the police chief says. (C33)

The AI in this system reads plates.

"Let's put this into code now, so that when power changes, when the political winds shift, as they always do, we have protections for the people, no matter who is overseeing and implementing the system."

Four rules, none of them law yet.

The clock barely changes. Who can change it does.

"When my constituents ask, 'What are we collecting? Who can see it? How long do we keep it?' Anchorage code can't answer any of those questions."

The money and the rule arrive on the same day.

Roughly $600,000 to ThunderCat Technology for video displays, sound equipment and video processing technology, from fine and forfeiture funds. The system it upgrades was approved at $11.8 million (C05).

The rule is still cheap to write.

Ask your Assembly member which of the four they will vote for on September 1st.
