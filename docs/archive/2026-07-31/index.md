# Struck. And Still Printed. Alaska's $500 Cap

> Alaska's statute, as codified, still prints a $500 cap on giving to a candidate.

Published 2026-07-31 by Alaska AI. 10 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-07-31/

## What we verified

- The Alaska Public Offices Commission's own published contribution limits table lists what an individual Alaska resident may give a candidate as Unlimited. [Alaska Public Offices Commission](https://apoc.doa.alaska.gov/filer-resources/contribution-limits/), primary document, 2026-07-29.
- The same APOC table lists a non resident individual's limit to an Alaska candidate as Unlimited, identical to an Alaska resident's. [Alaska Public Offices Commission](https://apoc.doa.alaska.gov/filer-resources/contribution-limits/), primary document, 2026-07-29.
- APOC's contribution limits page carried a July 29, 2026 date stamp, two days before this run and twenty days before the primary. [Alaska Public Offices Commission](https://apoc.doa.alaska.gov/filer-resources/contribution-limits/), primary document, 2026-07-29.
- The same APOC table lists a group based outside Alaska as Prohibited from giving to a candidate, while listing a non resident individual as Unlimited. [Alaska Public Offices Commission](https://apoc.doa.alaska.gov/filer-resources/contribution-limits/), primary document, 2026-07-29.
- The Ninth Circuit decided Thompson v. Hebdon on July 30, 2021. [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/30/17-35019.pdf), primary document, 2021-07-30.
- The Ninth Circuit reversed the district court on the individual to candidate limit, the individual to group limit and the nonresident aggregate limit, while upholding the political party to candidate limit. [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/30/17-35019.pdf), primary document, 2021-07-30.
- The Ninth Circuit opinion identifies the struck provision as the $500 annual limit on an individual contribution to a political candidate at Alaska Stat. section 15.13.070(b)(1). [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/30/17-35019.pdf), primary document, 2021-07-30.
- The Ninth Circuit held that Alaska's aggregate nonresident contribution limit violates the First Amendment. [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/30/17-35019.pdf), primary document, 2021-07-30.
- Lead plaintiff David Thompson sent candidate Wes Keller a $100 check in 2015 and the campaign returned it because it had already hit the $3,000 nonresident limit. [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/30/17-35019.pdf), primary document, 2021-07-30.
- Ballot Measure 1 appears on Alaska's August 18, 2026 primary ballot. [Alaska Division of Elections](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election/2026/Primary/SampleBallots/HD1.pdf), primary document, 2026-07-31.
- The printed ballot text says individuals could give $2,000 to candidates and $5,000 to political parties. [Alaska Division of Elections](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election/2026/Primary/SampleBallots/HD1.pdf), primary document, 2026-07-31.
- The printed ballot text also says groups besides political parties could give $4,000 to candidates and that individuals could give $4,000 to joint campaigns for governor and lieutenant governor. [Alaska Division of Elections](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election/2026/Primary/SampleBallots/HD1.pdf), primary document, 2026-07-31.
- The ballot text says the new limits would increase with inflation every ten years beginning in 2031. [Alaska Division of Elections](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election/2026/Primary/SampleBallots/HD1.pdf), primary document, 2026-07-31.
- The Alaska Constitution provides that an initiated law becomes effective ninety days after certification. [Office of the Lieutenant Governor of Alaska, Alaska Constitution Article XI Section 6](https://ltgov.alaska.gov/information/alaskas-constitution/), primary document, 2026-07-31.
