# Seven of 254, Alaska's Rural Health AI

> Alaska's seven rural health AI awards total less than one robotic surgery award in Ketchikan.

Published 2026-11-09 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-09/

## The story

254 rural health awards. Seven include AI.

At least $5.4 million of just under $249 million. About two cents a dollar.

Alaska's Year 2 award is $272,653,149. Below, every Year 1 award at one scale, largest first, one pocket each. Gold is AI.

Two read the body. Three write the chart.

The stroke network reads scans and Mat-Su Regional's headband reads brain waves. Maniilaq, Ptarmigan and Kodiak use AI to write clinical notes. MapHabit maps daily routines and the Independent Living Council sizes up assistive tech.

$3,156,626 to read stroke scans at 21 hospitals.

Two cloud platforms run on the hospitals' existing image systems, with no new imaging hardware. They also flag bleeds, lung clots, and aortic and spinal injury.

Each of the seven names what its AI should do.

What each says its AI should do.

Most of the AI money has no address.

About $4.5 million is filed "Statewide", including the stroke network, whose award names none of its 21 hospitals. The rest lands in four areas.

Year 2 money weighs progress, including ways to measure results.

The state plans a public dashboard of every award by year end. Put the seven AI aims on it.

Which of the seven should the dashboard check first?
