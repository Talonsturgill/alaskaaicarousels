# Fairbanks' data center pause, its fixed end and open start

> In the Mona Lisa Drexler Assembly Chambers on November 10th, all eleven Fairbanks planning commissioners backed a data center pause.

Published 2026-11-26 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-26/

## The story

Ends June 30th, 2028.

The start waits on the Assembly's vote.

Eleven for. None against.

Every commissioner present sent the data center pause to the Assembly with five amendments. The ordinance opens on "growing demand from artificial intelligence."

Five edits. Staff wrote two.

Six months added. Four went to the study.

The time left after the report grew about two months.

About seven and a half months to write the rules.

Counted in days from the ordinance's dates. A projection, not a schedule.

Every week the Assembly waits is a week less to write rules.

Wider, too.

Large now starts at 40,000 square feet of gross floor area, the line Title 18 uses for big entertainment venues, down from 50,000. Both 5 MW power triggers stay. The mayor sees one possible exception.

One kind of project may sit outside the pause.

"If it comes in as purely for a government purpose, I believe that would be not subject to our moratorium and our regulations." MAYOR GRIER HOPKINS, TO KTUU Outside the Eielson fence, he's confident the borough has jurisdiction, KTUU reported.

June 30th, 2028 finds new rules, an extension, or neither.

Which should it be?
