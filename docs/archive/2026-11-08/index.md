# Who Drafted UA's AI Statement?

> University of Alaska regents, your AI statement should have printed its credit line from the first draft.

Published 2026-11-08 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-08/

## The story

Who drafted UA's AI statement?

Claude wrote the first draft, two UAA professors say.

Gregory Hartley and Joel Potter wrote that Anthropic's Claude "generated the initial draft," a fact "concealed for months" until October 9th. They want the regents to "abandon the document."

Five faculty answer it was no secret.

"Anyone who met with administrators and board members knew how the original draft was constructed," they wrote. Adding "a small AI attribution" later was, in their words, "a good move."

Who said what happened when.

What the page would set.

Its stated intent, as Hartley and Potter quote it, is to "embrace artificial intelligence as a strategic enabler."

Against the light, the mark was always in the paper.

Both can be true. That's the problem.

People who met with administrators knew. The attribution on the page came later, a small one, by the rebuttal's own account. Known in the room is not disclosed in public. A mark only insiders can see is a watermark.

Print the credit line on the first draft.

On the page, where readers read it, from the first public draft. Hartley and Potter call the disclosure "too late." The five faculty call the attribution "a good move." Both treat it as owed.

Should every public document say who, or what, drafted it?
