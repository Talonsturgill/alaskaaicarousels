# Section 1200 Is Called Interruption

> Enstar, the gas utility for almost half of Alaska's population, publishes a tariff section numbered 1200 and titled Interruption.

Published 2026-09-11 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-11/

## The story

Cook Inlet storage read 7,648,351 Mcf on September 10th. Design volume is not what the field can deliver on any given day.

Enstar sells gas to almost half of Alaska's population and gets nearly all of its supply from Hilcorp. Southcentral runs more than 70 percent of its electricity on gas, per Alaska Public Media citing ISER.

Enstar president John Sims told state lawmakers in August the utility was about 3 Bcf short of ideal storage.

A failed day carries no number forward. Demand model 2.0 is published in full. Its demand is modeled, not measured.

It is published on Enstar's own tariff page. Enstar says reductions would be phased by customer class under the tariff. The divisions below the top one are not drawn here.

Kenai Mayor Henry Knackstedt met Enstar's president and other Southcentral mayors on August 21st, then told his city council what Enstar described.
