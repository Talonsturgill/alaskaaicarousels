What's Dated in the Houston Land Transfer and What Isn't

Alaska's Houston land transfer has a date for the lawsuit and the inauguration but not for the day 19,950 acres change hands.

The story

Dated. On October 22nd a lawsuit cited seven sections of the Alaska Constitution. On October 26th DNR Commissioner Crowther called approval before December 7th "certainly possible." On December 7th a new governor is sworn in.

Not dated. DNR's final decision. Survey, plat, title and closing. An appeal open to more than 2,000 commenters. The State's answer due 40 days after service.

Crowther said surveying and the appeal period likely push the handoff into the next term.

Inside are Crowther's words, the complaint and its summons, the four steps to a deed and a ledger of what's dated and what isn't.

Should the governor leaving, the one arriving or a judge have the final say if the decision beats December 7th and the deed doesn't?
