# The Ladder Stops at Fifth Grade

> 15 minutes a day. That is the routine technology cap a Kenai Peninsula Borough School District draft would set for kindergarten.

Published 2026-08-09 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-08-09/

## The story

A five year old gets a number. A senior gets an adjective.

One draft. Two kinds of answer.

The AI draft draws no grade line.

The district calls this a new administrative regulation brought forward in relation to the inclusion of AI. Two competing drafts were posted. The committee postponed it on July 6th.

They disagree about the line, not the verb.

One room. Two meetings. No posted outcome.

Seven documents. None adopted on the posted record.

The state framework is a benchmark, not a floor.

Alaska's Strategic Framework, Artificial Intelligence for K-12, sets seven guiding principles. It is the document the district group compared its own material against last fall. No published source makes it binding on the district.

The drafts are still drafts. The dates aren't.

Save this. None of it is settled.
