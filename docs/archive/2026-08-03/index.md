- Graphite One's planned Ohio anode facility is in Conneaut, and the Ohio EPA determined its air permit application complete and commenced technical review in July 2026. [Graphite One Inc.](https://www.graphiteoneinc.com/graphite-one-clears-key-ohio-epa-hurdle-for-planned-ohio-battery-materials-facility/), primary document, 2026-07-16.
- About 80 people attended the July 28, 2026 community meeting in Brevig Mission, hosted jointly by the Native Village of Brevig Mission, the City of Brevig Mission and the Brevig Mission Native Corp. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- KNOM reported that Brevig Mission residents voiced overwhelming opposition to the proposed Graphite One mine at the meeting. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- Brevig Mission is a village of about 400 people and the proposed mine site is about an hour by boat away. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- The proposed mine would be a roughly 1.1 mile wide open pit on state land near the Kigluaik Mountains and the Imuruk Basin. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- The current proposal estimates a 20 year mine life, with the possibility of an extension. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- Graphite One says the project could employ about 250 skilled workers. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers said it needs a full Environmental Impact Statement instead of the lesser Environmental Assessment, delaying federal permitting, which had been expected to wrap up this fall, by at least a year. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- Bering Straits Native Corporation has invested about $2 million in the project, and with the investment came a seat on a project advisory board. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- In November, Brevig Mission's tribe, native corporation and city passed a joint resolution opposing the mine, citing concerns for subsistence resources, cultural integrity, community wellbeing, and long-term sustainability for the region. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- Graphite One Chief Operating Officer Mike Schaffner told the meeting, if we can't put a mine in that honors what you just said, there shouldn't be a mine put in. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
- Darla Tocktoo, tribal office coordinator for the Native Village of Brevig Mission, told Graphite One representatives, I know you really want to dig this mine up for your own money, but we are stopping you for our survival. [KNOM](https://knom.org/2026/07/30/brevig-mission-residents-oppose-graphite-one-mine-at-community-meeting/), report, 2026-07-30.
