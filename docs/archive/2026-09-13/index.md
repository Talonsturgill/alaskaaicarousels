- The short title of H.R. 9340 is the Ratepayer Protection Act. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The reported version of H.R. 9340 carries House Report number 119-814. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 sits on the Union Calendar as number 713. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 was reported in the House on September 10th, 2026 in the second session of the 119th Congress. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-119hr9340rh), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 defines a large-load customer around a peak electric demand of 100 megawatts or more. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The 100 megawatt threshold in H.R. 9340 is measured at a single site or campus. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 reaches customers whose facilities require electric energy primarily to operate information technology infrastructure for data storage and computational applications. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The large-load customer definition in H.R. 9340 applies only to a non-residential consumer requesting or entering a contract on or after the date of enactment. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 would require rates to a large-load customer to be designed to recover the full, incremental cost of any generation, transmission or distribution upgrade needed to serve that customer. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The cost recovery requirement in H.R. 9340 continues to apply if the large-load customer terminates its contract or stops buying power. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 gives each State regulatory authority one year after enactment to commence consideration of the standard or set a hearing date. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 gives each State regulatory authority two years after enactment to complete consideration and make a determination. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- H.R. 9340 excuses a state from the consideration requirement if the state acted before the date of enactment of the subsection. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The first way a state qualifies for the H.R. 9340 exemption is having already implemented the standard or a comparable standard. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The second way a state qualifies for the H.R. 9340 exemption is having conducted a proceeding to consider implementing the standard. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The third way a state qualifies for the H.R. 9340 exemption is that the state legislature has voted on implementation of the standard. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9340rh/html/BILLS-119hr9340rh.htm), primary document, 2026-09-10.
