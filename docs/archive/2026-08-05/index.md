## What we verified

- The ANA final issuance appears in Federal Register Volume 91, Number 143, published Tuesday, July 28, 2026. [Federal Register (Office of the Federal Register)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The document begins at Federal Register page 47241 and runs through page 47243. [GovInfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-28/html/2026-15150.htm), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The issuance stands up three FY 2026 notices of funding opportunity, named EAGLE, the AI3 Action Institute and NCNTTA. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The agency's own document records that multiple commenters objected to replacing SEDS-AK with EAGLE and warned Alaska Tribes could face new barriers. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA responded that Alaska Native communities remain eligible across EAGLE project areas including IDEAS. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA states that the EAGLE IDEAS project area carries forward the spirit of Alaskan self-determination. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA also pledged to continue considering the distinct circumstances and needs of Alaska Native communities in future programming. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- IDEAS stands for Indigenous Designs to Empower and Advance Self-Determination. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- EAGLE has five project areas. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The EAGLE opportunity number is HHS-2026-ACF-ANA-NEG-0120. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- EAGLE was posted on July 28, 2026 and closes on August 27, 2026. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- EAGLE has total program funding of $24,000,000. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- EAGLE expects to make 31 awards ranging from $300,000 to $1,000,000. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/bb965470-bd8d-4529-b30b-39efe6f81147), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The AI3 Action Institute opportunity number is HHS-2026-ACF-ANA-NAI-0035. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- The AI3 Action Institute was posted on July 30, 2026, two days after the Federal Register issuance. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- The AI3 Action Institute is a single award with an award ceiling of $3,500,000 and an award floor of $2,500,000. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- Both EAGLE and the AI3 Action Institute fall under assistance listing 93.612, Native American Programs. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- AI3 eligibility explicitly includes Alaska Native villages as defined in the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act and nonprofit village consortia. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- Applications must be submitted no later than 11:59 pm Eastern Standard Time on the due date, in the listing's own wording. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- ANA states the Institute will complement rather than replace broader social and economic development efforts and will address ethical, practical and governance considerations of emerging technologies. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA describes the AI3 Action Institute as reducing the learning curve for hundreds of Native communities and supporting the thoughtful integration of AI across Indian Country. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- A commenter told ANA that consolidating training and technical assistance into a single NCNTTA agreement equates to a reduction of more than 70 percent in regional staffing capacity. [Administration for Native Americans, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/07/28/2026-15150.txt), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA corrected the commenter and said the NCNTTA funding level is 3.1 million per fiscal year rather than over three years. [GovInfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-28/html/2026-15150.htm), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- ANA frames EAGLE as an evolution of SEDS rather than a cancellation. [GovInfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-28/html/2026-15150.htm), primary document, 2026-07-28.
- The AI3 Action Institute closes on August 27, 2026, the same date as EAGLE. [Grants.gov, HHS Administration for Children and Families](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/8b106d7a-a5ec-4226-b3d4-90bc8c3eade1), primary document, 2026-07-30.
- Tribal Business News reports the new grants replace SEDS and SEDS-Alaska, which for more than 30 years let tribes propose economic development based on local priorities. [Tribal Business News](https://tribalbusinessnews.com/sections/economic-development/15712-ana-opens-27-5m-in-new-tribal-grants-replacing-decades-old-economic-development-program), report, 2026-08-04.
- Tribal Business News reports that ANA has not announced a SEDS funding opportunity for fiscal 2026. [Tribal Business News](https://tribalbusinessnews.com/sections/economic-development/15712-ana-opens-27-5m-in-new-tribal-grants-replacing-decades-old-economic-development-program), report, 2026-08-04.
- The Alaska Federation of Natives called EAGLE a very narrowly targeted program whose funding largely does not meet the needs of Alaska's tribes. [Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/currents/alaska-federation-of-natives-urges-action-ahead-of-deadline-on-key-economic-development-programs/), report, 2026-04-14.
- Tribal Business News characterizes the change as shifting federal support from tribally determined projects to administration defined priorities including greenhouses, microgrids, welding and artificial intelligence. [Tribal Business News](https://tribalbusinessnews.com/sections/economic-development/15712-ana-opens-27-5m-in-new-tribal-grants-replacing-decades-old-economic-development-program), report, 2026-08-04.
