## What we verified

- The FAA awarded Air Space Intelligence an $875 million contract to modernize the national airspace system. [Air Space Intelligence](https://airspace-intelligence.com/), primary document, 2026-06-23.
- Alaska Airlines reported that optimized routes using Flyways saved over 1.2 million gallons of fuel equivalent to 11,958 metric tons of CO2. [Alaska Airlines Newsroom](https://news.alaskaair.com/technology/how-ai-is-helping-alaska-airlines-plan-better-flight-routes-and-lower-emissions/), primary document, 2024-08-09.
- Flyways generates optimized route recommendations for dispatchers and pilots that are safe, ATC compliant, minimize fuel, reduce flight time, and avoid congested airspace. [Alaska Airlines Newsroom](https://news.alaskaair.com/technology/how-ai-is-helping-alaska-airlines-plan-better-flight-routes-and-lower-emissions/), primary document, 2024-08-09.
- Alaska Airlines says Flyways saves it tens of thousands of hours in the air. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- Flyways analyzes air traffic, wind, weather and other factors to anticipate what will happen during flight before it does. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- The FAA contract with Air Space Intelligence is worth $875 million. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- The FAA award to Air Space Intelligence is a 12 year contract. [ExecutiveBiz](https://www.executivebiz.com/articles/faa-awards-asi-875m-air-traffic-modernization-contract), report, 2026-06-23.
- The new FAA system is named SMART for Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes, and Trajectories. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- The contract also covers the Flow Management Data and Services platform known as FMDS. [ExecutiveBiz](https://www.executivebiz.com/articles/faa-awards-asi-875m-air-traffic-modernization-contract), report, 2026-06-23.
- The FAA targeted an initial operational deployment of SMART in fall 2026. [ExecutiveBiz](https://www.executivebiz.com/articles/faa-awards-asi-875m-air-traffic-modernization-contract), report, 2026-06-23.
- Alaska Airlines was the first airline to start using the Flyways system in 2021. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- Alaska Airlines operates roughly 1,500 flights on a typical day. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
- Alaska Airlines says Flyways saves it roughly a million gallons of fuel per year. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/10/nx-s1-5872752/airspace-reboot-alaska-airlines-flyways), report, 2026-08-10.
