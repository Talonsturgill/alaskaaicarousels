# A Say on 5 Megawatts at Eielson, Until September 30th

> You have until September 30th to comment on site work for a 5 megawatt microreactor near wetlands at Eielson Air Force Base.

Published 2026-09-28 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-28/

## The story

The public gets a say on 5 megawatts at Eielson.

A reactor site, east of the runway's south end.

The Air Force wants public input on work at the preferred site for a 5 megawatt Oklo microreactor, near Spruce Lake. It sits near wetlands, so federal law requires the ask first.

Clear it, dig it, fill it, level it.

That is the preconstruction work Air Force officials describe. Their wetland delineation for a draft Environmental Assessment finished in July. KUAC reports the draft is expected late next year.

Oklo would own it. The Air Force would buy the power.

It would supply the base electricity and heat. A 30-year fixed-price contract could follow a Nuclear Regulatory Commission license. Officials hope for 2030, if only an assessment is needed.

Same base. A second process.

Five spots near Eielson, offered to developers.

The Air Force solicitation covered land at JBER, Eielson and Clear, about 4,700 acres in all. Proposals were due June 29th. KUAC reported no project awarded as of mid-September.

5 megawatts, against a 100 megawatt bar.

Separate processes, shown for scale. Nothing in the record says the reactor would serve a data center.

"we are not able to support them because of an insecure energy base at this time"

Chugach Electric and Golden Valley Electric told ADN in May that they can't produce enough electricity for large data centers as Cook Inlet gas dwindles.

Comment by September 30th.
