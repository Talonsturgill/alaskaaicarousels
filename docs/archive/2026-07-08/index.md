# Alaska's AI Land Rush, Four Rooms, One Open Door

> Alaska got more than 500 public comments on a $10 billion North Slope AI data center. Fewer than a dozen said yes.

Published 2026-07-08 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-07-08/

## What we verified

- More than 500 public comments were submitted to Alaska DNR on the Stak Energy lease before the preliminary deadline. source, report.
- Fewer than a dozen of the public comments endorsed the Stak project. source, report.
- DNR extended the ADL 422741 public comment deadline to 4:30 p.m. Alaska Daylight Time on July 17, 2026 (extension notice ID 224224). source, report.
- The original comment deadline on the Stak preliminary decision was 4:30 p.m. on June 15, 2026 (original notice ID 223817, published May 12, 2026). source, report.
- DNR said it extended the comment period because of significant public interest, comment volume, and requests for extension. source, report.
- DNR's Division of Oil and Gas issued a preliminary decision to offer STAK Energy Corporation a 50-year lease. source, report.
- The proposed lease covers a 715.4-acre parcel of state land. source, report.
- The parcel sits adjacent to the Dalton Highway approximately 26 miles south of Deadhorse. source, report.
- The docket is ADL 422741 with applicant STAK Energy Corporation; notice 223817 (published May 12, 2026) is the original preliminary decision notice and notice 224224 (published June 17, 2026) is the extension notice. source, report.
- Stak's John Boyle said the full project would cost more than $10 billion. source, report.
- Stak estimated approximately $500 million for initial site evaluation, engineering, gravel road and pad construction, and initial camp and support infrastructure. source, report.
- Stak proposes 1 to 3 gigawatts of on-site natural gas-fired generation. source, report.
- Northern Journal reports that a gigawatt of generation is some 30% more than the peak demand of urban Alaska's entire grid. source, report.
- Lease documents indicate full simple-cycle operation could consume roughly 350 to 500 million standard cubic feet of gas per day. source, report.
- That daily gas burn annualizes to approximately 128 to 183 billion cubic feet per year. source, report.
- The project would require roughly 7.1 million cubic yards of gravel fill. source, report.
- Plans include a roughly 640-acre gravel pad. source, report.
- Plans include a 1.8-mile gravel access road. source, report.
- John Boyle said most comments were form letters lacking substance. source, report.
- John Boyle is Stak's chief strategy officer and previously served as commissioner of Alaska's Department of Natural Resources from 2023 to 2025. source, report.
- Opposition comments carried subject lines including 'HELL NO! To ANY DATA CENTERS' and 'NO AI'. source, report.
- One commenter urged the state to 'incorporate some AI (Alaskan intelligence) into making this decision'. source, report.
- One commenter noted their message was written 'with my own brain and fingers typing'. source, report.
- Comments arrived from Kodiak, Kotzebue, Seward, Valdez, and the North Slope village of Nuiqsut. source, report.
- Formal opposition or concern also came from the Northern Alaska Environmental Center, the Alaska Public Interest Research Group, and the Alaska chapter of Backcountry Hunters and Anglers. source, report.
- According to Stak, average annual temperatures at the proposed site are 12 F. source, report.
- Stak expects the development to need 10% or less of the water that typical data centers use for cooling. source, report.
- After the deadline passes, DNR will assess the comments before making a final decision on the lease. source, report.
- Stak said in a statement that it 'is committed to being a responsible steward of the land entrusted to us'. source, report.
- The Department of the Air Force is offering approximately 4,700 acres of underutilized land at JBER, Eielson AFB, and Clear Space Force Station for AI data centers. source, report.
- Twelve parcels have been identified for potential development under the program. source, report.
- The Air Force announced the Alaska AI data center Enhanced Use Lease opportunity on April 10, 2026. source, report.
- The Air Force released its Request for Lease Proposal on June 5, 2026. source, report.
- Developer lease proposals were due June 29, 2026. source, report.
- The RFLP identifies five potential parcels in the Eielson AFB area. source, report.
- Four parcels are under consideration around Clear Space Force Station, including one that would connect to the GVEA grid from the Parks Highway. source, report.
- One Eielson-area parcel is less than a mile from North Pole Middle School. source, report.
- Resident Sarah Hollister said living near data centers is described as like having a vacuum cleaner running around the clock. source, report.
- Sarah Hollister raised concern about data center heat discharge affecting permafrost. source, report.
- Hollister pointed out that three of the proposed sites would be expected to use the GVEA grid and asked whether that would affect members' bills. source, report.
- JBER commander Col. Lisa Mabbutt said Alaska is expected to receive up to $8 billion in Air Force investments over the next five years. source, report.
- Under Secretary of the Air Force Matthew Lohmeier toured JBER and Eielson AFB June 22 through 24, 2026. source, report.
- GVEA recommended that any major loads be connected to its 138-kilovolt line. source, report.
- The Air Force plans to choose proposals that minimize and mitigate impact and risk to the surrounding community, with utilities remaining accessible and affordable. source, report.
- GVEA's board will weigh whether to invest in a fuel-based generator during an executive session on July 13, 2026. source, report.
- A new LM6000 unit would cost GVEA about $120 million. source, report.
- An LM6000 can generate between 45 and 58 megawatts depending on the model. source, report.
- Costs to construct a new gas turbine power plant jumped 195% from 2019 to 2026, per a Bloomberg News report cited by the News-Miner. source, report.
- Turbine order waitlists have pushed into the early 2030s, per a Bloomberg News report cited by the News-Miner. source, report.
- Demand for the turbine units has increased due to a surge in large data centers that require a guaranteed power supply, per a Bloomberg News report. source, report.
- At its April 2026 board meeting, GVEA approved $50,000 to reserve an order spot for an LM6000 combined-cycle turbine. source, report.
- GVEA's fuel and purchased power charge rose by about 61 percent effective June 1, 2026. source, report.
- The increase costs the average residential member nearly $46 more per month. source, report.
- GVEA's power plant fuel prices roughly doubled from about $2.50 to $5.10 per gallon. source, report.
- House-Senate conference committee members unveiled a draft compromise AKLNG tax bill on Thursday, July 2, 2026. source, report.
- The draft compromise would replace the state's property tax with a volumetric tax on gas after five years of operation or once flow reaches 500 million cubic feet per day, whichever comes first. source, report.
- The draft extends the construction completion deadline for the gas line and phase one from 2032 to December 31, 2034. source, report.
- Phase one of the project is an 807-mile gas line from the North Slope to Cook Inlet. source, report.
- Glenfarne owns 75% of the Alaska LNG project. source, report.
- Lawmakers are tentatively planning House and Senate floor votes on the compromise bill for Thursday, July 16, 2026. source, report.
- Lawmakers are in a second special session called by Gov. Mike Dunleavy, scheduled to end July 19, 2026. source, report.
- Dunleavy has called the corporate income tax provision a 'poison pill' and pledged to veto any bill that includes it. source, report.
- The governor and the developer have said the S corp tax in the draft will hurt the project's ability to secure financing. source, report.
