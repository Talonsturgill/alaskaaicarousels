# Pitched as data centers. Passed as an inquiry.

> "Seems high" is how AIDEA's January minutes record board member Andrew Guy's view of $20 million for Alaska Data Corporation.

Published 2026-11-12 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-12/

## The story

Pitched as data centers. Passed as an inquiry.

A company and a platform, built on a January vote.

Gov. Dunleavy launched Alaska Data Corporation, an AIDEA subsidiary meant to facilitate distributed data centers, and InvestNorth, a state-sponsored platform for tokenized stakes, some possibly reserved for Alaska residents.

$__________

The resolution reached the board with the amount left blank. Board member Albert Fogle amended his motion to insert $20 million, as staff recommended.

"seems high"

That is how the minutes record board member Andrew Guy's view of the amount. He asked to see a budget for the funds. The resolution then passed unanimously, with one commissioner absent.

Small for the balance sheet. Two years of the budget.

Brefczynski told the board AIDEA has a $1.7 billion balance sheet and a $10 million annual operating budget. He described the $20 million as multi-year capital, with no intent to return for more.

"distributed data centers"

The text of the resolution never says data center. The January discussion did.

"No investment offering"

No specific projects. No construction schedules. No investment amounts. No job totals. No terms for AIDEA's capital.

Four weeks.

The next administration inherits a company capitalized with $20 million and a platform with no offering. The company's president, Brandon Brefczynski, presented the resolution in January as AIDEA's deputy director.

The resolution says feasible first.

Section 7 lets the platform proceed only after the inquiry finds it feasible and in the public interest, and AIDEA's chief financial officer approves.
