# Redoubt Weir. Lit To Install, Dark To Run

> Solar panels and a Starlink system on a lagoon float run an AI salmon counter at Redoubt Lake, 15 miles by boat from Sitka.

Published 2026-08-08 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-08-08/

## What we verified

- The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service listed a fiscal year 2024 Tribal Wildlife Grant to the Sitka Tribe of Alaska titled "Artificial Intelligence for Subsistence Salmon Monitoring and Management". [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-10/service-awards-more-64-million-tribal-wildlife-grants-advance-shared), primary document, 2024-10-16.
- The Redoubt Lake weir was installed on June 14th, 2026 for the 2026 season. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- The weir had counted 7,791 sockeye salmon by June 29th, 2026. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- The department said projected escapement for the 2026 Redoubt season exceeds 40,000 sockeye salmon. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- Salmon Vision reports a mean average precision of 80.2 percent for its detection model. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- Salmon Vision reports detecting 6 salmon species among 17 total species. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- Salmon Vision reports its models were trained on over 5 million annotated frames. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- Salmon Vision publishes its model code under an MIT open research license and its data and annotations under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/about/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- Salmon Vision describes itself as a collaborative effort by the Pacific Salmon Foundation, Wild Salmon Center, Simon Fraser University computing sciences and Lumax Ecological Analytics. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/about/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- Salmon Vision lists the Sitka Tribe of Alaska among its partners, alongside the Alaska Department of Fish and Game and Fisheries and Oceans Canada. [Salmon Vision Collaborative](https://salmonvision.org/), primary document, 2026-08-08.
- That Tribal Wildlife Grant to the Sitka Tribe of Alaska was $200,000. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-10/service-awards-more-64-million-tribal-wildlife-grants-advance-shared), primary document, 2024-10-16.
- The Service described the award as paying to install artificial intelligence enabled video producing automated real time counts of Redoubt Lake sockeye salmon and reducing real time monitoring costs. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-10/service-awards-more-64-million-tribal-wildlife-grants-advance-shared), primary document, 2024-10-16.
- The Service's Alaska release said the Sitka Tribe of Alaska would partner with the Wild Salmon Center and the U.S. Forest Service to install the AI enabled video chutes. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-10/us-fish-wildlife-service-awards-more-13-million-tribal-wildlife-grants-alaska), primary document, 2024-10-17.
- The Service said it was awarding more than $1.3 million in Tribal Wildlife Grants to federally recognized Tribes in Alaska in that round. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/press-release/2024-10/us-fish-wildlife-service-awards-more-13-million-tribal-wildlife-grants-alaska), primary document, 2024-10-17.
- The Alaska Department of Fish and Game raised the annual subsistence limit for Redoubt Bay and Lake sockeye salmon to 100 fish per individual or household. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- The same emergency order set the Redoubt subsistence possession limit at 25 sockeye salmon per individual or household. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- The same order set the Redoubt sport bag and possession limit at 6 sockeye salmon. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- The liberalized Redoubt limits took effect at 12:01 a.m. on July 1st, 2026. [Alaska Department of Fish and Game, Division of Sport Fish](https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR/index.cfm?ADFG=region.NR&Year=2026&NRID=4070), primary document, 2026-06-30.
- Jeff Feldpausch, resource protection director for the Sitka Tribe of Alaska, said there is no funding for the weir right now. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said the Tribe looked high and low for grant sources and all of them deal with systems that need remediation. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said there is lots of money to fix and monitor broken systems but no regular funding for a system that is not broken. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said the computer has learned to count sockeye and the program has reached about a 95 percent confidence interval on counting sockeye through the system. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said a commercial fishery is now taking place at Redoubt that would not happen if the weir were not in place. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said harvesters can now take up to 100 sockeye a year, up from 50, and that this would not have happened without the weir. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said conflicts between people and bears are emerging because bears cannot fish the weir pool away from where people fish in the saltwater. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Feldpausch said runs at Redoubt were significantly depressed in the early 1980s with around 800 sockeye returning. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- KCAW described the Redoubt setup as a white polyethylene plastic chute with a video camera on the side, with a float in the lagoon carrying battery packs, solar panels and a Starlink system. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- KCAW reported that data from the Redoubt system is uploaded online immediately and is accessible from the Tribe's office in Sitka. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- Redoubt Falls is a 15 mile boat ride from downtown Sitka. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
- KCAW identified the Redoubt counting system as coming from the British Columbia based company SalmonVision. [KCAW Raven Radio, reporting by Hope McKenney](https://www.kcaw.org/2026/08/05/theres-no-funding-for-the-weir-right-now-sitka-tribe-of-alaska-introduces-ai-monitoring-at-redoubt/), report, 2026-08-05.
