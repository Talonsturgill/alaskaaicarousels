# Outside AI Money in Alaska's Senate Race, Mapped

> Most Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI employees who gave in Alaska's Senate race live in California. Not one lists an Alaska address.

Published 2026-11-06 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-06/

## The story

No Senate race drew more from AI-company employees. Zero Alaska addresses.

About $119,000 from at least 19 donors.

Peltola, at least $56,000, nearly all from Anthropic employees. Sullivan, about $63,000, plus $35,000 to his True North leadership PAC that the $119,000 leaves out. Figures per the Alaska Beacon.

First among Senate races. Fifth and 14th among candidates.

Mike Rounds of South Dakota tops the candidates at $165,100 with PACs. Sullivan's $98,000 counts his PAC. Most donors gave close to the legal limit.

Count Big Tech too, and the order flips.

Counting Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI alone, Sullivan ranks 5th and Peltola 14th. Add Big Tech employees and Peltola ranks 5th, Sullivan 16th. Close to 150 Big Tech donors gave to Peltola, four to Sullivan.

Same map, second stream. In April the Air Force offered developers land on three Alaska bases for AI data centers.

Most of it moved through joint committees first.

More than half of Peltola's came through the Fish Family Freedom Fund, and Sullivan's mainly through One Team Senate Majority and Sullivan Victory. The Beacon traced it with an AI agent called DataTalk.

Same weeks, separate records.

As the Air Force set site visits at three Alaska bases, executives of Twenty First Century Utilities and Saige Consulting appear in Sullivan's FEC filings. Employer data shouldn't be read as proof of coordinated giving, Campaign Legal Center's Brendan Fischer told the Beacon.

637 votes apart, with about a quarter still to count.

Peltola 48 percent, Sullivan 47.7 percent, 215,912 ballots counted. Ranked choices are tabulated November 18th, with certification targeted for November 25th.

Save this for November 18th.

At least 19 donors, none with an Alaska address. 637 votes apart. The count is still open.
