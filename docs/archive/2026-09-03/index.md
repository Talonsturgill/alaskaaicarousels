# Five Turning, One Still, and a Load With No Number

> Terror Lake carries about 85 percent of the generation on the grid Kodiak Electric Association calls isolated.

Published 2026-09-03 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-03/

## The story

Five turning. One still. A new load with no number on it.

The Navy says the building runs off the existing grid.

What the island runs on.

In March the peak hit just over 31 MW.

The utility's own word for this grid is isolated.

The only number on this load came from the co-op.

A footprint, and no purpose visible from the road.

A denial is not a finding. A rumor is not either.

One number here was measured. The other one the co-op had to estimate.

## What we verified

- Kodiak Electric Association owns, operates and maintains an isolated grid. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Terror Lake Hydroelectric plant provides approximately 85 percent of Kodiak Electric Association's generation. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- The rest of Kodiak Electric Association's generation comes from six wind turbines, two flywheels and a battery energy storage system, with diesel generation kept as back up. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Kodiak Electric Association states its aim is to maintain 98 percent of energy sales with cost effective renewable power solutions. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/about-us), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Kodiak Electric Association's Terror Lake hydroelectric project began commercial operation in 1985. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/about-us), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Kodiak Electric Association's Pillar Mountain wind project was initiated in 2009. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/about-us), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Kodiak Electric Association completed the Upper Hidden Basin Diversion Project in 2019. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/about-us), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- Kodiak Electric Association achieved over 95 percent of its power generation from renewable sources by 2013 and maintained 98 percent by 2017. [Kodiak Electric Association](https://www.kodiakelectric.com/about-us), primary document, 2026-09-04.
- The U.S. Navy is building what it calls a multi-mission support building at its Special Warfare Center Detachment on Spruce Cape in Kodiak. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Navy spokesperson Lt. Samuel Hardgrove told KMXT by email on August 14th that the operational facility is not a data center. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Hardgrove said the new facility will be powered from the existing power grid and will have back-up generators. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Kodiak Electric Association CEO Dan Menth said the Navy has not told the utility specifically how much power the new facility will use. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Kodiak Electric Association expects the Navy's future energy demand could be up to an additional two megawatts at its Kodiak detachment, according to a utility planning document. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Kodiak Electric Association almost had to start diesel generators in March when the peak load hit just over 31 MW. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Kodiak's micro-grid generates nearly 100 percent of its power from renewable energy sources. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Pillar Mountain has six wind turbines each generating 1.5 MW. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- One of the six Pillar Mountain turbines has been offline since March of 2025. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
- Kodiak Electric Association CEO Dan Menth said the cooperative didn't have to turn diesels on but that growing load could make it a potential. [KMXT Public Radio](https://www.kmxt.org/news/2026-09-03/navy-says-new-facility-being-built-in-kodiak-is-not-a-data-center), report, 2026-09-03.
