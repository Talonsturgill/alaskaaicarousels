The story

UPDATE. DNR approved a 50-year North Slope data center lease and decided to convey about 6,800 acres near Houston to AIDEA. The third is Alaska LNG's 800-mile gas line. Its comments close at 5:00 p.m. November 25th.

DNR proposes converting the line's two conditional leases into full leases. Per DNR, that is not a permit to build. Seven conditions stay as later gates.

Hearings run virtually November 23rd and in Anchorage November 24th. A decision could come before the December 7th handoff.

The three files, the hearing in quotes, the seven conditions, Houston at true scale and every window on one calendar are all here.
