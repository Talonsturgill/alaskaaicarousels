## What we verified

- Waymo published a blog post on November 5th about how the Waymo Driver handles severe winter weather. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- Waymo said that this season in Alaska it will conduct closed-course evaluations and collect data in heavy snowfall. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- Waymo says it has driven tens of thousands of real-world miles in Upstate New York, Michigan's Upper Peninsula and the Sierra Nevada. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- The 6th-generation Waymo Driver uses cameras, radar and lidar. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- Waymo says built-in heaters and micro-wipers keep its sensors clear in icy conditions. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- Waymo says it is equipping its winter vehicles with snow tires and measuring traction changes under each wheel in real time. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
- Waymo says that when weather exceeds what it has validated it may reduce its service area or pause service entirely. [Waymo](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/11/winter-weather-preparedness/), primary document, 2026-11-05.
