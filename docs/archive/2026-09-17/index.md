Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-09-17/

subject employees to disciplinary action, up to and including termination

## What we verified

- The UA Generative AI Security Standard carries an effective date of January 2025. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-01-01.
- The standard was approved in January 2025. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-01-01.
- The standard was updated in March 2025 to include material from content area experts. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard sets its next review for January 2027. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- Information Security and Assurance Standards are mandatory and apply to the UA System and all users of UA computing resources. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard states that it supplements and supports Board of Regents Policy and Regulation R02.07. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard cites Board of Regents Policy and Regulation R02.07.060 as the requirement to secure University Information Resources. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- Board of Regents chapter 02.07 is titled Information Resources. [University of Alaska Board of Regents](https://www.alaska.edu/bor/policy-regulations/index.php), primary document, 2026-09-17.
- Systems that fail to abide by approved information security controls may face disconnection from the UA network. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- Violations may subject employees to disciplinary action up to and including termination. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- Violations may subject students to disciplinary action including expulsion. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard defines Public data by reference to Board of Regents Regulation R02.07.093 as data that can be freely shared with the public. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard describes Internal Use data as generally not available to parties outside the University of Alaska community. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard describes Restricted data as subject to disclosure laws and warranting careful management and protection. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard warns that sensitive information provided to generative AI can be stored on servers the University of Alaska does not control. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The standard does not flatly forbid Internal or Restricted data from generative AI and instead requires such use to meet published requirements for data storage, computing environments and model deployment. [University of Alaska Office of Information Technology](https://www.alaska.edu/oit/policies-standards/generative-ai.php), primary document, 2025-03-01.
- The University GenAI Policies and Guidelines page carries a last updated stamp of 03/11/2025. [University of Alaska](https://www.alaska.edu/ai/policies/), primary document, 2025-03-11.
- The University GenAI Policies and Guidelines page states that UA System regulations are currently under development. [University of Alaska](https://www.alaska.edu/ai/policies/), primary document, 2025-03-11.
- The University GenAI Policies and Guidelines page says adoption and use of these tools are guided by individual university and department policies that are currently under development. [University of Alaska](https://www.alaska.edu/ai/policies/), primary document, 2025-03-11.
- The main UA GenAI hub page carries a last updated stamp of 01/09/2026. [University of Alaska](https://www.alaska.edu/ai/), primary document, 2026-01-09.
- UAA Chancellor Cheryl Siemers wrote that UAA is launching AI@UAA this fall. [University of Alaska Anchorage](https://www.uaa.alaska.edu/news/archive/2026/09/building-uaa-we-love.cshtml), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- AI@UAA will be part of a new Leveraging Innovative Frameworks and Technologies Center, abbreviated LIFT. [University of Alaska Anchorage](https://www.uaa.alaska.edu/news/archive/2026/09/building-uaa-we-love.cshtml), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- The UAA post announcing AI@UAA and the LIFT Center was written by Chancellor Cheryl Siemers and published on September 10th. [University of Alaska Anchorage](https://www.uaa.alaska.edu/news/archive/2026/09/building-uaa-we-love.cshtml), primary document, 2026-09-10.
- UAF policy states that work created by an artificial intelligence engine is covered by the student code of conduct. [UAF Office of Rights, Compliance and Accountability](https://www.uaf.edu/orca/student-conduct/academic-misconduct.php), primary document, 2026-09-17.
- UAF states that findings of responsibility will not be based solely on AI detection software at this time. [UAF Office of Rights, Compliance and Accountability](https://www.uaf.edu/orca/student-conduct/academic-misconduct.php), primary document, 2026-09-17.
- UAS guidance tells instructors to avoid relying on AI detection tools alone because the tools have high error rates. [University of Alaska Southeast Center for Excellence in Learning and Teaching](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/generative-ai-guidance.html), primary document, 2026-07-24.
- UAS guidance tells instructors that the key is to clearly communicate their AI policies to students. [University of Alaska Southeast Center for Excellence in Learning and Teaching](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/generative-ai-guidance.html), primary document, 2026-07-24.
- UAF guidance tells faculty to clarify expectations for generative AI use up front in the syllabus. [UAF Center for Teaching and Learning](https://ctl.uaf.edu/ai-in-education/), primary document, 2026-09-17.
- UAA's IT page directs users to the university's policies on data classification, information security and privacy when using AI. [UAA Information Technology Services](https://www.uaa.alaska.edu/about/administrative-services/departments/information-technology-services/ai-resources/), primary document, 2026-09-17.
- KTOO reported on September 16th that the University of Alaska Board of Regents reviewed a draft artificial intelligence policy. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- According to KTOO the draft policy would require all three University of Alaska campuses to develop an AI plan covering students, faculty and staff. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- KTOO reported that the draft policy's guiding principles are preserving human talent, safeguarding data security and using AI to expand educational access. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- A 2025 Inside Higher Ed survey found that about 85 percent of college students use generative AI in their coursework. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- Ben Shier is the University of Alaska's Chief Information Technology Officer. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- Shier told the regents that AI amplifies existing questions rather than raising new ones. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- Shier said the university assesses tools against a data type and tells users where that data can and cannot be placed. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- KTOO reported that Shier said the university currently can't control and track every usage of AI. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- Regent Christine Ressler raised concerns about the university's ability to monitor AI use across the system. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- University leadership plans to submit a finalized draft AI policy for approval at the November regents meeting. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- The university plans to collect student and community input through listening sessions and surveys this fall, with no session dates scheduled as of KTOO's report. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
- The Board of Regents meeting where the draft AI policy was presented was held at the University of Alaska Southeast. [KTOO](https://www.ktoo.org/2026/09/16/university-of-alaska-regents-consider-draft-ai-policy/), report, 2026-09-16.
