# 2 to 16 polar bear dens, and FWS estimates 8

> The proposed terms for infrared polar bear den flights over the Arctic Refuge list last winter's dates.

Published 2026-11-27 by Alaska AI. 9 slides.
Canonical: https://alaskaaihq.com/archive/2026-11-27/

## The story

2 to 16 polar bear dens.

FWS estimates 8.

10,000 runs of a model. A middle answer of 8.

FWS wrote its den model in R and ran it 10,000 times for AIDEA's seismic plan. It estimates 8 land-based dens in the survey area, with a 95 percent interval of 2 to 16. It is a statistical simulation, not AI.

In bears, a median of 4. Proposed cap, 10.

For Level B harassment the den model's 95 percent interval is 0 to 14. Level A runs 0 to 11, median 2, cap 2. The cap of 10 also covers bears away from dens.

12 of 907.

1.32 percent of the stock. On a draft count of 819, 1.47 percent.

"We recognize the limitations of our predictive models and take estimates that significantly exceed historical data collected over decades of similar oil and gas activity on Alaska's North Slope."

Two infrared surveys, a day apart at least.

SnowDens, a snow model, maps where drifts build. Aircraft infrared then surveys them twice. FWS calls it four times more effective at finding dens than ground FLIR. Any putative den gets a 1.6 km exclusion zone.

The proposed terms list last winter's dates.

The mitigation text opens the first flight November 25th. The den model opens it December 1st. The proposed terms list December 1st to 25th, 2025. Comments close December 17th.

Then 12 rubber-tracked vibroseis vehicles.

The plan lays 37,500 wireless nodes and supports up to two 180-person camps. AIDEA's board set aside up to $175.0 million for permitting and 3D seismic work.

Which end of 2 to 16 should the final authorization plan for?

- AIDEA's board resolution authorizes up to $175.0 million for 1002 Area permitting and 3D seismic work. [Alaska Industrial Development and Export Authority, Resolution G26-05](https://www.aidea.org/Portals/0/Meeting%20Docs/2026BoardMeetings/051326/6._D_Resolution_No._G26-05%20_Section_1002_Area_Development_Budget_05132026.pdf), primary document, 2026-05-13.
- The same resolution authorizes up to $15.0 million for bids and rentals in the June 2026 Coastal Plain lease sale. [Alaska Industrial Development and Export Authority, Resolution G26-05](https://www.aidea.org/Portals/0/Meeting%20Docs/2026BoardMeetings/051326/6._D_Resolution_No._G26-05%20_Section_1002_Area_Development_Budget_05132026.pdf), primary document, 2026-05-13.
- The applicant for the proposed authorization is the Alaska Industrial Development and Export Authority (AIDEA). [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The activity area is the Coastal Plain 1002 Area of the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The seismic survey would begin during the winter of 2026-2027. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The seismic program would use 12 rubber-tracked vibroseis vehicles. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The program would deploy 37,500 wireless autonomous recording devices called nodes. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- Up to two 180-person camp seismic crews may be supported. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The advance crew will mobilize no earlier than January 10th. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS wrote the code for its den disturbance model in the program R. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS ran 10,000 iterations of the den model as a Monte Carlo simulation. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The model's inputs include the estimated number of dens in four regions of northern Alaska. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS estimated 8.0 land-based dens in the area of specified activity, with a median of 8 and a 95 percent interval of 2 to 16. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The den disturbance model gives a median of 4 Level B takes per winter, with a 95 percent interval of 0 to 14. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The den disturbance model gives a median of 2 Level A takes per winter, with a 95 percent interval of 0 to 11. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The den disturbance model puts the probability of at least one lethal take at 0.15, with a median lethal take of 0. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS proposes to authorize take of no more than 10 polar bears by Level B harassment. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS proposes to authorize take of no more than 2 polar bears by Level A harassment. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- Take of 12 bears is 1.32 percent of the Southern Beaufort Sea stock estimate of 907 animals. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- Under a draft stock estimate of 819 bears, 12 bears would be 1.47 percent of the stock. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- In the mitigation description, the first aerial infrared den survey would be flown between November 25th and December 25th. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- In the denning model, the first aerial infrared survey was set between December 1st and December 25th and the second between December 15th and January 15th, 2027. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The proposed authorization's mitigation terms list aerial infrared survey dates in December 2025 and January 2026, a year earlier than the windows in the den model. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The model requires a minimum of 24 hours between the two aerial infrared surveys. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS says aerial infrared has been found to be four times more effective at detecting dens than ground-based FLIR. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- AIDEA will create a 1.6 km (1 mi) exclusion zone around any putative dens it locates. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- The notice gives no numeric den detection probabilities and refers to the 2026-2031 Beaufort Sea ITR for them. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- AIDEA will use a modeling framework called SnowDens to simulate snow accumulation before starting aerial infrared surveys. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
- FWS says its modeling assumptions are more likely to contribute to an overestimate than an underestimate of take. [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Federal Register (govinfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-17/html/2026-23318.htm), primary document, 2026-11-17.
