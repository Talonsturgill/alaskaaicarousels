## What we verified

- Executive Order 14426 is titled Accelerating Access to Veterans' Benefits and Employment Opportunities. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-11/html/2026-18738.htm), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- The President signed Executive Order 14426 on September 8th, 2026. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 was published in the Federal Register on September 11th, 2026 as document number 2026-18738. [U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-11/html/2026-18738.htm), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- The Federal Register citation for Executive Order 14426 is 91 FR 58003 and the order runs from page 58003 to page 58005. [Office of the Federal Register, documents API](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2026-18738.json), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 has four sections, headed Purpose and Policy, Requiring Prompt Sharing of Military Personnel and Medical Records, Accelerating Veterans' Access to Quality Employment, and General Provisions. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- The phrase artificial intelligence appears exactly once in the full text of Executive Order 14426. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Section 2(a)(ii) of Executive Order 14426 directs the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to create and deploy new digital tools using artificial intelligence to streamline veterans' applications for benefits within 180 days. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- The 180 day deadline for the artificial intelligence tools in Executive Order 14426 falls on March 7th, 2027. [Office of the Federal Register, arithmetic by Alaska.Ai](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Section 2(a)(iii) of Executive Order 14426 sets a 30 day deadline, falling on October 8th, 2026, for the Department of War to share personnel and treatment records with the Department of Veterans Affairs immediately on a service member's discharge. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Section 2(b) of Executive Order 14426 sets a 120 day deadline, falling on January 6th, 2027, to review and modify existing information technology contracts so that records systems are interoperable. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Section 2(a)(i) of Executive Order 14426 gives the same 180 day deadline for updated information technology systems and policy guidance to allow permanent and ongoing sharing of Official Military Personnel Files and Service Treatment Records. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Section 3 of Executive Order 14426 gives the Secretary of War 180 days to update the Transition Assistance Program so that every service member leaving active duty is connected to an open job or enrolled in training before separation. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 binds the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and requires consultation with the Secretaries of Health and Human Services and Labor and the Director of the Office of Management and Budget. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 does not name Alaska or any other state anywhere in its text. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 states that it creates no right or benefit enforceable at law against the United States. [Office of the Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/09/11/2026-18738.txt), primary document, 2026-09-11.
- Executive Order 14426 blames outdated records systems between the Department of War and the Department of Veterans Affairs for delays in veterans' healthcare coverage and benefits processing.
