In 2021 one Alaska airline let a machine pick its routes. In 2026 the FAA bought that machine for the whole country.

The FAA award to Air Space Intelligence runs 875 million dollars over a reported 12 years. It funds SMART and the FMDS flow platform for the national airspace.

The machine is Flyways. It reads traffic, wind and weather and flags what a flight will hit, then recommends a route a dispatcher or pilot accepts. It never flies the plane.

Alaska runs it across roughly 1,500 flights a day and says it saves about 1 million gallons of fuel and tens of thousands of flight hours a year.

Slides map the 875 million dollar award, the SMART and FMDS scope, the fall 2026 date and the 2021 Alaska deployment.
