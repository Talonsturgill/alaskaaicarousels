A 1,000 kilometre blood flight out of Nenana is documented down to five logged sensor channels. The finding is not published.

The International Testing Agency published the flight on September 16th and said the results will come once the evaluation is complete. It intends to submit them to a peer reviewed journal. No date.

ACUASI, part of the University of Alaska Fairbanks, flew it on August 19th. The test began and ended at Nenana Municipal Airport. Closing the route is the control. That leaves the blood as the only thing that changed.

The deck holds the paired samples, the hangar laboratory at the same airport, and five logged channels as empty rows.

Alaska supplied the distance and the distance was the part held still. A route that isn't closed has to end somewhere. Which Alaska community should that be?
