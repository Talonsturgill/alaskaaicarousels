THE ONE WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS: **artwork craft and genuine detail**,
weakest in 7 of 10, mean 6.6, and scored 7 again yesterday on a run that
deliberately targeted it with two rendered heroes.

That last fact is the brief. No.36 climbed the rendered ladder twice, put an
aksdf raymarched storm on slide 04 and software-3D terrain on slide 08, and
STILL scored 7, with the named residual being that the storm "reads soft".
Two runs before it, No.34 built six art prototypes before writing a dossier
and scored 7.77 overall. The pattern is not that this studio fails to reach
for depth. It is that reaching for a rendered hero buys ONE spectacular slide
and leaves the other eight to be judged on the honest question the rubric
actually asks, which is whether every slide survives the zoom test with craft
in every region.

So this run does something different, and the difference is a MEASURED one:

  **The attack is line-density as a data channel, applied deck-wide rather
  than hero-deep.** Today's Phase 1 scan finally recovered the parameter set
  for Jobard and Lefer evenly-spaced streamline placement (d_sep, d_test,
  STEP_LENGTH at 0.1 to 1 percent of field width, N_STEPS 30 to 40, a density
  grid of d_sep-wide cells, perpendicular seeding at d_sep). d_sep is a knob
  that can be driven by a number from the story, which turns the drawing's
  own line spacing into an encoding rather than a texture. That is
  simultaneously the craft criterion (real line-weight and line-density
  hierarchy, drawn marks not fills) and the story-art fusion criterion, and it
  applies to EVERY slide instead of one hero.

  Concretely, the Phase 5 dossiers must satisfy three things this run:
  1. Every slide names a drawn line system whose SPACING or COUNT is a story
     number, stated as a mapping in the dossier.
  2. No slide's lower third is a gradient. The four-run top-loaded-composition
     warn is beaten by putting drawn structure down there, and drawn structure
     is what this technique produces.
  3. bespoke_check drawn share target 70 percent minimum against the 45
     percent floor, because the same gate measures the "blocky" failure and a
     streamline field is the opposite of a fillRect.

  Secondary target, because it is cheap and has never been worked: LEGIBILITY
  AND PLATFORM FITNESS is weakest in 2 of 10 and has been worked NEVER. The
  three shipping defect classes underneath it are tiny-text, outside-safe-zone
  and art-touching-glyphs. The parked ESRI halo/casing note from 2026-08-18 is
  the craft answer to the third, and this run will use a same-hue half-opacity
  casing under any glyph that must sit on drawn texture, rather than moving the
  glyph or reaching for a plate.
