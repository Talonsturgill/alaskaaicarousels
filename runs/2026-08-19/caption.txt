73 percent of Alaska likely voters back pausing new data centers until the state passes a law. Houston in the Mat-Su voted a ban down.

Data for Progress polled 605 likely voters from July 28th to August 4th. 63 percent of Republicans said pause too.

On August 13th the ban failed 4 to 2, per the Mat-Su Sentinel. The same council unanimously passed two objections to the state's land transfer process.

A poll covers a whole state. The ban covered one city, and only a small section of the land in question sits inside it. Anchorage adopted a permitting framework instead, a conditional use permit that regulates how and not whether.
