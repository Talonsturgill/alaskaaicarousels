# DECK HEADER

## THESIS

**A poll measures a preference and a vote allocates a power, and Alaska
currently has a 73 percent preference with no instrument attached to it.**

The deck refuses both cheap readings. Not "voters betrayed by their council",
and not "the poll is meaningless." Seventy-three percent (C01) is a real,
cross-party, honestly measured distribution. The Houston council on August 13th
was not holding a distribution. It was holding one ordinance that reached a
small section of one parcel (C30), and it reached for zoning instead (C26).

## HERO STRUCTURE

### NEW: THE FEATHERED PLATE

Every slide is a **discrete engraved statistical plate**: its own frame, its own
baseline, its own plate number, re-registered from scratch. Quantities are drawn
as **ruled line fields, never fills**. And the signature, which is the thesis
expressed as a drawing convention:

> **A poll has a feathered end. A vote has a hard end.**

- **MEASURED** quantities terminate in a **feather**. The ruling loses width and
  alpha across a span that IS the margin of error, plus or minus 4 points of the
  axis (C05). On the 920px content width a 0-to-100 axis puts one point at
  9.2px, so a feather spans 36.8px, drawn as 8 hairlines fading to zero.
- **UNPUBLISHED-SUBGROUP** quantities get a **double-wide phantom feather**,
  73.6px, dashed `30 5 6 5 6 5` at 1.25px (#67), because no subgroup n or
  subgroup error was published for the party or borough crosstabs (C02, C15,
  C16, C17). The drawing declines to be more precise than the source.
- **DECIDED** things (a roll call, an adopted ordinance, a certification date)
  have a **butt cap at `--w-hero` 5.5px** with a 6px perpendicular terminal
  tick, in kraft, no fade, no ruling. They stop dead.

The reader learns this on slide 03 and every later slide is legible in half a
second. Slide 09 lands because a preliminary count is feathered and August 31st
is a hard cap.

**WHY THIS DIVERGES FROM THE FOUR FORBIDDEN HEROES.** No.33 (an equal-award
chart projection seen square on), No.34 (a nursery floor under a dollying
camera), No.35 (a machined rail welded across the deck) and No.36 (a route over
a continuous terrain and cloud floor under a climbing camera) are one
construction in four coats of paint: ONE CONTINUOUS SURFACE held across nine
frames while a camera or a projection does the varying. In all four the reader's
job is to track a viewpoint across a world that persists.

This deck has **no continuous surface and no camera anywhere.** It has nine
surfaces, each beginning and ending inside its own frame, each re-inked, each
with a visible plate edge and its own contact shadow. The register is
orthographic and fixed, the lamp never moves, and continuity is carried by a
drawing convention and a handed-off element rather than by shared geography.
The forbidden four asked "where are we now." This one asks "what does this mark
mean." That is a different question at the structural level.

## ATMOSPHERE

### NEW: RAKING PRESS LIGHT

One low key, azimuth 118, elevation 24. No fog, no volumetrics, no sky, no
backlight, no room haze. The only air in this deck is the millimetre between the
reader and the plate.

Elevation 24 is a craft decision and not a mood: akengrave's note records that
elevation 20 to 30 spreads `ndotl` across nearly its full range so the swelled
line is legible, while a high key flattens every stroke to one width and gives
back the uniform hatch the bench exists to avoid.

Diverges from No.34 (hallway backlight, room fog), No.35 (radiograph bone under
machined light) and No.36 (cold dawn at altitude, volumetric cloud decks). Those
three all light a SPACE. This lights a SURFACE.

## GLOBAL RENDER RULES

- Seed `20260819 + slideNo` via `AK.reseed()` / `AK.rng()`. No `Math.random`.
- All text is DOM or SVG. Canvas draws art only.
- Canvas 2x backing: `canvas.width = W*2; canvas.height = H*2; ctx.scale(2,2)`.
- `window.renderReady` gates every slide.
- `await document.fonts.ready` before `AK.fitText` and before any `reserve()`.
- Text over art is defended structurally, never by a guessed plate. Slides using
  akengrave call `eng.reserve(AKENGRAVE.boxesFor('.hd, .body, .mono, .lab'))`
  after fonts are ready, so the colliding stroke is never generated. Slides
  without engrave use `AK.svgPlate` measured from the label's own `getBBox`.
- No GL anywhere in this deck. Canvas 2D plus DOM and SVG only, so there is no
  black-frame sentinel risk and no webglOK fallback to maintain.
- `AKPOST.grade` once on the art canvas, house grade, `exposure` in STOPS and
  held in -0.15 to +0.06. Then `AK.grainTile(280, 52, seed)` at 0.06 overlay.
- Every leader is a world-coordinate polyline ending on its target's own
  coordinates, declared in `window.__akLeaders` with `from`, `at`, `to` and
  `label`, and its label is DOM or SVG text.
- Every object that sits on the plate declares `data-contacts`. **The pool is
  lit FIRST** (`#2E4358` under where the object sits) and the shadow is cast
  into it AFTER `eng.surface`, targeting 8 L* of separation, never the 4.0 floor.

### C. ART DIRECTION

**7. Technique stack.**
- **Evenly-spaced streamline placement** (Jobard and Lefer, unparked this run).
  `d_sep = 5.5px` left of the 73 mark, `d_sep = 22.0px` right of it.
  STROKE WIDTH LEFT OF THE MARK IS 2.16 to 3.0px AND NOT THE 1.25 FINE TOKEN,
  and that is deliberate. `qa.py` measures a declared encoding as the MEDIAN
  CIELAB of each region, and a field at 36 percent ink has a median of bare
  plate, so at the token width the two regions would measure identical and the
  slide's own wordless claim would fail its own gate. The claim the field makes
  is where the density STEPS, at x751.6, which is 73 percent of the axis; it
  makes no claim about the ink ratio inside either half.
  `STEP_LENGTH = 4.6px` (0.5 percent of the 920px field). `N_STEPS = 34`.
  `MIN_STEPS_ALLOWED = 4`. Density grid of `d_sep`-wide cells, candidate tested
  against its own cell plus the 8 around it. Seeds perpendicular at `d_sep` both
  sides, queued. Seed `20260820`.
- Underlying flow angle field: `AK.fbm2(x*0.0016, y*0.0016, 4)` mapped to
  `2.1 * PI`, `AK.reseed(20260820)`.
- **akengrave (#93)** for the plate body. `AKENGRAVE.create({seed: 20260820,
  light: {azDeg: 118, elDeg: 24}})`. `form` = a shallow plateau, the plate's own
  bevel, precomputed into a 4px lookup grid with a bilinear sampler.
  `budget: {inkLo: 0.10, inkHi: 0.62, wMax: 2.2, crossDeg: 41, step: 3,
  feather: 6}`.
- Five-token line weights: hair 0.75 (streamlines right of 73), fine 1.25
  (streamlines left of 73, axis minor), std 2 (axis major), bold 3.5 (the 73
  graduation), hero 5.5 (the six roll-call marks).
- `AKPOST.grade(ctx, {exposure: -0.05, saturation: 0.96, contrast: 1.06,
  grain: 0.045})`, then `AK.grainTile(280, 52, 20260820)` at 0.055 overlay.

# THE SEVEN-SLIDE FALLBACK, if it comes to that

almost none of the land) folds into slide 02's body as one sentence, and
Mitchell's quote replaces Cole's on that slide. Slide 07's honesty convention is
already taught on slide 03 by the 63 bar's double-wide phantom feather, so the

sentence inside the vote slide means the reader meets the councillors as
obstacles before they meet them as people making a judgement. Slide 07's Mat-Su
note, that the room which voted sits in the lowest of the three measured
boroughs, is the deck's best quiet fact and it dies with that slide. Nine is the
right number.
