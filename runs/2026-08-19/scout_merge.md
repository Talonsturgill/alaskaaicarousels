## THE RUNNER-UP AND THE REST, in the order I would reach for them

**1. The NSF portfolio (Beat B).** Four NSF awards to Alaska institutions in
2026 carry NSF's Artificial Intelligence program element, $8,097,929 total,
every one signed between July 10th and August 5th and every one starting
between August 1st and September 1st. GAIA at UAF ($1,772,170, six years,
seismic and landslide digital twins). FIRE-WUI at UAF ($1,588,147, machine
learning on weather reanalysis to find prescribed burn windows). A critical
minerals collaboration split UAA ($3,824,575) and UAF ($913,037). Pulled
straight off the NSF award API, which is keyless and returns full abstracts and
PI lists; the human award pages are a JavaScript shell that reads as empty.
Neither campus has announced any of them. LOST ONLY ON THE LANE, which has been
No.20, No.26, No.31, No.33 and No.35. Keep it. It is the best unread primary
record in the pile.

**2. ENSTAR and HEX emergency arbitration (Beat A).** HEX stopped all
discretionary gas deliveries July 26th and is offering the same gas at $17.50
per Mcf against the $13.50 ENSTAR says a March side letter fixed. ENSTAR had
planned on 2.6 Bcf from HEX between July 2026 and March 2027. Petroleum News
read the contract documents on August 16th and published the ladder, $12.30 /
$13.50 / $13.69 / $17.50. Real, numeric, dated, and its AI connection is
contextual rather than direct, which Beat A said plainly. That honesty is why
it is not the deck.

**3. Cook Inlet storage, measured (Beat A).** CINGSA at 54.1 percent of design
on August 18th, 7,033,704 Mcf of 13,000,000, with injection AND withdrawal
restrictions both posted, Well 7 out for coil tube work, Compressor B down, and
a required semiannual shut-in September 14th to 21st. This is our own Gas Watch
record and CINGSA keeps no archive, so we are the only place it exists. It
belongs on the Gas Watch page, where it already is, not in a carousel.

**4. AKLNG's third special session collapse (Beat A).** HB 4001 introduced
August 12th, declared short of votes the same day, governor called it over on
August 13th. Passes to a legislature seated January 2027.

**5. AURORA-AI, announced August 11th (Beats C and E, independently).** UAF's
$725,000 DOE Genesis Mission award, Richard Wies, with Cordova Electric as the
live test bed. Beat C added the physical scale the UAF release omits, 7.25 MW
of hydro over 10.8 MW of diesel, hydro carrying about 80 percent of the town,
and Alaska's 200 plus remote microgrids. SHIPPED TWICE ALREADY, No.20 and
No.31, the second only eight days ago. Not available.

**6. NOAA Fisheries Technology Week, August 17th to 21st (Beat C).** The
Alaska stack is more machine-processed than anyone publishes. The 2025 Arctic
ice seal survey flew 58 flights over 39,663 km, collected more than 1.5 million
image sets and 26 terabytes, and ran an in-flight thermal ML model at better
than 90 percent accuracy. Otoliths aged in 30 to 50 seconds against manual.
Genuinely good and genuinely available; the window peg is a promotional week
rather than a decision.

**7. The machine learning earthquake catalog (Beat C).** Thousands of

deck on it when a peg appears.

## CONFLICTS AND CORRECTIONS

**The PauseAI date conflict.** Beat F reported an Anchorage roadside
demonstration on Monday August 10th (Alaska Landmine, published August 13th).
Beat E reported one on August 17th (Alaska's News Source, published August
18th). Both Mondays, both Anchorage, both PauseAI, both citing organizer Maxim
Ishchuk of UAA and the same April 2026 delegation trip to Washington. Either
two events or one badly dated. Handed to the fact-checker with instructions to
resolve it or kill both. It is a minor beat and killing it costs nothing.

**The 30 states deepfake claim.** Beats B, D and F all surfaced it and ALL
THREE flagged that it traces to a single ADN opinion column by Roger Kaye and
no independent tracker. Handed to the fact-checker to verify or kill. Do not
let a number into the deck on an op-ed's say-so.

**HCR 3 and HB 47.** Same problem, same source, and akleg.gov BASIS returned
403 to every scout that tried it. Beat B put its own finding at 0.45 confidence
for exactly this reason and said so. Correct behaviour.

**The Houston comment deadline.** Beat E's source said the DNR comment window
closed "Wednesday August 12th"; Beat A and the state notice both say 5 p.m.
Wednesday, August 19th. The state notice wins, and it is what Phase 3.5 wrote
into the docket. Not a deck fact either way.

## INFRASTRUCTURE LESSONS FOR FUTURE SWEEPS

- **The NSF award API is the find of the run.** Keyless, no rate limit hit,
  filters by `awardeeStateCode=AK` and by date, returns abstracts and PI lists.
  It surfaced three AI awards no Alaska newsroom had touched. Candidate for
  config/sources.yaml.
- **The Federal Register API** likewise, and it lets a scout prove a negative
  cheaply instead of inferring one from a failed search.
- **alaskabeacon.com returns 403 to every fetcher**, including its front page,
  but **its RSS feed works**. Use the feed for discovery, then find the fact in
  a republisher or a primary. Alaska Public Media republishes most Beacon
  copy and loads clean.
- **Reddit is entirely unreachable from this environment.** Beat F said so
  plainly and characterised no thread it could not read. Beat F is the
  community beat, so this is a standing structural gap in that beat and the
  prompt should stop implying otherwise.
- **rca.alaska.gov, akleg.gov BASIS, elections.alaska.gov, gi.alaska.edu and
  acuasi.alaska.edu all refused bots this run.** Five Alaska primary sources
  behind a wall is the single biggest constraint on this beat.
- Two scouts exhausted a 200 call WebSearch budget. WebFetch kept working.
  Worth knowing that the fallback is direct fetch, not failure.
