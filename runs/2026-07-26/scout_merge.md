## THE INSTRUMENTS (Beat D, the enforceability spine)

- SB 250 (Sen. Loki Tobin) would have required commission-approved utility
  contracts for data centers so their costs are not passed to other
  ratepayers, plus community benefit agreements. Introduced 2026-02-18,
  passed the Senate 2026-05-16, one House Community and Regional Affairs
  hearing 2026-05-18, DEAD 2026-05-20 at sine die.
  Sources: https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1974439 ,
  https://www.dwt.com/blogs/energy--environmental-law-blog/2026/06/alaska-data-center-development-next-steps
  DWT verbatim: "Following Senate passage, SB 250 was referred to the House
  Community and Regional Affairs Committee, where it died."
  DWT also reports amendments requiring financial security when utility
  capital investment exceeds $1,000,000, and monthly water usage and
  wastewater discharge plans filed with municipalities.

- SB 2 (Sen. Shelley Hughes) covered "disclosure of election-related
  deepfakes; relating to use of artificial intelligence by state agencies;
  and relating to transfer of data about individuals between state
  agencies". Introduced 2025-01-22, one recorded hearing (Senate State
  Affairs, 2025-04-29), DEAD 2026-05-20.
  Source: https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1775660

  Energy Committee as of the 07-18 run's research. Status now unknown.
