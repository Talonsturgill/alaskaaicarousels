# DECK HEADER

## THESIS

A promise is not an instrument. Alaska's governor signed a voluntary,
non binding commitment that AI data center load will pay for its own
power, and the only Alaska utility on that pledge's signatory list is a
1,566 customer cooperative whose wires reach no other community, while
the one Alaska instrument that would have made the promise binding passed
the Senate and died in a House committee.

**PDF document title (24 chars).** `One Line Out`
Full title string passed to assemble.py, `One Line Out. Alaska's AI Power Pledge` (38 chars).

## THE GOVERNING RULE (one rule, three states, every slide)

Every span in this deck is in exactly one of three states, and the states
are SHAPE changes so they survive the 432px thumb.

| State | Drawn as | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **CARRIES** | solid aluminum conductor, clamped both ends, live | a thing that actually holds |
| **TERMINATES** | dead end clamp, conductor tied off, nothing beyond | a thing that existed and stopped |
| **ONLY DRAWN** | gold phantom dash (#67 dash kit), no metal, never lands | a promise |

Gold is INVERTED from house habit on purpose. In this deck #FFC72C does
not mean the prize or the authority. It means THE PROMISE, and a promise
is the one thing in the frame with no metal in it. That is the whole
argument in a single glyph.

## SLIDE COUNT RATIONALE

Nine. Inside the 8 to 10 default band. Nine is the floor for this story
because the argument needs four load bearing beats that cannot be merged,
the list (2), the mechanism (3), the death (6) and the holding ladder (7),
plus a place beat (4) that makes the reader care and a scale beat (5) that
makes the stakes legible. Eight would force the death and the ladder onto
one slide, which is exactly the collapse that would turn the synthesis
into a spoiler.
