"If the hand count is to check on the machines, then why do we have the machines?" Barbara Haney sponsored Fairbanks Proposition 3.

Did anyone check them? Yes. In 2025 a canvass board hand verified 3,338 ballots, two precincts of 32 plus early voting. The machines came back 100 percent accurate on that sample.

What counted as failure? The ordinance said so up front. A discrepancy over 1 percent. About 33 ballots. The gap was zero.

What does a yes do? Hand counting starts in 2027, so October 6th is still machine tabulated. The ordinance carries no fiscal analysis. A yes locks it for two years, past the Assembly and the voters.

The clerk's ballot title, the tolerance beside the measured gap, Haney's case in full and the five dates still open.
