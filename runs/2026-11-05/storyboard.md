# STORYBOARD, run 2026-11-05, carousel No.106
# "NOTICE OVER NUMBERS": the clock that stayed in policy, an UPDATE of No.84

## THESIS
On the night of November 4th the Anchorage Assembly passed AO 2026-108(S) as amended 11 to 1 and answered the
question No.84 asked: the 14-day limit on plate data stays APD policy, not city code. The amendment that would have
written 14 days into code was rejected, a warrant rule failed 5 to 7, and the one limit that passed unanimously is a
notice rule, 30 days of advance notice to the Assembly before APD changes a material policy, storage limits included.
Anchorage chose oversight over limits and notice over numbers. The deck states that plainly and leaves the verdict
to the reader with one fair question.
PDF document title (copy.json): "Anchorage Kept the 14 Days in Policy, Not Code".

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here
Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 5 of the last 10 runs (mean 7.15). Four changes. (1) The masterful
depth frame, 05, is built and reviewed FIRST, alone, and it de-risks the letter model 01 and 09 reuse. (2) Every
frame's largest object names its modelling and its key-versus-face relation: the key is upper left at azimuth 300,
elevation 28, and every hero face is turned so the key rakes it (never front-lit). (3) Five mark-making families, none
on more than three frames, no shared whole-frame ground module. (4) The texture list from No.99 to No.105 applied
before the first render: grain at size 1, noise functions never linear hashes, device-resolution per-pixel fields,
dithered gradients, gold drawn after the grade or held with AKPOST.holdInk.
