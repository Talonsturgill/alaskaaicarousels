If you drive in Anchorage, a patrol car's plate reader can log your plate, and APD's own policy deletes it after 14 days.

An amendment to put that clock in city code was rejected. Alaska's News Source reports the ordinance passed 11 to 1.

Erin Baldwin Day's Amendment 12, passed unanimously, requires a memo to the Assembly 30 days before APD changes a storage limit. The filed text gave notice within 30 days after.

The Anchorage Daily News reports a committee will meet monthly for a year to recommend, not discipline, and that APD relies more on AI to aid investigations. The city chose notice over numbers.

Five votes from November 4th sit beside the notice rule as filed and as amended, the committee's limits and the filed AI definition.

Should shortening the 14 days take only a memo, and lengthening it take an Assembly vote?
