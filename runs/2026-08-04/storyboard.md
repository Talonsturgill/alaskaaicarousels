# DECK HEADER

## THE DIRECTORS ROOM, AND THE SYNTHESIS

Three lenses pitched. Cartographer, "No Common Unit", a plan-and-section
engineering sheet whose match line breaks at the turn. Historian-of-the-future,
"Measured to a Tenth of a Megawatt", a weathered staff gauge carrying 26 years of
record as deposit banding, standing beside an identical post with no plate on it.
Systems-illustrator, "Drawn to Scale, Except the Demand", a ten-sheet orthographic
drawing set that runs out of ruler when it reaches the demand side.

**THE SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR WINS, and the reason is honesty rather than beauty.**

The historian's deck was the most beautiful object pitched and it disqualified
itself in its own self-critique, correctly. Its hero is an invented staff gauge
rendered in convincing PBR steel, and no verified claim says such an instrument
exists at Bradley Lake. On a deck whose entire argument is that one public record
is precise to a tenth of a megawatt and the other is empty, rendering a beautiful
object that appears in NEITHER record would be committing the deck's own
indictment. This studio made exactly this call on 2026-08-03, when two of three
directors built heroes from invented objects on a deck about a company putting
more weight on a sentence than it could carry. Same trap, refused the same way.

The cartographer's deck was close and lost on a narrower point. Its dominant
register is a vertical section occupying about 54 percent of every frame, and the
only cited vertical quantity in the entire story is sixteen feet. Its own director
named this and defended the frame anyway. A section whose elevations must be
stamped FORM ONLY, NO ELEVATION CLAIMED is an honest drawing, but it spends the
deck's largest area on an axis carrying almost nothing verified.

The systems-illustrator's set draws only what the amendment itself generates, a
hydraulic profile, a dam elevation and section, and a full section of one
component. Nothing is invented. And its mechanism is PHYSICS.

## ORGANS GRAFTED FROM THE LOSING PITCHES

1. **THE MECHANISM REFUSES TO RUN ON A PROJECTION** (from the historian). The
   systems director named an honesty seam it could not close, and it was real.
   Lightness falling with hydraulic head is physically true and a reader will
   correctly read it as physics. Streak density at 1.38x for "38 percent more
   generation" is an INVENTED encoding wearing the same clothes, in the same
   water, under the same tonal law, and the legend distinguishing them is the
   first thing that dies at feed scale. The historian's deck solved this by
   construction, its deposit field physically cannot run on a forward-looking
   number. ADOPTED, AND IT IS THE MOST IMPORTANT DECISION IN THIS STORYBOARD.
   The head ramp encodes head and NOTHING ELSE. The 38 percent is never drawn in
   streaks, never in density, never in lightness. It is drawn as PHANTOM DASH
   WITH ZERO STREAKS AND ZERO FILL, in every place it appears. The generative
   system is physically incapable of rendering a projection, so measured and
   projected can never be confused, at any scale, with or without a legend.
2. **THE DENSITY GATE** (from the historian). No slide may contain a contiguous
   region larger than 180 by 180 CSS px whose lightness standard deviation is
   below 0.02, excluding declared text plates. This turns VISUAL_DENSITY 5 from a
   mood into a measurement and is the structural attack on dead lower zones.
3. **THE TEXTURE-VALUE LAW** (from the historian). Any texture pass may only add
   ink where it INCREASES value separation. Scatter and streak passes draw only
   where the underlying pixel L is below 0.30. Wet sheen draws only where L is
   above 0.45. Density rises and the notan never muddies.
4. **EQUAL STAGING, MEASURED** (from the cartographer). On the firewall slide the
   two half-sheets must match in top edge, width, plate treatment and MEAN
   LIGHTNESS WITHIN 0.03 L, measured, so neither record is visually favoured.
5. **"0.3 MW IS ONE PIXEL AT THIS SCALE"** (from the cartographer), printed as a
   rule on the energy balance rather than left for the reader to infer.
6. **THE HUE GATE**, on which all three directors independently converged.

## THE MECHANISM - THE HEAD RAMP

**What it is.** One lighting law applied to every water pixel in the deck.

```
L(h) = 0.88 - 0.72 * (h / h_max)      hue 158 held, chroma bell peak 0.045 at L 0.55
```

`h` is hydraulic head in feet below the declared datum (the proposed normal
maximum operating pool). Built through `AKC.ramp` and `AKC.mixOklab` in OKLCH
only. No colour helper output is ever fed to a hex parser.

**What drives it, and what deliberately does not.**

- **Head drives it, and head is the only thing that drives it.** L runs 0.88 to
  0.16, a span of 0.72, which is the deck's entire value structure. This is not an
  invented encoding. It is what light does in water carrying glacial rock flour,
  which means the deck's tonal structure is a physical property of its subject.
- **C08, the 38 percent, DOES NOT DRIVE IT.** The winning pitch proposed encoding
  the projection as 1.38x streak density inside the same water under the same
  tonal law, and named the seam that creates. A reader who correctly reads the
  first as physics will read the second as physics too. REFUSED. The 38 percent is
  drawn as PHANTOM DASH, ZERO STREAKS, ZERO FILL, everywhere it appears. The
  generative system physically cannot render a projection, so measured and
  projected can never be confused at any scale.
- **C05, the 16 ft raise, is deliberately NOT encoded in the ramp.** Sixteen feet
  against roughly 1,100 feet of head is a change below perceptual threshold, and
  claiming otherwise would repeat No.17's sundial mistake. It is drawn as a
  measurable dimension between two surface lines at a printed vertical scale.

**Declared gate, stated before the build so a critic can contradict it from the
renders alone.** Probe rectangles at three declared heads must return mean L
within plus or minus 0.05 of the printed ramp law, and the AUC separating the
shallow probe set from the deep probe set must exceed 0.85 at 432 px width. If it
does not clear 0.85, the ramp's range widens or the deck ships without claiming a
mechanism. This is the direct answer to No.24, whose mechanism was real and
measured AUC 0.51 at the hero with 0 percent visible at feed scale.

**Where it runs, on every slide.**

|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 0 to 40 ft, bore water | 0.88 to 0.62 | plus the 1:24,000 key's reservoir at L 0.70 |
| 02 | 0 to 180 ft, reservoir section as the sheet's ground | 0.80 to 0.30 | densest, widest streaks in the lower band |
| 03 | 0 to h_max, full range | 0.88 to 0.16 | the mechanism's most legible slide |

## NOTAN, DECK LEVEL

Water is the LIGHT MASS. Every solid is the DARK MASS. No third thing. Glacial
water at Bradley Lake is turbid with rock flour, so it is not a transparent blue
window, it is backlit translucent milk and the brightest surface in the deck.
Concrete, steel, rock, gravel and sheet ground are all dull, wet, low-chroma and
dark. Squint any slide and you see a pale connected water shape against a dark
connected solid shape, with the water surface line as the only high-contrast edge.

**FALSIFIABLE VALUE GATE.** On the cover and the hero, at 432 px, no more than 10
percent of pixels may fall in L 0.42 to 0.58, and the histogram must show a light
lobe centred above L 0.70 and a dark lobe centred below L 0.22.

**DENSITY GATE.** No contiguous region larger than 180x180 CSS px may have
lightness standard deviation below 0.02, excluding declared text plates.

**TEXTURE-VALUE LAW.** Scatter and streak passes draw only where underlying L is
below 0.30. Wet sheen draws only where L is above 0.45.

## ATMOSPHERE - SUBMERGED WORK LIGHT

No fog. No haze. No veil. No sky. No horizon in ten slides. Two lights only.

1. **The work lamp.** One cold-white clamp lamp of the kind bolted in a powerhouse
   gallery, azimuth 285, elevation 38, key to fill 5 to 1. It rakes wet concrete
   and steel and throws short two-part contact shadows coloured as darkened ground
   hue, never black.
2. **Transmission through the water.** The second source is not a lamp, it is the
   water body acting as a translucent emitter lit from behind and below.

Depth from six cues with no atmospheric wash anywhere: overlap, scale gradient,
attenuation with head, one tack-sharp plane (the cut face) with the far bore wall
softened because suspended silt genuinely scatters, two-part contact shadows under
every drawn body, one global light direction deck-wide.

NOT high-altitude sheet light (22), NOT low-angle terminal glow (23), NOT sea fog
at the basin (24), NOT ice fog (20), NOT copy-stand rake (21).

## LINE VOICE - DRAFTING, WITH ANSI SECTION LINING (one NEW element)

Five-token weights (hair 0.75, fine 1.25, std 2, bold 3.5, hero 5.5), profile
heaviest on every object silhouette, one arrowhead shape deck-wide (filled 3 to 1
technical).

**NEW, and logged if it ships and reads: ANSI SECTION LINING AS THE MATERIAL
CODE.** Real drawings encode material in the section hatch itself, and this deck
uses the standardised set as its texture system. Steel as evenly spaced 45 degree
lines at differing pitch, concrete as stipple with small triangles, earth and fill
as broken irregular lines, water as alternating horizontal dashes. Ancestor is
technique 65, Cross-Hatch Material Code. The new part is that the patterns are the
standardised drafting set rather than invented density tiers, so material is
legible without colour AND hatch pitch drives region lightness, feeding the notan
rather than fighting it.
