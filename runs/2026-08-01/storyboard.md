**THE MODELLED SURFACE (built once, sampled at every station).**

The height function is defined in GEOGRAPHIC space, not screen space, so every
station shades the same terrain and the ranges sit in the same place at 0.86x and
at 6.5x. This is what "build one surface properly and reuse it" means for a map,
and it is why the deck cannot degenerate into ten backgrounds.

```
seed        AK.reseed(20260801)
base        fbm2, octaves 5, lacunarity 2.0, gain 0.5
            nx = px_A/1080 * 3.4 , ny = py_A/1350 * 4.2
            (px_A, py_A are the sample's coords under the station-A full-state fit)
ridged      r = (1 - |2*base - 1|)^1.6
spine mask  M = sum of three gaussian ridge arcs on the REAL ranges
            Brooks    (-160,68.2)(-146,68.6)(-141,68.9)   sigma 1.1 deg
            AK Range  (-155,62.6)(-150.5,62.9)(-146,61.7)(-142,61.3) sigma 0.9 deg
            Coast     (-152,59.6)(-148,60.5)(-137,58.5)   sigma 0.7 deg
H           clamp(0.10 + 0.20*base + 0.78*r*M, 0, 1)
```

*Generalisation, the Imhof procedure.* A 3x3 MEDIAN filter over H, two passes,
never a blur (a blur over-smooths ridges; a median preserves ridge and canyon
definition). Then fade back fifty percent, `H' = H + 0.50*(Hmedian - H)`. This
runs on our own Float32Array before it is handed to `AK.reliefShade` through its
`height(u,v)` callback, because akrelief ships no median generaliser and shading
is not where structure work belongs.

*Lighting.* Broadly north-west, adjusted per landform via an aspect-weighted set,
which is the honest implementation of Imhof's "adjusted locally".

| Light | Azimuth | Elevation | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| key | 315 | 38 | 0.62 |
| support | 270 | 30 | 0.20 |
| support | 360 | 30 | 0.18 |

`strength 3.4`, `ambient 0.20`, `diffuse 0.95`, `gamma 1.05`.

*The elevation-keyed contrast rule.* Applied after Lambert, before the ramp. One
line, and it is the entire difference between a hillshade and a Swiss sheet.

```
s' = 0.52 + (s - 0.52) * (0.45 + 0.55 * H')
```

Lowlands run at about half of full contrast, peaks at full.

*Ramp and clamp.* `low #14222F` to `high #AEC2D4`. After the ramp, luminance is
clamped to the equivalent of 3 to 85 percent of the sheet, so no shaded slope
reaches page black and no lit slope reaches paper white.

*THE THREE VALUE BANDS, and where they fall.*

| Band | Range | Hex span | Where it falls | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Water and off-sheet | 3 to 12 pct | `#060D18` to `#0B1420` | 40 to 52 pct of the frame at wide stations | THE DESIGNATED TEXT GROUND. Body copy lives here or in a reserved field, nowhere else |
| 2 Lowland and interior | 20 to 46 pct | `#16283A` to `#46617C` | Interior, Yukon flats, Susitna | legend, bars, quiet mass. Internal contrast damped to 0.5 |
| 3 Ranges and uplands | 30 to 85 pct | `#223247` to `#AEC2D4` | THE LOWER THIRD at stations A, B and F (Alaska Range, Chugach, Kenai) | full range, sharpest edges |

Because of the projection and Alaska's actual shape, the ranges sit at the bottom
of the frame. **The lower third is composed by geography, not by furniture.**

*Honesty rule, per the `akrelief.js` header, printed in every ground dossier.*
The relief is FORM shading. No story number touches the heightfield and no
magnitude is ever read off a mountain.

*Finish.* `AKPOST.grade` on the art canvas after shading and before any
compositing (bloom 0.05, exposure +0.02, saturation 0.92, log-contrast 1.06,
split-tone lift `#101A26` gain `#C9BCA2` at 0.08, grain 0.06, IGN dither on).
Grade first, per the injected instinct. **There is zero WebGL in this deck**, so
the 34-second post-GL grade penalty is retired by construction.

**TEXT DEFENSE, deck-wide.** All text is DOM or SVG; there is no `fillText`
anywhere. `renderReady` awaits `document.fonts.ready`, runs `AK.fitText` on every
display line, reads `getBoundingClientRect` on every `.reserve` node, and passes
those rects to the shader, which damps local contrast to a flat band-2 value
inside every box PLUS 22px of padding (14px measurably failed on 2026-07-31,
22px held). A stroke under a glyph cannot exist, because the stroke is generated
after the glyph is measured. Every mono map label additionally rides
`AK.svgPlate` sized from its own `getBBox`. No plate width and no layout position
in this deck is a typed constant.

## THE SURFACE, and the one place the dossier was wrong

1. **Heightfield frequency.** The dossier's `nx = px/1080*3.4` is so smooth that
   the Sobel returned near-flat normals and the whole state shaded as one pale
   mass. A DETAIL OCTAVE was added at 26 by 32 units, weighted toward the ranges,
   and the height expression became
   `0.08 + 0.17*base + 0.62*r*M + 0.20*det*(0.30 + 0.70*M)`.
   `strength` went 3.4 to **13.0**, ambient 0.20 to **0.17**, diffuse 0.95 to
   **1.00**. This is the change that made the terrain read as terrain.
2. **The elevation-keyed contrast rule was WRONG AS SPECIFIED, and it caused the
   defect this run set out to kill.** The dossier's
   `s' = 0.52 + (s-0.52)*(0.45 + 0.55*H)` damps the flats to 45 percent of
   contrast. In this projection the ranges sit mid-frame and the bottom third is
   Gulf water and peninsula lowland, so the rule itself produced a dead lower
   third and qa.py failed slides 1, 2 and 10 on frame_balance. The floor came up
   to **0.62**, giving `s' = 0.52 + (s-0.52)*(0.62 + 0.38*H)`. Contrast still
   sharpens toward the peaks, which is the Imhof principle, but the flats stay
   alive. Worth recording plainly, because the run's own craft rule created the
   run's own recurring defect.
3. **Ramp** low `#14222F` to **`#0F1A26`**; clamp ceiling 0.85 to **0.90**.
4. **NOT IN ANY DOSSIER, added during the build.** The water gained (a) a
   low-amplitude modelled swell and (b) **bathymetric contours**, six thresholds
   through `d3.contours` off the same noise field, drawn as cased hairlines. A
   graded sea is honest and still reads as a dead cell to frame_balance, and
   depth contours are what real charts use. This is annotation furniture run
   down into the lower band, which is what the doctrine asked for.
5. **Station A extent** `[[-70,300],[1150,1210]]` to **`[[-470,300],[1550,1266]]`**.
   The state was rendering small and centre-right with a large empty sea; it now
   fills the frame and the Aleutians bleed off the left edge as station A always
   specified.
