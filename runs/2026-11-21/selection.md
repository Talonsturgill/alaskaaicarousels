## Decision
Senate passage of H.R. 2347 as amended (the Capito substitute, the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act), 62 to 33 on November 19th, with Murkowski and Sullivan yea (S01 to S03). The Alaska hook is in the primary text, read in full by the showrunner: the bill names Alaska once, in new Federal Power Act section 224(d), where the Secretary of Energy may name an Alaska congestion area only "subject to the approval of the Regulatory Commission of Alaska" (S07, S08, S26). Everywhere else the Secretary names areas after comments, with no approval step (S09). The same bill writes a data center cost floor, new FPA 229, "No subsidy of data centers" (S12), with no megawatt threshold in the passed text (S21) against 20 MW as introduced (C17), and lets a State bill data centers as their own retail class (S16).

Criteria: (1) concrete Alaska impact, both senators' votes and a state commission given a veto in federal law; (2) visual potential, a 689,857-character text with one Alaska subsection, a four-vote ladder 73/70/65/62 (C01 to C04), a cost floor; (3) tangible, every claim from primary text; (4) a coworker forward, yes for utility, RCA, legislative staff.
