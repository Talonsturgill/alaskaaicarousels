One subsection. That is the only place the Senate's energy bill names Alaska.

Section 224(d) lets the Energy Secretary identify an Alaska congestion area only "subject to the approval of the Regulatory Commission of Alaska." Elsewhere there is no state approval step, only comments.

The Senate passed it 62 to 33 on November 19th. Murkowski and Sullivan voted yea.

Section 229 bars charging other customers any part of a data center's incremental cost. The data center keeps paying if it quits early. A State may bill data centers as their own class on less favorable terms.

The clause in its own words opens the carousel, four Senate roll calls follow, and the data center cost rule closes it.

The bill now heads to the House.
