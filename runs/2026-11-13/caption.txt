Waymo's cars have been plugging into the chargers at Golden Valley Electric's Fairbanks lot, and a co-op staffer saw it herself.

The sun left that lot at 3:49 p.m. on November 13th.

In town, Mayor Mindy O'Neall called the testing "a great opportunity". Resident Gwen Jackson expects "they'll probably just stall out".

For the state, Governor Mike Dunleavy sees "no better proving ground for winter driving than Fairbanks".

How long the testing runs, why Fairbanks and whether driverless cars follow were still unanswered as of Alaska's News Source's November 10th report.
