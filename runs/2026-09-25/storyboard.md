# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 68 — September 25th

## THESIS

The November 3rd ballot chooses who holds the state's pen on two of the six data center sites
now in play in Alaska, the North Slope lease at Milepost 390 and the Houston land, plus the veto
on a likely data center bill. The Air Force and FERC sign the other four, whoever wins. And the
power condition two candidates put on their support is already how the North Slope file is
drawn. Position taken on jurisdiction; none on the candidates.

PDF document title (60 chars max): `Six data center sites. Two answer to Juneau.`

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here

Artwork craft, weakest in 8 of the last 10. The fix is in the PLAN: every frame's window was
chosen, in a prototype, so its lower third holds measured real relief, and the flat Slope frame
(04) was given the finest DEM (z11) and the Sag terraces. One bench (akscribe) does the whole
tone system; the rendered pen is the deck's single object.

## PROTOTYPE FINDINGS, before any slide was committed

1. akhachure strokes every cell, so on a light table flat ground became FUR. akscribe's
   `AKS.scribeRelief` now cuts only where the measured slope clears a threshold in metres per km.
2. Strokes alone read as fuzzy clumps. A continuous lit-face glow (`AKS.glow`, a Lambert of the DEM
   above flat ground's own value) under the strokes gives ridges form.
3. The first DEM box was too narrow and printed its own edge as a slab; the corridor DEM now runs
   163 W to 136 W, 58.6 N to 71.6 N.
4. The pen's lathe was culled by a negative scale; fixed, and the resin needs a strip light in the
   IBL to hold a rim on dark coat.
