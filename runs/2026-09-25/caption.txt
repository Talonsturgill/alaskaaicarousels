From the North Slope to Cook Inlet, six sites could host data centers, and Alaska's next governor could reach two.

At Milepost 390, DNR's preliminary decision describes a 1 to 3 GW gas plant that feeds its computers and no Alaska grid. Near Houston, a DNR notice proposes conveying about 19,950 acres to AIDEA. Neither decision is final. The governor appoints DNR's commissioner.

The rest are federal. Air Force proposals for JBER, Eielson and Clear closed June 29th with no award. FERC takes Cook Inlet permit filings until November 2nd. Alaska votes November 3rd. The winner can veto any data center bill.

The maps ink the two state sites in gold and ring the four federal ones in dashes. All four candidates are quoted at equal size.

How much should data centers count in a vote for a governor who could reach two sites and holds a veto?
