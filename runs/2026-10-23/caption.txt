A proposed Fairbanks ordinance says today's code permits a large data center in only two zones. Neither is industrial.

Heavy Industrial is 3,555 mapped acres. Light Industrial is 5,929.

The two zones it names are General Use districts GU-1 and GU-5. That is about 97 percent of the zoned acres. The code names no data center use.

The draft would pause large data center permits through December 31st, 2027. Its first test is 5 MW of peak demand. GVEA's first alert came October 12th and reached Level 2. Level 2 means reserves below 15 MW. Two units were in maintenance, a third went offline.

Argue the 5 MW now. The agenda gives the Planning Commission 90 days to recommend.

The zoning map by acre, the industrial sliver, the floor test drawn to scale and 5 MW against GVEA's alert lines.

If you pay a GVEA bill, how many megawatts is large?
