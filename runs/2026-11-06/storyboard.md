# "THE ADDRESS LINE": outside AI money in Alaska's U.S. Senate race

## THESIS
Alaska's Senate seat drew more money from AI-company employees than any other 2026 Senate race, from donors none of whom
listed an Alaska address, in a race separated by 637 votes that only Alaskans count. The money is legal and comparatively
small. What the record shows is where it came from and how it moved (joint committees that make the candidates' own
filings show a fraction of it), and the deck draws that honestly, both candidates on one scale, with no implied exchange.
Document title (<= 60 chars): `Outside AI money in Alaska's Senate race, mapped` (48 chars)

## ARC, nine frames
01 HOOK: first in the nation, zero Alaska addresses (globe at dusk, 19 cords cross the gold coast).
02 PAYOFF: about $119,000 from at least 19 donors, split by candidate on one scale at the coastline.
03 KEEPABLE DATA: the national field on one scale, Rounds the top candidate, Alaska the top race; the counting rules ledger.

05 BREATHER: same map at night, a second stream; the Air Force's April offer of base land.

08 THE COUNT: 637 votes, a quarter still uncounted, tabulation November 18th (temperature peaks on the tie).
09 CLOSE: polar night, Polaris over the address line, one ask.
