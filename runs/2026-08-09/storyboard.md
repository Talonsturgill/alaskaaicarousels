# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 30 — 2026-08-09

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis

**Kenai's rulebook can measure a five year old to the minute and can only
describe a seventeen year old with an adjective, and the AI regulation moving
through the same room the same morning draws no grade line at all.**

The argument is about ORDER and PRECISION, never about bad faith. The district
built a measuring instrument that stops exactly where the machine gets useful,
and it bought the machine before it finished the rulebook. Both of those are
ordinary. Together, sixteen days before school, they are the story.

### Title (PDF document title, 43 chars)

`The Ladder Stops at Fifth Grade`

### The directors room, and what was taken from where

Three lenses pitched. No single treatment won outright and the synthesis takes
a named organ from each.

- **DATA JOURNALIST** supplied the hero, **THE OPEN TREAD**, a stepped
  instrument whose tread depths ARE the published minute caps and whose sixth
  tread has no material in it. It is the thesis as one object, and it was the
  pitch that diverged most cleanly from all four banned heroes. Also supplied
  the orthographic discipline (doctrine forbids perspective on quantities, so
  the hero renders under an orthographic camera and every measurement stays
  parallel-projected while still climbing to GPU PBR).
- **FIELD DOCUMENTARIAN** supplied **THE TWO MATERIALS** and the gold rule,
  which is the best single idea in the room. Clay means proposed, stone means
  already true, and gold marks a fixed point. Nothing that is a draft ever
  touches gold, so the deck's hardest guardrail (nothing is adopted) is
  enforced by the material system rather than by a reviewer remembering. Also
  supplied the palette family and the 6:00 PM warm turn.
- **SYSTEMS ILLUSTRATOR** supplied the run's most important factual save. Two
  of the four evening documents are listed "First Reading & Action" (C28, C30),
  so the board MAY have acted on them, and a deck drawing seven un-adopted
  documents would have asserted seven non-adoptions and been wrong twice. That
  is why the adoption seat has a third state, UNKNOWN, and why slide 06's
  schedule prints it. Also supplied the edge discipline, the area encoding on
  the ladder (height is minutes, width is grade span, so the widest span has
  zero area), and the demand that the missing-minutes fact carry a claim-id.

Two directors independently arrived at nearly the same cover line and all three
independently asked for the light register. Both are taken as signal.

The systems lens also refused to draw an absence without an id, which sent the
showrunner back to the source. `egov.kpbsd.org/meetings/666/minutes` and
`/667/minutes` both render a 404 while `/661/minutes` renders in full, so the
absence is real and is now **C56**. That re-fetch also turned up **C57**, which
is better than the fact it was checking. The July 6th minutes are marked
"Meeting Minutes-Approved (08-03-2026)". The district approved the previous
meeting's minutes on the very day whose own minutes are still not posted.

### Continuity system

**Three devices. None appears on the banned list (THE HOLLOW FIGURE, THE
STANDING COUNT, THE SINGLE TAKE, THE FALLING KEY).**

**(A) THE FLOOR DATUM.** The bottom-third device this run requires. A physically
extruded graduated plinth lying on a lit birch floor. Top face is a
parallelogram from y=1040 at its back edge to y=1120 at its front edge, front
face y=1120 to 1178, and below it the floor runs to y=1350 carrying the contact
shadows. It is form-shaded with `AK.reliefShade`, it has three value steps
(top, front, side), and every object in the deck stands on it and casts a
two-part shadow into a **lit birch pool** raised about 10 L* under the contact.
It is not a plate, not a rule and not a hairline. It **changes its graduation
unit** slide to slide and prints that unit in the title block, which is how a
drawing declares its scale.

**(B) THE TWO MATERIALS.** Every solid in the deck is one of exactly two
substances and which one is a factual statement.
- **CLAY**, matte and pale, `#F4F1EA` lit and `#B9B4A6` shade with a cool
  ambient drift toward `#8E9AA6`, baseline drawn in phantom dash. **Means
  proposed.** Every policy in this deck is clay.
- **STONE**, dense and dark, `#5A5F63` lit and `#2A2E33` shade, one crisp gold
  arris. **Means already true.** Exactly two objects in nine slides are stone.
The difference reads at 432px because it is VALUE plus EDGE, not a hue tint.

**(C) THE OPEN TREAD.** The absence itself, present in some form on all nine
slides, and never sealed. On 09 it is still open, because the record is.

### C. ART DIRECTION

**7. Technique stack.** `#87 akthree` GPU PBR under `THREE.OrthographicCamera`,
`AKT.rigs.galleryWhite` cooled to 6200K, key azimuth 300 elevation 26,
PCFSoft shadow map 2048, procedural softbox IBL, ACES. `setPixelRatio` BEFORE
`setSize` via the bench factory, never hand-rolled. `await AKT.snapshot(R)` and
check `.ok` (an ortho object hero on a bright ground is exactly the coverage

onto the 2D art canvas, then grade. Plus `#39` cabinet reference for the datum
side face, `AK.reliefShade` on the datum top (height callback, macro bevel,

`#89 AKPOST.grade` once on the art canvas.
Seed `const rnd = AK.rng(20260810)`. No `Math.random`, no `Date`.
**akpost high-key variant**, because the house grade is written for dark arctic
and will crush this deck: bloom 0.05, exposure +0.05, saturation 0.92,
