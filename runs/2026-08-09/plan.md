### The ONE standing weakness this run attacks, and how

**Top-loaded composition. Six of the last ten decks shipped it and the most
recent is yesterday.** It is not the weakest CRITERION (that is artwork
craft, worked one run ago), it is the single defect CLASS that keeps
shipping inside a WARN, and it is upstream of two others on the list: when
every element is packed into the top two thirds, the bottom third goes dead,
which is exactly the "dead lower zone" the dossier gate's field 4a was
invented to kill, and crowding the top is what produces "art touching
glyphs" and "busy art under text".

The attack is at Phase 5, in the dossiers, not at Phase 9:

1. Every dossier's field 4a must name a **load-bearing element whose visual
   centre of mass sits below y=900** of 1350, and it must be an element with
   modeled tone, not a plate, not a caption, not a rule. Where the deck's
   subject genuinely lives at the top of the frame, the lower third carries
   the *consequence* of it, drawn.
2. The deck adopts **one continuity device that lives in the bottom third**
   across all nine slides, so the zone is structurally occupied by
   construction rather than by remembering.
3. At Phase 8 the pixel critics are told top-loading is this run's named
   target and asked to answer it explicitly per slide, rather than leaving it
   to qa.py's warn, which has been ignored six times.

Secondary, carried but not the headline: artwork craft. Countered by the
dials above (visual_density 2 forces fewer, better-drawn marks rather than
more of them) and by the bespoke_check drawn-share floor.
