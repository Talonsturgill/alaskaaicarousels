# Selection, run 2026-11-22, Carousel No.122

## Decision
S1, Administrative Order 359. Dunleavy's standing order (August 4th, 2025) orders an annual Government Efficiency Review of every executive branch agency, run by OMB, and tells every agency to "Utilize technology and artificial intelligence (AI) to review large datasets in order to determine how State of Alaska funds are actually being spent." It "remains in effect until revoked." On November 18th Governor-elect Kreiss-Tomkins named Alexei Painter, leaving the Legislature's budget staff, as OMB director, the office the order makes responsible. He takes office December 7th.

Why: (1) Alaska impact, the order binds every department, board, commission and public corporation in state government; (2) visual potential, the order has countable structure (six review objectives, seven agency duties, two of them AI, two initial focus areas) and a clean handoff date; (3) tangible, accounts payable and grants to non-state entities are named targets; (4) an Alaskan would forward it, a state AI mandate few readers know exists, at the moment its owner changes. It is also the first deck in two weeks that is not about data center land or power, which the ledger shows saturated (No.100, 102, 104, 108, 111, 112, 113, 119, 120).
