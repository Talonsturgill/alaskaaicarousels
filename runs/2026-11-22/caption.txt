Alaska's order telling every agency to review spending with AI has no end date. A new governor inherits it on December 7th.

Leaving it alone is a decision too.

Mike Dunleavy signed Administrative Order 359 on August 4th, 2025.
Every agency is told to use AI to see how state funds "are actually being spent."
Two of its seven agency duties name AI.
Grants to non-state entities and accounts payable are the first focus areas.
The order puts the Office of Management and Budget in charge.
On November 18th Governor-elect Kreiss-Tomkins named Alexei Painter to lead that office.

Swipe for the AI clause word for word, the order's seven duties and its own 2025 case, then the new budget director and the December 7th handoff.

Should a standing AI order over the budget stay one governor's call, or should the Legislature get a say?
