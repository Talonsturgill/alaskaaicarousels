# THE STRUCK REGISTER

## THE SYNTHESIS, AND WHAT CAME FROM WHERE

Three treatments came back. The **data-journalist's THE STRUCK REGISTER** wins
and is the chassis, for one reason above the others: it found a FINDING rather
than a restatement. On a logarithmic rule the distance from 11 integrated to 238
registered is wider than the distance from 238 to the 750 ceiling, which is
invisible on a bar chart and invisible on No.40's census grid, and it is true of
numbers that came off one page on one date.

Three organs were transplanted, each named so the retro can trace it.

**FROM THE SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR, the ordering law on slide 07.** Draw what IS in
code BEFORE what is not. Its treatment put a fitted, aged 2023 coupling on the
rail to the left of an empty seat, so a reader who stops early has still learned
that Anchorage has a rule. That ordering is adopted verbatim as a constraint,
because the deck's worst available error is implying Anchorage has no rules on
police AI, and ordering kills it more reliably than any caption.

**FROM THE SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR, the closed vocabulary.** Not a list of things to
avoid, a list of primitives the drawing code is permitted to contain. See THE
UNDRAWABLE LIST below.

**FROM ALL THREE, the Alaska problem.** Every director independently named "there
is no Alaska in this deck" as their own top weakness. Three independent agents
converging on one criticism is the strongest signal this process produces, so it
is fixed, and it is fixed the data-journalist's own way because that way is the
honest one. Slide 04 carries the Municipality of Anchorage as a BLIND EMBOSS,
deboss lips catching the raking key, NO INK, and critically NO CAMERA DOTS,
because camera locations are not in claims.json and drawing them would invent
data. One place, once, no ink.

The essayist's CONTROL JOINT is not used. Its central metaphor is excellent and
its own risk assessment is why it lost: it admitted three of nine frames were a
sentence on a gray surface, which is the exact 6.0 artwork score this run is
attacking.

## THESIS

On one night in one room the Anchorage Assembly funded the camera network and
left the rule governing it unstruck, and every number in this story sorts into
two piles, what has been voted into effect and what has not, so the deck draws
that sort and refuses to draw anything else.

## HERO STRUCTURE

**NEW: THE STRUCK REGISTER.** Every slide is a fresh intaglio proof pulled on
warm laid stock. Value is made the way an engraver makes it, by how densely the
copper is worked, and the paper's own white is the only true light in the deck.
This is the mezzotint physics inverted for a light ground: where a dark deck
scrapes lights out of a rocked ground, this deck pushes darks into a bare one,
and every type reserve is a BLIND-DEBOSSED TROUGH the press has driven into the
sheet, with a lit upper lip, a shadowed lower lip, paper tooth on its floor and
laid chain lines visibly bending into it.

That is the plan's first instruction satisfied literally rather than
metaphorically. The type block is a hole the drawing cut, the hole is modeled,
and there is NO PLATE anywhere in this deck.

Continuity is the paper, the light, the ink law and the motif. It is not a shared
camera. Nine slides, nine different figures: a trough, an arc of struck counters,
two seals with a broken bracket, a log rule, three gate marks, a kerf, a two
column register, a mirrored day rule, a burnished seal.

## ATMOSPHERE

**PRESSROOM RAKE, warm bounce on laid stock. Key azimuth 296 degrees, elevation
26 degrees. Key to fill 5 to 1.**

Low and over the reader's left shoulder. Elevation 26 is chosen from akengrave's
own note that a low raking key spreads `ndotl` across its full range and makes
the swelled line legible, where a high key flattens every stroke to one width.
The fill is warm bounced paper light, not arctic ambient, which is why every
shadow in this deck is brown-neutral rather than blue. One global light for the
whole deck. Every deboss is lit on its upper lip and shadowed on its lower.

## THE UNDRAWABLE LIST (adopted from the systems-illustrator, verbatim)

The render code contains no function capable of producing any of these. This is a
closed vocabulary, not a list of things to avoid.

**FORBIDDEN PRIMITIVES.** Faces. Person glyphs. Body outlines. Heads, torsos,
crowds, silhouettes. Bounding boxes of any kind, with or without corner ticks.
Reticles, crosshairs, targets. Lens irises or apertures, because an iris reads as
an eye. Any aimed camera. Any matching, scanning or recognition motif. Any line
joining two data marks.

**PERMITTED PRIMITIVES.** Laid paper, chain lines, deboss troughs and their lips,
plate bite edges and bevels, struck discs, struck squares, seals, kerfs, hatch
fields, engraved lays, decade ticks, chevrons, stop bars, dimension calls,
extension lines, leaders, tolerance brackets, registration marks.

A camera in this deck is a COUNT and never a picture of a camera, and that
constraint is also the thesis, which is why it costs nothing.
