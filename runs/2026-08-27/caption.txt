Two drones released 374.3 grams of silver iodide over the Kenai Peninsula on August 23rd, about the mass of a can of soup.

Rainmaker's report puts the precipitation attributed to seeding at a mean of 57.62 acre feet, in a 5th to 95th percentile range of 41.70 to 89.35.

The flare releases ran between 06:38 and 09:33 UTC. Soundings put the average freezing level near 1,874 metres.

A summary post the same day says "approximately 19 million gallons".

The lowest radar beam sits near 1,800 to 2,200 metres. Rainmaker says its range doesn't cover what happens below that beam.

An operation measured to the gram produced a number that spans a factor of two.

The deck carries the operations log as published, the beam floor drawn to scale and one figure at three widths.
