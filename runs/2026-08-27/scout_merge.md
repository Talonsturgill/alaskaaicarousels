## THE LEAD CANDIDATE

**A California startup flew machine-learning-directed drones over the Kenai
Peninsula, claimed 19 million gallons of rain it says would not otherwise have
fallen, and no Alaskan appears to have been asked first.** (Beat F)

Rainmaker Technology Corporation says two Elijah drones ran seven cloud seeding
missions over the Kenai on August 22nd and 23rd, 2026, releasing 19 silver
iodide flares, and published a technical validation report on August 24th.

Why this is the run's story:

1. It is genuinely new. Zero Alaska mainstream coverage as of the 27th. Not the
   Peninsula Clarion, not KDLL, not KSRM, not ADN, not the Beacon, not Alaska
   Public Media. One Alaska outlet, Alaska Watchman, on August 25th. The
   national pickup skews conspiracy-adjacent. A physical operation over Alaska,
   covered everywhere except Alaska, is itself the finding.
2. The AI is real and load-bearing, not decorative. Rainmaker's Stratus is a
   machine learning platform with 20 plus data integrations and 8,000 plus
   telemetry variables feeding quantitative precipitation estimation, and its
   July 2025 alliance with Atmo supplies ensemble deep learning weather models
   that simulate thousands of seeding strategies to pick targets. This is
   ML-directed targeting flying autonomous aircraft over Alaska.
3. The honest frame is a structural one this page already practices. The
   19 million gallons is not a measurement. It is a modeled counterfactual
   subtracted from radar-derived precipitation, and Rainmaker's own report
   publishes a band of 41.70 to 89.35 acre-feet around the 57.6 mean, a factor
   of more than two. Cook Inlet Gas Watch publishes measured storage, modeled
   demand and the derived residual separately for exactly this reason. The deck
   can hold a company to the discipline the house already keeps.
4. It is visually rich and it suits this run's surface-density plan. Real
   geography in the repo's own geodata, a 249.53 square kilometre affected area,
   374.3 grams of silver iodide, seven runs across three hours thirty six
   minutes, release altitudes 11,500 to 14,100 feet into cloud between minus 5
   and minus 15 Celsius, and an error bar that IS the argument.

EDITORIAL CAUTION, carried from the scout and adopted. Three of the six pickups
are conspiracy-adjacent outlets. The frame must be the arithmetic gap between a
modeled counterfactual and a measurement. A "they are spraying us" frame is
dishonest and a "look at this breakthrough" frame is credulous about a number
the company itself brackets by more than two.

## RUNNER-UP

**The Permafrost Discovery Gateway now watches Arctic lakes drain in near real
time, and about 30 northwest Alaska lakes went in June and July.** (Beat C)

Tool launched August 13th, 2026. Anna Liljedahl of UAA and Ingmar Nitze of the
Alfred Wegener Institute published the explainer August 26th. AI over satellite
imagery plus aerial photography back to the late 1940s. About 4,600 lakes
watched on the Seward and Baldwin peninsulas, of which about 200 lost more than
a quarter of their area in summer 2018. Four million Arctic lakes mapped, 70
million thermokarst ponds across Alaska tundra. A millennia-old lake can empty
in a few hours. NSF award 1546024 and a $5 million Google.org grant.

Strong, visual, in window, and squarely Alaskan. Held as the fallback because
the Rainmaker story is the one nobody else is telling.
