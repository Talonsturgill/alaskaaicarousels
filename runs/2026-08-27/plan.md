## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 9 of the last 10 scored runs,
mean 6.65, and 6.0 on the most recent deck. It has been the weakest criterion
for so long that treating it as news would be dishonest. The question is what
this run does DIFFERENTLY, in the plan and in the Phase 5 dossiers, rather than
in a Phase 9 repair pass.

The diagnosis from reading the last four artwork ledger entries is that recent
decks have been strong on SYSTEM and thin on SURFACE. Every one of them
invented a genuinely new hero structure (the locked lens, the vacant fixture,
the 750 apron, the horizon ladder) and then rendered it in large clean masses:
pads, cradles, aprons, horizons. The system reads at thumb size, which is why
the scores hold at 8.5, and there is very little to find at 100 percent, which
is why artwork craft keeps scoring 6. The zoom test in DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 5
is the criterion being failed, not section 6.

So the deliberate divergence is **SURFACE DENSITY AS THE HERO**, and it is
budgeted in the dossiers as a required field rather than left to the build:

1. Every slide's dossier must name a **mark-level texture system** that is
   drawn per-mark and not per-region: hachure, stipple, engraved line, scratch
   contour, dry brush, dither. The 2026 craft scan (Phase 1 below) says the same
   thing from the trend side, that editorial illustration is moving to grain,
   visible stroke and paper tooth. bespoke_check's drawn-share floor of 45
   percent is the machine floor. This run targets a drawn share ABOVE 70
   percent, with rectangle calls reserved for the type furniture.
2. **No large fill ships flat or ships as a single gradient.** Every fill over
   about 200 by 200 design px carries either a drawn texture pass or a modulated
   ramp with grain. A gradient inside a fillRect is still a box.
3. **Line-weight hierarchy is declared per slide**, 3 to 4 distinct weights
   each with a stated meaning, per doctrine section 5.
4. The hero climbs the rendered ladder (akthree or aksdf) with an akpost grade,
   and every akthree slide carries its designed Canvas fallback.

The three shipping defect classes (tiny text, top-loaded composition, busy art
under text) are all downstream of the same choice, so the dossiers also carry:
the type reserve is DESIGNED into the art as a quiet region routed by the
composition, never added afterwards as a plate; and the bottom band carries
modeled tone on every slide per doctrine section 1.
