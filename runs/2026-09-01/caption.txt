The Air Force offered federal land for AI data centers. The pause Fairbanks North Star Borough asked for can't reach a foot of it.

Resolution 2026-25 sets 25 priorities, 21 state and 4 federal. One heading appears on both lists. It asks for a moratorium on new commercial data centers on state-owned or municipal lands.

Twelve parcels were on offer at three installations. Only Eielson is in this borough. Responses closed May 29th.

Presiding Officer Scott Crass told KUAC by phone that federal land was intended too. The text adopted on August 27th says state or municipal lands.

The heading printed twice, the seven standards it waits on, the twelve parcels and the property line between them.

One sentence, two governments. Which copy does the work, the one whose words fit its target or the one whose intent does?
