In 2027 camera hardware moves into Alaska's fisheries monitoring budget while the AI that could review the video waits on staff.

Who pays for the camera? Congress has, since 2023. That money is expected to run out this year. The draft puts hardware at $6,002.96 a year per fixed-gear camera boat, inside a preliminary $5.45M budget.

Will your trip get picked? In the draft an observer rides 31.35 percent of Bering Sea and Aleutian fixed-gear trips and 9.31 percent in the Gulf. Under 40 feet, nobody is chosen.

Who watches your footage? A review provider, priced per hard drive reviewed. NMFS lists the AI project for 2027 "pending availability of staff resources."

The slides show the budget, draft odds for all ten strata and the AI line as printed.

How much of that $5.45M should reach the AI review line before more cameras go aboard?
