Why, by the criteria in order:
1. Concrete Alaska impact: armed drone live fire now happens on the training area beside Anchorage
   and Eagle River, and the Army's Arctic division is shaping its drone kit around Alaska's cold and
   mountains. Named units, places, dates and soldiers, all from primary documents.
2. Visual potential: an object (the FPV quad), a place (the Chugach front, the Tanana flats), a
   physical problem with geometry (a radio shadow behind a ridge that a relay fills) and a physical
   problem with heat (a battery losing charge in the cold). Each frame has a different drawable.
3. Tangibility: dated live-fire exercises and a competition with a winner, not a plan.
4. The coworker test: an Alaskan in defense, aviation, the borough or the university sends this.
