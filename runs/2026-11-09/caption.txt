Alaska's seven rural health AI awards total less than one robotic surgery award in Ketchikan.

About $5.4 million for the seven. About $7 million for the one. Both preliminary. Robotic surgery isn't AI.

The largest of the seven is the Alaska Stroke Coalition's $3,156,626 imaging network across 21 acute care hospitals its award never names. Three AI awards filed "Statewide" hold about $4.5 million. Seven of 254 Year 1 awards. About two cents a dollar.

CMS awarded Alaska $272,653,149 for Year 2 on November 9th, second only to Texas. The state health department plans a dashboard of every award by year end.

A blister web holds all 254 Year 1 awards at one scale with the seven AI awards in gold. A table gives their aims and a map shows where the AI money is filed.
