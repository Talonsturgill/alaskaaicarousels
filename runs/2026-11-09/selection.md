# SELECTION, 2026-11-09, No.110

## The pick
ALASKA'S RURAL HEALTH TRANSFORMATION MONEY, YEAR TWO, READ AGAINST YEAR ONE'S FINAL LEDGER.

CMS announced the second-year awards on November 9th (the run date): Alaska $272,653,149, second only to Texas. Alaska's own Year 1 cycle closed on October 22nd with 254 awards and just under $249 million, and the state's final Notice of Intent to Award workbook now lets the deck read the whole first year line by line. Parsed by the showrunner: 7 of the 254 awards buy an AI tool, $5.44 million together, about 2.2 percent of the awarded total; a statewide stroke AI imaging network across 21 hospitals is more than half of that. The rest of the money went to access (40 percent), technology that is not AI (telehealth, records, portals), workforce and pay-for-value. CMS's Year 2 fact sheet says initiative funding will be judged at checkpoints that include results measurement, and the state has promised a public dashboard by the end of the calendar year.

3. Tangible: a federal award announced today and a closed, published award list.
4. Would an Alaskan send it to a coworker: every rural clinic and tribal health organization applied for this money, and the Year 2 application round closed October 28th with notices due as early as December.
