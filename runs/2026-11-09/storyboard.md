# "SEVEN OF 254": Alaska's rural health transformation money, Year 2 awarded, Year 1 read pocket by pocket

## THESIS
Alaska's Year 1 rural health list has 254 awards and seven of them include an AI tool, about $5.4 million, roughly two cents of each dollar awarded. Most of that AI money is filed "Statewide", with no site named. Each of the seven says what its AI should do (read a stroke scan, spare a transfer, halve charting time), and Year 2's $272,653,149 now rides partly on progress checks that include establishing methods for measuring results, so the public dashboard the state has promised by year end should report those seven aims. Not "AI is underfunded" and not "AI is hype": it was bought with stated aims, so count them in public.
UPDATE framing: the program was covered by No.35, No.45 and No.59 (all outside 30 days); the new developments are the November 9th Year 2 award, the closed Year 1 list (254 awards) and the CMS Year 2 rules. The kicker on 02 carries the news.
