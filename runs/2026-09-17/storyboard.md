### Variety ledger check, and the required divergence

Last four decks: No.57 a single water column descended on one depth mapping;
No.58 a concrete pad with a bronze monument disc; No.59 a cargo hold with a
repeated unit crate; No.60 nine nadir plan views with cut plates. Three of those
four were AN OBJECT UNDER A DECLARED KEY AT LOW ELEVATION and the fourth was a

**This deck's divergence is structural, not cosmetic.** The subject is a
CONTINUOUS LINE STRUCTURE crossing the frame and receding, not an object on a
surface. There is no plinth, no table, no staged prop with a shadow puddled at

### THE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS DECK ATTACKS, and how it is measured

`frame_balance` has warned top-loaded in 4 of the last 10 runs including No.60,
and artwork craft has been the weakest scored criterion 5 times in 10 at a mean
of 6.9. They are one defect. qa.py measures the bottom third's craft density
against the slide's own frame average, FAILS below 0.60 and WARNS below 0.80,
and a cell only counts when it carries MODELED tone, meaning luminance spread at
or above 8.0 AND tonal histogram entropy at or above 0.55. A flat plate scores
about 0.2. No.60 shipped two slides at 0.77 and 0.79.

**This deck answers it by construction, not by patch.** The camera is above the
snow and pitched down on every frame, so the horizon sits in the upper quarter
to upper third and 59 to 79 percent of each frame is ground. That ground is
carved drift under a 14 degree key with the fence's own barred shadow lying
across it toward the camera, so the bottom band carries modeled tone, direction
AND information, because the bar duty cycle IS the panel's porosity. The target
is every slide at or above 0.95, and no slide below 0.85. It is checked on the
FIRST render of every slide, before any critic is spawned, because a defect the
machine can measure should never reach a reviewer.

Second target, riding the same decisions: Legibility, weakest 2 of 10 at a mean
of 6.8 and never worked on. No rendered string in this deck is below 24 px, and
every string carrying reader meaning is 26 px or above. Set at planning time
here, not discovered at scoring.

### CAMERA MATH (solved once, implemented not eyeballed)

Canvas 1080 x 1350. Vertical fov 54 degrees on every frame, so
`f = (1350/2) / tan(27deg) = 675 / 0.50953 = 1324.4 px`, and `cy = 675`.
Convention: `horizonY = cy - f * tan(pitchDown)`. A ground point at distance d
and camera height h projects to `y = horizonY + f * h / d`.

THE CONTRACT IS THE horizonY IN PIXELS. If ak3d.js or three.js signs the pitch
the other way, the builder solves for pitch and the frame is unchanged.
