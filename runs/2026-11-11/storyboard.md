# "LIGHT PILLARS AT MILEPOST 390": UPDATE of No.96 and No.100, DNR's Final Finding and Decision on ADL 422741

## THESIS
The state priced Alaska's first gigawatt-scale data center lease by the megawatt-hour, and the price falls at the top. DNR's November 10th decision gives STAK Energy 50 years on 715.4 acres at Milepost 390 for an appraised Base Rate floor plus a surcharge on power delivered to compute: no surcharge under 100 MW of average load, $0.20 to 500 MW, $0.40 to 1,000 MW, then $0.30 and $0.16. DNR says the decline is there to encourage the full buildout. The deck shows the shape, sizes it against all of Alaska's 2024 generation, and asks whether a rate should fall as the campus grows.
Document title (PDF): see copy.json `document_title`.
