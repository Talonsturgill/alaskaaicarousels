$0.40 a megawatt-hour, then $0.16. Alaska's new data center lease cuts its power surcharge once the campus passes a gigawatt.

DNR approved the 50-year lease on November 10th for 715.4 acres a mile west of the Dalton at Milepost 390.
STAK's rent is the larger of an appraised Base Rate and a surcharge on each megawatt-hour delivered to compute.
No surcharge under 100 MW of average load. $0.20 to 500 MW. $0.40 to 1,000 MW. Then $0.30 and $0.16.
DNR says the decline is meant to encourage the full planned buildout.
The top tier ends at about three times all the electricity Alaska generated in 2024.

Five columns of light carry the tiers at true scale, beside DNR's reason for the drop, Alaska's 2024 output and the permits still ahead.

A fair incentive to finish the build, or the state charging less right where the load is largest?
