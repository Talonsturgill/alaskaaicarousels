## THESIS
Two companies asked for the same empty gas field under the Kenai airport and read the same rock two ways. Its preliminary finding still treats the empty pore space as the state's, and the City of Kenai says it isn't. Nothing is leased yet, and the public can weigh in until 5 p.m. November 13th.

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here
Artwork craft was the weakest criterion in 6 of the last 10 runs, mostly a frame's largest object being its least modelled, texture artifacts and dead lower thirds. Here the largest object on every frame is the specimen or a part of it, and each is built as a modelled material first (PBR sandstone with grain in the albedo, clearcoat coal, engraved slabs with lit face, lee and cast), before any type goes on. The masterful depth frame, 08, is built first and reviewed alone. Grain is a small tile, never a full-frame turbulence rect.

## HONESTY GUARDS (bind every frame)
- The finding is PRELIMINARY. Nothing is leased. "Favors" or "preliminarily chose", never "awarded" or "leased" (C01, C55).
- Every Hilcorp-critical statement is DNR's characterization and is attributed ("DNR says", "per DNR"). Hilcorp couldn't comment before Alaska Public Media's October 16th story (C67) and the deck says so on 04.
- Ownership is disputed and is never settled by the deck. The state's position (C47) always appears with the city's (C57).
- No source ties a data center to Kenai Loop. The context line on 07 says so in those words; the data center figure is a tally of proposals (C71).
- "18 days short" is ENSTAR's conditional claim, relayed by Alaska Public Media, with "could" and "depending on temperatures" (C64).
- The dynamic reservoir model is a physics simulation, not AI (C19 notes). The deck never calls it AI.
- 196 Bcf is a production peak in 2006 and about 70 Bcf is in-state demand now (C35, C36). They are never drawn as one series.
- Strata positions inside the interval are drawn from the finding's description; exact contacts are [design] and every specimen frame says ILLUSTRATION.
