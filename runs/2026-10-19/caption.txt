Under Kenai's airport, ownership changes once, then splits.

The city owns the surface. The state got the oil and gas estate by federal patent on August 25th, 1986.

ENSTAR and Hilcorp want the Kenai Loop sands 8,600 to 10,500 feet below sea level for gas storage. By DNR's count Hilcorp gave about one quarter of the requested data and no dynamic model like ENSTAR's. DNR found ENSTAR's 3.9 Bcf estimate of gas left more reliable than Hilcorp's 6.4 and preliminarily favors ENSTAR. Evidence was the right test.

DNR asserts clear state authority over the pore space. City Manager Terry Eubank says Kenai owns it, on a Pioneer Law opinion. No model settles a deed.

Nothing is leased yet. Comments are due 5 p.m. November 13th.
