# SELECTION, 2026-10-19 (No.90)

## Decision
The Kenai Loop gas storage lease. On October 13th the Alaska DNR Division of Oil and Gas issued a preliminary written finding that a natural gas storage lease in the depleted Kenai Loop Gas Field, under the Kenai airport, should go to ENSTAR rather than Hilcorp. Comment runs to 5 p.m. November 13th. The state chose on the record each applicant handed over: ENSTAR supplied its maps, pressure data and a dynamic reservoir model; Hilcorp supplied about one quarter of what was asked and told the state it lacked a solid understanding of the reservoir's geometry. The City of Kenai says the state does not own the empty pore space at all and that two conflicting leases could follow. ENSTAR says it could be 18 days short of gas this winter.
