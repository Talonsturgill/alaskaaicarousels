# "THE PAD AT PRUDHOE": a plant the size of Alaska, and the one thing on the pad you can't see

## THESIS
On the ground near Prudhoe Bay everything about North Slope Power can be measured except the deal. Its first phase is about the size of all 152 power plants in Alaska combined and the full plan more than three times it, for AI data centers, with $6.2 million of the borough's public money in so far. Before a borough builds that, the public should be able to read the terms.
Document title (PDF): `More Than Three Alaskas of Power, Planned for Prudhoe Bay`
