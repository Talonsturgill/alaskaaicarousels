# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 23 — 2026-08-02

# DECK HEADER

## Thesis

Alaska is buying statewide speech-to-text for eight named classes of public
body on a deadline the public can act on, and the public notice announcing it
prints no technology term at all, which matters because the notice is the only
part of a procurement most Alaskans ever see.

**PDF document title (45 chars).** `What the Notice Buys and What It Doesn't Print`

## The directors room, and how this was synthesized

Three directors pitched under three lenses. All three independently placed the
deck in a records world, all three chose a dark warm register that is not navy,
all three built a state machine of small cells or tags as the continuity device,
and all three built a printed pixels-per-day date rail as the keepable
instrument. Third consecutive run in which blind convergence settled the
material.

**WINNER, the data-journalist's THE POSTING LANES**, on two grounds.

FEASIBILITY. Its camera never moves and its projection is flat and parallel on
all ten slides, so it breaks the one-object-N-stations shape by construction
rather than by argument. One GPU beat, everything else Canvas 2D.

HONESTY. It is the only treatment that solved the trap in this story. An
absence has no magnitude, and a bar whose length encodes a count of zero argues
nothing. Its answer is to encode PRESENCE, filling each notice's lane with one
lit cell per character of that notice's own verbatim text, so the missing term
is a fixed-size hole in a dense lit field rather than a short bar. It prints
`AN EMPTY CELL HAS NO LENGTH` on the slide. That is the single best idea the
room produced.

**GRAFTED FROM THE EDITORIAL-ESSAYIST.** Its decision to cut C23 and C24
entirely. At confidence 0.62 and 0.60, secondary and out of window, the Moriarty
quote buys one sentence of colour and costs the deck its cleanest honesty claim.
The data-journalist had it attributed on S05. It is cut. The deck now asserts
nothing whatsoever about why the Air Force is offering the land.

**GRAFTED FROM THE HISTORIAN-OF-THE-FUTURE.** The instruction that one cool
material must sit on the composition's focal line on every slide, so the thumb
always shows a bright cool or gold horizontal against warm dark.

**REJECTED, from the historian.** THE FIELD BLOCK, five archival rows
(PROVENANCE, METHOD OF CAPTURE, ACCURACY FLOOR, AUDIT RIGHT, DATA RESIDENCY)
ruled and unfilled under a NOT IN THE NOTICE stamp. The director's own strongest
objection was right and is upheld. Public notices essentially never carry those
terms because they live in the solicitation package, so a slide printing their
absence at headline scale discovers that a summary is a summary. The SEARCH
REGISTER survives instead, because it records terms actually searched against
text actually read, which is a defensible finding.

**REJECTED, from the editorial-essayist.** THE RECORD STACK's carbon-paper copy
set, on the director's own self-named objection. Nothing in this story is carbon
paper, and its BREATH stratum, an acoustic mass, was the element most likely to
render as generic noise wallpaper, which is the exact defect this run exists to
kill.

**THE OBJECTION CARRIED INTO EVERY SLIDE.** The data-journalist's sharpest
self-criticism was that its instrument is grander than the argument it carries,
and that the rail, the lattice and the register will all look more certain than
the thesis. That is the failure mode this deck must actively resist. Every
headline is therefore modest and every claim is scoped to the document actually
read. The deck never asserts that a machine will do this work, because no
verified claim says so.

## THE THREE MATERIAL REGISTERS

This is the run's declared answer to artwork craft, weakest in 8 of the last 10
runs at mean 6.38. The diagnosis is not too little machinery, it is UNIFORM
detail. Each recent deck built one generative system and ran it at one weight
across every region of every slide, and uniform detail is wallpaper.

| Register | Detail frequency | Line weights | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE BED** (coarse) | features every 40 to 90 px | hero 5.5px silhouette, bold 3.5px lane wall, otherwise unstroked modeled mass | extruded lanes, gate blocks, the eight relit plates, the Eielson ground dome, the posting board slab. `AK.reliefShade` Sobel form-shading, strength 60 to 200 on macro domes, cabinet extrusion, two-part contact shadows |

**BINDING RULE ONE.** Within a slide, the three regions must carry three
DIFFERENT registers. No region inherits its neighbour's by default.

**BINDING RULE TWO, and this is the mechanism.** THE TOOTH's density is a
function of distance from the slide's declared focal point and falls to 0.25x
beyond 380 px. That is the direct structural answer to one system run at one
weight everywhere, and it is computed rather than eyeballed.

**BINDING RULE THREE, from the 2026-08-02 craft refresh.** Every jitter,
endpoint offset and colour perturbation is drawn from a NORMAL distribution with
a small standard deviation, never a uniform one. Uniform randomness is the tell
of machine-made marks.

## Global build contract

- Seed `AK.reseed(20260802 + slideNo)` and `AK.rng(20260802 + slideNo)`. Zero
  `Math.random`, zero `Date.now`.
- Canvas at 2x backing store. Text is DOM or SVG only, never canvas fillText.
- **TEXT AGAINST GEOMETRY, no third option.** Every string over canvas or SVG
  art ships EITHER on a knockout sized by `AK.svgPlate` / `AK.canvasLabel` from
  the MEASURED string after `await document.fonts.ready`, OR inside a field
  where the lattice and tooth generators multiply density by zero with an 18px

  This is the answer to the hard fail that hit 3 of the last 10 runs and that
