### The ONE standing weakness this run attacks, and how

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest criterion in 7 of the last 10
runs, mean 6.55, last deliberately worked 9 runs ago. It is not a fact about
those runs, it is a prediction about this deck unless the plan is different.

The pattern behind the number, read across those runs, is that the craft
lives in the ANNOTATION LAYER rather than in the rendered image. The deck
gets a rail, a plate, leaders, ticks and mono labels, all of which are
drafting furniture, and then the actual pictorial surface is thin: a fill, a
gradient, a few strokes. That is why "genuine detail" scores low while
"legibility" scores fine. The zoom test is failing in the middle of the
frame, not at the labels.

So this run's attack has three parts, all of which are decided HERE and
enforced in the Phase 5 dossiers rather than repaired at Phase 9:

1. **The rendered ladder is mandatory for the hero and for at least two
   more slides.** Rung 1 (akthree GPU PBR) or rung 2 (aksdf raymarch), with
   an akpost film grade and akcolor OKLCH ramps on the finished canvas.
   No.37 was flat by concept and scored 8.07 with artwork at the bottom.
   Flat is still allowed as a choice, but it competes against a machine that
   can render, and this run has lost that competition seven times.

2. **A substrate pass on EVERY slide, declared in field 9 of its dossier.**
   Every large fill carries a real material: paper tooth, weave, stipple
   density, dither, sediment, grain tile. A slide whose dossier cannot name
   the material of its largest region does not pass the storyboard gate.

3. **A drawn-share budget written into the storyboard header.** bespoke_check
   fails under 45 percent drawn share; the reference deck measures 82. This
   run targets 70 percent and each dossier states how its own frame gets
   there. Axis-aligned rectangles are counted against the budget, gradient or
   no gradient.

The secondary target, since it is cheap to take at the same time, is the
**top-loaded composition** warn (3 of the last 10 runs, latest August 15th).
Field 4a of every dossier names what the bottom band carries with modeled
tone, and dossier_check enforces it before any code is written.
