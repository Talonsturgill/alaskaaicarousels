# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 38 — 2026-08-20

## THE DIRECTORS ROOM, AND WHY THIS TREATMENT WON

Three treatments, three lenses.

**Cartographer** pitched THE RELIEF BLOCK ON THE CUSTOMS TABLE, a carved plaster
Alaska on a felt map table whose scale bar walks from 1:6,000,000 to 1:1 across
nine frames, with the hero sawn open on slide 07 so the customs line that ran
around the coastline reappears inside an airframe. Beautiful, and the scale
collapse IS the thesis. It lost on one thing. The hero is a MODEL of Alaska, and
a model carries an implied authority about terrain that this deck has no data
for. Its own author flagged the second risk, that slide 07's picture asserts a
customs outcome the sources do not support.

**Systems-illustrator** pitched THE CUSTOMS TIPPLE, a bolted steel sorting
machine whose hopper depths are the duty rates and whose conveyor belt is a
calendar at 3.2 px per day. The belt-as-calendar is the single best data-in-art
idea any director produced. It lost on the risk its own author named first and
most honestly. The machine does not exist. There is no physical customs sorter,
and the better it renders the more it reads as a description of how Customs and
Border Protection actually operates, which is a claim nobody verified. This
publication's entire product is that its pictures are true.

**Field-documentarian** pitched THE LOCKED LENS, THE SUBSTITUTED GROUND, and it
wins. One camera, bolted down, six numbers held to the pixel across six slides,
and the GROUND changes underneath it as the story moves the object through real
rooms. Every object in it exists. Wet gravel on a village pad, a stainless
customs bench, plywood and foam in a crate, February snow. The argument is
carried by the object's STATE rather than its position, which is what makes it a
genuine divergence from No.34's dollying camera rather than a repaint of it.

It also has the two best single frames in the room. Slide 05 puts the federal
printout and the state's own release side by side on one wet bench, with the
word count stencilled in the margin, and needs no caption to make its point.
Slide 07 sprays an origin plate on the side of a real machine and leaves the
brackets EMPTY, which turns the claims this run had to kill into the picture
itself. No deck in thirty seven runs has drawn a missing fact as a thing you
could walk up and touch.

### Organs grafted from the losing treatments

- **From systems-illustrator, the Annex I honesty flag.** Slide 03 sorts four
  shapes into two rates, which is an assertion about scope. A mono line now sits
  under the shelf reading that Annex I sets the actual scope by tariff line and
  this deck does not quote it. Adopted verbatim in spirit; it is the difference
  between reporting the category text and claiming a customs outcome.
- **From systems-illustrator, the certification condition drawn as a
  mechanism.** The 15 and 10 percent caps on slide 08 never appear without the
  importer-certification condition printed beneath them.
- **From cartographer, the source-provenance tag pair.** Slide 07 carries one
  solid SOURCED plate and one dashed NOT PUBLIC bracket, so the deck draws its
  own sourcing limit instead of footnoting it.
- **From both losing treatments, independently, the same warning.** Cartographer
  and systems-illustrator each flagged that a gold contour drawn around interior
  parts reads as "these are the imported ones" whether or not the caption says
  so. Two directors converging on one risk is a strong signal, so slide 06's
  interior contours are DASHED PHANTOM, not solid, and carry the label ORIGIN
  NOT ESTABLISHED HERE.

### Showrunner changes to the winning treatment

1. **The missing hiker is not named.** The treatment named him from C36. The
   search outcome is not in the record, and using a named private person whose
   fate we do not know as a rhetorical device in a marketing carousel is not
   something this page does. The deck says "a missing hiker" and keeps every
   verified fact around it.
2. **Slide 05 drops to a 2D bench preemptively.** The treatment's own
   self-critique named `bespoke_check` similarity as its likeliest mid-run
   failure, because a locked camera plus five akthree builds converges the art
   code by construction. Its named fallback was to convert slide 05 to Paper
   Tooth plus akengrave. Taking that at plan time rather than at Phase 8 costs
   nothing and removes the risk before it exists.
3. **Slide 06's gold contours go dashed**, per the graft above.

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis

The September 3rd duty is charged at the border and not at the factory, so it
can't reach the US assembled aircraft Alaska already flies, and the real
exposure is a quieter 25 percent on imported components landing February 9,
2027, inside an airframe that counts as American.

Nine. The default band is 8 to 10. The argument needs four load-bearing beats
(border trigger, category scope, component seam, dates) plus a breather, a
cover, an honesty beat and a close. Eight would cost the honesty beat, which is
the slide a professional trusts the page for. Ten would add a frame with no job.
