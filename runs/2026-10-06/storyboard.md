# STORYBOARD, Carousel No.79, run 2026-10-06
# "THE HALL OF 10,500": one seat is one student, and the rule that sorts them says "devices"

## THESIS
UPDATE to No.61. UAA's first public AI conduct count puts 119 of 150 cited students under one clause of the conduct code about "devices not authorized by faculty members", read through each instructor's own syllabus. The regents take up systemwide AI principles in November, and the draft's answer as reported is that "a human decides". Under today's clause that human is each instructor, which is the system that produced this count. The deck asks whether the principles should say what a student may do.
PDF document title: `Of 150 Cited, 119 Used AI. The Rule Says Devices.`

## HONESTY GUARDS (bind every frame)
- The two years are counted differently and are never subtracted. This year's report counts students CITED (150, 119 for AI); last year's counted students FOUND RESPONSIBLE (115, "about two-thirds" AI). The deck prints each in its own words and never computes a rise between them. The only rises printed are the report's own, 73 percent (AI plagiarism) and 56 percent (misconduct overall), attributed to the report.
- The report itself is not public. Every count is "the Dean of Students Office report, as reported by the Anchorage Daily News", and the frames that print counts say so.
- "About two-thirds" is never converted into a number of students; the 2025 block carries no AI colouring.
- 1.1 percent is 119 of more than 10,500; it is never restated as 150 of anything.
- The hall is ILLUSTRATIVE and says so. Its fan law (row r holds 81 + 2r seats, r 0 to 69) sums to exactly 10,500 [design]; rows beyond are bare steps in haze, uncounted, which is how "more than" is drawn. 150 cited seats are a seeded sample, 119 of them gold; positions carry no meaning, only the count.
- November: the regents' calendar in Alaska time (testimony 4 p.m. November 2nd, committees from 1 p.m. November 4th, full board November 5th and 6th in Fairbanks) and the reporting's "in November" for the principles. No frame puts the principles on a specific day and no frame predicts the vote.
- The report was produced with assistance from generative AI tools: stated flatly, never as a gotcha.
- Quotes are exact; paraphrases carry no quote marks (Taaga's pushback, Harnish on checkers). C21 ("AI cannot act alone") is not used.
- No first person. No "cannot". No banned words. No colons, no em or en dashes, straight quotes, ordinal month-first dates, no current year.
