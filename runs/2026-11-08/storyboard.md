# "A WATERMARK IS NOT A CREDIT LINE": the University of Alaska regents' AI statement and its disputed first draft

## THESIS
A disclosure that only people in the room could see works like a watermark, not a credit line: a public document owes its readers a printed line, from the first public draft, saying who drafted it and with what tools. Both op-eds can be true at once, and that is the failure. The deck takes no side on whether AI is good for UA and never says how the November 5th vote went (killed claim 1).
PDF document title: `Who Drafted UA's AI Statement?` (30 chars)
