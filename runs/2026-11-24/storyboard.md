# STORYBOARD, run No.123, 2026-11-24 · THE THREE COUPLINGS

## DECK HEADER

- **Thesis.** Petersburg's Ordinance 2026-24, as introduced, asked three exact questions of any data center, one at each place a data center touches a town: can the utility supply it from today's grid, in writing; does it need generators for its everyday load; does its cooling stay 100 percent in the loop. On November 16th the Assembly voted 5 to 2 to widen the freeze to all data centers, then passed it 7 to 0, and the final reading is November 30th. Position: a full pause is defensible, but the three tests are the part worth keeping for when it ends on September 30th, 2027.
- **Document title (PDF, <= 60 chars):** `Petersburg drafted three data center tests. Then it widened the freeze.`  (shortened in copy.json to fit 60: `Petersburg's three data center tests, and the wider freeze`)
- **UPDATE.** This is an UPDATE of No.102 (November 1st), which covered the October 5th direction to draft. The material new development is the drafted text and the November 2nd and 16th votes, and the cover says UPDATE.
- **Arc.** 01 hook (the module and its three couplings, each clamped in gold) -> 02 payoff (5 to 2, then 7 to 0; the gold now wraps the whole module) -> 03 test one, the grid in writing -> 04 test two, generators -> 05 test three, the closed loop (the lit peak) -> 06 breather (Mitkof Island at night: there is no data center here) -> 07 the five tallies -> 08 the clock to September 30th, 2027 (keepable) -> 09 close (one ask). Temperature: night and quiet 01 to 02, procedural and close 03 to 04, the bright working light of 05, the darkest rest 06, record-keeping 07 to 08, a calm close 09.
