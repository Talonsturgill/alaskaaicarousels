On November 2nd, Petersburg's draft data center freeze had three tests. On November 16th, a 5 to 2 vote widened it to every data center.

Three drafted tests, five Assembly tallies and the clock to 2027.

The tests were exact. The utility had to confirm in writing that today's grid could fully supply the load. No generators for everyday power. Coolant had to stay 100 percent in the loop.

Jeff Meucci's amendment tied 3 to 3 on November 2nd and passed two weeks later. The Petersburg Pilot says it closes the opening left for Mazzella's 2-megawatt project.

As of mid-November, Petersburg had no data centers. The final vote is scheduled for November 30th. A full pause is defensible.

The tests are worth keeping. If the freeze ends on September 30th, 2027, should they become the rules, or should Petersburg start over?
