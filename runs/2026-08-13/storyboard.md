# STORYBOARD, Alaska.Ai carousel No. 32, 2026-08-13

## THE DIRECTORS ROOM, AND HOW THIS WAS SYNTHESIZED

Three lenses ran in parallel: editorial-essayist, systems-illustrator,
cartographer. Two of the three independently arrived at the same hero object, a
letterpress composing stone with a locked chase on it, because both discovered
the same fact: **in printing, "furniture" is the trade's literal name for the
blank material that fills a page and takes no ink.** The selection memo had
already used the word loosely ("the full furniture of a local paper"). It turns
out to be a technical term, and it means exactly what this story is about, form
that occupies the space where labor should be. Two directors finding one object
from different directions is the strongest signal a room can give.

**WINNER: the editorial-essayist's THE LOCKED CHASE**, on four counts. It has a
real turn (slide 6) and a plant-and-payoff on a single claim (C11 planted on
slide 3, detonated on slide 8), which is what makes it an essay rather than a
list. Its cover needs no metaphor decoded at all, because it is simply the
site's own disclosure notice blown up to masthead size. Its slide 6 is legible
at thumbnail with zero words. And it fires no GPU path, so the render risk
collapses to CPU stroke count, which is measurable before the build.

**GRAFTED FROM THE SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR**, whose treatment was the runner-up:

- **THE TYPE-HIGH TEST**, now slide 3, and the best single image any director
  produced. Type-high is 0.918 inches, the plane where the roller touches. A
  machined gauge lies across the forme; hundreds of blocks stop short of it by a
  dimensioned shadow gap; exactly one quoin rises and touches. That is
  "A human editor reviews flagged content before publication" (C09) drawn to
  scale, and it makes the story's most important nuance visible instead of
  explained.
- **THE NOT-FITTED BAY**, a parts-list row reading `REPORTING, QTY 0, NOT
  FITTED`, folded into slide 7's anatomy.
- The 19th plate slot, machined and railed and empty, folded into slide 5.

**GRAFTED FROM THE CARTOGRAPHER**, whose central insight is the sharpest
sentence produced by the room even though its structure lost: **a nautical chart
carries a source diagram, a small panel telling you which soundings came from a
2019 multibeam survey and which came from a lead line in 1901. It is the chart
telling you where to trust it. Bylines, datelines, corrections and an imprint
are a newspaper's source diagram. This site publishes all of it, and publishes
its survey quality honestly, and it changes nothing, because nobody reads a
source diagram in a search result.** That is the thesis, and it goes into the
caption brief and into slide 7. Also grafted: slide 7's content shape, three
Anchorage locations that check out against one claim that has no position at
all, which is a better rendering of C21 and C22 than the essayist's checklist.

**REJECTED, with the reason.** The cartographer's azimuthal-equidistant plate
centered on Anchorage is a beautiful piece of thinking and it dies on its own
self-critique, which was honest enough to name it: eighteen rays converging from
a foreign continent onto Anchorage reads, to a fast scroller, as "this site is
made in Australia." The record does not support that and selection.md forbids
it. A mono footnote at 432px is a texture, not a sentence, so the guard cannot
carry the weight. Structural risk, not fixable by annotation. Also rejected: the
systems-illustrator's slide 8, which it correctly identified as its own weakest.

Both winning directors flagged the same danger and it is real: **letterpress is
a register a large share of the audience will not decode.** The mitigation is
structural rather than explanatory. The cover carries no metaphor at all. Slide
6 reads with zero words as nineteen dull blocks and eighteen bright ones. Every
frame must work as metal under a hard lamp first and as a pun second, and where
a slide would need a caption to explain the joke, the joke goes and the image
stays.

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis

The Daily Anchorage disclosed everything and it changed nothing, because what
separates a local paper from a page that looks like one was never the label, it
was the obligation to still be there tomorrow, and that is the test this site
failed on July 26th.

The emotional turn is **slide 6**. Slides 2 through 5 hunt for a tell inside the
pages and keep not finding a decisive one, because the site is honest and its
content braids real Anchorage addresses with figures nobody can check. Slide 6
relocates the argument off the page and onto the calendar.

**What is withheld until slide 6.** The shape of the date range. Slides 1 to 5
show one date at a time in a corner slug, so the reader accumulates July 8th,
July 15th, July 24th, July 26th without ever seeing the run laid out. Slide 6
puts all 37 days on the stone at once and the cliff is undeniable before a word
is read.

### Slide count rationale

Nine. CAROUSEL_CRAFT's 8 to 10 default band, and the arc needs exactly this
many: the essay cannot land its turn without four slides of honest hunting
before it, and it cannot pay off the slide 3 plant without a synthesis slide
after the turn. Eight would cost slide 4, the breather, and the deck's rhythm
already runs dense. Ten would add a slide with no job.

**Divergence stated explicitly.** No.31 made scale itself the variable under a
relabelling rail. This deck holds scale rigidly constant, because type-high is a
real physical constant (0.918 inches) and a constant is the point. No.30 put a
stepped instrument under an orthographic camera at a declared px-per-minute;
nothing here is stepped and every block is the same height. No.29's hero was a
volume of light rendered by a raymarcher; this fires no SDF and no GPU path at
all, and its brightest region is a wet-ink specular on an opaque face, a surface
phenomenon rather than a source. No.28 was N discrete cards floating on void
with no ground; this is one bounded iron rectangle whose entire function is to
lock things in, and every object is in contact with a surface and throws a
two-part contact shadow onto it.

**And the standing note that five of the last six decks let the camera carry the
concept.** The answer here is aggressive: one camera position, one focal length,
one look-down angle of 30 degrees, unchanged across all nine slides. Nothing on
this deck moves except what is lying on the stone. The subject carries all of
it.

### Variance dials

**design_variance 4, visual_density 4, type_temperature 2.**

Density 4 is spent as a HIERARCHY, not as uniform coverage. This is the run's
central craft correction, from today's FIELD_NOTES entry: detail spread evenly
across a frame does not read as detailed, it reads as noisy, because the eye
needs quiet regions to know where the loud ones are. Every dossier below names
**two focal clusters** where the micro-detail pass runs. Everywhere else the
engraved lay runs at low budget, mainline only, no crossline, no interdots.

Type temperature 2 is honored by three sizes per slide maximum, no italic
anywhere, no display flourish, and a 1.333 modular ratio.

### Global technique stack

Seed base `20260813`, per slide `20260813 + i`.

- **#93 akengrave** is the primary bench and this run's answer to seven of ten
  runs with artwork craft weakest. Its own library entry records that it was
  committed for No.21 and then went unused for five decks while artwork craft
  was the weakest criterion in four of them. `AKENGRAVE.create({seed,
  light:{azDeg:292, elDeg:24}})`, then `await document.fonts.ready`, then
  `eng.reserve(AKENGRAVE.boxesFor('.knock'))`, then `eng.surface(cx, {region,
  form, tone, budget, inkLo:0.06, inkHi:0.62, wMax:2.6, crossDeg:41, step:2.2,
  feather:9})`. Elevation 24 sits inside the documented 20 to 30 legible band and
  does not get raised for prettiness. LOW `tone` MEANS DARK.
- **`form` IS A STORY QUANTITY on every slide**, per the No.27 precedent, and
  every frame prints its own `SURFACE =` stamp in mono. Listed per dossier.
- **#2 Grain Pass.** `AK.grainTile(280, 52, seed)`, repeating tile, mix-blend
  overlay, alpha 0.08. Never a full-frame feTurbulence rect.
- **#89 akpost.** `AKPOST.grade` on the ART canvas after all drawing. **exposure
  is in STOPS here**, house range -0.15 to +0.06, and this deck uses `+0.04`.
  akpost throws past 0.75 stops.
- **#90 akcolor.** All metal and paper ramps from one `AKC.ramp` call, resolved
  to hex arrays once at module top. No nested colour helpers.
- **#70 Scotch Rule**, **#71 Rule Terminals** (45 degree cut, deck-wide), **#68
  pathLength dash symmetry**, **#58 Profile-Heaviest**, **#75 Hatch Knockout
  Windows**, **#84 Instrument Corner Readouts**, **#45 Layered Shadow
  Elevation**, **#42 Multiplane repoussoir**.
- **Five-token weights by meaning.** `--w-hair 0.75` stone tooth and graticule,
  `--w-fine 1.25` proof creases, leaders, dimensions, phantom dashes, `--w-std
  2` block seams and chamfers, `--w-bold 3.5` cut edges of the chase, `--w-hero
  5.5` the chase's outer iron and the type-high gauge.

### Build-critical constraints, carried from the directors' feasibility notes

1. **No GPU anywhere.** Nine slides of Canvas 2D plus DOM and SVG text. No
   akthree, so no `webglOK()` fallback to design and no black-frame sentinel.
2. **akengrave `form` is precomputed into a 4px lookup grid with a bilinear
   sampler** on every slide, because `normalAt` calls `form` four times per
   sample. `cx.filter` is never set inside a stroke loop.
3. **Cast shadows need a lit ground first.** Stone base `#0E1B2C` over `#050A13`
   is more than 4.0 L* of separation, and every contact shadow is cast AFTER
   `eng.surface` so the lay never paints over it.
4. **Text against geometry is killed structurally.**
   `eng.reserve(AKENGRAVE.boxesFor('.knock'))` on MEASURED boxes after
   `document.fonts.ready`, so the colliding stroke is never generated. Knockout
   plates ship under mono readouts as a second, independent layer.
5. **Mirrored type is canvas ink, never DOM.** A DOM transform would put
   reversed strings into the PDF text layer and feed garbage to the semantic
   ranker. All readable copy is DOM, all mirrored relief is drawn.
6. **bespoke_check drawn share, target 0.68.** No `fillRect` marks. Every block
   face is an eight-point chamfered polygon with seeded corner wear at plus or
   minus 0.4px, because worn type metal is not square, so nothing is
   axis-aligned. The only rectangle is the base wash, one gradient element.
7. **Pairwise similarity under 0.60.** The nine `form` functions are structurally
   different. Shared bench setup lives in one run-local module so it is not
   duplicated text across slide files.
8. **Determinism.** Every seed derives from `20260813`. No `Math.random`.

## SLIDE 01 COVER

**1. Beat.** The hook. A verbatim self-disclosure from the subject, true and
completely damning of the reader's assumptions. Plants the open loop: whose
article, and where. Inherits nothing; plants everything.

**2. Copy, final.**
- Mono kicker, 22px, tracked +10%, 2 lines: `THIS ARTICLE WAS WRITTEN BY AI FROM` / `THE LINKED SOURCES AND` (55 chars)
- Display, Instrument Serif, `AK.fitText({min:96, max:132, maxLines:3})`: `was not reviewed by a journalist before publishing` (49 chars) [C06]
- Mono footer, 20px: `PRINTED ON ARTICLES AT DAILYANCHORAGE.NEWS` (41 chars) [C06]
- Counter: `01 / 09`. Dateline slug: `JUL 15`.

**3. Reader takeaway.** A site publishing Anchorage news says on its own
articles that no journalist read them.

**4. Layout map.** Twelfths. Kicker cols 2 to 8, rows 2 to 2.5. Display cols 2
to 11, rows 3 to 5.5, focal point at the rule-of-thirds intersection (720, 450).
Eye path: display line 1, then kicker above it, then the wet inked block below
left. Quiet zone: cols 9 to 12, rows 1 to 2, roughly 14 percent of frame, well
inside the quarter-frame ceiling. One permitted grid violation: the display's
left edge is pulled 6px out of the margin for optical stem alignment.

4a. **Lower-third treatment.** The bottom band carries the chase's near iron
rail running the full width, cabinet-extruded with a lit top face in `#6A7686`
and a shadow face in `#262E3B`, and seated on it the single wet-inked disclosure
block, its face bearing a hard 4 percent specular crest where the rake catches
still-tacky ink. Under both, a two-part contact shadow, tight 6px at 0.34 alpha
and wide 34px at 0.10, cast onto a deliberately lit pool of `#12202E` laid over
the `#050A13` void before any shadow is drawn. In front of all of it, the near
lip of the composing stone runs as a blurred repoussoir bleeding off the bottom
edge, drawn to an offscreen canvas and blurred once at composite. The engraved
lay wraps the rail's cylinder form so the bottom band is modelled tone, not a
plate on bare ground.

**5. Depth plan.** Z-stack: void wash, stone with engraved lay, chase iron rail,
inked block, contact shadows, proof edge at the right cut, DOM type, grain tile.
Depth cues, five: occlusion (block over rail over stone), two-part contact
shadow, atmospheric falloff toward the upper right, DOF on the repoussoir lip,
and specular gradient across the rail's cylinder. Focal plane is the inked
block's face at y 1180.

**6. Continuity state.** Inkless slug is in the chase, unlabeled, top face
catching the rake. Dateline `JUL 15`. Proof edge present, halftone and fold
crease crossing the right cut at y 742.

**7. Technique stack.** #93 akengrave `{seed:20260814, light:{azDeg:292,
elDeg:24}, wMax:2.6, crossDeg:41, step:2.2, inkLo:0.06, inkHi:0.52}`, `form` =
`ONE INKED BLOCK (C06)`, a single plateau of height 1.0 over the block's
footprint falling off as `exp(-d/70)`, precomputed to a 4px grid. #39 Cabinet
Extrusion for the rail and block, depth 0.5 at 45 degrees, three-face light. #16
Halftone Plate on the proof at the right cut, screen angle 45 degrees, radius
from sampled luminance. #43 for the wet-ink specular. #42 repoussoir. #2 grain.
#89 akpost, exposure +0.04.

**8. Data-in-art.** `form` height 1.0 marks the only block on the stone that
carries ink, which is C06's notice. Every other block's height is 0.0, so the
engraved lay flattens across the rest of the frame and the detail budget spends

**9. Palette assignment.** bg `#050A13`, stone `#0E1B2C` lit / `#081120` shadow,

**10. Type spec.** Kicker JetBrains Mono 500, 22px, tracking +10%, leading 1.5,

**11. Anchor spec.** The literal anchor is the inked disclosure block, a

**12. Reference intent.** A compositor's stone photographed for a trade journal
