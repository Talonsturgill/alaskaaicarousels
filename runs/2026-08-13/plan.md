### The one weakness this run attacks, and how

**Artwork craft and genuine detail. Weakest in 7 of the last 10, mean 6.0, and
it has not moved.**

Read as a prediction rather than a report: today's deck scores 6.0 on artwork
craft unless the dossiers change something. The diagnosis from the last three
runs is consistent. Decks have been strong at CONCEPT (a falling frame, a
measure that stops) and thin at SURFACE. A frame reads as an idea executed in
five elements, and the zoom test finds empty paper between them. Density has
been dialled DOWN precisely while the criterion asking for genuine detail was
failing.

The attack has three parts, all of them in Phase 5 dossiers rather than in a
Phase 9 repair pass:

1. **A per-slide DETAIL BUDGET, written into every dossier.** Each slide names
   three scales of information it carries: the read at thumb size, the read at
   feed size, and what only appears at 100 percent. A dossier that can't name
   the third scale is incomplete and gets sent back at the storyboard gate.
   This is the zoom test moved from review to planning.
2. **Drawn share targeted at 65 percent, well above bespoke_check's 45 floor.**
   The gate measures axis-aligned rects against drawn marks. Passing at 46 is
   how a deck lands at 6.0. Every dossier names its drawing technique from
   TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY by entry, with parameters, and no slide's structure may be
   carried by fillRect.
3. **Density dial at 4, and the lower third is a drawn region.** The dossier
   check already forces field 4a; this run spends it on modelled tone that
   belongs to the slide's own subject, not a plate.
