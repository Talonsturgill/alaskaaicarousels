UAF's new guide counts a state transportation container of drone imagery as a data center.

Its type is edge, the bottom rung at about 0.1 to 0.5 MW. Enterprise runs 0.5 to 10 MW and holds the University of Alaska and Providence Hospital. Golden Valley Electric had 15 MW to spare at its first Energy Emergency Alert on October 12th. Its planned battery would supply 70 MW for up to 2 hours.

Hyperscale runs 50 to 100+ MW or even gigawatts, with none operating in Alaska. Interior Secretary Doug Burgum said people "want to drop 10 billion on a data center". He named no investor, and 10 billion fits no rung.

Every frame is 100 MW wide at one scale, so the carousel is one gigawatt long. On it are the 15 MW cushion, five data center sizes and a planned 70 MW battery.

What size of data center, if any, should Fairbanks say yes to?
