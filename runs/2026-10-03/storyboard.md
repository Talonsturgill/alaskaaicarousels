# "THE SUNDIAL FIELD": Fairbanks votes Tuesday

## THESIS
None of the six Fairbanks North Star Borough Assembly candidates told Alaska's News Source they support data centers (C27). So Tuesday's vote doesn't decide whether data centers come. It picks three of the nine at-large voices (C04, C10) who will carry the borough's questions about power, water, noise and air (C14) to a land offer the Air Force holds (C28, C30) and a moratorium that is a request on a priority list (C13, C15, C16). Position taken on the structure, never on a candidate.

## VARIETY LEDGER CHECK, and this deck's required divergence
Last four hero structures: the cut-and-paste notice (72), one strait at five scales (73), the long table of the record (74), the inspection bench (75). Three of them were indoor still lifes of paper, wood and steel. THIS DECK: NEW hero structure "THE SUNDIAL FIELD", six standing straight-quote posts on a frozen Interior plain whose shadows are a sundial for election day, each frame printed in a different hand (a printmaker's progressive proofs of one field). Outdoors, horizon-based, no table, room, bench or paper.
Atmospheres, last three: marine overcast ceiling, the long window, the inspection softbox, all diffuse or interior. THIS DECK: NEW "THE COMPUTED OCTOBER SUN", one hard low sun whose position is solved per frame, never above 20 degrees, twilight and night bookends.
Continuity, last two: gold ballots, split-tone arc, edge-tease, lit nosing; reading cursor, uncut field, one camera through one room. THIS DECK: sun clock, quote posts, day arc. No ballot, no edge-tease, no camera-through-room.

Palettes, last three: Pasagshak sedge, Senate mahogany and ivory, surface plate and layout blue. THIS DECK: NEW "Interior freeze-up under a low sun": spruce night, frost, straw-lit snow, timber, rose dawn.
Type, last two: Archivo condensed over Archivo with Instrument Serif; Fraunces over Fraunces. THIS DECK: Space Grotesk alone with JetBrains Mono, no serif anywhere.

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here
Artwork craft has been weakest in 7 of the last 10 runs. Here: one object system, so the largest object on every frame is a known, modelled thing (timber posts with lit face, lee and rim, on snow with drifts); every frame's primary mark-making is different (no family repeats); the lower third is always the modelled plain; reserves are punched as blurred rectangles from measured line boxes before any field is drawn (art touching glyphs and canvas-near-text were the two shipping warns); the masterful depth frame (01) is built first.
