Alaska has about 0.2 percent of the country's people and about 7.3 percent of its funded E-Rate commitments.

That is $195.2 million in USAC funded commitments for funding year 2025. Only California and Texas drew more. Lower Kuskokwim School District alone holds $91.0 million of it, 46.6 percent of Alaska's total.

The FCC asks whether E-Rate should be limited or sunset. It also asks what connectivity schools need to use AI-enabled tools. The full notice never uses the word Alaska. Comments are due October 13th.
