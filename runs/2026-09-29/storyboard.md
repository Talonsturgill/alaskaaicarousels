# STORYBOARD, run 2026-09-29, Carousel No.72

## THESIS
The FCC has asked, for comment until October 13th, whether E-Rate should be limited or sunset, and it asks in national terms. Alaska's numbers are too lopsided for any national answer to describe (third in the country, 46.6 percent of the state total in one district), so Alaska gets into the record only if someone pastes it in. The deck takes a position on the QUESTIONS (they need Alaska's figure in the file) and never on cutting or keeping the program. Title for the PDF (60 characters or fewer): `Limited or Sunset? What Alaska Adds to the E-Rate Record`.

## ARC, nine slides
Grounds alternate as call and response. The notice speaks on PAPER frames (01, 03, 06, 08). Alaska's record speaks on INK frames (02, 04, 07, 09). Frame 05 is the breather, split by a torn seam.

1. COVER, the quoted question. The FCC's own two words are cut through four sheets, and Alaska's share is pasted underneath. Plants: 7.3 percent of what, from how many people?
2. THE NUMBER. $195.2 million, third in the nation, drawn as two reams of 100 sheets (dollars and residents). Plants: nearly half of it lands in one district.
3. THE SHARE QUESTION. The notice's "disproportionate share" question, and one district's 46.6 percent as 100 square apertures, 46 and a bit pasted gold. Plants: who is next?
4. THE ROLL. Ten districts as rows of sheet edges, one sheet a million dollars, top three gold, a paper-cut Alaska with pins. Plants: two camps already argue this on the record.
5. TWO VOICES (breather). Both sides quoted, neither judged. Plants: the notice turns to AI.
6. THE AI QUESTION. "What level of connectivity" cut through the paper, the rest set as letterpress. Plants: what does a line cost here, and what changed?
7. THE LINE. One district's largest internet line, cut-paper numerals whose type area equals dollars. Plants: what would you paste in?
8. THE WORKSHEET (keepable). Six FCC questions against six Alaska numbers, the last slip empty. Plants: the deadline.
9. CLOSE. Ten years of funded commitments as a stack, an eleventh gold sheet on top, one ask.
