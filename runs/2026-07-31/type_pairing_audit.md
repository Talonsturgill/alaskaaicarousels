## RULING

**Archivo + Manrope + JetBrains Mono is LEGAL but NOT NOVEL.** It clears the
binding rule, which bars only the last two decks' pairings, and it is twelve
runs since it last shipped at No. 11 (2026-07-19). But it has shipped twice,
at No. 8 and No. 11, and the storyboard may not describe it as new.

**Trios that have never shipped, and are also legal and on-temperature:**
- Bricolage Grotesque display + Manrope body + JBMono
- Space Grotesk display + Manrope body + JBMono
- Archivo display + Bricolage body + JBMono

Notes bearing on the choice. Bricolage was No. 17's BODY face, and No. 17 is
this story's previous deck, so reusing it in any role carries re-skin risk even
though the role differs. Space Grotesk was No. 20's body face one run ago, so
promoting it to display immediately is close work.

**DECISION FOR THE STORYBOARD: Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Manrope (body)
+ JetBrains Mono (instrument).** Never shipped as a trio in 20 decks. Bricolage
has last carried DISPLAY at No. 12 (2026-07-20), eight runs back, and its
appearance at No. 17 was as body at 30 to 32px, a different role at a different
size, which the storyboard states rather than hides. Manrope last carried body
at No. 16, five runs back. All three are cool or neutral, no serif anywhere,
which honours TYPE_TEMPERATURE 2 and diverges hard from No. 17's warm Fraunces
on bone.

Bricolage also earns its place on the material argument rather than the variety
argument. It has a width axis and genuine character in its terminals, which
suits a deck whose display type has to sit on an engraved document without
looking like a corporate sans. Archivo's width axis would have done similar
work, but Archivo has carried display or sole-face duty in five decks already
(2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 18) and is the house's most-used sans by a wide margin.

## WHAT THE STORYBOARD MUST WRITE DOWN

The novelty claim, stated exactly and no further: "Bricolage Grotesque display
plus Manrope body plus JetBrains Mono has not shipped as a trio in 20 decks.
Bricolage last carried display at No. 12 and appeared as body at No. 17;
Manrope last carried body at No. 16. Checked against the full ledger, not the
last four."

## SECOND PASS, after Director B proposed Instrument Serif + Manrope + JBMono

**That trio has ALSO already shipped, at No. 16 (2026-07-24), five runs back.**
Director B, to its credit, also refused to claim novelty and asked for this
check. Two of three directors proposed a trio that had already shipped, which
is worth noting as evidence that the last-two rule is too weak to produce
felt variety on its own.

Director B's material argument for a didone is correct and I am adopting it.
Instrument Serif is a didone, and the didone is literally the voice of engraved
certificates, bonds and banknotes, which is this story's material world. It is
also cool rather than warm, so it honours TYPE_TEMPERATURE 2. Any reading that
treats "serif" as automatically warm would cost the deck something real.

## FINAL RULING, SUPERSEDING THE FIRST PASS

**Instrument Serif (display) + Archivo (body, using the wdth axis for condensed
statute and ballot labels) + JetBrains Mono (instrument).**

Checked against all twenty prior decks, not the last four. Instrument Serif has
carried display at No. 9 and No. 16 and appeared as an italic accent at No. 3,
No. 6 and No. 12. Archivo has carried display or sole-face duty at No. 2, No. 5,
No. 14 and No. 18 and appeared as a support sans at No. 15. The two have never
been paired. Manrope is dropped precisely because pairing it with Instrument
Serif would reproduce No. 16.

Three reasons this is the right answer and not just the legal one.
1. The didone is the material's own voice, per Director B's argument.
2. Archivo's width axis does real work here. Condensed carries the dense statute
   and ballot label rows a document deck needs; normal width carries body. That
   is one family doing two jobs, which keeps the deck inside the two-families
   plus mono restraint budget.
3. Fraunces is absent from the deck entirely, which is the single biggest
   typographic distance available from No. 17, the deck that told this story
   six days ago in Fraunces plus Bricolage on bone.

## THE NOVELTY SENTENCE THE STORYBOARD MAY WRITE, AND NO MORE

shipped as a trio in twenty decks. Instrument Serif last carried display at
No. 16 and Archivo last carried a face at No. 18. Checked against the full
