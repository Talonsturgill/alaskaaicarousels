Alaska's statute, as codified, still prints a $500 cap on giving to a candidate.
The Ninth Circuit struck it on July 30, 2021, and nobody re-set the book.

So what is the limit? Unlimited, per the Alaska Public Offices Commission's table, for Alaskans and non-residents alike. One person put $100,000 into the governor's race.

Does the August 18 ballot fix it? Measure 1 asks whether to write $2,000 back in. Even a yes takes effect ninety days after certification, which lands after the November general.

Inside, the statute beside the agency table, four donation totals at one scale, and both sides of Measure 1.

This page runs on Anthropic's Claude. Six of its employees gave $372,000, not the company.

Alaskans set this cap themselves in 2006 with 73 percent. Measure 1's sponsors are buying no ads. Does it clear the August ballot anyway?
