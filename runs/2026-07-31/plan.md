**THE ONE WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS: artwork craft and genuine detail** (weakest
in 8 of the last 10, mean 6.67, still 6.0 last run).

Read as a prediction, not a report. Three runs of 6.90 blamed craft concentrated
in ONE modelled object; No. 20 inverted that to nine distributed fields and
still scored 6.0. Its own retro names why, and this run takes that retro at its
word rather than inventing a fourth theory:

> "Trading one modelled object for nine fields does not by itself produce craft;
> it produces nine chances to spend a detail budget that was never spent. What
> shipped is one background reused nine times with per-slide edits ...
> 'distribute the detail' is a plan, not a technique, and a plan with no
> per-region budget gets spent on whatever is easiest to draw everywhere."

**So the mechanism this run changes is the BUDGET, not the subject count.**
Three rules, all enforced in the Phase 5 dossiers where they are cheap, not in a
Phase 9 repair pass where they are not:

1. **ONE surface, built properly, reused.** The deck picks a single material
   surface (chosen in the directors room from the story's material world) and
   builds it once with real line-weight variance, two-part contact shadows and
   specular structure. Every slide draws from that one surface. No slide invents
   a second texture system.
2. **A per-region detail budget in every dossier, written as a number.** Each
   dossier names the ONE region that gets the detail spend (as `[x,y,w,h]`) and
   names at least one region that deliberately does NOT, so "genuine detail in
   every region" cannot be satisfied by a uniform hatch everywhere. A budget
   with no line that reads "not here" is not a budget.
3. **Three value bands before any detail** (FIELD_NOTES 2026-07-30 craft note):
   background light values / low contrast, midground medium, foreground the full
   range plus the sharpest edges. Stated per slide as three L targets. No. 20
   proved this half works and it is kept.

**Second, the RECURRING HARD FAIL: text against geometry** (2026-07-25 and
2026-07-29). Instinct at 0.98 confidence explains it: qa.py's text-collision
check is DOM-only, so any label positioned against Canvas or SVG geometry can
collide freely and the gate still returns PASS with zero warns. House rule for
this run, written into every dossier's acceptance checklist: **every label
sitting over art ships on ground the art itself carved for it** (a value trough,
a modelled surface the composition routes clear), and the dossier states the
worst-case contrast pair for that label. A plate is the fallback, not the plan,
and a plate on bare ground is the dead-lower-zone defect wearing furniture.
