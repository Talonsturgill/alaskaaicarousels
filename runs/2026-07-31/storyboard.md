# PART 0  . THE DIRECTORS ROOM, AND WHAT SURVIVED IT

Three directors pitched under three lenses. **All three independently chose the
same material**, line engraving on a printed instrument, without knowing what
the others were doing. That convergence is the strongest signal the room has
produced in twenty runs, and it settles the surface question outright.

**Winner: Director A's chassis (one sheet under a copy stand, revisited at ten
camera stations), strengthened by the best organs of B and C.**

Grafted from **B (historian of the future)**:
- `AKENGRAVE.reserve()`, the single most valuable idea in the room. Generate
  the art AFTER text layout, from MEASURED boxes, so the engraving never has
  permission to exist under a glyph. This is the structural kill for the
  recurring hard fail and it replaces every plate in the deck.
- **The rosette IS the vote.** The hero's guilloche takes its three parameters
  from the 2006 official returns. Nothing else in twenty decks has fused a
  generative system to a number this exactly.
- **The 2026 rosette can't be drawn.** Same generator, parameters PENDING,
  renders as phantom outline only. The absence of the future drawn as the
  absence of ink.
- **The cut is countable.** Four engraved rules, three burnished out, one left
  inked, because the court upheld the party-to-candidate limit. It protects the
  deck from the sloppy "the court threw out campaign finance limits" framing.

Grafted from **C (data journalist)**:
- **WHITE-LINE engraving, not black-line.** C's argument is decisive and neither
  other director saw it. A black-line engraving on paper is a LIGHT deck, and
  No. 17 spent the one-per-eight-runs light allowance six decks ago. White-line,
  where cut lines print light against a solid inked field, is the dark
  register's native form of the same craft.
- The burin field as label ground, and the eight-worst-pixels acceptance check.
- The precision discipline on derived counts.

**Cut, and each on the proposing director's own self-critique:**
- **C's panorama spine and its "one slide equals $10,000" converter.** C named
  both problems itself and was right about both. The converter turns floors and
  rounded aggregates ("nearly $9 million") into counts that sound engineered to
  three significant figures out of numbers nobody can defend to two. And the
  panorama concentrates the whole deck into two functions, so a bad light means
  ten wrong slides with no per-slide repair at Phase 9. C offered the exact cut
  and I am taking it.
- **B's double-strike misregistration as the primary device.** Beautiful, but a
  20 px offset is invisible at 432 px, and the deck already has two motifs that
  change SHAPE. Kept only as a texture detail on the cover.
- **A's guilloche-ratio lobe flip (500/2000 to 2000/4000).** Superseded by B's
  rosette, which encodes a real verified vote rather than a ratio of two caps.
- **C's epitrochoid-as-candidate-count.** C's own weakness (b) is right that the
  currency register pulls toward insinuation. One rosette, once, meaning a vote.

**C's weakness (b) is adopted as a deck-wide restraint, in its own words:** the
register is CERTIFICATE AND STATUTE, never currency. No scrollwork borders, no

## THE ONE SURFACE  . `assets/js/akengrave.js` (NEW, committed)

WHITE-LINE INTAGLIO. Cut lines print LIGHT against a solid inked field, which is
the dark register's native form of line engraving and keeps the deck out of the
barred light ground.

```
AKENGRAVE.surface(cx, {
  region:[x,y,w,h], form:formFn, tone:toneFn,
  light:{azDeg:118, elDeg:34}, seed:20260731+slideIndex,
  budget:{main:<gap px>, cross:<gap px|0>, dots:<bool>}, alpha:<0..1>
})
```

**Rule 1, the lay wraps the form.** Direction at any point is
`normalize(rot90(grad(form(x,y))))`, sampled on a 24 px grid and bilinearly
interpolated. Not a constant angle. The direction field IS the modelling, before
any value is applied. This is exactly what No. 20's uniform contour drift got
wrong.

**Rules 2 and 4, the swelled line, and thickness is a lighting decision.** Two
independent weight mechanisms, which is what produces real variance rather than
a garnish:
```
w_stroke = 0.75 + 2.75 * pow(1 - clamp(dot(N,L),0,1), 1.6)   // across strokes
w(t)     = w_stroke * pow(sin(PI*t), 0.7)                     // along each stroke
```
Every mainline is a FILLED TAPERED POLYGON (#59) sampled every 6 px, never a
stroked path, so the taper is real geometry. Acceptance target, measured
stroke-width standard deviation above 0.55 px inside any full-budget region.

**Rule 3, three separately controllable channels.** Mainline always. Crossline
only where `tone < 0.45`, at lay +78 degrees (off-90 so lozenges read hand-set),
at 0.6x the mainline weight. Interdot only where `tone < 0.22`, Poisson, r 0.6
to 1.1 px, blue noise. **This is the per-region budget expressed as a drawing
system.** "Everywhere" is not an argument the function accepts.

**`AKENGRAVE.reserve(boxes)`.** Measured after `document.fonts.ready`, inflated
by 14 px body or 8 px mono plus half any halo stroke, density multiplied by zero
inside with an 18 px feather so strokes TAPER out rather than clip. Technique
#75 promoted from a knockout to a generative reservation.

**Two-part contact shadow.** `AKSNOW.contactShadow()`, tight 6 px alpha 0.62 plus
wide 60 px alpha 0.18, colour `#0D1512`. The helper throws on pure black, which
is the point.

**Performance.** `cx.filter` applies PER DRAW OP. The whole engraving pass draws
into ONE offscreen canvas and is blurred and composited exactly once. This is
the exact defect that blew the 45 s budget on 2026-07-30.

**Determinism.** Seed 20260731 everywhere. Interdot hashes integer cells, never a
per-slide RNG. Grain is `AK.grainTile(280, 52, 20260731)` at 0.07 overlay, final
layer, never a full-frame feTurbulence rect.

**Colour safety.** All interpolation through `AKC.mixOklab` or integer-indexed
`AKC.ramp` steps. **No nested colour helpers anywhere**, because `lerpHex`
returns an `rgb()` string and nesting one feeds `rgb(...)` into a hex parser,
every channel returns NaN, and canvas silently keeps the previous fillStyle with
a clean gate.

**Banding.** Every fill that runs to the frame bottom is painted LIGHT TO DARK,
first stop lightest. Dark to light means the last and lightest band covers
everything behind it and the whole mass flattens, which is what shipped on
2026-07-30's first pass.

## WHAT THE BUILD CHANGED FROM THE DOSSIERS

Every number below is a dossier value the build actually moved. Generated
before any pixel-critic was spawned, per the hard ordering added 2026-07-25,
because run 2026-07-25 spawned its critics first and roughly a third of their
findings measured the renders against superseded numbers.

**1. The hero's rendered rung. SHIPPED FLAT, ARGUED, NOT DEFAULTED.**
The dossier called for akthree GPU PBR on the S05 sheet substrate with a
designed AK.reliefShade fallback, and made the smoke test mandatory because all
three directors independently flagged that hairline engraving resampled onto a
mesh by a software GPU averages to flat gray, which is No. 19's failure in a new
material. The relief path is what shipped. The reason is the one the directors
gave and it held up in the build. This deck's craft lives in about 1,300
individually light-weighted strokes per spend region, and every one of them is
authored at native resolution. Mapping that to a mesh and resampling it is the
one operation guaranteed to destroy it. The substrate and the engraving share
the same height field, so the form shading and the lay cannot disagree, which is
most of what the GPU path was going to buy. Recorded as a deliberate choice, not
a degradation.

**2. The type floor moved, deck-wide.** Notes and kickers were specified at 19
to 22 px in several dossiers. The engine's mobile floor is 24 px and qa.py
warned on 31 nodes. Every note, kicker and counter is now 24 px minimum. This
reflowed the lower half of slides 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08 and 09, and every
position below was re-derived from MEASURED boxes in render_report.json rather
than re-guessed, which is the instinct at 0.95 confidence applied to layout
instead of to plates.

**3. Slide 04's bars were rebuilt.** The dossier's scale was k = 816 px per
$1,600,000 anchored at x 560, which put the Heilala bar's terminal at x 1274,
off the frame. The bars now start at x 132 with k = 840 px per $1,600,000, so
the four lengths are 735, 276, 195 and 52 px. The caption's binding promise is
"four donation totals at one scale" and the first version did not keep it. Bars
are also 38 px tall with a lit windward edge, a dark lee edge and an internal
engraved hatch, because at the dossier's height they read as slivers on the
contact sheet.

**4. Slide 06's table moved from canvas to DOM.** The first build drew the four
row labels and their STRUCK and UPHELD values with fillText. qa.py correctly
warned that canvas text ships as a bitmap in the vector PDF and is invisible to
the LinkedIn ranker, to copy_sync and to accessibility. All eight strings are
now DOM nodes; the canvas draws only the plates and the burnish.

**5. Slide 08's sheet was moved right, from x 132 to x 500.** The left column
carried the body copy, the inference tag and the constitution note over the lit
