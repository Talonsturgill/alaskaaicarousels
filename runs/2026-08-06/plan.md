### The ONE standing weakness this run attacks, and how

**ARTWORK CRAFT AND GENUINE DETAIL.** Weakest criterion in 7 of the last 10
scored runs, mean 5.78, last 5.0, and the trailing score line has fallen from
8.37 to 7.27 across the last four runs. This is not a fact about the past; it
is a prediction about today's deck unless the plan does something different,
and the place to do it is here and in the Phase 5 dossiers.

The diagnosis is already on the record and it is not vague. Run No.26 made 126
axis-aligned rectangle calls against 73 drawn marks, a 37 percent drawn share
against the bespoke reference deck's 82, while the technique library's entire
bench of flow fields, contours, stipple, hachure, relief, raymarch and PBR sat
unused; the maintainer's own words were "blocky, almost like a kid was drag and
dropping shapes into the slides". The same run reached NO rung on the rendered
ladder despite specifying one in its storyboard. Five of nine frames read as
the same picture.

**THE ATTACK, in three clauses, each checkable:**

1. **THE RECTANGLE IS DEMOTED TO FURNITURE.** No slide's principal mass may be
   an axis-aligned fill. Every drawn mass in this deck is an accumulation of
   marks. Checked by `scripts/bespoke_check.py`'s drawn-share measure, which
   this run targets at 70 percent or better rather than at the 45 percent
   floor, and by a per-slide count written into each dossier.

2. **THE DECK'S MARK IS THE SWELLED STROKE, DRAWN BY A BENCH THE STUDIO
   ALREADY OWNS AND FORGOT.** Phase 1 went looking for stroke-width modulation
   and found `assets/js/akengrave.js`, committed 2026-07-31 for No.21, which
   already implements the engraver's three tone variables (direction, spacing,
   width) as four rules: the lay wraps the form, the swelled line, three
   separately gated channels, and thickness set per stroke by the light. It has
   NO ENTRY IN TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY.MD, which is the file the directors room
   reads, so it has been invisible to planning since the day after it shipped
   and has been used in exactly one deck. Runs 22 through 26 never touched it
   and artwork craft was the weakest criterion in four of them. This run's art
   is built on it. Declared per object in dossier field 7 as a form field, a
   light vector and a width ratio, so a pixel critic checks a number.

   It also answers the secondary offender structurally. `reserve()` takes
   MEASURED text boxes after `document.fonts.ready` and multiplies lay density
   by zero inside them, tapering across a feather, so a stroke that would
   collide with a label is never generated. There is no plate to size wrong.

3. **THE RENDERED LADDER GETS CLIMBED AND VERIFIED, NOT SPECIFIED.** The hero
   reaches akthree or aksdf, and Phase 7 proves it from the render report and
   the pixel, not from the storyboard. A rung claimed and not built is scored
   as zero this run.

Secondary, because it is the recurring HARD FAIL class at 3 of 10 runs: **text
against geometry**. Every on-slide label that sits against Canvas or SVG
