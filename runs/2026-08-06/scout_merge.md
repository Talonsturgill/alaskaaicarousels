## The live candidates

### A. THE PROMISE AND THE SUPPLY (Beat A story 2 + Beat F story 3) — LEAD

Alaska is marketing 4 to 5 cents per kilowatt-hour to AI data center prospects.
Inside this window, three things happened to the machinery behind that number.

1. **The gas utility started saying no.** Enstar's president said a repeat of
   last winter would bring likely rolling blackouts, and that Enstar has already
   REJECTED new large-scale customers to protect existing ratepayers
   (Alaska Public Media, 2026-07-28). That is a decision, not a warning.
2. **A Railbelt utility wrote a blackout plan.** Matanuska Electric described
   rolling 30 minute outages and diesel burn at Eklutna under a worst-case cold
   snap. Cook Inlet storage about 4 Bcf against a roughly 7 Bcf winter target, a
   3 Bcf gap (Mat-Su Sentinel, 2026-07-29).
3. **The pipeline underwriting the promise went into its third special session.**
   Alaska's third LNG special session convened July 27th on HB 381, an about 90
   percent property tax exemption over more than 30 years for the Glenfarne
   project. House Speaker Bryce Edgmon put passage chances "very low".

The price ladder is fully sourced: Enstar standard $8.78/Mcf, weighted average
cost $10.80/Mcf, a $17.50/Mcf premium offer that still bought no new supply,
imported LNG at $22/Mcf. Against that, large commercial users pay Chugach 14 to
17 cents/kWh delivered today, and the state's pitch to compute prospects is 4 to
5 cents. Hilcorp has drilled 192 wells since 2011 and 27 more in 2026 and the
deliverability is still not there.

WHY IT IS NOT A DUPLICATE. No.4 was a single RCA prudency ruling. No.10 was an
explainer on on-grid vs off-grid cost allocation. No.6 was a GVEA turbine vote.
None of them is this: a marketed PRICE, and the three separate mechanisms that
have to hold for it to exist, two of which moved against it inside ten days.
Still requires dedupe_check and a semantic read before the directors room.

VISUAL: a rate ladder (17 cents today, 4 cents promised, the gap shaded and
labeled with what it is contingent on); a 7 Bcf vessel filled to 4; a dependency
chain whose second link is drawn broken and dated July 27th.

annualized rise in US business investment in the first half of 2026. Paired
