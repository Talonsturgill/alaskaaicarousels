# STORYBOARD, No.98, 2026-10-28, THE EXPLODED SECTION

## THESIS
On October 21st AIDEA's board put two instruments on the table and wrote them very differently. The fiber tool is mostly armour: a $75 million all-in ceiling, staged transfers, eight conditions on any binding deal, fiber that must physically land in Alaska, and an expiry on December 31st, 2028. The $25 million NuCube stake is shares at $9.25 with half a warrant each, counting toward a merger's minimum cash, with no Alaska site, utility, customer or date named. Cut both open and the reader sees what is bought and what is locked; the one that names data centers is the one without the locks.

Document title (copy.json `document_title`, under 60 characters): `Two Checks From One Board, Cut Open`
