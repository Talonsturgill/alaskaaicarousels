The proposed terms for infrared polar bear den flights over the Arctic Refuge list last winter's dates.

The first window runs December 1st to 25th, 2025. The den model in the same notice sets them this winter.

FWS wrote it in R and ran it 10,000 times for AIDEA's seismic survey.
It estimates 8 dens, with a 95 percent interval of 2 to 16.
Its median takes are 4 Level B and 2 Level A against caps of 10 and 2. The 10 also covers bears away from dens.
FWS says its estimates significantly exceed decades of North Slope data and lean toward an overestimate.
Comments close December 17th.

A coastal plain map leads, then the take medians against the caps, 12 bears of 907, FWS on its own numbers, three flight calendars and the vehicles.

Should the final terms plan for the top of the model's range or for what decades of North Slope work recorded?
