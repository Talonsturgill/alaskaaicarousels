## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS, AND HOW

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest criterion in 6 of the last 10 runs,
mean 6.8, and the four defect classes underneath it are all the same shape: ink
and type competing for the same pixels (canvas mark near reserved text, art
touching glyphs, busy art under text), plus contact shadows that do not read.

The attack is two moves, both made at PLAN time and not at repair time.

1. **The hero climbs the rendered ladder, and it is the only slide that does.**
   The last four decks answered "depth" with CPU relief shading and struck
   relief, which is a value trick rather than a rendered one, and artwork craft
   scored 6 to 7 through all four. This deck commits one hero frame to a real
   GPU pass (akthree PBR or an aksdf raymarch) with an akpost film grade and
   akcolor OKLCH ramps, with its designed Canvas fallback and the snapshot
   sentinel, per DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 4. One rendered hero, argued in the
   dossier, and the rest of the deck earns its craft in line weight and texture
   rather than in more GPU.
2. **Type sits in a designed hole in the field, never on top of it.** Every
   text block's reserve is handed to the field generator as a BASIN it draws
   around, sized from the block's own box blurred and inset by the blur radius
   (No.58's finding: a linear gradient in a fillRect has two findable edges and
   four critics called it a plate; a hard outline trades one legibility warning
   for another). Dossier field 4a names what the lower band CARRIES on every
   frame, with modeled tone, before any code is written.

Third, smaller, carried from No.58's editorial retro: **distribute the Alaska
content**. Three of the last five decks were "a federal instrument, and whether
Alaska is inside it", a shape that parks the local content on two frames. Beat
briefs this run ask scouts for stories whose Alaska content is spread across
places, people and numbers rather than concentrated in a single jurisdictional
hinge, and selection will weigh that.
