Alaska's rural health program awarded $250,000 for a drone plan on August 14th. On September 4th it awarded $4,171,115 for aircraft.

This page reported that planning year as the whole machine story. Three weeks later it is not.

Tanana Chiefs Conference took the planning grant. A year of beyond visual line of sight waiver work with ACUASI at UAF before one box flies.

Ryan Air got the implementation grant. A family line flying 80 villages out of Unalakleet since 1953. Its award names "autonomous cargo drones and electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft".

Both award rows are drawn as freight on one aircraft floor, with the ten rows out of 217 that name a machine.

Roughly $65 million is unawarded before an October 30th deadline. Which of those two grants should the rest of that money look like?
