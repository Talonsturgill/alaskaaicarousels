Alaska's Rural Health Transformation Program paid Ryan Air $4,171,115 on
September 4th to fly autonomous cargo drones and electric vertical take off and

### THE INK LAW, DECLARED ON EVERY FRAME

**Gold means one thing in this deck, a locked implementation.** It appears on
exactly six surfaces deck wide and nowhere else. The cover's `UPDATE` rule. The
cam buckle on 05. The one gold tick on the data slide's IA column. The unit
legend's crate glyph on 08. The Polaris on 09. The strap buckle on 03.

**Forget-me-not `#6EA5FF` means a planning row, and it is always dashed and
never filled.** It never warms and it never touches a gold surface except on
05, where their adjacency IS the argument.

Every frame declares both on `<body data-ink>` with `state` present or absent,
so a law that holds on eight frames is not unchecked on the ninth.

## TYPE SYSTEM

- **DISPLAY, Archivo**, variable width axis 62 to 125, weight 600 to 700, ALL
  CAPS ONLY, tracking -2 percent, leading 0.98. Cover 146 px at `wdth 118`.
  Slide heads 74 px at `wdth 96`.
- **BODY, Archivo**, `wdth 88`, `wght 400`, 34 px, leading 1.38, measure 32 to
  40 characters, sentence case. **The same family narrowed is a different
  voice, not a second sans**, so the two-similar-sans type crime is avoided by
  construction rather than by taste.
- **INSTRUMENT, JetBrains Mono**, 400 and 600, `tabular-nums lining-nums`, 18
  to 26 px, tracked +9 percent for small caps labels. Every claim tag, every
  figure, the counter, the manifest tags, the watermark.

**THE SEPARATION RULE, so a stranger can follow it.** Archivo display never
appears below 48 px and never below weight 600. Archivo body never appears
above 36 px and never above weight 500. They can't occupy the same size, case
or weight band. Three type sizes maximum per frame.

Every display headline is fitted with `AK.fitText(el, {min, max, maxLines})`
inside `renderReady` after `await document.fonts.ready`, and **every line break
in display type is authored with `<br>` at a sense boundary**, because
AK.fitText honours an authored break and soft-wraps everything else, and all
nine of No.58's headlines broke against meaning for exactly that reason.

## THE BASIN SYSTEM (how type is seated, and it is never a plate)

No plates. No linear gradient in a rectangle. No hard outline. No radial punch.
Not once, anywhere in this deck.

1. After `await document.fonts.ready`, measure every text block **per LINE
   BOX** with a Range against `getClientRects()`, so a ragged block reserves
   unequal rects and the hole follows the rag. Reserving the paragraph's
   bounding rectangle is the plate this approach exists to avoid.
2. Inflate each rect by 16 px.
3. **Hand those rects to the field generator BEFORE it draws.** The rivet runs,
   the insulation stipple, the floor hachure and the dust field multiply their
   own density to zero inside the boxes with a feather, so the colliding stroke
   is never generated.
4. For any field already drawn, punch on the OFFSCREEN canvas with blurred
   RECTANGLES by `globalCompositeOperation = 'destination-out'`, blur 24, inset
   by the blur radius so the visible hole equals the measured box. Never a
   radial, whose outer radius is half the box's longest side and which strands
   the two ends of a 900 px line, which qa.py reads as a worst point contrast
   failure against a healthy box mean.
5. The same basin alpha subtracts from CAST SHADOW alpha, so a long shadow can
   never crawl under a headline.

`ctx.filter` is never set inside a stroke loop. All blurs happen once, offscreen.

## ATMOSPHERE, DECLARED ONCE FOR ALL NINE FRAMES

**LOW SEPTEMBER SUN THROUGH AN OPEN CARGO DOOR, HOLD OTHERWISE UNLIT.**

- **Key. Azimuth 76 degrees, elevation 12 degrees.** Resolved in screen space
  through `lightVec(az, el) = [cos(el)sin(az), -cos(el)cos(az), sin(el)]` with
  +y DOWN, which gives `x = +0.949` and `y = -0.237`, so **the light is to the
  UPPER RIGHT and every cast runs to the LOWER LEFT**. That sentence is written
  here, in this file, and repeated verbatim in every slide's inline comment,
  because No.51 typed one azimuth, hand shaded for the opposite direction, and
  carried two light directions through nine frames and five critics.
- **Fill.** Skylight bounced off the bare aluminium ceiling, cold, anchored at
  `#6E8AA3`.
- **Key to fill 6 to 1.** Steeper than the doctrine's usual 4 to 1 because a
  single small aperture in a metal tube genuinely is that contrasty, and the
  steepness is what keeps this a dark arctic deck while the light is daylight.
- **Elevation 12 is a craft decision, not a mood.** It sits low enough that
  `ndotl` spreads across nearly its whole range, so relief and specular stay
  legible instead of flattening to one uniform weight.
- **Shadows are `#0A121C`,** a darkened hold hue, never black, two part on
  every object, tight contact plus wide ambient, plus a hard narrow seam.
- **The contact triple, in the only order that measures.** Lit pool FIRST, then
  the cast at `foot + 2`, pool centre at `foot + 14`, pool width in the 70 to
  110 range regardless of the object's own width, then the seam. Ground and
  shadow probe rects go SIDE BY SIDE at the object's base line and never
  stacked. Every declaration is written by
  `python3 scripts/contact_probe.py --render-dir <render> --slide N --base cx,cy`
  off the rendered PNG and re-read with `--slides-dir <slides> --verify`.
  **The 4.0 L star floor is not a target. If a contact reads, stop.**

number below is what the RENDER does, not what the dossier planned. The
gate lines under it are written by `gate_status.py --sync` and are never
typed by hand.

**SLIDE 02**
- Headline rewritten. `THE STATE MARKED IT / IA FOR IMPLEMENTATION` became
  `THE AWARD SAYS / IMPLEMENTATION / NOT PLANNING`, three lines instead of
  two. Every arrangement of the planned words leaves a line ending on `IA`
  or on `for`, which is the ragged-line rule, and at 59.5 px that break is
  the first thing a feed sees.
- Body cut from 44 words to 29. The project-title sentence [C09] came out;
  the title is carried on the cover.
- The Service Area list shortened to four bare place names. At the planned
  length it ran off the manifest tag, off the 80 px margin, and its last
  line fell onto the bulkhead behind the tag.
- Both tag blocks moved. `.tag.a` 700,660 w352 to 700,638 w300; `.tag.b`
  700,762 w356 to 700,770 w300. The planned pair overlapped each other by
  one line.
- `.tagl` letter-spacing .12em to .08em, so `SERVICE AREA FIELD` holds one
  line in the narrower block.

**SLIDE 03**
- THE COUNTABLE HARDWARE MOVED TO A SECOND RAIL. The 24 rivet pairs [C30]
  and 8 tie-down rings [C30] were planned along the loaded seat track from
  t 0.40 to 0.86. Freight sits on a loaded track by definition, and the
  mail tote and the banana stack buried five of the 24 and one of the 8:
  the composited frame could be counted to 19 and to 7. A count a reader
  can't take off the picture is not an encoding.
  The deck now carries a SECOND, NEARER seat track, parallel to the loaded
  one, running (268,1497) to (1118,989) in design px and stroked from
  t 0.385 so it clears the claim-id fixture line. The isotype lives on it,
  rivet pairs over t 0.42 to 0.92, rings over t 0.455 to 0.905, both with
  a shallower recession (1 - 0.30t, was 1 - 0.48t) so the far marks stay
  probeable. Nothing is drawn over that rail after it.
- The loaded track keeps its three rails and loses its hardware.

**SLIDE 05**
- CAMERA. Orthographic as planned. `R.camera` is replaced after
  `AKT.setup`, VH 9.4, position (7.4, 3.9, 10.4), looking at (0.10, 1.55, 0).
- THE KEY LIGHT. The first build walked every directional light in the rig
  and set all three to one position at intensity 3.0, which is not a 6 to 1
  key to fill ratio, it is one light struck three times. Only the rig's
  first directional light is moved now, to (kx*13, ky*13+5.2, kz*13).
- THE SEAT TRACK MATERIAL. Planned metalness 0.90, roughness 0.34,
  envMapIntensity 1.0. A metal that reflective under a dim environment is
  BLACK: it put a dead band across the frame's lower third and gave the
  contacts nothing to fall on. Now 0x8494a2, metalness 0.55, roughness
  0.40, envMapIntensity 3.0.
- EXPOSURE AND GROUND. `AKT.setup` exposure 1.05 to 0.80 and the ground
  0x2b3a49 to 0x1b2937. At the planned values this frame composited two
  stops above the eight drawn frames and the contact sheet read as one
  slide borrowed from another deck.
- ENVIRONMENT intensity 0.42 to 0.58; crate material 0x93a2b0 to 0x7f93a8
  at envMapIntensity 0.52, so the crates keep the deck's blue.
- THE CONTACTS ARE 2D ON THE COMPOSITE. The orthographic shadow frustum
  does not put a cast where this camera can see one. The lone crate gets
  one contact at w 118, k 0.50; the stack's bottom row gets one per column
  at w 172, k 0.34, because a single contact sized to a 500 px base throws
  a pool half the frame wide. Every one is placed from `AKT.screenBox`.
- THE CAM BUCKLE was hardcoded at 178,1006 and the render put the buckle
  at 82,786, so the frame carried a gold lozenge floating on bare floor
  200 px under the crate it straps. It is now drawn from the buckle mesh's
  own screen box.
- THE 16 MARK POINTS moved from each crate's face centre to its lit lid
  band, `b.y + 14`. A flat face carries no local contrast and the probe
  could not establish that any crate had painted.
- `.lg` top 300 to 352 (it sat on the headline's second line);
  `.lbl.l` width 340 to 430 (its longest line wrapped to a third row).
- `data-contacts`, `data-encodes` and `__akMotifs` rects were all planned
  by eye and are now MEASURED: contact shadow [141,843,26,10] against
  ground [33,843,26,10], dL 30.6 by `contact_probe.py`; the lone crate at
  [55,722,172,124] and the stack at [455,470,512,470].

**ROUND 2, AFTER THE PIXEL CRITICS**

Five critics reviewed the nine frames in parallel and five of them named the
same object. `AKHOLD.contact` laid a SCREENED radial blob of rgba(196,216,232)
down-light of every foot in the deck, and it was the brightest region on nine
frames out of nine with no source anywhere in any of them. The three words
that came back independently were "a glowing manhole". One critic put the
physics plainly: ground DOWN-light of an object is the ground in shadow, and
that is where the routine was putting its brightest paint. None of the nine
frames had the LONG shallow cast an object throws at elevation 12 either.

`assets/js/akhold.js` now draws a tapering quad inside a squashed transform,
in four nested layers so its long edges are soft without a blur pass, plus the
tight seam at the foot. The lit ground became the SLIDE's to ask for, as
`o.lit`, a hard-edged sliver at low alpha in source-over on the KEY side of
the foot. Frames 03, 04, 05, 06 and 09 ask for it because their floors measure
L* 3.6 to 8.9; 01, 02, 07 and 08 do not, because their own wedges already
light the ground. Every contact declaration in the deck was re-measured with
`contact_probe.py` afterwards and none of them is a guess.

The rest, per frame.

**WHAT THE CRITICS ASKED FOR AND DID NOT GET, and why**
Slide 04's Alaska coastline through the aperture, drawn true through AKGeo as
a stroked polyline, is the one substantial finding left on the table. The
frame carries a graded world and a ridge silhouette instead. It is a real gap
against the dossier and it is recorded here rather than quietly dropped.
Several critics also asked for the declared `__akLeaders` polylines to be
restored across the deck. They were removed in Phase 7 because they were
declared and never drawn, which is the worse of the two failures; slide 08's
is the one that came back, drawn.

**ROUND 3, AFTER THE FLOW CRITIC AND THE FIRST SCORE (8.11 of 8.3)**

THE FLOW CRITIC FOUND A STALE RENDER AND IT MATTERED. Round 2 changed a
SHARED library, `assets/js/akhold.js`, and then re-rendered only the slides
whose own HTML had changed. Slides 01, 02 and 07 were never re-rendered, so
three of the nine frames in `final/` still carried the additive pool the round
had been convened to remove. `render.py` warns when a slide's HTML is newer
than its PNG and has no way to know a shared asset moved underneath it. The
whole deck is re-rendered now and every frame is built against the current
library. Logged to incidents.md for Phase 12.

THE LIT SLIVER WENT TOO. Round 2 kept a lit ground as `o.lit`, a hard-edged
low-alpha ellipse up-light of the foot, to keep qa.py's dL floor satisfied
where a floor was near black. The scorer named it as the same defect in a
second costume, an unexplained highlight beside an object, brightest pixel in
the frame on 04. It is removed from all six frames that used it. Every contact
still clears the floor, because the round 2 floors and the real casts were
already doing the work and the sliver was propping up nothing.

**SLIDE 04, THE LITERAL ANCHOR.** The dossier asks for the Alaska coastline
projected true through AKGeo and stroked, and the frame had shipped a
procedural fbm wobble instead, which is the definition of wallpaper on the one
frame whose job is looking out of the door. It now draws
`assets/geo/alaska-state.geo.json` through the house conic at parallels 55 and
65, zoomed 5.4x onto Norton Sound, the water Ryan Air's own 80 villages sit
around, land filled and shore stroked struck-then-lit, and smeared with the
same 14 px flight-vector convolution as everything else inside that aperture.
This was the one substantial finding round 2 left on the table and the
scorer's one-sentence fix named it.

**SLIDE 06.** The unit crate is drawn, distant and aft at the deck's own
footprint, unstrapped, with the coil beside it. Its absence was the deck's
only undeclared motif drop and it fell on the frame immediately after the hero
taught the reader that one crate is $250,000. The aperture also stopped being

**SLIDE 05.** Every crate carries a real lid lip in GEOMETRY, a thin proud box
in a darker material, so the horizontal join is a drawn line on every face and
not only on the ones turned away from the key. The right column had been
reading as one pale pillar and the stack as twelve crates and a tower. The cam
buckle came down from 0.17 to 0.115 world units, because at the old size it
read as a plate rather than as the frame's one saturated mark.

**SLIDE 03.** The freight silhouettes changed, not their surfaces. Round 2's
printed bands and cut hand vents do not survive the 0.4 downsample, so at feed
scale four boxes still shared the unit crate's outline, which is the glyph
slide 05 spends on a QUANTITY. A produce box is now wider than it is tall and

**THE CAPTION NAMES WHAT IT UPDATES.** The cover kicker said UPDATE and
nothing in nine frames or in 886 characters ever said of what, which the
scorer called the deck's strongest structural asset spent as a single word,
and which is also the thing that makes a 15 day repeat of No.45's award file
legitimate rather than a dedupe near miss. The caption carries it in its
second paragraph now. The Alaska DOT line about 402 communities came out to
make room; the deck still carries it on slide 04 with its own claim tag.

**THREE ARTIFACTS THAT DESCRIBED PIXELS THAT DID NOT SHIP** are corrected:
copy.json's editor notes, aggregates.json's slide 08 entries, and this
section's own round 2 line, all of which said the slide 08 footer reads BY OUR
COUNT. It reads NOT A STATE CATEGORY, and the reason is in incidents.md item 4.

**WHAT DID NOT CHANGE**
