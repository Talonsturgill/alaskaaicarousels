# STORYBOARD — Alaska.Ai Carousel No. 33 — 2026-08-14

## DECK HEADER

**THESIS.** NSF's remedy for uneven access to AI compute is distributed at a
flat rate, and the problem it names is not flat. The rule counts states, and a
state is a shape with an area, so Alaska's ceiling on a national program is one
award, identical to the ceiling on a state 552 times smaller, and it was set
before any proposal was read.

**DOCUMENT TITLE (37 chars).** `The Equal-Award Projection, 552 to 1`

**ARC.** hook (the two facts on one sheet) → payoff (the rule's own picture,
fifty states) → the rule verbatim in its own ink → the money, both figures →
BREATHER, NSF's own words for the problem → the keepable ranked plate → the
fair reading, NSF's case made properly → the date and the 82 days → close.

Emotional temperature shifts twice. It rises from S1 to S3 as the rule gets
specific and colder, drops deliberately on S5 where NSF speaks for itself, and
turns from argument to usefulness on S8 when the deck stops disagreeing and
hands over a date.

**SLIDE COUNT RATIONALE.** 9 slides, 1 cover, 7 content, 1 close. Inside
CAROUSEL_CRAFT's 8 to 10 band. Seven content beats, each carrying one idea, is
what sets the number. The Phase 1 craft refresh found a claim that exactly 7
slides outperforms all other lengths and a contradicting claim that 8 to 15 is
optimal, neither citing a corpus, so the count is set by the beats and not by
the blogs.

### HERO STRUCTURE (NEW): THE EQUAL-AWARD PROJECTION

The deck is one continuous chart sheet seen square on, and the variable is not
the camera and not the subject but THE PROJECTION. Every honest map prints a
note in its lower margin declaring what it preserves. This deck prints one too,
it updates every slide, and on the slides that draw NSF's rule the note has to
admit that the rule's projection preserves the state count and gives up area,
population and capacity. The scale bar is computed from the live projection
scale, so when the projection goes flat the bar disappears and a magenta
`SCALE NOT PRESERVED` stamp sits in the hole where it lived.

It is a TWO-PLATE PRINT. Plate one is the land, white-line intaglio on a dark
sheet, warm, modelled, full of coastline. Plate two is the rule, printed in
regulated magenta as an overprint, the ink convention charts use for a
restriction laid over a place that was already there. The rule does not describe
the land. It is printed on top of it.

**Divergence from all four forbidden heroes, specifically.**

- **THE LIT APERTURE (29)** was a backlit slot in tannin water, an aksdf
  raymarched mass, camera dollying in z. No aperture here, no raymarcher, no
  transmissive material, and the camera never translates in z at all. Depth
  comes from a raking engraved surface and one cabinet-extruded paper solid.
- **THE OPEN TREAD (30)** was a staged stepped instrument standing on a lit
  floor in a room, with a floor-datum plinth as its continuity. There is no
  object here and no room. The sheet bleeds off all four edges on seven of nine
  slides, so there is nothing to stand on and no datum.
- **THE FALLING FRAME (31)** made scale itself the variable and relabelled nine
  rungs against a fixed subject. This is the inverse and is designed as one.
  THE RULER IS WELDED DOWN, one conic equal-area projection, one printed
  statute-mile scale bar, one 48 px ring never resized on any slide, and the
  GEOMETRY is what deforms. No rungs, no ladder, no return trip.
- **THE LOCKED CHASE (32)** was a letterpress composing stone at a fixed 30
  degrees with furniture grammar and a wet-ink specular. This sheet is at 0
  degrees, square on, with no furniture, no quoins and no wet specular anywhere.
  A chase LOCKS rectangles into place; a neatline is the thing a coastline
  refuses to obey, and on S2 Alaska breaks out of its cell and crosses two
  others.

Structurally the break is a category change rather than a detail change. All
four forbidden heroes are ONE OBJECT under a controlled camera in an interior.
This is a POPULATED FIELD, fifty things at true relative area, under a parallel
projection that has no viewpoint to move.

### ATMOSPHERE (NEW): NEAR-EDGE PLATE LAMP

A single warm lamp clamped at the NEAR edge of a chart table, not the far
corner, which is why light arrives from below and to the right and why the
brightest modelled tone in every frame lives in the bottom third by
construction.

- **Key azimuth 118 degrees, elevation 26 degrees.** Elevation sits inside
  akengrave's legible band of 20 to 30, where `ndotl` spreads across nearly its
  full range and the swelled line actually swells. Above 30 every stroke
  flattens to one width and the uniform hatch this run is trying to kill comes
  straight back.
- **Key to fill 2.8 to 1**, and the fill is a broad bounce off the sheet
  itself, so every shadow carries the sheet's hue and none is grey.
- **Scatter, not fog.** A 3 percent paper-fibre scatter lifts the deepest
  engraved value off true black. No volumetric shafts. This is a lamp on a
  table, not a cathedral.
- Divergence: the three forbidden atmospheres sit in a 292 to 305 azimuth band.
  118 is roughly 174 degrees away from all of them, so the deck is lit from the
  opposite side of the sky from the last three decks. No. 32 was a hard 6 to 1
  with no fog, No. 30 a 2.4 to 1 north window at azimuth 300, No. 31 cold slate
  at elevation 21.

**ONE GLOBAL LIGHT.** `akrelief` lights, `akhachure` sun angle and every cast
shadow in the deck read azimuth 118 and elevation 26 from a single declared
constant. Shadows are two-part, tight contact plus wide ambient, coloured as
darkened sheet hue and never black.

### TYPE PAIRING (NEW)

Retiring **Instrument Serif**, **Archivo** and **Unbounded** entirely, which
clears both forbidden pairings with room to spare.

- **DISPLAY, Bricolage Grotesque.** `wght 800, wdth 92, opsz 96`, tracking
  minus 2 percent, leading 0.98, hand-broken to 3 lines maximum, always through
  `AK.fitText`. Visible ink traps and irregular joins at display size carry the
  type_temperature 5 dial on their own. Proven in prototype P5 at 1080 px.
- **THE RECORD, Fraunces.** `opsz 144, wght 600, SOFT 60, WONK 1`. WONK is on,
  which is the expressive move and is what separates this from No. 31's Fraunces
  usage, which ran WONK off beside Unbounded. **Used ONLY to set words NSF
  actually wrote.** The typeface itself is the quotation mark.
- **INSTRUMENT, JetBrains Mono.** `wght 400`, tracked plus 10 percent,
  `tabular-nums lining-nums`. Projection notes, scale values, claim-ids,
  coordinates, the counter, the site line.

**THE TYPE SYSTEM CARRIES MEANING.** The ink a word is set in tells you which
plate it belongs to. NSF's own words are Fraunces. The deck's own voice is
Bricolage. The record is Mono. Anything belonging to the RULE is Mono set in
magenta, on the overprint plate, never in the land's inks. No slide mixes
Fraunces and Bricolage inside one text block.
