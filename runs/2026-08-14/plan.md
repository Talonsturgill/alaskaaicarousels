### THE ONE WEAKNESS THIS RUN IS DELIBERATELY ATTACKING

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 7 of the last 10 runs, mean 6.0, and 6.0 again last run. Last worked on four runs ago, so it is stale by the report's own reckoning. It is not a fact about the past; it is a prediction about today's deck unless this run does something different here in the plan and in the Phase 5 dossiers.

Read the last two runs and the failure has a shape. No. 32 shipped a beautiful CONCEPT (a locked letterpress chase) and still scored 6.0 on craft, because the craft budget went into ONE technique, akengrave, run across all nine slides. No. 31 did the same with a scale rail. The deck reads as one good idea nine times rather than nine drawings. bespoke_check measures the code and passes that; the scorer measures the eye and does not.

So the attack is not "try harder on the art". It is three structural commitments made now, priced into the dossiers, and gated before any critic sees a pixel:

1. **A TECHNIQUE BENCH, NOT A TECHNIQUE.** The storyboard header must name at least FIVE distinct TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY techniques and assign each to specific slides, with no single technique carrying more than four of the nine. The library has 80+ entries and the last two decks used effectively one apiece. Flow fields, contours, stipple, hachure, relief shading and raymarching are all sitting on the bench unused while craft scores 6.0.
2. **DRAWN SHARE WELL ABOVE THE FLOOR.** bespoke_check fails a drawn share under 45 percent. The bespoke reference deck measures 82. This run targets 65 percent minimum and treats anything under that as a rebuild, not a warning. A gradient inside a fillRect is still a box.
3. **THE BOTTOM BAND IS DESIGNED FIRST.** dossier_check field 4a already forces each dossier to name what the lower third CARRIES. Two of the five recurring defect classes (top-loaded composition, outside safe zone) are the same defect seen twice, and the scorer has named the dead lower zone in six consecutive runs. At visual_density 5 this is the region that will fail. Every dossier writes its bottom band as modelled tone with its own subject matter, before its hero is written.

Secondary target, taken for free: **legibility and platform fitness** (weakest 3 of 10, worked on never). The cheapest available win is the instinct already at 0.99, that qa.py's collision check is DOM-only. Every label set against canvas or SVG geometry ships on an opaque knockout plate by default this run, which makes non-collision structural rather than a registration-tuning exercise.
