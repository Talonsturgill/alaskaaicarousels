On the Wood River a person on a tower clicks each passing salmon by hand, and on the Naknek near King Salmon a sonar will listen.

The tower count goes back to 1955. Crews at nine Fish and Game sites click in 10 minute intervals around the clock.

The sonar is new. BBRSDA announced a $188,268 NFWF award on October 29th, National Fisherman reports. It is meant to count returning salmon automatically and feed an online dashboard.

The Wood tower's 2,586,432 sockeye this year sat inside the river's goal.
