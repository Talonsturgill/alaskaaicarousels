# STORYBOARD, run 2026-10-01, Carousel No.74

## THESIS
The count makes the headline and the date makes the story. All three of Alaska's votes in Congress were yea on H.R. 9340 and the Senate still fell three short of cloture. For Alaska, then, the bill is two clocks that would start on a day that hasn't happened, plus an exemption that only counts what a state did before that day. The deck predicts nothing about the lame duck, grades nothing, and never says whether Alaska is or isn't exempt.

UPDATE of No.58 (September 13th), declared on the cover kicker. The material new development is on the cover: House passage 417 to 3, Senate cloture 57 to 43, motion withdrawn, Senate gone until November 9th.
