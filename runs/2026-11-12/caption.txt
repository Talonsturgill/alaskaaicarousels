"Seems high" is how AIDEA's January minutes record board member Andrew Guy's view of $20 million for Alaska Data Corporation.

He asked to see a budget. Resolution G26-03 had arrived with the amount blank. A motion wrote in $20 million and it passed unanimously, one commissioner absent.

On November 9th Gov. Dunleavy pitched the company for distributed data centers and paired it with InvestNorth, a platform for tokenized stakes. The resolution is titled an inquiry. Its text never says data center. The January discussion did. No offering was available at launch.

Inside are the blank and the vote, $20 million against AIDEA's $1.7 billion balance sheet and $10 million yearly budget Brefczynski cited, and what the launch left out.

Would you buy an InvestNorth stake before reading the feasibility finding the resolution puts first?
