# "THE INSTRUMENT NICHE": Alaska Data Corporation, InvestNorth, and the resolution behind them

## THESIS
On November 9th the state launched a company and a platform to sell Alaskans stakes in "distributed data centers", on $20 million the AIDEA board passed in January under a resolution titled an inquiry, from a staff memo that left the amount blank, and nothing is offered yet. The honest name for Alaska Data Corporation today is a $20 million question, and the resolution's own Section 7 says the answer (feasible, in the public interest, approved by the CFO) comes first. Position: Alaskans should see that finding before anyone offers them a stake.
PDF document title (<= 60 chars): `Pitched as data centers. Passed as an inquiry.`
