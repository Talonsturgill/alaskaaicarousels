Five agencies police the Matanuska-Susitna Borough. The borough is not one of them.

On September 15th its assembly adopted Ordinance Serial No. 26-106 unanimously, the same night as the public hearing, in force on adoption. The chapter bars borough departments, employees, contractors and money from acquiring or operating automated plate readers. One section bars the borough from even receiving data from an operator. Eight borough officials had signed the routing block in August.

The ordinance and its information memorandum carry six of the nine frames. The borough's own law enforcement page carries two. The Anchorage Daily News carries one.

That reach is real and it stops at the borough's own desk. So which sentence of MSB 23.30 still binds anything if the troopers mount a plate reader on a Mat-Su road tomorrow?
