# SCOUT MERGE — 2026-08-12 — six beats, window 2026-08-02 to 2026-08-12

Six `scout` subagents ran in parallel. All six returned. Total 25 findings.
Selected story and full reasoning in selection.md.

## CONVERGENCE (the signal that decided the run)

Beats B and C, briefed differently and run independently, both returned the
UAF AURORA-AI release of August 11th as their strongest in-window item, with
the same $725,000 figure and the same primary URL. Beat E, on a different beat
entirely, returned the Cordova data-center-inside-a-powerhouse story from the
National Laboratory of the Rockies. Neither B nor C mentioned that Cordova
already hosts compute; E did not know Cordova was AURORA-AI's utility partner.
Putting the three together is what produced the deck. Note the standing
caution that convergence is not verification, since B and C may simply have
read the same page; the fact-checker was told so explicitly.
