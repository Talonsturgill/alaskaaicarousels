## HARD LINTS THE CAPTION MUST SURVIVE

- 300 to 900 characters. Note 08-03 shipped at 898 of 900 and the scorer called
  it "the third time in nine runs the caption shipped within six characters of
  the ceiling." Aim for 600 to 780 and leave room.
- Hook completes inside 140 characters.
- Exactly 3 niche hashtags as the final line.
- Under 6.2 commas per 100 words of body. Split the sentence, never just delete
  the comma.
- Write "can't", never "cannot".
- Dates take the ordinal, month first. "August 11th". With a year the plain
  form "August 11, 2026" is correct.
- No colons in prose (clock times excepted). No em or en dashes. No semicolons.
  Straight quotes. No emojis. No first person. No sentence opening on "And" or
  "But".
- No links, no sources list, no credits. Those live only in the first comment.
- Ranges as "X to Y".
