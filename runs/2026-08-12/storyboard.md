# DECK HEADER

## THESIS

Cordova can't ship its surplus power anywhere, because no wire leaves town, so
it moved the customer to within twenty feet of the turbine instead. The federal
money that became public on August 11th is not for that data center. It is for
the software that would run the grid underneath it.

**PDF document title (48 chars).** `No Wire Leaves Cordova. So the Load Moved In.`

## SLIDE COUNT RATIONALE

Nine. The band is 6 to 12 and the default is 8 to 10. Nine is what the descent
needs. Five slides fall (01 to 05), one holds at the floor as data (06), and
three climb back out (07, 08, 09). Cutting to eight would cost either the
breather at 04, which is the only rest in a dense deck at visual_density 5, or
the widening at 07, without which the turn at 08 is a claim about one town.
Ten would add a slide the record can't fill, because the room itself is almost
entirely unsourced and the deck deliberately visits it once.

## LIGHT PLAN, ONE GLOBAL SOURCE

- **Key azimuth 305 degrees, elevation 21 degrees.** The elevation is a craft
  decision and not a mood. akengrave sets stroke width from `pow(1-ndotl,1.6)`
  and akhachure keys stroke width off slope against `sunEl`, so a low rake
  spreads the light term across nearly its full range and the swelled stroke
  becomes legible instead of merely present. Above about 40 degrees every
  stroke flattens to one width and the field reads as uniform hatch.
- **Key to fill 6 to 1** on map and section slides, softening to 3.2 to 1 in
  the exterior half of 04 (a large overcast source, which is the honest August
  light in Prince William Sound).
- **One practical** on slide 05 only, a service luminaire at roughly 2700K
  giving 4.5 to 1 locally with fast falloff. This is what makes the lit pool
  the contact shadows bite into. **Declared reconstruction**, inferred from C26,
  which places two people installing equipment on that floor.
- **Fog `#7C8C90`**, alpha 0.06 near to 0.14 far, radial on plan slides and
  exp2 on the oblique. Never grey, never blue. Glacial silt haze is physically
  correct for this water. **Zero fog inside the building** on 05, because air
  does not scatter over twenty feet, and zero fog on 06, 07 and 08.
- **Shadow `#0A1413`**, a cooled darkening of the ground hue, never black.
  Two-part everywhere, tight contact at 0.42 alpha and 6 px, wide ambient at
  0.16 alpha and 60 px.
- Shadows share one direction across all nine slides, down and to the right.

## DECK-WIDE LEGIBILITY GATES, checkable before render

This deck's declared attack is legibility and platform fitness, the only
criterion in the ten-run trend report never worked on, weakest 4 of 10 runs,
most recent value 4.0.

1. All DOM text inside x [80, 1000], y [80, 1270]. Display blocks pulled
   optically left to x = 76 so glyph stems land at 80.
2. **At least 30 percent of non-background ink below y = 900 on every slide**,
   and at least one modeled solid with a two-part contact shadow in that band.
3. Smallest type 26 px anywhere. Anything below 34 px is non-essential
   furniture whose removal does not change the takeaway. Every takeaway is at
   62 px or above, which is 22 px at 432 px thumb width.
4. No text block on a ground measuring L\* 35 to 55.
5. Every leader is a world-coordinate polyline terminating on its target's own
   coordinates, declared in `window.__akLeaders`.
6. Every plate sized from the MEASURED string via `getBBox` or `measureText`
   after `document.fonts.ready`, or built with `AK.svgPlate`. Never a typed
   constant. There is no mono in the working type, so no per-character shortcut
   exists and none is used.

**13. Risk flags.**
- The display block sits over the hachure field. **Mitigation:** the field is
