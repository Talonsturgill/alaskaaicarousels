## THE DIRECTORS ROOM, and what was taken from where

Three lenses ran in parallel. **The cinematographer's THE RETENTION SHEET wins
the hero and the camera**, grafted with the essayist's palette law and the data
journalist's equivalence.

**What the cinematographer won it on, and it is one observation.** Looking at
the showrunner's prototype it said the ink was the right substance rendered
with the wrong physics. Every deposit was BRIGHTER than the sheet with a soft
outward falloff, so the eye read emission. Those were not blooms, they were
lamps, and a field of lamps has no scale argument in it because light has
brightness rather than size. That is exactly right and it was verified by
building it. The spike at `proto/render/slide-01.png` inverts the value, ink
colder and darker than the rag, visible by a mineral drying ring catching a
grazing fill rather than by glowing, and the frame stops being decoration and
starts being a record.

Its second contribution is the camera. **Nine plates of one sheet at declared
magnifications**, 1.0, 1.6, 12, 1.0, 3.0, 1.0, 1.2, 3.6, 0.85. Distance to a
flat surface is a camera move that needs no stage, it breaks nine frames of one
medium out of monotony, and it is the honest way to tell a 2,000 to 1 ratio,
because a magnification change BETWEEN frames, printed on both, is a microscope
while a log axis inside a frame would be a lie.

**From the editorial essayist, the palette law, and it is the editorial key.**
GOLD MEANS THE RULE, NEVER THE MONEY AND NEVER THE DONORS. The brightest thing
on every page is the thing the voters passed. The six get no gold at any point
in nine slides. The deck cannot be read as villainising six people because the
palette will not let it. Also taken, its counter-case slide built at full
strength rather than as a caveat, and its reserve contract, that a type reserve
may move a mark sideways and may never delete one.

**From the data journalist, the equivalence and the printed constant.** The six
gifts and 93 maximum contributions are the same quantity of ink, which is C24

ruler can reconstruct any number in the deck.

**What was NOT built, and why.** The cinematographer's height-field ink, where
deposits displace the substrate before shading, is the better idea and it is
four novel systems in one run. Its own author named the risk and named the
fallback, painted deposits with the coaxial gloss and the value inverted, and
said the fallback is an improvement on the prototype regardless. The spike
confirmed that. Taken. The data journalist's Lorenz curve is cut for a reason
found in the data rather than in taste, below.

## THE HERO STRUCTURE

### `NEW: THE RETENTION SHEET`

**One dark absorbent sheet, photographed nine times at nine declared
magnifications, with the campaign's entire 2026 disclosure record soaked into
it.**

Every one of the 4,588 positive contributions in the record (C42) is one deposit, and the 16 refund and adjustment rows are not drawn, which is why the plate prints the drawn count rather than the transaction count. Nothing stands on anything
anywhere in nine slides. There is no ground plane, no horizon, no object and no
cast shadow in the deck, so the contact-shadow failure class that has warned in
five of the last ten runs does not exist here rather than being passed.

**THE INK IS DARKER AND COLDER THAN THE PAPER.** This is the rule the whole
look rests on. A deposit is built in three parts and only the third one is
light. A soak halo drawn in `multiply` at 1.85r, so the halo is a DARKENING of
whatever it crosses and never an addition to it. A body in `#0C1A1E`, colder as
well as darker than the rag, because hue separation is what keeps two dark
values apart at low lightness. And a MINERAL DRYING RING in `#6F8C86`, or `#8AA49C` on the smallest marks, at the
boundary, the one mid value in the frame, with a lee-side shadow offset
down-left so the ring reads as a ridge under the grazing fill. Ink is visible
because it is glossier than the fibre around it, which is how dark ink on dark
paper is actually photographed.

## ATMOSPHERE

### `NEW: COAXIAL READ ON DAMP RAG`

A lighting MODEL divergence rather than a parameter divergence, which matters
because two of the last three atmospheres were raking keys.

- **Key, near axial.** Broad and soft, almost down the lens. Matte rag scatters
  it away and goes dark. A dried ink film is glossier than the fibre and
  returns it.
- **Fill, one weak grazing term**, `lights:[{az:205, el:14, w:1.0}]` on
  `AK.reliefShade`. Its only job is to throw a shadow off the lee side of every
  drying ring so the rings read as ridges. It is a fill and not a key.
- **The substrate**, laid first, always, `noiseScale 0.075, octaves 5, warp
  0.30, strength 2.6, low #130F0B, high #2A2118, ambient 0.30, diffuse 0.80,
  scale 2`.
- **No weather, no lamp, no scene, no fog, no exterior, no time of day.**
- **Divergence.** Not raking fluorescent on cut paper, the key is coaxial and

## THE RENDERED LADDER, argued because the rubric requires it argued

`aksdf` was tested this run and rejected on two measured grounds recorded in
`proto/FINDINGS.md`. It paints its `sky` colour as a hard rectangle across the
destination box, and a smooth raymarched mass reads as exactly the CGI
softness this deck exists to escape. An ink bloom's character lives in its
EDGE, and an edge is a two dimensional phenomenon, so a raymarcher would give a
rounded volume with a soft terminator, which is a rendered blob.

The rendered depth here is `AK.reliefShade`, which is real per-pixel Sobel
normals Lambert-shaded against a declared light, over a substrate that is a
genuine height field. That is not flat. Four depth cues without a single cast
shadow, occlusion by multiply overlap in ascending dollar order, atmospheric
falloff through the soak halo's transmission, scale gradient by amount, and
depth of field between the diffuse halo and the tack sharp drying ring, which
is the deck's one focal plane.
