## Standing weakness this run attacks (trend_check, window 10)

    weakest 8/10  mean 6.6  last 6.0   Artwork craft and genuine detail
    defect classes: top-loaded composition (7 runs), busy art under text (5),
    outside safe zone (5), contact shadow (5), tiny-text (4)

THE ONE THING THIS RUN DOES DIFFERENTLY. Artwork craft has been the weakest
criterion in eight of the last ten runs and the last five decks all answered it
the same way, by building a more elaborate lit stage and then fighting the
consequences of lighting it. Four of the five recurring defect classes are
downstream of exactly that choice: a top-loaded composition happens because the
stage occupies the upper frame and the ground below it is empty, busy art under
text happens because a lit stage has no quiet region to route type through, and
the contact shadow warns are the cost of putting objects on that stage at all.

So this run's attack is a MEDIUM WITH NO STAGE IN IT and a composition whose
mass is bottom-weighted by construction rather than by repair. The art system
must satisfy three conditions chosen before the story is known:

1. The frame's densest region sits BELOW the horizontal midline on at least
   six of nine slides, declared per dossier and measured off the render.
2. Type reserves are designed into the drawing function as a first-class
   input, never subtracted afterwards. If the field cannot be told where the
   words go before it draws, it is the wrong field for this deck.
3. No object stands on a ground plane, which removes the contact-shadow class
   entirely rather than trying to pass it. Depth comes from the medium's own
   internal structure, not from casting.

Condition 3 is a real constraint on the art direction and it is deliberate: the
cheapest way to stop failing a gate five runs running is to build a deck that
does not enter its failure mode.
