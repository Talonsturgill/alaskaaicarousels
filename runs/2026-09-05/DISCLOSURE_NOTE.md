# A DISCLOSURE THE MAINTAINER HAS TO SEE BEFORE POSTING

This deck reports that six people who list **Anthropic** as their employer gave
$372,000 to the leading candidate in Alaska's governor's race.

**This routine runs on a Claude model, which Anthropic makes.** The machine
writing the deck is reporting on the staff of the company that built it.

That is not a reason to spike the story. It is a reason to say so, and to be
able to show the work, which this run can.

- Every figure about the money comes from the State of Alaska's own Campaign
  Disclosure Income database, pulled in full this run, 4,604 rows, and saved to
  `out/2026-09-05/evidence/apoc_kreiss_tomkins_2026_income.csv`.
- The totals were computed twice by two separately written scripts sharing no
  code, and they agree exactly.
- An adversarial fact-checker read the six rows verbatim and independently
  killed twelve claims this run, including four the showrunner had wrong. It
  corrected the cap that applies to these gifts from $2,000 to $4,000, corrected
  the Bay Area count from four to six, caught that the California to Alaska
  comparison only holds because the six gifts are inside the California figure,
  and caught that a line the run nearly printed as a candidate quote was
  actually an Instagram parody.
- The deck states, as claims rather than as hedges, that the gifts were lawful
  when made, that the company gave nothing and has said nothing, and that no
  published account reports any donor asking for or receiving anything.

The editorial frame is deliberately NOT "an AI company bought a candidate". It
is that Alaskans voted 72.20 percent to cap contributions and the cap arrives
after the election it was voted in. The six gifts are the sharpest illustration
of a rule's start date, and the candidate who received them is the one
proposing an Alaska data centre moratorium, which the deck also prints because
it cuts against the easy reading.

If the maintainer would rather not post a deck about Anthropic's employees from
a page whose studio runs on Anthropic's model, that is a legitimate call and the
draft is a draft. The recommendation here is to post it and, if anything, to say
the quiet part in the first comment.
