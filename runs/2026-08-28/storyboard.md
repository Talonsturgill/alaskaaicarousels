# STORYBOARD — Alaska.Ai Carousel No. 43 — 2026-08-28

## THE DIRECTORS ROOM, and what the showrunner took

Three lenses pitched. Cartographer, field documentarian, historian of the
future. Two of the three independently invented the same hero, two sheets held
in register on a light table lit from below, which is a real signal that the
REGISTRATION idea is the story's natural picture. The showrunner did not take
it, and the reasoning matters.

**The winner is the FIELD DOCUMENTARIAN'S "THE DRAIN SCAR".** The deck is built
standing inside a drained thermokarst basin in northwestern Alaska at boot
level, not looking down at a table.

The decisive argument is story-art fusion. C10 says a drained lake leaves "a
circular basin of bare ground behind." A deck built inside that basin has a
hero structure that IS the verified claim, so a stranger could guess the story
from the art alone, which is the rubric's 10 descriptor. The two light table
treatments are pictures of the INSTRUMENT, and this deck's thesis is explicitly
that the story is the GROUND and not the dashboard. They would have illustrated
the press release and this illustrates the finding. Second argument, Alaska
authenticity. Wet silt, tussock sedge, spruce lath stakes with flagging tape,
iron seep, caribou tracks and a plywood datum board are Alaska. A light table
is anywhere. Third, tangibility, which selection.md named as the third
criterion, and a lake bed you can stand in beats a table you can't.

**Organs taken from the two losing treatments and grafted in.**

From the HISTORIAN OF THE FUTURE, the 1949 contact print, which the field
documentarian had also reached for at slide 07 but the historian argued better.
Crucially, the historian's own self critique is adopted as a hard guard. Its
words, and they are right: "The deck argues from an archive it never shows a
photograph of. A reader who zooms in expecting a 1948 negative finds a
beautiful fabrication of one." So slide 07's print margin carries
`ILLUSTRATION. NO SURVEY FRAME IS REPRODUCED HERE.` Also taken, the historian's
banned string guard. This lens tempts a sentence about what the surveyors
intended or knew, and C14 supports nothing about anyone's intent, so the words
INTENDED, KNEW and PURPOSE appear nowhere in this deck's copy.

From the CARTOGRAPHER, the "never one sum" discipline for C07 against C08, and
its insistence that a hedge belongs ON the slide at 432px rather than in a
footnote. The field documentarian's version is better executed, a 12 mm channel
routed into the plywood so the separation is physical rather than typographic,
and that is what gets built. Also taken, the cartographer's own flagged risk,
which is the same risk all three lenses found independently. Drawing flagged
lake positions the source never published is lying with the grammar of a map.
Both slides that show the thirty print `POSITIONS ILLUSTRATIVE`.

**What was rejected from the winner.** Nothing structural. Its own named risk,
that a pale front parallel board on eight of nine slides reads as nine cards
over a landscape, is accepted along with all three of its mitigations, and the
showrunner adds a fourth. Slide 06 is a declared register break where the board
IS the frame, so the board's dominance becomes a once only event rather than a
drift.

## SHIPPED AT SIX, AND WHY

The storyboard above planned nine slides and the run built six. This is the
degradation ladder's step (b), a REDUCED deck with the shortfall named, and it
was a decision taken with three slides on disk rather than a discovery made at
the end.

The reason is build cost, and it is worth writing down precisely because it is
the kind of thing that repeats. Every slide in this deck is a GPU rendered
landscape with a canvas drawn type carrier composited over it, and the run
found three separate traps that each cost render rounds. `AKT.setup` builds its
camera with a far plane of 200 while this deck's basin is 250 metres in radius,
so the terrain was clipped to the clear colour and read as an empty frame. A
14 degree key on near flat ground gives a cosine term around 0.24, which is a
black picture. And a cast shadow spanning an object's whole width has no
horizontal contrast in it, so the contact gate measures nothing whatever the
alpha. Each was diagnosed and fixed, and all three are going to FIELD_NOTES.

WHAT SHIPPED is the spine of the planned arc, unbroken. Hook, payoff,
mechanism, the keepable tally, the caveat, the close. Every open loop the six
plant is paid inside the six.

WHAT WAS CUT, and what each cost. The BREATHER (planned 04) was the cheapest
loss, since a six slide deck does not need a rest beat the way a nine slide
deck does. The STAKES slide (planned 05), where you can build and where you
can't, is the real loss, because it carried C16, C17 and C22, and C22 is the
figure that makes this an Alaska infrastructure story rather than a science
story. It survives in the caption, which carries roughly 80 percent of Alaska
land and more than 140 communities, so the argument reaches the reader even
though the picture of it did not. The PHOTOGRAPH slide (planned 07) carried
C14, C18, C19, C21 and the run's most argumentative single image, the late
1940s contact print registering against the real strandline. Its news peg,
the August 13th, 2026 launch, is the caption's opening move, so the deck is
not undated. Its honesty guard, the line saying no survey frame is reproduced,
moved into the first comment.
