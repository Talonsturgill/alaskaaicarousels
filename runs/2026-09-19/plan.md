## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 6 of the last 10 runs, mean 6.9
against a deck that otherwise scores 8.6. It is the only criterion that is both
chronically weakest and stale.

Read as a design brief rather than a report, the prediction is that today's deck
scores 6.9 on artwork craft unless something changes, and the place to change it
is the Phase 5 dossiers, not a Phase 9 repair pass.

**The mechanism, decided now and binding on the storyboard:**

1. **Every slide budgets its detail by region before any code is written.** Each
   dossier must name, for each of the frame's three depth zones (near, mid, far),
   one material that is actually rendered rather than filled: a grain, a weave, a
   corrosion, a wear pattern, a fibre, a condensation. The zoom test is the
   acceptance criterion and it goes in the checklist per slide.
2. **The drawn share is the target, not the floor.** `bespoke_check.py` fails
   under 45 percent drawn marks. The reference deck measures 82. This run aims at
   the reference, and the dossiers name which TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY entry does the
   drawing in each region, with parameters and seeds.
3. **No axis-aligned fillRect carries a material.** A gradient inside a rectangle
   is still a box, and that is exactly what the "blocky, drag and dropping
   shapes" complaint named. Where a plane is needed, it is drawn with a technique
   that has edge behaviour (hachure, relief shade, contour, stipple, flow).
4. **The three recurring machine_qa warn classes get designed out at the dossier,
   not patched after the gate.** Art touching glyphs, top-loaded composition, and
   contact shadow have each shipped in 3 of 10 runs. So: every type block declares
   its reserve as part of the layout map, the composition's weight is checked
   against the lower third in the dossier, and every contact declaration is
   MEASURED off a render with `contact_probe.py`, never computed off camera math.
