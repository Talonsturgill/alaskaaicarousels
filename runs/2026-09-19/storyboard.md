# THE SEASON DISC AND THE BROKEN-OUT WEDGE

## DIRECTORS ROOM, AND THE SYNTHESIS

Three lenses ran, and all three returned complete treatments.

**The winner is the DATA JOURNALIST's hero**, because it solves this deck's
single hardest problem. The paper publishes NO magnitude for the freeze-up
error (C12), only a direction, and a data lens is exactly the lens that most
wants to print a size. Instead of going vague, it reached for a real drafting
convention. When a length is real but not drawn to scale an engineer draws a
BREAK LINE and writes NOT TO SCALE, and every reader in a hard hat understands
that the gap is real, the order is certain and the number is withheld. So the
deck's signature mark is a dimension call with its value field deliberately
empty, sitting inside a declared discontinuity in the axis.

The second reason it wins is structural and it is the better one. The same
carved disc carries, twelve degrees away, the place where the two records
AGREE (C17), machined flush in the same material. One object, two transitions,
opposite outcomes. **That makes the fairness requirement impossible to drop.**
A deck printing C12 without C17 is a takedown and is not what the paper says,
and here C17 is not a dutiful slide bolted on, it is a feature of the hero.

**Strengthened by the EDITORIAL ESSAYIST on one decision that changes the
whole palette.** That treatment refused to colour-code the model against the
record, on the grounds that a two-team palette would contradict C15 on the very
slide it is printed on. The data treatment had proposed steel `#7FA0B0` for the
model against forget-me-not `#6EA5FF` for the record, which is more legible and
is wrong. **Adopted: both records are drawn in the SAME ink at the same value,
and are told apart by LINE VOICE alone**, ruled and machined for the model,
swelled and hand-registered for the observation. It costs nothing to render and
it is the deck's best idea.

Also adopted from the essayist: an on-slide instrument line stating plainly what
MOM6 COBALT ARC11k is, so no reader infers a machine-learning system from the
publication's name.

**Strengthened by the FIELD DOCUMENTARIAN on the cover and on one ethic.** Its
cover is far stronger than the data lens's flat statement of the disagreement,
because it opens on a human sentence and turns it into a machine state, and the
shock is a category mismatch rather than a contradiction. Adopted whole, with
its hook archetype. Adopted also, and without qualification, its refusal to
draw a person, a face, a parka, a boat, an ulu, a whaling crew, or any object
belonging to a specific community's practice. There is no source that would let
this studio draw those truthfully.

Runner-up organs deliberately NOT taken are recorded at the foot of this file.

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis

A coupled ocean model got Utqiagvik's seasons broadly right, and the one season
it got wrong was found not by a metric but by people standing on the beach
waiting for ice, which is why those people are on the paper as authors rather
than inside it as data.

**PDF document title.** `The Model Froze Utqiagvik Early. The Coast Froze Late` (52 characters)

7. **Technique stack.**

All seeds derive from 20260919. One shared projection function, nothing
eyeballed:

`P(r, theta, z) => [648 + r*cos(theta), 812 + r*sin(theta)*0.53 - z*0.85]`

where 0.53 is sin(32 degrees). Parallel oblique, never perspective, because the
disc carries quantities.

1. `AKENGRAVE.create({seed: 20260919, light: {azDeg: 168, elDeg: 22}})`, then
   `await document.fonts.ready`, then
   `eng.reserve(AKENGRAVE.boxesFor('.t-reserve'))`, then `eng.surface` on the
   side wall with a cylinder-proxy form, `tone 0.30`, `inkLo 0.10`,
   `inkHi 0.58`, `wMax 3.2`, `crossDeg 41`, **`seedDeg 63`**, `step 2.5`,
   `feather 14`. The `seedDeg` is not optional: a cylinder falls in one axis,
   its iso-lines are parallel, and without turning the seeding raster every seed
   lands on one iso-line and retraces a single curve as a knotted ribbon.
   `_layCheck` must read below 0.90.
2. Carved snow ground, seed 20260920, `lightDeg 168`, `windDeg 204`, drawn to an
   offscreen canvas and composited once, because `cx.filter` applies per draw op
   and blurring 850 strokes individually blows the render timeout.
3. Contact triple, pool then cast then seam, in that order and never reversed.
4. Fracture walls, columnar ice crystal habit, 6 to 14 px prisms, 3 to 5 facets
   each, filled tapered polygons with per-facet ndotl. The deck's most expensive
   square centimetre and the only place gold lives on this frame.
5. `AKC.ramp('#9FB6C4', {steps: 7, keyHue: 42, ambientHue: 232, drift: 0.16})`.
   Shading reads off ramp steps, never ad-hoc lightening.
6. `AKPOST.grade` on the ART canvas only, IGN dither ON because a large soft sky
   gradient is present and banding is otherwise guaranteed, unsharp 0.35 at
   radius 1.2, chromatic aberration 0.
7. Grain via `AK.grainTile(280, 52, 20260919)` at overlay 0.07. Never a
   full-frame feTurbulence, which is the 10 to 40 MB PDF trap.
