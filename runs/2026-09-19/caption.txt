Validation is what science does to data to decide if it is good enough. A paper published September 18th inverts that.

Did the model fail? No. It generally caught seasonal timing, especially the end of open water.

Where did it break? During the 2019 marine heatwave the model had ice freezing up earlier than the ocean did. The records cover more than 1,500 km of coast and show late freeze-up and storms that made hunting hard. That fall Utqiagvik took one bowhead, against past fall harvests of up to about 30.

The paper's line is that Indigenous Knowledge "does not require validation by scientific frameworks". Observers are co-authors.

The deck holds a season disc broken open where the records disagree, the observer's October 1st, 2019 entry, and ten author slots.

When a model and a coastline disagree about the same week, which one gets corrected?
