### THE ONE WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS, and how

**Top-loaded composition. Seven of the last ten decks shipped it, the most recent being yesterday.**

Artwork craft is the weakest CRITERION and top-loaded composition is the weakest MEASURED DEFECT, and
they are the same fact seen from two directions. A top-loaded frame is what happens when a deck's
headline is designed first and the picture is dropped underneath it, and a picture dropped underneath a
headline is exactly the picture a scorer calls thin. Five separate runs have now attacked artwork craft
through the ART (a new lighting model, a new depth technique, a new hero structure) and the composition
warn shipped anyway, because none of those attacks changed WHERE THE MASS SITS.

So this run attacks it through LAYOUT, at the dossier stage, and it is a rule rather than an intention:

> **THE LOWER TWO THIRDS CARRY THE DECK.** Every slide's principal subject mass has its centroid at or
> below y 700 of 1350, and the type block that names it sits ABOVE the mass, not below it. The frame is
> read top down as words first then object, and the object is the thing the reader's eye rests on when
> the swipe stops.

That inverts the house habit (headline top, art bottom, lower third dying) without reintroducing the
dead lower zone the dossier gate was written for, because the lower zone is now where the SUBJECT is
and not where a caption plate is. Each dossier's field 4a therefore names what the bottom band carries
in terms of modelled subject, and any dossier that answers "a label" fails its own checklist.

Density 2 is the enabling dial. Few elements, each fully modelled, each big enough to hold the lower
half of a 1080x1350 frame on its own.

Secondary watch, not the target: contact shadow (5 of 10). If this deck has a ground plane at all,
every declaration is MEASURED with `scripts/contact_probe.py` off a render, never computed off camera
arithmetic, and the floor is not a target.
