# STORYBOARD — Alaska.Ai carousel No. 40 — 2026-08-25

## THE ROOM'S DECISION

Three lenses pitched: DATA-JOURNALIST, SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR, HISTORIAN-OF-THE-FUTURE.

**WINNER, the chassis: THE 750 APRON (data-journalist).** It is the only one of
the three whose hero structure is literally the story's own count, so the
field-is-subject mandate and the argument are the same object. It also arrived
with the single best device any director produced, a colour key in which gold
means already true and blue means only proposed, which turns this deck's number
one hard-fail risk into something a pixel critic can call from the render alone.
And it came with nine separately specified generators, written against
`bespoke_check.py` rather than around it.

**GRAFTED FROM THE HISTORIAN, and this is the more important graft.** The
PHANTOM LAW. No slide may draw an unrealized state in solid ink. Every proposed
or not-yet element is a phantom dash `30 5 6 5 6 5` at 1.25px in `#7FA0C8`
(TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY 67), every current or committed element is solid. That is the
same accuracy claim as the colour key expressed in a second, independent channel,
which matters because a critic reading a 432px thumb may not resolve hue but will
resolve dash. Two channels, one meaning. Also grafted: placing the accuracy slide
EARLY, at 04, so it protects every slide after it rather than apologising at the
end, and Scout's own sentence as the breather, which is the warmth the
data-journalist's own self-critique correctly said its deck lacked.

**GRAFTED FROM THE SYSTEMS-ILLUSTRATOR.** The undrilled boss. An AI capability
the system does not have is drawn as a VOID in the apron with a rule laid over
it, never as a dark version of a lit thing. This makes the deck's worst possible
error, implying live AI identification of people, structurally impossible to
draw rather than merely forbidden. Also its "PRACTICE NOT CODE" stamping, which
becomes slide 07's guard.

**REJECTED, with reasons.** The ORDINANCE MANIFOLD is the most beautiful of the
three and it is a metaphor. An anodized signal plant is not Anchorage, it carries
one literal anchor in nine slides, and DESIGN_DOCTRINE 6.4 puts the floor at one
literal anchor plus one annotation per composition. The SEPTEMBER SURFACE's
haze-is-data idea (fog volume equals ungoverned volume) is genuinely elegant and
was cut on build risk, not on merit. Its scribe line lost to cell 0016 only
because the motif should be a member of the counted set, not a line laid over it.

**THE DATA-JOURNALIST'S OWN SELF-CRITIQUE IS ACTED ON.** It named slide 02, a
stepped date terrace, as the weakest drawing in its pitch and sitting in the slot
that must not be weak. Slide 02 is replaced. The calendar moves to 08, where the
historian had it and where it lands the ask, and the new 02 is THE LAUNCH STATE,
a 1:1 read of what actually opened in September 2025. That is also the second
answer to "this deck runs cold", because one employee in a room is a person and
750 is not.
