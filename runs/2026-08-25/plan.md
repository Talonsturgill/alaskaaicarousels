**The one weakness this run attacks is ARTWORK CRAFT AND GENUINE DETAIL.** It has
been the weakest criterion in 8 of the last 10 scored runs at a mean of 6.45,
while every other criterion sits above 7.75. It was last worked on eleven runs
ago. The scores are healthy at 8.0 to 8.9 and this one criterion is what caps
them.

**The diagnosis, and it is not a lack of technique.** Section 1 established that
the library already holds every device the frontier writing describes. Read
against the ledger, the real mechanism is the staged-object chassis. One object
in a large dark field puts most of the frame's area into GROUND, ground is not
the subject, so ground gets filled with the cheapest thing that passes a
checklist, which is flat furniture, and DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 5's zoom test
then fails in exactly the regions no dossier was ever written about. That is why
the defect survives per-slide acceptance checklists and only the scorer, looking
at the whole frame, ever names it.

**So the attack is a chassis where detail is structurally mandatory, plus a
budget that makes it countable.** Two commitments, both binding on Phase 5:

1. **THE FIELD IS THE SUBJECT, not the ground behind the subject.** This deck's
   hero structure must be a continuous data-bearing field, terrain, or dense
   system that occupies the full frame, so that there is no region of any slide
   whose content is "background". Every region is carrying information because
   the field IS the argument. This also satisfies the chassis divergence in
   section 2. A staged object on a bench is out of bounds this run.

2. **THE DETAIL BUDGET IS DECLARED PER SLIDE AND IT IS A NUMBER.** Every dossier
   states THREE distinct texture octaves and names what each one ENCODES, at
   three different spatial frequencies, coarse structure, mid modulation and fine
   grain. Any octave that encodes nothing is ornament and gets cut. This turns
   "genuine detail" from a hope at scoring time into a planned quantity at
   dossier time, and it puts the check where DESIGN_DOCTRINE 1 already learned
   the lower-third fix had to go, which is at planning altitude and not at the
   ship gate where rebuilding art is no longer affordable.

Both get restated in the storyboard header so the pixel critics and the scorer
can grade against them, and field 4a's lower-third treatment stays mandatory on
top of them.
