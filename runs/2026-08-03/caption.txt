AI supply chain here means one item on a five item list.

Graphite One's July 22 release lists AI infrastructure among five end markets for natural graphite as a class. No customer, no offtake, no volume.

The pit would run about 1.1 miles wide on a strip of state land ringed by corporation lands. 20 years, about 250 jobs. Bering Straits Native Corporation invested about $2 million and took an advisory board seat.

About 80 people met in Brevig Mission on July 28, reported as overwhelming opposition. That is a room, not a vote. A member of the company's Subsistence Advisory Council spoke the other way.

The five end markets at equal weight, the land tenure, both sides at one size, and the permitting clock.

Who decides this, the 80 who turned out in Brevig Mission, the corporation with a seat, or the federal office running the review?
