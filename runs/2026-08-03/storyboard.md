# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 24 — 2026-08-03

## THE SYNTHESIS (how the directors room resolved)

Three lenses pitched. **THE CUT BLOCK (cartographer) wins**, with two organs
grafted from the runners-up.

**Why it won, and the reason is honesty, not taste.** All three treatments were
strong. The Occupied Room (field-documentarian) and the Standing Core
(historian-of-the-future) both build their hero out of an INVENTED OBJECT. No
source describes the Brevig Mission building, its tables, its chairs or its
light, and no drill core appears anywhere in the record. Both directors said so
themselves, unprompted, in their self-critiques, which is why they are good
directors. But this deck's own subject is a company placing more weight on a
sentence than the sentence can carry. A deck that argues that while drawing a
room nobody described would be doing the same thing it is criticising.

The Cut Block is made of TRUE GEOMETRY end to end. A real projection, a real
coastline from committed geodata, a real village coordinate, a pit drawn at
true scale from the record's own number. Nothing on the hero is invented. That
is worth more here than either rival concept's superior drama.

**GRAFTED FROM THE HISTORIAN-OF-THE-FUTURE:**
- **The SOLID / PHANTOM RULE.** Solid stroke means it happened. Dashed phantom
  in bone-grey means expected, proposed, or non-binding. One grammar carries
  C04, C05, C13, C15 and C18 without a paragraph of caveat on every slide. This
  is the single best idea in the room and it is now continuity device B.
- **The fairness line** on the close, which is the showrunner's stated position
  said better than the showrunner said it. "A year of review is not obstruction.
  A July order is not consent."
- **The palette test's bottom-band clause**, which specifically kills the "warm
  headline zone over a navy floor" failure that beat the last two palette claims.

**GRAFTED FROM THE FIELD-DOCUMENTARIAN:**
- **TWO SENTENCES, ONE TABLE (S06).** C16 and C17 at identical size, identical
  weight, identical distance, no arbitration. The cartographer had split these
  two quotes across two slides and buried one in a breather. Staging them as a
  matched pair is better, and it turns the deck's fairness obligation into its
  most screenshot-able frame.
- **The Archivo width-axis semantic.** wdth 62 for anything said inside the
  village. wdth 125 for anything issued from Washington, Vancouver or a bank.
  One axis, one meaning, checkable on any slide. It is free and it is new.
- **Gold never sets type and gold never touches a number.**

**REJECTED, and why, recorded so it is not re-pitched:** the Occupied Room's
occupancy hachure field. The director named the flaw itself. A hachure field
draws SLOPE, and the slope of a crowd-density field is the crowd's EDGE, so the
packed middle and the empty back shade identically. The probe numbers would
pass while the picture underdelivered, which is precisely the failure mode this
run exists to kill.

**THE HACHURE FIELD (the run's declared craft attack).**

Runs on the block's TOP FACE on S01, S03 and S04, drawn flat into an offscreen
canvas in projected map space and transferred onto the top-face parallelogram
with one exact affine `setTransform` (parallel projection of a plane is affine,
so this is exact, not an approximation), clipped to the face polygon.

`height(u,v)` contains NO NOISE OF ANY KIND. This is a hard build constraint a
critic can verify by reading the slide source.

```
H(u,v) = 0.62 * ridge(u,v) + 0.38 * shore(u,v)
ridge(u,v) = exp(-(d_crest / 9km)^2)      d_crest = true projected distance to the Kigluaik axis
shore(u,v) = 1 - exp(-d_shore / 6km)      d_shore = true projected distance to the committed coastline,
                                          clamped to 0 over water so no stroke draws on the basin
```

Both terms are real geometry at true lon/lat, from `assets/geo/alaska-state.geo.json`
(376 coastline vertices resolve inside the Seward Peninsula bbox, verified before
the build) and the canonical projection (conic equal area, parallels 55/65,
rotate 154).

Parameters: `cell 12, lenScale 1.9, passes 4, sunAz 300, sunEl 40, sunJitter 10,
alpha 0.11, color '#1A140D', minWidth 0.5, maxWidth 3.6, lightBias on,
seed 20260803 + slideNo`.

Probes: `kigluaik front`, `imuruk flats`, `coastal bench`.

**THE FALSIFIABLE CRAFT CLAIM, declared BEFORE the build. Pixel critics are
instructed to CONTRADICT these from the render alone:**
1. `stats.widthRatio >= 4.5` over the field.
2. `meanWidth(kigluaik front) >= 2.0 x meanWidth(imuruk flats)`.
3. A critic shown only the render, and NOT told which region is which, must be
   able to name which of the two declared regions is the steep one.
4. Stroke bearing flips by roughly 180 degrees across the crest line, which a
   critic can check with a straightedge at 100 percent.

If (3) fails, the mechanism did not ship, exactly as No.23's tooth falloff did
not ship, and it is a REVISE and not a note.

**WHAT THE RECORD DOES NOT SUPPORT, AND HOW THE MAP HANDLES IT.** The record
gives a TIME to the deposit, about an hour by boat (C09), and no distance and no
coordinate. So the deck draws no measured line between the village and the pit.
Brevig Mission sits at its true coordinate; the pit is drawn at true scale; and
the hour is drawn as a PHANTOM ARC of possible positions, in the phantom voice,
labelled as a duration. The map's honesty about what it does not know is the
art, which is the correct cartographic answer and not a compromise.

### What the build actually changed from the dossiers

Generated after the build, before the flow critic, per the hard ordering rule.

**THE WINDOW MOVED, and it was the single most important change.** The dossiers
specified a Seward Peninsula window of about 178 km. At that width the pit,
which is 1.77 km across, drew FIVE PIXELS, and the hero's own subject was
invisible. The window tightened to about 70 km (BB -167.30, 65.02 to -165.62,
65.72), which puts the pit at roughly 23 px and the hour arc at roughly 230 px.
True scale is preserved; only the frame changed.

**THE TOP-FACE SUBSTRATE WAS LIGHTENED**, #3A2F1E to #6B5233, and the hachure
alpha went 0.11 to 0.19 with maxWidth 3.6 to 5.2 and passes 4 to 5. The dossier
values rendered dark ink on a dark ground, then got squashed about 0.5x
vertically by the axonometric transfer, and the field read as a flat gradient.
This is the exact failure the run exists to kill, caught in the render.

**THE COVER'S NOTCH WAS DELETED.** The dossier put a notch on the cut face as
the wordless claim. It rendered as an orphan parallelogram off the slab,
unlabelled, and its gold outweighed the pit's by roughly 8x, inverting the gold
hierarchy. The pit itself became the cut instead (rim lit only up-sun, bench,
floor, inner wall shadow) and gained a leader to a printed "1.1 MILES WIDE
[C10]", which is what the cover's second magnitude needed all along.

**SLIDE 07's HEADLINE CHANGED** from "The order took one day" to "Two days to
answer it. At least a year to review it." Nothing in the record says how long
the order took to draft, so the original was not derivable. Two days IS
derivable, C03 on 20 July to C01 on 22 July, and it is a sharper point. The
aggregate gate is what forced the question.

**SLIDE 07's MINE-LIFE SEGMENT WENT DASHED.** It was the only solid stroke past
the declared axis break, which states in the deck's own line-voice grammar that
a 20-year mine is on the record. It is not. A pixel critic caught it.

**SLIDE 02's LOCATOR RING WENT FROM GOLD TO BONE.** Gold on a non-pit object
breached the deck's own budget, and on a slide whose argument is that five end
markets are equal, a gold ring made one of them the most salient object in the
frame. It argued the opposite of its own point.

**SLIDE 08's LEGEND IS NOW MEASURED FROM THE LAID-OUT DOM**, not drawn on a
fixed pitch. Rows 3 and 5 wrap to three lines, and a constant row height had
walked every symbol off its own meaning: the stop stamp sat beside the Conneaut
row and the last row had no mark at all. Symbols and separators now read
offsetTop and offsetHeight from the rendered rows.

**THE HACHURE FIELD WAS ADDED TO SLIDES 02, 05, 06, 07 and 08** and strengthened
on 09. Four critics independently reported that the deck's declared mechanism
was visible only on the block slides, which made it a property of three slides
rather than of the deck.

**PLANNED AND NOT SHIPPED, stated plainly.** No akthree GPU hero was built. The
storyboard claims no rung on the rendered ladder, which is the deliberate
opposite of the previous run's failure of claiming one and skipping it. The
dimensional payload is a real extruded solid with a lit face, a shaded face and
a two-part contact shadow.

**PROCESS DEVIATION.** The copywriter subagent was not spawned. The showrunner
wrote copy.json, the first comment and the document title directly under the
same rules, because the session's context budget was the binding constraint by
that phase. Disclosed here, in the score prompt and in the email.
