## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS, AND HOW

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest criterion in 8 of the last
10 scored runs, mean 6.25, and 6.0 as recently as yesterday. Two upgrades
have been aimed at it and the number has not moved.

Read as a prediction rather than a report, the trend says this deck will
score 6.0 on craft unless something changes here, in the plan, before any
code is written.

The two most recent post-mortems say precisely why, and they agree:

1. No.23 (2026-08-02): "The mechanism was three material registers at
   different detail frequencies with a computed tooth falloff. The
   registers DID read; the falloff did not, on any slide, per multiple
   independent critics." And: "the akthree GPU beat planned for slide 04
   was simply never built, so the deck claimed a rung on the rendered
   ladder and skipped it. A mechanism that runs in code and not in pixels
   earns nothing."
2. The 2026-08-02 frontier park diagnosed the general case: "our detail is
   uniform because our stipple/TOOTH fields are parameterised by POSITION,
   so density falloff is a gradient laid over a texture that is otherwise
   the same everywhere, and a reader cannot see it."

So the attack has three parts, and each is falsifiable before the build,
the process fix that actually worked on No.23:

**(a) DETAIL PARAMETERISED BY DATA, NOT BY POSITION.** Every generative
field in this deck is driven by a story quantity evaluated at that point,
never by a positional gradient laid over a uniform texture. Where the deck
carries modelled mass, detail comes from a slope-and-aspect hachure field
(the parked Woodruff technique, FIELD_NOTES 2026-08-02): one short stroke
per grid cell whose WIDTH encodes local slope and whose ROTATION encodes
local aspect, strokes longer than their cell so neighbours blend, cell
positions jittered, each stroke bent slightly, and the whole field redrawn
several times at low opacity with the sun angle varied a little each pass
so shadow detail ACCUMULATES. Non-uniformity is then structural instead of
applied. This run promotes it from PARKED to BUILT, with a worked
TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY entry carrying our own parameters.

**(b) THE FALSIFIABLE CRAFT TEST, stated before the build.** Copying
No.23's palette-hue mechanism and pointing it at craft. Every pixel critic
will be given, in its prompt, a numeric detail test it is instructed to
try to CONTRADICT: the hachure field's stroke width must vary by at least
2.5x between the deck's flattest and steepest declared regions, and the
critic must name the two regions and say which is which from the render
alone, without being told. If a critic cannot tell them apart, the
mechanism did not ship, exactly as the tooth falloff did not ship on
No.23, and it is a revise.

**(c) THE RENDERED LADDER RUNG IS BUILT OR IT IS NOT CLAIMED.** No.23
claimed akthree and shipped no GPU frame. This run either builds the
akthree hero and proves it with the snapshot sentinel and a qa.py pass, or
the storyboard claims no rung at all. There is no third option, and the
Phase 8 build-reconciliation section will state which happened, generated
by gate_status.py rather than written by hand.
