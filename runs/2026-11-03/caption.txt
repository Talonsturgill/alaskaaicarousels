Four candidates for Alaska governor gave three answers on data centers. A 20,000-acre land decision may not wait for the winner.

Ranked choice on November 18th, if needed, and certification by November 25th can settle who governs. Neither writes a data center law. The House never took up the bill the Senate passed 14 to 5.

DNR's commissioner calls a Houston decision before noon December 7th "certainly possible." The site could include data centers. AIDEA has maintained they're unlikely.

Two frames hold the candidates' words. One each holds the early count, the DNR hearing, the Pentagon offer, a Fairbanks town hall of about 150 and the Senate vote.
