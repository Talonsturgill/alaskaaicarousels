## TOP INSTINCTS INJECTED INTO EVERY SUBAGENT THIS RUN

1. Never treat a machine_qa PASS as composition approval. The pixel critics
   judge composition, hierarchy and collision at full size and at thumb size.
2. qa.py's text-collision check is DOM ONLY. Any label positioned against
   canvas or SVG geometry can collide freely and the gate still returns green,
   so a type reserve must be designed in rather than checked for afterwards.
3. A lit ground is SCREENED over the surface and the cast is an ATTACHED
   subtraction from that lit ground thrown down-light. Measure the pair with
   contact_probe.py, never compute it from camera arithmetic.
4. Apply grain as a small repeating tile (AK.grainTile), never a full-frame
   feTurbulence rect.
5. A constraint you can't point at is a constraint you invented. There is no
   context budget and no token budget in this routine.

**THE ONE WEAKNESS: artwork craft and genuine detail**, weakest in 5 of the
last 10 runs at a mean of 6.8, and 7.0 as recently as yesterday.

Read as a prediction rather than a report, it says today's deck scores about
6.8 on craft unless something changes upstream. The pattern underneath it is
visible in the variety ledger: the last four decks are all NEAR-BLACK INTERIOR
OR NIGHT SCENES lit by one hard key (new ice under black water, a night pour
under a work lamp, a cargo hold at altitude, and before them an overcast map
sheet). A near-black frame hides thin detail for free, which is exactly why the
craft score sits where it does. Detail that can't be seen can't be graded.

**What this run does differently, decided here and binding on Phase 5:**

1. **Daylight, high key, and a light ground.** The deck is lit so that detail
   is visible rather than implied. This also clears the atmosphere and palette
   divergence windows in one move.
2. **Drawn share is a design target, not a gate to squeak past.**
   `bespoke_check.py` fails under 45 percent drawn marks; the bespoke
   reference measures 82. This deck targets the reference, and the storyboard
   must name, per slide, the generative mark system that produces it
   (stipple, hachure, contour, flow field, relief), with its seed and
   parameters. No frame's structure may be carried by axis-aligned fillRect.
3. **Type reserves are designed in at dossier time.** Every one of the four
   recurring machine_qa warn classes above (canvas mark near reserved text,
   art touching glyphs, busy art under text, top-loaded composition) is one
   defect wearing four names, and it is a LAYOUT decision rather than a
   repair. Each dossier's layout map must carve the type reserve out of the
   mark system's own domain, so the generator never emits a mark there,
   instead of drawing everywhere and masking after.
4. **Contact shadows are measured, not computed,** with `contact_probe.py`,
   and the floor is not a target. This is the fifth recurring warn class and
   it cost No.41 two rounds.
