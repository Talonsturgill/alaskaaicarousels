# STORYBOARD, run 2026-09-27, Carousel No.70

## THESIS

The Dixon Glacier diversion is the right kind of fix for a gas-short grid, because it buys energy rather than megawatts: about 38 percent more electricity a year from the same plant. But its dam is slated for 2030 and its transmission for 2031 or 2032, so every AI data center pitched onto the Railbelt before then is pitched into the winters AEA's own chief says have "nothing for tomorrow."

Document title (PDF, 60 chars max): `A Bigger Tank, Not a Bigger Engine: Bradley Lake and 2030`
