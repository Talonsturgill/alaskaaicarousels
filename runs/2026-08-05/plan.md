**The ONE weakness this run attacks is artwork craft and genuine detail, via
SILHOUETTE-FIRST LINE HIERARCHY.**

Last run's notan attack was the right idea and it half-landed. Declaring the
two-value mass arrangement before the technique stack did change the build,
but a deck-wide lightness law rendered the cover's lower third as one dead
near-white field, and the fix was per-slide sub-ranges discovered by looking.
Value structure alone is not enough. The complementary axis, and the one the
series has never made a rule out of, is LINE.

The rule, from this run's craft refresh (Phase 1) and codified here:

1. A line's weight is a function of the DEPTH DISCONTINUITY it represents,
   never of how important the thing is. Silhouette against background is
   heaviest. An interior edge where two surfaces of the SAME object meet is
   thin. Detail inside a face is thinnest.
2. The corollary is the one that pays. **A contact line stays thin.** Where an
   object meets the ground it sits on, a heavy line reads as a gap, and the
   object floats. That is the exact defect the doctrine has been describing
   from the other end for six runs as "flat plates and hairlines floating on
   bare ground", and it now has a mechanical cause and a mechanical fix.
3. Every drawn object in this deck therefore declares three weights in its
   dossier field 7, with the contact edge named explicitly, and the pixel
   critics get the ratio to check.

Two supporting commitments, both by construction rather than by vigilance,
aimed at the recurring hard fail and the repeat warn classes:

1. Never treat a machine_qa PASS as composition approval. qa.py's collision
   check is DOM-only, so any label against Canvas or SVG geometry can collide

2. Size every plate from the measured string, not from an estimate.
