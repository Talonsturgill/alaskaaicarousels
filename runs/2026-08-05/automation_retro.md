## 2. THE REPEAT-OFFENDER DECISION: WORKED, NOT DEFERRED

Top repeat offender is **Artwork craft and genuine detail**, weakest in 7 of
the last 10 runs, mean 5.78, last worked five runs ago. **It is being worked
this run**, and it is being worked at exactly the point where this run lost
the criterion.

The reason it is workable today and was not on 2026-08-01 or 2026-08-02 is
that this run finally produced a piece of the criterion that is MECHANICAL.
"Artwork craft" as a whole is a taste judgement and every previous attempt to
gate it has failed calibration on exactly that ground (see `encoding_reads` in
qa.py: two candidate metrics separated the known-bad from the known-good
BACKWARDS, and the function was left as a measurement rather than a gate). But
the defect that cost No.26 its craft score is not a taste judgement. It is
arithmetic:

    #1A0F08 at alpha 0.55 composited on a table already near #0B0906
      -> a 1.2 L* change at feed scale
    four pixel critics, independently: contact_edge_reads: no
    qa.py: 0 fails

A shadow is a subtraction. It needs something to subtract from. That is a
one-dimensional question about two adjacent regions, it needs no semantics and
no shape reading, and the slide can be made to ask it itself. So the slice of
artwork craft that got taken this run is the slice that is objectively
checkable, and the rest of the criterion stays open.

What is still deferred, stated plainly: the other three-quarters of the
scorer's craft complaint (bare cream over the lower 40 to 50 percent of the
page plane on four slides, the hero's gold mark landing mid-word, the S06 comb
floating off the block, no rung on the rendered ladder). Those are composition
and execution, not a measurable invariant, and a gate drawn through them would
be a threshold on taste. What would have to be true to tackle them: a fitted
corpus. `scripts/craft_corpus.py` exists; the exercise is declaring the missing
invariants across the back catalogue so a threshold can be FITTED from real
known-goods and known-bads instead of guessed, which is a session of its own,
not a slot at the end of a run.
