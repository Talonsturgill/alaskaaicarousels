## CANDIDATE 1 ANA closes the Alaska-only door and opens a national AI one, same week (Beat D, corroborated by Beat C)

The Administration for Native Americans published its final FY2026 program
policies on 2026-07-28 (Federal Register doc 2026-15150), replacing SEDS and
the Alaska-specific SEDS-AK with a single nationwide EAGLE competition. Two
days later, on 2026-07-30, it posted a brand new single-award AI3 Action
Institute, ceiling $3,500,000, floor $2,500,000, one expected award,
opportunity HHS-2026-ACF-ANA-NAI-0035. Alaska Native villages are listed
eligible for both. Both close 2026-08-27 at 11:59 pm Eastern, which is
6:59 pm Alaska time.

The Federal Register text carries the objection in the agency's own record.
Commenters raised "the potential loss of an Alaska-specific funding
opportunity and the possibility that Alaska Tribes could face new barriers in
competing for funding under a new NOFO." ANA's answer is that Alaska Native
communities remain eligible across EAGLE project areas.

Why it is the strongest candidate: primary documents throughout, a live
deadline an Alaskan can act on, a structural argument rather than an
announcement, and a shape no recent deck has used. Beat C's independent
Federal Register sweep of all 49 Alaska documents in the window surfaced this
same item as the ONLY Alaska AI item in the register, which is corroboration
from a scout that was not looking for it.

Risks to clear at fact-check: nothing has been awarded, so no Alaska winner
may be implied. The $27.5M package total and the "more than 30 years" figure
are trade press (Tribal Business News, 2026-08-04), not the NOFO. The count of
Alaska federally recognized tribes was NOT verified by any scout and must not
be asserted without a fetched source.

## CANDIDATE 2 NSF GAIA starts 2026-08-01 with Alaska landslides as the test bed (Beat B)

NSF award 2608510 to UAF, $1,772,170, start 08/01/2026, expiration
07/31/2032. PI Carl Tape; co-PIs Michael West (Alaska Earthquake Center
director), Franz Meyer (ASF/SAR), Ronni Grapenthin. Abstract: "The
investigators test the full system on landslides in Alaska and the Pacific
Northwest" and the fourth thrust builds "real-time digital twins that
forecast geohazards." Companion awards: UW $2,209,093, EarthScope $449,448.

Strong material, excellent visual hooks (Barry Arm's 650-foot slump, the
Taan Fjord 620-foot runup, a 5-minute warning target).

DEDUPE CONCERN, and it is the reason this is candidate 2 rather than 1:
No.20 on 2026-07-30 was "278 federal AI projects. Alaska has one," a UAF
federal-AI-award deck six days ago. A second UAF-wins-federal-AI-money deck
is the same shape with different nouns, which is exactly what the
anti-template law exists to stop. It would need a genuinely different frame
to earn the slot.

customers to focus on supplying existing ratepayers." CEO John Sims on this
winter, "If it's like last winter, we won't make it without some sort of

first, then businesses, then churches, schools, hospitals and homes last. Gas

back. Weakness: the "large-scale customers" line does not name data centers,
so the AI spine is our inference, and No.20's caption already opened on
Enstar's blackout warning six runs ago.
