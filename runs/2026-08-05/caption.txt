To the Administration for Native Americans. You stood up three notices for FY 2026. SEDS-AK is not one of them.

Your response to comments prints the objection. Commenters warned Alaska Tribes could face new barriers. The same pages answer that Alaska Native communities remain eligible across EAGLE.

No page anyone could reach shows what SEDS-AK was worth. The provable change is who names the work, and one of the three is an AI institute.

Enclosed, the objection and the reply as printed, EAGLE at $24,000,000, the AI3 Action Institute at $3,500,000, and the blank where SEDS-AK's number would go.

Both windows close 27 August. Until then a village office has to fit its own priority into a greenhouse, a microgrid, Welders to Elders or IDEAS, the one a community designs itself.

Is IDEAS wide enough to carry what SEDS-AK carried?
