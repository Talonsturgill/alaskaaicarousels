# "THE BORROWED RULER": Fairbanks Ordinance 2026-42 at the Planning Commission (UPDATE of No.94)

## THESIS
Fairbanks is right to pause, but the staff amendment measures a data center by its shell: 40,000 square feet of gross floor area, the line the code already draws for an 800-seat entertainment facility, a category whose standards address building aesthetics, maintenance and parking lot design. What commenters asked the borough to regulate (water and air quality, power and water supply, noise, vibrations, size) runs inside the shell, and the ordinance's own 5 MW tests and its six month study are the parts that read it. The deck takes the position that the floor line is a placeholder and the study is the real test, and it is fair to the ordinance: two of its three tests already measure power.
PDF document title (60 chars max): `Fairbanks would size a data center like an 800-seat venue`
