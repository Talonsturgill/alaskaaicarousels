## THESIS
The data center was never the real question. The question for October 26th is why DNR proposes to give AIDEA 19,950 acres north of Houston at no cost with no take-back clause, when it kept one on 885 acres at Port MacKenzie, and until then the only answer the record holds is one sentence: "The Governor's office has not given us permission to attend." Position taken: the reverter waiver is the thing the commissioner should have to explain.
Document title (PDF, 60 chars max): `Houston's 19,950 acres, and the answer owed October 26th`

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here
Artwork craft was the weakest criterion in 7 of the last 10 runs, most often because a frame's largest object was its least modelled. Here every frame's largest object IS its plate, and each plate is built on a bench that models tone per pixel or per stroke (akthree PBR, akbench relief, akengrave swelled line, aksnow carved surface). The type column is designed onto unlit ground (no plates under type), and the masterful depth frame (09) is built first.
