On January 30th the University of Alaska put 829 acres at Port MacKenzie up for lease on a notice that named no tenant.
Terra Energy signed a 20-year lease in late May for a campus the lease calls "a large-scale industrial park," UA's land director Adrienne Stolpe told the Mat-Su Sentinel.

The Mat-Su Assembly overrode a veto 6-1 in March to market borough land jointly with Terra to big power users like data centers. Yet the university signed without telling borough officials. To port manager Dave Griffin, that "blows my mind."

Per the Mat-Su Sentinel, major state land actions need a best interest finding that weighs public comment, and university land is exempt.

Four parcels to scale and the notice's dates, the rent by year, the affiliate's coal plan and the 6-1 vote.

Should a 20-year university lease stay exempt from the best interest finding?
