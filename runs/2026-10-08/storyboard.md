# STORYBOARD, Carousel No.81, run 2026-10-08
# "THE BLANK TAG": 829 acres, 20 years, and a notice that named no tenant

## THESIS
The University of Alaska asked the public to comment on leasing 829 acres at Port MacKenzie through a notice that named no lessee and no use. Comments closed March 2nd. In late May the land went for 20 years to Terra Energy, for an industrial park tied to its affiliate's coal plant, a plant DOE's own page says would serve data centers among other loads, and university land skips the best interest finding the state owes on its own land. The deck's position: a university land notice should name the use before the comment clock runs out. It never says the lease is for a data center (C16); it says exactly where data centers do appear in the record (C40, C41).
PDF title: `829 Acres, 20 Years, and a Notice That Named No Tenant` (55 chars).

## ARC, nine slides
