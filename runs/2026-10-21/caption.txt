On June 23rd, 2024, four fixed-wing aircraft began flying the Kuskokwim for the state's McGrath survey.
Lines ran 400 meters apart and about 100 meters up.

Federal Earth MRI grants paid for McGrath and its data is a free download. Four more state surveys came out this year.

On October 20th Geomorphic AI formed Golden Dyke Exploration in Alaska. Geomorphic calls itself an AI-driven minerals prospect generator that packages targets and keeps an interest. It hasn't said whether it uses these surveys.

One real McGrath flight line's magnetic profile, a map and a card of five state surveys, the Ganes Creek radiometrics printed with three grant numbers, and an empty pocket for a first Alaska property, none named yet.

The state says the data will promote resource exploration. Does packaging targets for others to drill count as that exploration?
