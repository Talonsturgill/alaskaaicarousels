# STORYBOARD, No.92, 2026-10-21: THE FLOWN LINE

## THESIS
Alaska's geological survey published five airborne surveys of the middle Kuskokwim this year, paid for by federal Earth MRI grants, flown on lines 400 meters apart about 100 meters off the ground, and released as a free download under an open license. On October 20th an AI prospect generator, Geomorphic AI, set up an Alaska subsidiary, Golden Dyke Exploration, whose business is to package targets and keep an interest in them. It names no Alaska property yet and hasn't said whether it uses these surveys. The input is public; the output will be private. Publishing the data free is right. A target built on public surveys should say which ones it used.
PDF title: `The State Flew the Kuskokwim. An AI Prospector Arrived.` (55 chars)

Artwork craft was the weakest criterion on 6 of the last 10 decks: the largest object drawn with least care, one drawing on four frames, dead lower thirds, texture artifacts. Here the largest object on every frame is real data built first as a lit form (the profile stack, the line map, the lifted tile, the terrain section, the rendered terrain, the card's outlines, the printed plate, the stipple field), each with a different primary mark-making, and every lower third carries the rail's modelled ridge plus the frame's own art.
