# STORYBOARD, No.97, 2026-10-27, THE RECORD SET DOWN ON THE LAND

## THESIS
December 7th ends a term, not a land transfer. DNR's decision on the Houston conveyance can still land before the inauguration ("certainly possible", C28), but the steps that actually move 19,950 acres (an appeal open to more than 2,000 commenters, survey, plat, title, closing, a lawsuit whose 40-day clock starts at service) carry no date on the record, and by the commissioner's own account they likely run into the next governor's term (C30 to C32, C53, C55, C15). The deck takes a position on the clock and none on the transfer, the suit or the candidates.

PDF document title: `The decision could beat December 7th. The handoff likely won't.` (49 chars, copywriter may refine)
