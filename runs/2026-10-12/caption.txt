22 legislative candidates were in a Palmer theater on October 4th as the crowd answered a question on AI data centers.

57 of 112 chose "Outlaw them entirely." 47 chose strict regulations and 8 chose allow. That's a room with its tally on screen, not all of Mat-Su.

On Mat-Su land, the question's "state" is three rooms. DNR is deciding whether AIDEA gets about 19,950 acres near Houston. AIDEA lists data centers among possible uses and says none is proposed. The University of Alaska leased 829 acres at Port MacKenzie.

The third is the Legislature, where data center bill SB 250 passed the Senate 14 to 5 and stalled in a House committee. Early voting opens October 19th.

Pictured are the room's phones, the Houston land, the Port MacKenzie parcels, a gavel on SB 250 and the voting calendar.

Should only the Legislature set the rules for data centers on state land?
