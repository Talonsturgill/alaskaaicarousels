# STORYBOARD, run 2026-09-28, Carousel No.71

## THESIS

At Eielson the public gets a say this week on one thing, the site for a 5 megawatt reactor beside a wetland, and the window closes September 30th. The same base also offered land for AI data centers, a load class the federal permitting order starts at more than 100 megawatts, through a solicitation that took developer proposals in June. Use the open door, and know how small it is.

Document title (PDF, 60 chars max): `A Say on 5 Megawatts at Eielson, Until September 30th` (the copywriter's title in copy.json, which the PDF carries; the room's working title was "The One Door Open at Eielson")
