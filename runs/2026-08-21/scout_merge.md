**A genuinely PRIMARY verified fact, and it is the strongest thing found all
run.** Beat A queried the Federal Register API on the term DeepGreen, all
agencies, all dates, on August 21st, 2026, and got **zero documents**. The
same API returns FERC "Notice of Preliminary Permit Application Accepted for
Filing and Soliciting Comments" notices for every comparable Cook Inlet tidal
permit going back twenty years, including Ocean Renewable Power Company
P-15116 (published May 11th, 2021), Littoral Power Systems P-15317 (September
12th, 2023), ORPC Alaska 2 P-13821 (October 27th, 2010) and Turnagain Arm
Tidal Energy P-13509 (July 24th, 2013). FERC's own spokesperson Celeste
Miller told ADN the sequence, "After staff determine the application is
complete, the commission will request comments from the public."

Six months after filing, that step has not happened, so no Alaskan has a
comment window on this. The absence is the news and it is citable.

   FERC's New England branch, with a 45 day cure window. Beat F merged the two;
   Beat A kept them separate and Beat A is the reading the page supports. Two
   letters, two projects, two dates. Do not print one date for both.

   Wednesday" and is dated August 13th, 2026. August 13th, 2026 is a Thursday,
   so the vote was **Wednesday, August 12th**, reported the 13th and carried by
   WABI on the 14th. Beat F's "August 13th to 14th" is the reporting window,
   not the vote.
3. **The PowerLink cost, and this one is a trap.** Three figures attach to one
   cable. AEA's own page says $142 million. The 2023 federal award under the
   infrastructure law was about $206 million. This week's reporting, and
   DeepGreen's own FERC narrative, say $400 million. Separately the AEA board
   endorsed a $400 million financing package for the BRADLEY LAKE expansion on
   August 9th, which is a different project and a different $400 million.
   Prefer AEA's page. If the deck cannot resolve it, the deck does not print a
   PowerLink cost.
4. **The turbine count.** ADN says "up to 350"; the Maine Monitor says
   "approximately 330 to 350". Write the range, not the round number.
5. **No FERC project number for the Alaska application exists in any source any
   scout could read.** The Maine sibling is reported as P-15424 by Data Center
