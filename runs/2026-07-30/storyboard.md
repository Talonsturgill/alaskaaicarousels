# STORYBOARD — 2026-07-30 — Carousel No. 20
# THE INHABITED DARK

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis

Every AI story in Alaska this month has been about AI arriving to take power,
and the one row Alaska holds on the federal government's flagship AI list
points the other way, at Alaska's own grid, which the state's utility
executives said six days later they can no longer guarantee.

**PDF document title (38 chars):** `Alaska's One Row on the Federal AI List`

### The synthesis, and why this deck is shaped this way

Three directors pitched. The showrunner took the ESSAYIST's argument spine and
hook, the DATA JOURNALIST's projection discipline, and the CINEMATOGRAPHER's
light. The three disagreed on exactly one thing that mattered, and resolving it
is what this deck is.

The cinematographer wanted 278 lights strewn through a perspective valley. The
data journalist objected, correctly, on doctrine: parallel projection for
quantities, NEVER perspective. The essayist felt the same problem from the
other side and wrote it into their own self-critique, that strewn dots are
close to the wallpaper the doctrine bans.

**The resolution, and it governs every slide.** The landscape is a PLACE and is
drawn in perspective, because a place is not a quantity. The count lives in a
REGISTER that is flat, ordered, parallel and countable, and it is present on
every single slide. Nothing in the landscape is ever asked to encode a number.
The two readings of the same object, a valley of lights and a list of awards,
sit in one frame in two different projections, and the deck moves between them.

### Variety ledger check

- **Hero structure, THE INHABITED DARK.** A winter Interior valley at night as
  a five-plane multiplane field, revisited from nine camera stations, where
  every light source in the world is human. NOT the Adjudicated Margin op-ed
  chassis (16), NOT the Milled Register bone slab under one camera (17), NOT
  the Dead End insulator String on a yoke plate (18), NOT the Hour Column
  quantity column (19). It is a FIELD rather than an object, which breaks all
  four at once and is the point of this run.
- **Atmosphere, ICE FOG AT FORTY BELOW.** Cold high-chroma blues, snow as the
  major surface, warm cream only where a person is awake. Ice fog is a
  combustion product, made by the town itself, which is thematically exact for
  a deck about a place making its own light. NOT gallery-lit bone (17), NOT sea

  since the one light deck per eight runs was spent on 07-25.

- **Hook archetype, THE DIRECTION REVERSAL (new).** State the transaction the
  audience already believes is happening, put a counted fact under it as the

  (18), NOT the definitional correction (19). Nothing is corrected here. An
  arrow is turned around.
- **Type pairing, Fraunces display plus Space Grotesk body plus JetBrains Mono

### THE DECK-WIDE TEXT DEFENSE (the recurring hard fail, closed by construction)

`text against geometry` hard-failed 2 of the last 10 runs. qa.py's DOM
collision check cannot see canvas or SVG geometry, so a green gate is not a
green composition. Three layers, and EVERY text block in this deck declares
which it uses:

- **THE PERMANENT QUIET CORRIDOR, y 80 to 150, on all nine slides.** The
  running line sits at left, the counter at right. On every slide that band is
  sky or far dark, the darkest and lowest-frequency band in the frame by
  construction. This closes the fixture case deck-wide, in one decision.
- **(R) ROUTED.** The composition routes a quiet zone under the block. Used
  wherever type sits in the sky band above the horizon. Preferred everywhere it
  is possible.
- **(P) PLATED.** The string sits on a knockout built by
  `AK.svgPlate(textEl, {padX, padY, fill, stroke})`, sized from the label's own
  measured `getBBox` after `await document.fonts.ready`. **No plate width is
  ever typed by hand anywhere in this deck.** JetBrains Mono at 24px with
  0.10em tracking advances 16.8px per character and the eye estimates about 14,
  which is how six labels shipped off their plates on 07-29.

### Depth plan, deck-wide

**Value grouping FIRST, detail second**, per this morning's craft-refresh note.
Three declared bands with numeric OKLCH lightness targets, and they are the
reason this deck exists in this shape:

- **BACKGROUND, L 0.28 to 0.38, minimal contrast, soft edges.** Far range,
  second range, high sky. No detail permitted beyond a silhouette and one
  hazed shoulder.
- **MIDGROUND, L 0.18 to 0.30, moderate contrast.** Ice fog mass, pole line,
  settlement plane. This is the tack-sharp focal plane on most slides.
- **FOREGROUND, L 0.06 to 0.55, FULL range, sharpest edges, warmest chroma.**
  The drift, its sastrugi, its two-part shadow, one blurred repoussoir bleeding
  off frame. All spent detail lives here.

**Hard numeric guard against the flat single-value-group failure that has cost
this series 6.90 three runs running: no more than 12 percent of frame pixels
may land within plus or minus 0.03 of the mid-band centre L 0.51.** The pixel
critics get this as an explicit check.

**The six depth cues, all delivered:**
1. **Atmospheric perspective.** Five planes; per plane i of 5, lerp toward sky
   by `(i/5)^1.4`, desaturate by `x(1 - 0.6*i/5)`, contrast falls with
   distance. Numbers from `AKPOST.depthGrade(d)`, never by eye.
2. **Overlap.** The drift overlaps the settlement plane; the fog bank cuts the
   mid range at its base; poles overlap poles. Free and strongest.
3. **Scale gradient.** Pole repetition at `0.72^i` with vertical position
   rising toward the horizon.
4. **Depth of field.** ONE tack-sharp plane per slide. Far range blur 2.5px,
   second range 5px, foreground repoussoir 6px with a hard bleed off frame.
5. **Fog.** exp2, `1 - e^-(d/z)^2`, in fog hue `#23405E`. Never gray, never
   black.
6. **One key light, and it is not the sun.** The ice-fog dome over the
   settlement, low, screen right. Key to fill 5:1. Shadows `#101F33`, a
   darkened fog hue, never black, one global direction, two-part on every
   foreground form (tight contact plus wide ambient).

### Render approach, and the deliberate argument for no GPU

This deck is Canvas 2D plus SVG plus AK3D heightfields (#34) inside a
multiplane stack (#42), finished with `AKPOST.grade` and OKLCH ramps from
`AKC.ramp` (#89, #90). **There is no akthree path and no aksdf path.**

This is a decision, not a shortcut, and the dossier argues it as
DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 4 requires. The diagnosis this run exists to fix is
craft CONCENTRATED in one rendered object across three consecutive 6.90 decks.
The fix is nine evenly-crafted fields. Spending the render budget on one PBR
object would be taking the same side of the wager that just lost three times.
AK3D is real software 3D with a real camera, so the deck is not flat; it simply
puts its dimension into atmosphere and parallax across nine frames instead of
into one mesh. Every art canvas is graded with `AKPOST.grade` and IGN dither
forced on, which is the stated craft floor. No GL also means no `webglOK()`
fallback branch, no black-frame sentinel risk, and no silent 1x render.

Deck seed for all noise: **20260730**.

### WHAT THE BUILD CHANGED FROM THE DOSSIERS

Every number below is a dossier number the build actually changed. Critics must
measure the renders against THESE, not against the planned values above.

1. **The scrim strategy changed twice, and the second change is the one that
   held.** The dossiers declared every body block (R) ROUTED into a quiet band.
   The first render proved that wrong on five slides: qa.py returned
   `label crossed by art` on S03, S04, S06, S07 and S09, because the fog,
   settlement and drift reach higher into the frame than the plan assumed.
   A declared scrim (technique 7) was added, and it FAILED on three slides
   because a hand-sized scrim cannot track a wrapped block, which is the same
   class of error as a hand-typed plate width. The body copy on S04, S06 and
   S07 now carries its own knockout plate (`.txtplate`), which grows with the
   copy and which the engine treats as the text's own plate rather than as a
   foreign occluder. Those three blocks are (P), not (R).

2. **Register y positions moved on two slides, because the register is SVG and
   SVG paints above the DOM.** S06 planned y 648 and S04 planned y 940; both
   ran the 278-tick strip straight through the body copy. S06 is now y 848 and
   S04 is now y 1010. The hollow gold tick on S06 therefore sits lower in the
   frame than the dossier's `[738, 634, 12, 28]` region; its measured region is
   now approximately `[738, 834, 12, 28]`.

3. **Type floors were raised.** The dossiers specified a 20px running line and
   22px mono labels. Both are under the engine's 24px mobile floor and every
   slide warned. All mono voices are now 24px. This is a legibility improvement
   the plan got wrong.

4. **Range relief was tripled after the first render read flat.** Far range
   amplitude went from 46px to 150px and mid range from 62px to 190px, and both
   now compute as `baseline + A * (0.58 - ridged(...))` so peaks rise above the
   baseline. Sky mixing dropped from 0.70 to 0.52 (far) and 0.34 to 0.16 (mid).

5. **The drift band order was inverted, and this was the run's most important

   loop ran dark-to-light, so the lightest band painted over every band behind
