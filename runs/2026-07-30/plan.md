### The ONE weakness this run attacks, and how

**Artwork craft and genuine detail**, attacked at its actual cause rather than
at its symptom, plus the RECURRING hard fail it drags with it.

Read the two rows together. Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 8
of the last 10 runs, and the score has sat at exactly 6.90 for the last three
scored runs. Three consecutive decks (17 milled bone register, 18 dead-end
insulator string, 19 hour column) shared one recipe: ONE precisely modelled
object, rendered or relit, surrounded by drafting-instrument furniture, with
the deck's argument carried by a state machine written onto that object. That
recipe has now failed to clear 7.0 three times running. Its failure mode is
consistent and is written down in FIELD_NOTES for 07-29: the object is
technically correct and reads as one uniform mass, because the craft went into
the model and not into the FRAME AROUND the model. Detail concentrated in one
600px object leaves the other three quarters of the slide to hairlines and
plates, which is exactly what the scorer keeps naming.

So this run does not build a better object. It changes what carries the craft:

1. **Detail is distributed, not concentrated.** Every slide must hold genuine
   modelled tone in at least three of the four quadrants, not one hero mass on
   bare ground. The dossier states, per slide, what fills each quadrant.
2. **The line voice is NOT drafting-instrument.** Three decks running have used
   dimension calls, leader discipline, phantom dash kits, datum triangles and
   corner readouts. That vocabulary is retired for this run. Whatever replaces
   it must come from the story's own material world.
3. **No single-object hero.** The hero slide is a FIELD, a place, or a system
   with foreground, midground and background, so atmospheric perspective,
   overlap and depth-of-field can actually do work (DESIGN_DOCTRINE 4 cues
   1, 2, 4). A deck whose depth lives in one object cannot use half the stack.

### The recurring hard fail, closed at the plan stage

`text against geometry` has hard-failed 2 of the last 10 runs (07-25, 07-29).
Cause is known and is instinct 0.98: qa.py's collision check is DOM-only, so a
label positioned against Canvas or SVG geometry collides freely behind a green
gate. 2026-07-29 shipped two fixes (render.py `svg_plates` measurement, and
`AK.svgPlate` sizing plates from the measured string). Those close the SVG-plate
case. They do NOT close the CANVAS case, which is where 07-29's own hard fail
lived.

This run's rule, stated before any code is written: **no primary text sits over
canvas-drawn geometry without a declared, measured protection.** Either the
composition routes a quiet zone under the text (preferred, doctrine 3), or the
text sits on measured furniture built by `AK.svgPlate`. Every dossier names,
per text block, which of the two it uses. Phase 12 will consider whether the
canvas case can be measured the way the SVG case now is.
