The FCC cleared GCI's Arctic fiber deal partly by citing Starlink, a rival the record can't agree sells wholesale middle mile in Alaska.

The line is 1,176 miles of subsea fiber from Prudhoe Bay to Nome.

The check is a hedge. The FCC found nothing to suggest Starlink couldn't sell wholesale service "regardless of whether" it does today.

Sea ice cut the line near Oliktok Point in January 2025. The outage ran seven to nine months by differing accounts.

The real check is on paper. Contracts over $5,000 a month per circuit go to the FCC under seal.

Pictured are the route, ACS's objection, the 2025 cut and GCI's conditions. The line ends at Prudhoe Bay, where a gas plant is pitched for AI data centers the order never mentions.

GCI's wholesale filings end seven years after closing. Will Starlink sell wholesale middle mile on that coast by then?
