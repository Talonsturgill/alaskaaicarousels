# STORYBOARD, run No.101, October 31st: REGARDLESS OF WHETHER

## THESIS
The FCC let GCI buy Quintillion, the subsea fiber along Alaska's Arctic coast, and its competition check is a sentence it hedged twice: the record disagrees on whether Starlink sells wholesale middle mile service in Alaska today, and the FCC approved "regardless of whether" (C18, C19). What the 13 planned communities on that line can actually hold GCI to is the written conditions (C24 to C33), and the larger contracts filed under them go to the FCC under seal (C33).
PDF title (60 chars or fewer): `Regardless of Whether, the FCC's Check on Arctic Fiber`
