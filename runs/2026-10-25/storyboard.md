# STORYBOARD, No.96, 2026-10-25, THE SOUTH-UP SHEET

## THESIS
On October 21st the USGS counted a mean 96 trillion cubic feet of undiscovered conventional gas on the North Slope, and on the same page wrote that as of 2026 "there is no method for bringing natural gas to markets at large commercial scales". Until a pipe exists, the only buyers planned on the Slope are the ones that come to the gas, and both of them are AI computing: STAK Energy's lease 26 miles south of Deadhorse and North Slope Power's 3 to 10 gigawatts near Prudhoe Bay. So first claim on North Slope gas is being settled by a state land lease and a borough venture. Position taken: the Legislature should decide who gets it first, before the leases do. PDF title: "96 Trillion Cubic Feet, No Way to Market" (copy.json carries the final one).

UPDATE of No.78 (2026-10-05, "More than three Alaskas of power, planned for Prudhoe Bay."). Material new since No.78: the USGS province-wide assessment and its own statement that the gas has no route to market (October 21st), STAK Energy's lease beside it, and Alaska LNG still without a final investment decision. The cover says UPDATE.
