# SELECTION, run 2026-10-25, No.96

## The story
UPDATE of No.78 (October 5th, "More than three Alaskas of power, planned for Prudhoe Bay."). On October 21st the USGS published Fact Sheet 2026-3031 (C01, C13): a mean of 96 trillion cubic feet of undiscovered, technically recoverable conventional gas in the Northern Alaska Province (C01), a range of 17,326 to 276,681 billion cubic feet at F95 to F5 (C02, C03), counted in 21 assessment units in three stratigraphic sets (C06), from the Brooks Range front to about 3 miles offshore (C07), on top of roughly 35 tcf of proven reserves (C12) and a Prudhoe Bay gas cap of about 25 tcf (C11). The same fact sheet says that as of 2026 "there is no method for bringing natural gas to markets at large commercial scales" (C09), and most gas produced with oil is pumped back underground (C10). The only buyers planned for the Slope itself are AI computing: STAK Energy's 715.4-acre, 50-year lease 26 miles south of Deadhorse for a gas-powered facility with modular high-performance computing units (C18 to C21), a gigawatt or more per the Anchorage Press (C24); North Slope Power's 3 GW first phase, 10 GW in all, for colocated AI data centers near Prudhoe Bay (C27 to C30). Alaska LNG, the 800-mile pipeline to Cook Inlet (C32), still has no final investment decision (C35), whatever the president calls it (C31).

Material new development since No.78: the USGS province-wide number and the USGS's own statement that the gas has no route to market. No.78 sized one plant against Alaska's fleet; this deck is about the fuel under both North Slope plans and who reaches it first.

## Position
The 96 tcf headline is being read as a reason to build a pipeline. Read the fact sheet's next sentence and it is just as much a reason to build on the Slope: a data center at the wellhead is a buyer that comes to the gas, and two are already filed or planned. Alaska is choosing who gets first claim on North Slope gas through land leases and borough ventures, without ever voting on it. Say so before the leases decide it.
