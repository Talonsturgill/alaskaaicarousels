# STORYBOARD, No.100, 2026-10-30, THE UNIT LADDER

## THESIS
On one South Anchorage ballot the two candidates' ideas of a right-sized Alaska data center sit 30 to 60 times apart, Rep. Ky
Holland's 50 to 100 MW against the 3 GW that ADN uses for the project of John Boyle's employer, STAK Energy. Every line Alaska
has actually drawn sits far below both (UAF's 100 kW unit, Fairbanks' proposed 5 MW, SB 250's 15 MW), and the one statewide line
that passed a chamber was held in the House. Tuesday seats one of the people who would start that line again. Structural, never
partisan: no endorsement, no ranking, no implied impropriety.
PDF document title: `One Unit, Counted Up. Alaska's Data Center Scale Ladder` (55 chars)
