House District 9 runs from South Anchorage to Whittier. Its data center debate reaches a proposed lease 26 miles south of Deadhorse.

Independent Rep. Ky Holland wants data centers of 50 to 100 MW. Republican John Boyle, STAK Energy's chief strategy officer, is open to big ones on the North Slope. ADN describes STAK's project as 3 gigawatts, 30 times Holland's top size.

Alaska's own lines sit far lower. On May 16th the Senate passed SB 250's 15 MW line. On May 18th a House committee held it. Fairbanks' borough mayor now proposes a 5 MW permit pause. District 9 votes Tuesday, November 3rd.

A box of about 100 kW is the unit, stacked to every size on record, with maps of District 9 and the Slope lease.

Both candidates say a big customer could spread energy costs among more users. Would you want one on your grid at 100 MW, and at a gigawatt?
