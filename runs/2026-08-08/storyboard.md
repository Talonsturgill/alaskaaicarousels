# STORYBOARD — Alaska.Ai Carousel No. 29 — 2026-08-08

## THE SYNTHESIS, AND WHY

Three directors pitched: cartographer, cinematographer, historian-of-the-future.
All three were strong enough to build. The **cinematographer wins the chassis**,
and the reason is specific rather than aesthetic.

Its central device makes the thesis a RENDERED, MEASURED fact instead of an
illustrated one. Slide 05 and slide 06 are the same camera, the same scene
function, the same fish at the same world position. Between them the key light
falls from 8:1 to 1:1. Nothing about the object breaks. Only the light that
makes it legible is gone. A grant officer looking at frame 06 sees nothing to
fix, which is exactly Feldpausch's complaint, and the deck never has to say so.

Then it goes further, and this is what won it. Because the argument is light, it
is a number, so it becomes a BUILD GATE. Slide 05 must measure at least 40 L*
between the slot core and the wall shadow. Slide 06 must measure 12 L* or less.
The deck's central claim cannot ship unproven, because the render fails loudly
if the light did not do what the argument says it did.

That is the correct answer to this run's standing weakness. Artwork craft has
been the weakest criterion in 7 of 10 runs, and the named recurring failure is
RENDERED LADDER DECLARED AND NOT REACHED, four decks in six. A dossier that
asserts a rung is exactly what failed four times. A dossier that gates the rung
on a measured pixel separation cannot fail the same way.

### WHAT THE ROOM AGREED ON WITHOUT CONFERRING

Four independent convergences across three blind pitches. Treated as settled:

1. **C13, the 80.2 percent mean average precision, is struck from the entire
   deck.** All three directors reached this independently and for the same
   reason. The hard rule says the two accuracy figures may never meet. An
   editorial rule that depends on nobody making a mistake is weaker than one
   that removes the material. If the number is never rendered, the collision is
   structurally impossible rather than carefully avoided. The cost is real and
   it is being paid on purpose, since C13 is the only published checkable metric
   in the package. C22, the operator's self-report, appears alone on slide 08,
   in italic, attributed, and labelled as not a published validation.
2. **Instrument Serif + Archivo + JetBrains Mono.** Fraunces is out this run.
3. **aksdf over akthree**, for the same structural reason each time: there is no
   GL context to fail, therefore no fallback to silently slide into.
4. **A tannin-water palette** drawn from the story's material world.

### WHAT WAS GRAFTED FROM THE LOSING PITCHES

**From the historian, THE ITALIC IS THE UNVERIFIED.** Instrument Serif Italic is
reserved exclusively for the epistemically soft register and never touches a
documented figure. Only three things in this deck are italic: C26's roughly 800
fish (a recollection the speaker himself hedged with "I think"), C12's 40,000
projection, and C22's 95 percent self-report. A pixel critic can audit the whole
deck's epistemic status in one pass. This is cheap, checkable, and it converts
an editorial rule into a visual one, which is the same move that won the room.

**From the historian, GOLD MARKS MONEY THAT EXISTS**, with a declared px-squared
budget per slide and the key printed once in mono. The cinematographer already
starved slide 06 of gold deliberately; making the rule explicit lets a critic
check it.

**From the cartographer, WIDTH ENCODES DOMAIN.** Archivo at wdth 62 is the
compressed institutional voice, wdth 100 is the body voice. The institutions are
narrow and clipped, the human sentences are not.

**The cartographer's own strongest image was dropped on its own advice.** Its
slide 07 drew an INVENTED grant-eligibility surface in the visual grammar of
MEASURED survey data, with hachures, streamlines and a divide line. It flagged
that hazard itself and offered to rebuild. It is right, and the risk peaks at
exactly the point where the argument peaks, so it is out. The cinematographer's
light argument carries the same claim with no fake dataset anywhere.

### THE CINEMATOGRAPHER'S OWN TWO WORRIES, ANSWERED

**"The key light is a metaphor, and metaphors can be argued with."** Real. The
mitigation is that the pair is never asked to work alone. Slide 06 carries the
deck's second-largest type stating the argument in words, the shot log prints
`KEY 8 TO 1` then `KEY 1 TO 1` and `DOLLY 0.00 M`, and field 11a declares the
wordless claim so the pixel critics must answer `encoding_reads` on it directly.
If the critics say it reads as one underexposed picture rather than one argument
in two frames, the fix is more separation in the light rig, not more words.

**"Three raymarched frames off one scene function is concentration risk."** Also
real, and it is the one thing that could trip `bespoke_check`. Run 2026-08-05
shipped nine frames at 0.940 median pairwise similarity and wrote the excuse
into its own storyboard. The arithmetic here is different and it is worth
stating rather than assuming: 9 slides make 36 pairs, and only 3 of those pairs
(01 to 05, 01 to 06, 05 to 06) share the scene function. A median over 36 values
is not moved by 3 outliers, and the other six slides have entirely independent
drawing code. The gate is run before the deck is reviewed, not after, and if the
median clears 0.60 the deck is rebuilt rather than argued for.

## THE RENDERED LADDER, AND THE EVIDENCE THAT PROVES IT

**Rung 2, `aksdf` CPU sphere-tracing, TECHNIQUE #88.** Chosen because there is no
GL context to fail and therefore no fallback to slide into. This deck uses
`akthree` nowhere, so there is no `webglOK()` branch and no black-frame sentinel
that can false-negative. aksdf's only degradation path is `deadlineMs` dropping
soft-shadow rays to AO, and an AO-only raymarch is still a raymarch.

Stack per hero frame: primitives, `opSub` for the tunnel, quadratic `smin` on the
fish body, fbm thin-shell displacement on the bed (`|d| < 0.12` only),
`opRepXZ` pickets clamped by `opInter`, tetrahedral normals, 5-tap AO applied to
sky and indirect ONLY and never the key term, single soft-shadow ray `k = 14`,
fresnel rim, two-tone warm-key cool-shadow ramp, ACES, gamma. Internal 480x720
for 06, 440x660 for 01 and 05. `maxSteps 96, eps 0.0015, deadlineMs 12000,
seed 20260808`. One shared `scene(p)` used by all three frames.

**ORDER OF OPERATIONS, WRITTEN AS A GATE.** 2D atmosphere first (mesh wash, exp2
fog band), then `AKPOST.grade(ctx)` at about 0.9 s, and only then
`AKSDF.render` compositing into its box. Grading AFTER a composite costs about
34 s and is what forced two historical fallbacks. aksdf already emits ACES and
gamma, so the raymarched region takes only a local IGN dither and the shared
`AK.grainTile(280, 52, 20260808)` at 0.07 overlay.

**THE EVIDENCE, defined before any slide is coded.**

1. **Pre-build proof.** Render `scene(p)` standalone at 240x360 with
   `deadlineMs 4000` and print wall-clock ms. Gate: it completes, and the slide
   06 camera renders under 5 s at that resolution. Only then is a slide file
   written.
2. **Declared rung.** Each hero slide sets
   `window.__akHero = {rung:'aksdf', scene:'redoubt-chute', internal:[w,h],
   primitives:9, degraded:<bool>, ms:<measured>}`, so a pixel critic reads a
   number instead of trusting the dossier. A missing field means the slide fails
   its own acceptance check and is rebuilt, not shipped. `degraded:true` still
   ships and is recorded honestly in the ledger.
3. **Three luminance probes**, sampled from the ImageData before upscale and
   published to `window.__akProbes`: `slot_core`, `wall_shadow`, `open_water`.
   - Slide 05 gate: `slot_core - wall_shadow >= 40 L*`.
   - Slide 06 gate: `slot_core - wall_shadow <= 12 L*`.
   - Both: `open_water < 12 L*`.
   **The falling key is therefore a measured quantity and the deck's central
   argument is a build gate.**
4. **Geometry identity.** Slides 05 and 06 import the same camera constant
   object. A diff of the two slide files must show changes only inside the light
   rig block. That diff is the proof of thesis and it is one command.

Target drawn share 65 percent against bespoke_check's 45 percent floor.
