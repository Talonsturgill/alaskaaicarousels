## BEAT C, arrived after the selection was made

Beat C returned last and could not change the Phase 4 call, but its lead is the
strongest thing in the sweep that is not the winner or the runner-up.

**The Fisheries Science Modernization Act, introduced August 6th 2026** by
Murkowski and Whitehouse, with Sullivan cosponsoring, six cosponsors in total.
It would create a NOAA environmental-DNA stock assessment pilot, amend
Magnuson-Stevens to fold eDNA into fisheries research, and stand up a National
Aquatic Biomolecular Coordination Network. The AI hook is explicit and in the
bill's own capacity title, which directs NOAA to establish procurement pathways
encouraging **AI-enabled autonomous sampling systems**. Five of the twelve
endorsing organizations are Alaska fishing or ocean-observing bodies.
PRIMARY: https://www.murkowski.senate.gov/press/release/murkowski-and-colleagues-work-to-modernize-fisheries-science

This also closes a hole Beat B left open. Beat B tried the Murkowski newsroom,
got 410 on two paths, and dropped the story rather than guess. Beat C found the
working path. Two scouts disagreeing about whether a source exists is exactly
what the parallel sweep is for.

It was not selected because a bill introduced two days ago is a weak peg. Most
bills die, there is no deadline, and there is nothing a reader can do about it
this month. It is queued.
