# TREATMENT B — HISTORIAN OF THE FUTURE (summary held by the showrunner)

## THE OBJECT

`NEW: THE RAGGED ANNULUS`. The record drawn as TERRAIN, not as a timeline. A
sphere-traced ground of concentric growth ridges under a 22 degree raking key,
each ridge a documented year at Redoubt, labelled with mono year tags on
drafting leaders. No table, no plinth, no product camera. Scale deliberately
ambiguous, it could be three millimetres or three kilometres, and that ambiguity
is the historian's posture.

**The record is ragged at both ends, and that is the deck's credibility.** The
inner edge is a hedged recollection (C26). The outer edge is an unfinished
season (C11, C12) followed by bare unlit ground where nobody is funded to cut
the next ring (C19, C20, C21). The solid documented middle is exactly three
ridges (C32, C32, C31). A three-ridge archive honestly drawn is a more
devastating object than a fifty-year staircase.

## THE BEST MAPPING IN EITHER TREATMENT

**The broken outer ring is drawn at exactly 70.1 degrees**, because 7,791
counted by June 29th against ADF and G's projection above 40,000 is
7791/40000 of a full turn. Then a lit cut face, then bare ground. **The season's
incompleteness IS the ring's incompleteness.** The remaining 289.9 degrees is
unlit and carries NO phantom dash, because a projection is not a drawn future.

This is DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 6.3 executed exactly. The generative system's
own parameter IS a story number, and a critic can check it with a protractor.

## THE DISCIPLINE IT VOLUNTEERED

It refused to call the object a fish scale or an otolith anywhere, because that
claim is not in claims.json. The object is captioned as what it is, "the count
at Redoubt Lake, drawn as one object". The resemblance does its work without a
factual assertion. That is the house rule applied to the director's own best
idea, against its own interest, unprompted.

## TWO CONTINUITY DEVICES WORTH TAKING WHOLE

**`THE ITALIC IS THE UNVERIFIED`.** Instrument Serif Italic is reserved
exclusively for the epistemically soft register. Recollections, projections and
self-reports. It never touches a documented figure. Roman plus mono equals
documented. Only three figures in the deck are italic, C26's 800, C12's 40,000
projection and C22's 95 percent self-report. **A pixel critic can audit the
deck's epistemic status in one pass.** Cheap, checkable, and it turns an
editorial rule into a visual one.

**`GOLD MARKS MONEY THAT EXISTS`**, with a measured px-squared budget per slide
and zero on every slide where no money is attached. Gold peaks once, on the
$200,000 install slab stamped TO INSTALL, and pools only in the fractured ground
on the synthesis slide. The key is printed once, small, in mono.

## THE SAFER VERSION OF THE ARGUMENT SLIDE

Where the cartographer draws an invented eligibility SURFACE in the grammar of
measured survey data (and flagged that hazard itself), the historian draws two
GROUND CONDITIONS on one lit terrain. Fractured ground on the left with lit
crack walls where gold has pooled to a specular meniscus. Intact smooth ground
on the right where gold finds no purchase and there is none.

Same argument. Physical metaphor rather than fake dataset. No hachures, no
streamlines, no divide line, so nothing says survey to a reader. This is the
version that ships.

## CONVERGENCE WITH THE CARTOGRAPHER, UNPROMPTED

Both directors, working in parallel and blind to each other, independently:

1. Struck **C13's 80.2 percent from the entire deck**, on the same reasoning,
   that two numbers cannot meet if only one of them exists.
2. Chose **Instrument Serif + Archivo + JetBrains Mono** as the type pairing.
3. Built a **tannin-water palette** from the story's material world.
4. Chose **aksdf** over akthree for the same structural reason, that it has no
   GL context to fail and therefore no fallback to slide into.

Four independent agreements is the strongest signal the room produced.

## EVIDENCE CONTRACT

`window.__akRender = {bench:'aksdf', key, internalW, internalH, primitives,
steps, aoTaps, shadowK, elapsedMs, degraded}`. Absent field means the slide
fails its own acceptance check. `degraded:true` still passes and is recorded
honestly in the ledger.

Plus a **ridge swell transect**: a declared 3px radial transect across the 2024
ridge. A raymarched swelled ridge under a 22 degree key produces an asymmetric
luminance profile with peak-to-trough at least 22 L*. A Canvas 2D concentric
stroke fallback produces a flat or symmetric profile. The number is asserted
before the render and the pixel critic is invited to contradict it. That is a
falsifiable test that a fallback silently shipped, which is exactly the defect
this run is attacking.

## SELF-CRITIQUE IT VOLUNTEERED

1. The hero may not read as a RECORD at 432px, and if it does not, the chassis
   is just a texture. The concept is partly carried by 2D annotation rather than
   by the raymarch, which is the very gap between what a dossier claims and what
   a render delivers. Its own remedy, stated in advance, is fewer ridges and
   larger tags rather than more grade.
2. Register breaks on slides 02 and 04 leave the record entirely, so a fast
   swiper may read the deck as an ordinary explainer until slide 05.
