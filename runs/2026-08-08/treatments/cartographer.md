# TREATMENT A — CARTOGRAPHER LENS (summary held by the showrunner)

Full pitch received. Recorded here in condensed form; the synthesis and the
reasoning for what was taken and what was cut live in storyboard.md.

## THE MOVE THAT WON THE ROOM'S RESPECT

Asked how a map of one weir escapes being a dot, it answered with THREE NESTED
CATCHMENTS at three scales, and made the third one the argument.

- Water has a catchment. One notch on Baranof Island.
- Harvest has a catchment. About 35 percent of Southeast's subsistence sockeye
  converges on that notch (C33).
- **Money has a catchment too.** Grant money runs downhill into damage. Redoubt
  sits on high ground, so nothing flows to it. **A working system is a
  topographic high in the funding surface.**

That last line is the deck. It is a claim only cartography can draw.

## NAMED SYSTEMS

- HERO: `NEW: THE ONE-APERTURE BASIN`. A sphere-traced watershed lip carved so
  the whole mass drains through one lit notch, shot from water level, camera
  climbing across the deck. A landform, wet, moving. Diverges from four
  consecutive rigid-object-under-fixed-camera heroes in letter and spirit.
- ATMOSPHERE: `NEW: THE TANNIN BOUNCE`. Southeast overcast. Cold key from
  above-left at elevation 24 degrees, warm fill from BELOW, bounced off
  tea-stained lake water. Inverts the house warm-key/cool-shadow norm, and it is
  what a tannin lake under a marine layer physically does.
- HOOK: `NEW: THE DISQUALIFYING VIRTUE`. The cover states that the property
  making the thing valuable is the property making it ineligible.
- PALETTE: `TANNIN WATER AND WHITE POLY`. Base #05101A, water #14303A, tannin
  #1E1206 to #B98A45, spruce relief #2E5A52, sockeye red #C6412C locked to
  harvest quantities only, white polyethylene #F2F6F4 as the deck's ONLY pure
  white on one object, gold #FFC72C one mark per slide.
- TYPE: Instrument Serif + Archivo + JetBrains Mono, with **WIDTH ENCODING
  DOMAIN**. Physical geography labels at Archivo wdth 62. Institutional labels
  at wdth 118. The institutions are wide, the country is narrow.

## CONTINUITY

- `NEW: THE MIGRATING DIVIDE`. One ticked cartographic boundary crosses every
  slide and its MEANING migrates from hydrology to harvest to money. Every state
  change is a shape change, never a brightness change.
- `NEW: THE SCALE THAT CHANGES UNITS`. A checkerboard scale bar in a fixed
  position whose unit climbs with the camera, then stops measuring distance and
  admits it (`NO SCALE. ELIGIBILITY, NOT DISTANCE.`), then returns to 300 km.
- Edge-tease cut by the right edge on 01, 03, 04, 05, 07.

## THE DECISION THE SHOWRUNNER IS ADOPTING WHOLE

**C13, the 80.2 percent mean average precision, DOES NOT APPEAR ANYWHERE IN THE
DECK.** Not on a slide, not in the caption. If the number is never rendered, the
two incommensurable accuracy figures can never meet, cannot be averaged and
cannot validate each other. The director paid the cost deliberately and named
it: the deck gives up the strongest published metric in the package to make the
collision structurally impossible rather than editorially avoided.

This is the right call. An editorial rule that depends on nobody making a
mistake is weaker than a rule that removes the material.

## THE EVIDENCE CONTRACT FOR THE RENDERED LADDER

The reason this treatment answers the run's standing weakness rather than
gesturing at it. Declared BEFORE the build and checkable from the render alone:

1. Five named pixel probes written to `window.__akHeroProbe` and sampled from
   the ART canvas. Declared expected separations, lit rock lip minus shadowed
   water at least 12 L*, boulder contact shadow minus lit water ground at least
   6 L*. **If the probe object is absent, the ladder claim is FALSE and the
   slide is rebuilt rather than shipped.**
2. `__akHeroProbe.ms` and `.degraded`. If the deadline dropped shadow rays, the
   shipped notes say so in plain words. A degraded hero claiming a full one is
   the exact lie this run is attacking.
3. Field 11a wordless claim declared with regions.
4. Determinism, rendered twice and hash-compared.

## SELF-CRITIQUE IT VOLUNTEERED, AND IT IS RIGHT

1. Slide 07 draws an INVENTED eligibility surface in the visual grammar of
   MEASURED data, at exactly the point where the argument peaks. Hachures,
   streamlines and a divide line all say survey to a reader. Its mitigation is a
   mono stamp, and a stamp is a weaker instrument than the picture it qualifies.
   It offered to rebuild the slide in an obviously diagrammatic register and
   accept a less beautiful frame.
2. The deck changes register four times and asks a 20px mono scale bar to hold
   it together at 432px, where that size is at the legibility floor.

Both are real. Both are addressed in the synthesis.
