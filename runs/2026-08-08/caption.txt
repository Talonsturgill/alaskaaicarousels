Solar panels and a Starlink system on a lagoon float run an AI salmon counter at Redoubt Lake, 15 miles by boat from Sitka.

Fish and Game put the Redoubt annual subsistence limit at 100 sockeye per household effective July 1st. Jeff Feldpausch of the Sitka Tribe told KCAW it was 50 before, and that the weir is why.

A federal grant titled "Artificial Intelligence for Subsistence Salmon Monitoring and Management" paid $200,000 to install the system. That was installation money. Feldpausch told KCAW there is no funding for the weir right now. Every grant source the Tribe found deals with systems needing remediation.

The deck has the grant title in full, weir counts from 2023 to 2025, and the accuracy number he gave the radio.

What would this weir have to break before a grant program could see it?
