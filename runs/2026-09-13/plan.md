## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS

**Artwork craft and genuine detail**, weakest in 6 of the last 10 runs, mean 6.8.

Read as a prediction rather than a report: unless this run builds differently,
today's deck scores about 6.8 on artwork craft too.

The three defect classes under it are one defect wearing three hats. "Canvas
mark near reserved text" (5 runs), "art touching glyphs" (4 runs) and "busy art
under text" (3 runs) all say the same thing, which is that type is placed onto
art that was drawn without knowing the type was coming. The repair has been
attempted at Phase 8 for six runs running, where it always costs a round and
always produces the fourth hat, "top-loaded composition", because the cheapest
way to clear a text collision late is to push the art up.

**What this run does differently, decided here and binding on Phase 5:**

1. **THE TYPE WELL IS DRAWN FIRST AND IS PART OF THE PICTURE.** Every dossier
   declares a type well as a world-space region of the art itself, not as a
   plate laid over it, and the drawing loop READS that region. Marks do not
   stop at its edge, they THIN into it by a declared density ramp, so the well
   is a place where the material is sparse rather than a hole where the
   material is absent. That is a compositional decision with a name, and it is
   checked at Phase 8 by whether the well reads as part of the material.
2. **THREE DECLARED SCALES OF DETAIL PER FRAME, each with its own generator.**
   Macro form (the thing you see at 432px), mid structure (what resolves at
   about 1080px), micro texture (what rewards a zoom). A frame that names only
   two is incomplete and gets fixed at the dossier, which is where it costs a
   paragraph. "Genuine detail in every region" is the criterion's own wording
   and it has been failing because runs generate one scale well and fake the
   other two with grain.
3. **NOTAN BEFORE PIXELS.** Every dossier states its intended three-mass value
   structure, which mass dominates, and where the dominant mass reaches the
   bottom third. `scripts/value_structure.py` measures exactly this off the
   render, so the plan and the measurement speak the same language and a
   disagreement is actionable rather than a matter of taste. Run it on every
   frame in Phase 8 before the critics, not after them.
4. **VISUAL DENSITY DIAL AT 5.** The dial is the instrument for this criterion
   and recent runs have sat at 2 to 4. Density 5 with the type well drawn in is
   the combination that has not been tried; density 5 with type dropped on top
   is how "busy art under text" happens, which is why point 1 comes first.

   feTurbulence rect.
