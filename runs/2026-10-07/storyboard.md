# STORYBOARD, Carousel No.80, run 2026-10-07
# "THE CORRIDOR SURVEY": the second wire's passenger list, and who is not on it

## THESIS
The Energy Department will put up to $150 million into a second Railbelt wire, Beluga to Healy, and names the military and mining as the reasons. On the same Railbelt bases the Air Force has offered land for AI data centers, and the bases' own senator says the energy base can't carry them yet. The release never says data center. The deck's position: the line's planning should say on the record whether data center load rides it, now, while the federal money goes to engineering, planning and permitting and no route is drawn. Setting the records side by side is the deck's own reading and the deck says so (08).
PDF title: `Alaska's second wire has a passenger list` (41 chars).
