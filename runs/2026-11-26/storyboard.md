## DECK HEADER

- **Thesis.** On November 10th the Fairbanks North Star Borough Planning Commission sent the data center pause, Ordinance 2026-42, to the Assembly 11-0 with five amendments (C01, C08, C03 to C07). Two dates moved: the end, from December 31st, 2027 to June 30th, 2028 (C23, C06), and the study, from six months to ten (C25, C07). The end is now a fixed date, while the start waits for the Assembly's vote (C26, C40). Six months were added and four went to the study, so the time left to write actual rules after the report grew by about two months, whatever day the Assembly adopts it (aggregate). If it adopts on January 14th, that window is about seven and a half months (aggregate on C40, C26, C07, C06). Position: the number to keep is the rules window, not the 18 months in the headlines, and every week of delay comes out of it.
- **Document title (PDF, <= 60 chars):** `Fairbanks' data center pause, its fixed end and open start` (59).
- **UPDATE.** This is an UPDATE of No.108 (November 7th), which previewed this hearing and staff's two amendments. Material new development: the 11-0 recommendation and the three amendments the commission added itself (water quality, the June 30th, 2028 end, the ten-month study), from the clerk's action memorandum. The cover kicker says UPDATE.
