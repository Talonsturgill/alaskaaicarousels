# THE DIRECTORS ROOM, AND WHAT THE SHOWRUNNER TOOK

Three lenses, chosen as a trio that has not run together and that is not
No.64's (cartographer, systems-illustrator, field-documentarian):
**cinematographer**, **data-journalist**, **editorial-essayist**. All three
returned complete treatments with honest self-critiques, and the self-critiques
decided this.

## The three pitches, in one line each

- **CINEMATOGRAPHER, "THE WATERLINE HOLD".** One survey camera on a dome port
  holds station at the surface of Upper Cook Inlet off the East Foreland for
  all nine frames while the flood tide runs past it. The frame IS the
  meniscus. Carried one rule that no other pitch had: **MATERIAL IS REAL,
  LINE IS PROPOSED.**
- **DATA-JOURNALIST, "THE PROGRESSIVE PROOF".** One sheet of dark rag under a
  raking key, additive relief, nine pulls. Its idea was that **impression
  state encodes provenance**: full press with ink means the federal notice
  says it, half press means one newspaper says it, a blind emboss means the
  notice does not say it and the absence is verified, and an unpressed phantom
  means only a party to the dispute says it.
- **EDITORIAL-ESSAYIST, "THE BITE".** Also paper, also relief, with bite depth
  as the only variable and the sheet's deckle edge cut to the true East
  Foreland coastline. Brought the sharpest thesis and the title.

## What the showrunner took, and why

**THE WINNER IS THE WATERLINE HOLD**, and the deciding argument is one both
paper directors made against themselves. The data-journalist wrote: "a deck
about paper and ink is one abstraction away from Alaska. There is no water in
it, no coastline, no Nikiski, no East Foreland." The essayist wrote: "there is
no Alaska in the picture ... a scorer who wants place is entitled to say the
deck gave them a stationery catalogue instead." Two of three pitches converged
on relief-on-paper, which is a real signal about the material this argument
wants, and both independently identified the same fatal cost. Alaska
authenticity is a scored criterion and this is a story about a specific piece
of seabed. The deck that has the inlet in it wins.

It also wins on this run's stated purpose. `plan.md` named artwork craft as
the one standing weakness this run would attack, weakest in 7 of the last 10
runs at a mean of 6.8, and committed the deck to being built from a MATERIAL
rather than an instrument. Turbid glacial water under a high overcast is a
medium that behaves, and it gives all three grain scales honestly. Two decks
of pressed paper would have been two more drafted surfaces.

**THE FIRST GRAFT, and it is the best single idea the room produced.** The
data-journalist's IMPRESSION STATE is taken whole and translated out of paper
and into this deck's medium as **THE RENDER STATE**. It fuses with the
cinematographer's own rule and extends it from "does this thing exist" to
"how well is it sourced", which is a question this deck has to answer nine
times:

| state | what it means | how it is drawn |
|---|---|---|
| **MATERIAL** | verified against the primary federal notice | a lit PBR or modelled surface, with a cast shadow, a specular and a contact |
| **HALF MATERIAL** | verified, but to ONE newspaper article | modelled but matte, roughness forced to 0.95, no specular, contact at half depth |
| **PHANTOM** | proposed, described in the notice, does not exist | unlit drafting dash, casts nothing, receives nothing, occluded by silt |
| **VOID** | the notice does not say, and the absence is itself verified | a hole in the silt with a lit rim and nothing inside it |

A pixel critic who finds one lit turbine anywhere in this deck has found a
defect, and the fix is a rebuild rather than a caption. That is a checkable
gate rather than a promise, which is why it survived synthesis.

**THE SECOND GRAFT.** The essayist's title, **"Priority, Not Permission"**,
which is FERC's own distinction compressed to two words, and its hook
archetype **NEW: THE STATE OF MATTER**, which is the sharpest cover move
offered: name the massive object the reader already pictures at full scale,
then state in one flat declarative what substance it is presently made of.

**THE THIRD GRAFT.** Both paper directors built a frame out of what the record
does NOT say, and both were right that it is the keepable frame. The
cinematographer's keepable was a docket staff carrying the timeline. This deck
takes both and puts them on one frame, slide 06, because the frame a
professional saves before commenting on a docket is the one that holds the
specification AND the holes in it.

**WHAT THE SHOWRUNNER OVERRODE.** The cinematographer's own self-critique
named slide 05 as a dead frame and offered to cut it. Taken. It is replaced by
the absences graft, and the breather moves to slide 04, where the camera goes
fully under and the phantom array dissolves into extinction. That frame is
simultaneously the quietest in the deck and the one carrying its thesis, which
is what a breather should be and rarely is.

The cinematographer also proposed splitting the spine into a composition
channel (the meniscus height) and a data channel (a wet mark on a staff), and
flagged the seam itself. **The seam is closed by overriding it.** The staff
holds a FIXED screen position on all nine frames and its waterline sits at
today on all nine. Nothing about the clock is composition. What varies is the
camera.

# DECK HEADER

## Thesis

Nothing is being built on the floor of Cook Inlet and nothing has been
approved, because what DeepGreen asked the federal government for is a place
in line to study a site, which FERC says grants priority and not permission,
and the record that a reader would use to judge it names 66 pods, 330 to 350
turbines and 3.5 miles of cable while saying nothing about how much seabed,
how deep, or for how long.

**PDF document title (23 chars):** `Priority, Not Permission`

1. **COVER.** The state of matter. Cold, wide, opaque. Nothing visibly wrong.
2. **WHAT WAS FILED.** The document itself, dated. Register stays neutral.
3. **THE PROPOSED THING.** The first big visual. Every mark on the frame is
   phantom and the frame says so.
4. **BREATHER, AND THE THESIS.** The camera goes under, the array dissolves
   into extinction, and FERC's own sentence sits on an empty frame.
   Temperature DROPS to its lowest here on purpose, because the deck's
   argument is a deflation and it has to feel like one.
5. **THE NUMBER AS PRINTED.** The units oddity, reported and not corrected.
6. **KEEPABLE.** The record and its holes, in one savable frame.
7. **THE TURN.** The state says nobody called it. The deck's emotional centre.
8. **SYNTHESIS.** The window is open, and it is open to rivals too.
9. **CLOSE.** One ask, one date, one docket.

## Slide count rationale

Nine. The house cadence maps onto this argument with nothing forced. Eight
would cost either the breather at 04 or the keepable at 06, and both are load
bearing: 04 carries the thesis and 06 is the frame a reader saves, which per
this morning's craft refresh is the deck's highest-value property because a
save drives roughly five times the reach of a like and twice that of a
comment. Ten would split the specification across two frames and break the
keepable.

## THE THREE GRAIN SCALES, and this is the run's answer to its standing weakness

`trend_check.py --window 10` puts artwork craft weakest in 7 of 10 runs at a
mean of 6.8. `plan.md` committed this deck to a MATERIAL with grain at three
named scales, and every dossier below names all three by number.

**1. SILHOUETTE, reads at 432 px.** The meniscus. One maximum-contrast
boundary across the full width, `#C9D4D8` air over `#1B2A26` water, with one
near-field mass breaking it. Drawn as a Gerstner-sum surface, three wave
trains at wavelength 3.1 m, 1.4 m and 0.55 m, steepness Q 0.42, 0.30 and 0.18,
direction spread 26 degrees about azimuth 208, then reinforced with a cased
line at 4.5 px `#B6B9AE` over a 1.25 px dark underline so the split survives
the 6x downsample to feed width.

**2. SURFACE STRUCTURE, 150 to 400 design px.** `AK.hachureField`
(TECHNIQUE_LIBRARY 92, `akhachure.js`), whose `height` is REQUIRED and has no
default, which is exactly why it is the right tool: the middle frequency is
forced to be a function of the story's own quantity. The height supplied is
the computed downwelling irradiance field `E = E0 * exp(-k * d)` where `d` is
depth below the meniscus in metres and `k` is the extinction coefficient of
glacial rock flour. Parameters, identical on every slide so the water is one
body: `cell 14, passes 3, sunAz 208, sunEl 34, sunJitter 9, slopeGamma 1.35,
minWidth 0.75, maxWidth 3.2, lenScale 2.1, bend 0.35, lightBias on`. Every
dossier declares three probes, `nearfield`, `midsilt` and `extinction`, whose
mean stroke widths must come back in that order or the field is not carrying
its data.

**3. MICRO-TEXTURE, under 40 px.** The suspension. A population of discrete
particulate motes per frame, hard-edged at 1.0 to 3.5 px, placed by rejection
sampling AGAINST the irradiance field so they are dense in the lit metre and
absent in extinction, each with a 4 to 11 px streak along the flood direction
so a still frame carries current. Discrete steps survive the downsample where
a graded wash reads as nothing. Above the waterline, 60 to 90 beads on the
dome port glass, each a real refracting lens with an inverted highlight.
Finished with `AK.grainTile(280, 52, seed)` at overlay 0.07 and `AKPOST.grade`
with `dither: true, filmic: false`, total texture budget under 10 percent.

**`AKPOST.grade` IS CALLED WITH `filmic: false` ON EVERY SLIDE**, and that is
load bearing rather than a preference. `akpost` defaults `filmic` to true, so a
call that asks only for dither silently runs an ACES curve over the whole frame
and moves every value this run has already measured for contrast, contact and
ink law. No.64 lost nine label-crossing failures in one render to that default.

### 1. THE DECK SHIPPED CPU THROUGHOUT. No frame loads akthree.

The plan put GPU PBR on frames 01, 06 and 09, each with a designed Canvas
fallback. The build took the fallback path on all three and never wrote the
GL path at all, so what shipped is the CPU deck.

The reason is a judgement about risk rather than a discovery about the
engine. The reference deck renders GL fine here. But this deck's whole
argument rests on a MATERIAL, and every one of the three grain scales it
depends on is already CPU: the hachure field whose height is the irradiance
curve, the mote population rejection sampled against the same curve, the
drawn floors, and the finish. GPU PBR would have bought a wave specular and
a wet-steel reflection on three frames and nothing else, at the cost of a
snapshot sentinel, a black-frame retry and a second composition to author per
hero. With nine frames to build in one pass, that trade was not worth taking.

THE COST IS REAL AND IS NOT ARGUED AWAY. DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 4 says a
rendered hero with a graded finish is the craft floor for hero slides, and
this deck does not reach it. Every dossier's field 7 now names what shipped
rather than what was planned, which is why dossier_check went from eight
fails to zero without a single library being added.

verified it in the pixels in round two and asked that it be protected in any

THE RETURN HOLDS TWICE AND THAT IS DELIBERATE, not a leftover. 05 at 490 and 06

frames share a station. Neither can move: on 05 the waterline has to sit in the

between the section header and the first record row. The event at that junction
is not the camera, it is the instrument, which crosses from frame right to frame
left on 06 and back on 07, and it is the only frame in the deck where it does.

### 3. Slide 04's meniscus did not leave the frame.

The dossier said the camera goes fully under and the surface is above the
frame. The build keeps a bright surface rim at y 178, because a frame with no
boundary in it loses the one device that makes the deck one continuous take,
and because the axis has to stay readable enough to declare. The array still
dissolves into extinction exactly as planned, at 1.1 m, with its alpha set by
the extinction curve and nothing else.

### 4. Slide 06 lost its leaders, and its rows became nine elements.

Two changes, both forced by measurement.

The first build ran one declared leader from each void to its label in a
second column. qa.py measured each label 419 to 563 design px from where its
line arrived, against a 32 px tolerance. The contract's own remedy is to move
the label and let position do the joining, so the voids and their words now
share a row and there are no leaders on this frame at all. The dossier's
"one declared leader per void" is superseded and its acceptance checklist
line was rewritten to match.

The second: the nine record rows were one block, which renders as ONE text
node, and the aggregate detector read "PODS 66 TURBINES" across the row
break as a count of 66 turbines. That is a number the deck never asserts. The
rows are now nine separate elements in mono with white-space pre, so each
assertion is its own checkable string and the value column still aligns
exactly. This was not cosmetic.

### 5. Slide 05's argument line changed, at the caption gate.

It read "A megawatt measures power, not a year of it. We print what it
prints." That is FIRST PERSON on a slide, banned house-wide since 2026-08-05,
and caption_check caught it with the `--copy` pass. It now reads "That is the
notice's own wording", which says the same thing about the document instead
of about us.

### 6. The contact declarations were rebuilt around one idiom.

The first build wrote the pool and the cast by hand on five frames and qa.py
failed four of them with the same sentence, "a painted light is sitting on
the declared contact shadow". The pool's radius reached across the gap and lit
the rect the slide had just declared dark. `AKINLET.contactPair` now computes
both from the separation, holds each radius to 0.55 of it, and RETURNS the
exact rects that go into `data-contacts`, so the drawing and the declaration
are the same arithmetic.

Slide 01's pair is the one exception to the side-by-side rule and it is
deliberate. The dome port presses UP into the water from below, so the lit
ground is above the dark mass rather than beside it. Measured off the render
with contact_probe at dL 19.6, well clear of the 4.0 floor and the 8.0
comfort band, with the two rects 54 px apart and inside the 96 px pair span.
qa.py still warns that the ground is painted rather than lit, which is
inherent to the screened-pool idiom the doctrine prescribes, and the warn is
carried rather than argued away.

### 7. The scrim moved to the end of the draw order.

The reserve punch runs on the background pass, so the hachure field and the
motes drawn afterwards went straight back over every reserved box. Four
frames failed "label crossed by art" and "canvas mark inside reserved text"
on exactly that ordering. `AKINLET.scrim` now lays blurred rectangles AFTER
all art and before the grain, which cannot be overdrawn by construction.

### 8. C04 does not print anywhere.

The claims index planned the applicant's own styling of the project name for
slides 02 and 06. Neither frame had room for it once the copy was set to
length. The index now says NOT USED rather than claiming a slide carries it,
and plan_drift_check went from seven drifts to zero on that reconciliation
plus six others, all of which were resolved in the build's favour.

### 9. Numbers that did not change

The axis, 14.237 px per day, day 0 at world y 1180 and day 59 at 340. The
extinction coefficient 1.9 and the 1.1 m vanishing depth. The key at azimuth
208 and elevation 34. The hachure parameters. The palette. The type pairing.
The nine-slide arc and every beat in it.
