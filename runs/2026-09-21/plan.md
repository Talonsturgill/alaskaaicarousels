### THE ONE WEAKNESS THIS RUN DELIBERATELY ATTACKS

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 7 of 10. Mean 6.8 against a
deck mean near 8.6, so it is the single criterion holding the score down, and
it has been for weeks. It is a prediction about today unless this run does
something different.

The diagnosis from reading the last four decks: they are all *instrument*
decks. A machined disc on a bench, an orthographic section with no camera, a
plan-view mapping, a snow fence. Precise, correct, and thin in the middle
frequencies, because a drafted object gives the eye a silhouette and a label
and very little between. The zoom test finds clean geometry and no material.

The attack, committed here so the Phase 5 dossiers have to carry it:

1. **A MATERIAL, NOT AN INSTRUMENT.** The deck's subject gets drawn as a
   physical substance with grain at three scales: the silhouette (read at
   thumb), the surface structure at 150 to 400 design px (read on the swipe),
   and the micro-texture under 40 px (read on the zoom). Every dossier names
   all three by number and says what draws each one.
2. **NO FRAME MAY SPEND MORE THAN 45 PERCENT OF ITS DRAWN MARKS ON
   AXIS-ALIGNED RECTANGLES.** bespoke_check already fails a drawn share under
   45 percent; the dossiers aim at 70 plus by putting the technique library's
   actual bench to work (hachure, stipple, contour, flow field, relief) rather
   than reaching for a fillRect with a gradient in it.
3. **THE LOWER THIRD CARRIES MODELLED TONE ON EVERY FRAME.** dossier_check
   field 4a enforces that it is named; this plan makes it a material
   continuation of the frame above, never a plate or a caption band. The dead
   lower zone is the sub-defect the scorer has named most.
4. **CONTACT SHADOWS ARE MEASURED, NEVER COMPUTED.** contact_probe.py off the
   first render, per slide, before any declaration is written. Two of the last
   ten runs shipped a contact-shadow warn.
5. **COMPOSITION IS NOT TOP-LOADED.** Two of the last ten shipped that warn.
   Every dossier states where the frame's visual mass sits and what holds the
   bottom 40 percent.
