## S1, the Anchorage crime centre, and its AI verdict

The gating question was whether a fetched page DOCUMENTS artificial
intelligence in that system. It does, and the answer is more precise than the
scout's. The Anchorage Daily News states verbatim that patrol cruiser dash cams
run Axon software reading any plate in view and that "Artificial intelligence
deciphers the plate numbers and automatically runs it against databases to
check for stolen vehicles or felony connections", and separately that
"Detectives in the department are using artificial intelligence systems similar
to commercial large language models like ChatGPT and Gemini Notebook to manage
case files", attributed to Chief Sean Case.

What is NOT supported, and what a deck on this story would have had to avoid,
is facial recognition, gunshot detection and Flock cameras, the last directly
contradicted by Case saying "Right now, we don't have Flock safety cameras in
the state of Alaska". Above all, no page says the money buys AI. ADN says the
$598,998 pays for "the audio-visual equipment to make the facilities more
functional". The AI in that story was already deployed and was not what the
Assembly voted on.

Two corrections worth keeping. The vote date of September 3rd is WRONG, that
day is a Thursday, and both outlets say the vote happened Tuesday. And the
McCormick "nationwide backlash" line reads as reporter's prose rather than
attributed speech, so it dies as a quotation.
