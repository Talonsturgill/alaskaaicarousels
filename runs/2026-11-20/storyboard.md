# STORYBOARD, run No.120, 2026-11-20 — THE SEAL IS STILL UP

## DECK HEADER

- **Thesis.** In the outgoing administration's last weeks one department, Alaska DNR, is turning three proposals into land rights. Two are decided (a 50-year North Slope data center lease and about 6,800 acres near Houston for AIDEA), and the largest, Alaska LNG's 800-mile corridor, is the only one the public can still shape before 5:00 p.m. November 25th. Position: the question is not whether the gas line should be built; it is whether a land right that size should be granted in a holiday week by an administration with days left, partly on a case the public can't read.
- **Document title (PDF, <= 60 chars):** `Two land rights decided. One still open.`
- **Arc.** 01 hook (press, lever up, UPDATE) -> 02 payoff (three sheets, two pressed, one circle) -> 03 the room (attributed words, rushed vs expeditiously) -> 04 what conversion is not (seven gates) -> 05 what the yes rests on (sealed envelope) -> 06 breather (Little Troublesome Creek in snow) -> 07 Houston at true scale (the borough's letter) -> 08 the two decided files as paper (1,200 and 2,300 responses) -> 09 keepable calendar -> 10 single ask (the open circle). Temperature: cool and procedural 01 to 05, a quiet cold rest on 06, warmer and denser evidence 07 to 08, the lit keepable peak on 09, a dim graphite close on 10.
