# STORYBOARD, Carousel No.82, run 2026-10-09
# "THE WRONG JOB": Alaska's newest rare-earth AI won't hunt for ore

## THESIS
NSF has put about $6 million (C17) behind a UAA-led plan (C01, C14) to pull rare earth elements out of coal refuse and ash with microbes (C19). Its AI has two jobs, and neither is prospecting: rank which waste is worth feeding to the bacteria (a Virtual Assay, C20, C28), and run the bioreactor on whatever power is available (reinforcement-learning controllers inside a digital twin linked to microgrid simulators, C21, C29, "energy-constrained", C23). The prospecting AI is a different NSF effort (C31, C43). The deck's position: the power budget is the Alaska half of this bet, and it is a plan with an end date of August 31st, 2030 (C06), so it gets graded on that date, not applauded now.
PDF title: `Alaska's Newest Rare-Earth AI Won't Hunt for Ore` (48 chars).
