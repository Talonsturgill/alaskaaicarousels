Drones seeded clouds over Kachemak Bay on August 23rd. On October 20th the Kenai Peninsula Borough's pending seeding rule passed 7 to 0.

In the sky, two drones Rainmaker calls autonomous released 19 flares with about 374 grams of silver iodide.
On the ground, the borough found out from Rainmaker's press release.

In the sky, no state rule applies and no DEC permit was needed.
On the ground, seeding from borough land needs a lease or permit and ten disclosures down to each flare's serial number.

Inside are the night of the flights, the payload, a radar estimate against a rain gauge, each government's own words and the ten-line form.

The borough asked the Legislature to ban cloud seeding or require a permit. Its ten disclosures are the right start. Should a state permit stop at those ten or need a yes from the borough under the flight?
