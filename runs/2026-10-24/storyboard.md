# STORYBOARD, No.95, 2026-10-24, THE TRIPOD ON THE FLATS

## THESIS
On August 23rd Rainmaker Technology flew two drones it calls autonomous into the clouds over the head of Kachemak Bay and released 19 silver iodide flares. On October 20th the Kenai Peninsula Borough Assembly answered 7 to 0 with the only written rule any Alaska government now has for it, KPB 17.10.165, and that rule governs the ground the drones launch from, because the federal law asks only for a report, the state has no framework and requires no permit, and the borough can't fully regulate the air. Position taken: the borough did the one thing in its reach, and the ten-line list it wrote is the right starting point for the Legislature it already asked to ban or permit the practice. PDF title (copy.json carries the final one): "The Sky Has No State Rule. The Ground Does Now."

Not an update: no topic in the last 30 days covers weather modification, Rainmaker or drones over the Kenai (selection.md, dedupe gate).
