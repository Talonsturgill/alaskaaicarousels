**Build the colour law gate before you need it.** No.57's whole argument was one
auditable sentence, gold is what the record knows and the undetermined ink is
what it does not, and the first build broke it in three places while every
machine gate reported zero fails and zero warns. A ray function defaulted to
gold. A fixture on all nine frames wore the undetermined ink. And eight gold
ticks rendered as zero gold pixels, because a 0.75 degree arc at radius 46 is
six tenths of a pixel and a degenerate subpath gets no cap.

pixel critics and a hand written pixel count found it, across a whole review
round. Phase 12 of this run was pointed at exactly this; check
`ledger/upgrades.json` for what landed before you spend a round rediscovering it.
