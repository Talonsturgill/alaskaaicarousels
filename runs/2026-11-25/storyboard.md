## DECK HEADER

- **Thesis.** The Port MacKenzie rail extension is settled: the Alaska Railroad's board gave final approval to the $311 million, 32-mile line (C01, C02), a $249 million federal grant pays most of it (C03) and the Legislature backed it 58-1 (C12). The argument has moved to its two ends. Both are pitched as data center ground, the Houston conveyance as land whose uses "could include an industrial hub or data centers" (C43) and the port, where the Alaska Beacon reports a coal-fired computing data center announced (C13), and neither end has a named data center (C45, C13, C49). AIDEA's share of the match goes back before its board when a recessed meeting resumes Friday, November 27th (C24, C30, C33), inside the governor-elect's request to freeze added spending (C54). Position: the line is fine to fund; the public record should name what it is for before more public money is matched to it.
- **Document title (PDF, <= 60 chars):** `Port MacKenzie's $311 million line, and its unnamed ends` (56).
- **UPDATE.** This is an UPDATE of No.81 (October 8th, the UA lease to Terra at Port MacKenzie) and of the AIDEA Houston thread (No.111, No.120). Material new development: the rail extension's final railroad board approval this week, its $249 million grant (never a deck), and AIDEA's match back on an open agenda Friday. The cover says UPDATE.
