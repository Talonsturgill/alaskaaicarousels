# STORYBOARD, run 2026-11-01, carousel No.102

## THESIS

Every other Alaska data center fight is about gigawatts. Petersburg asked the opposite question, and the right one. Its draft rules test what a data center plugs into, not how big it is. The draft pauses the off-grid case its own sponsor called "probably far-fetched" and leaves the grid-tied kind open to apply, the kind its utility projected would hold rate increases down. The rules that will decide Petersburg's real data center are the ones still being drafted.

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here

Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 7 of the last 10 runs. This deck spends density on modelling: each frame has ONE largest object, named below with its lit face, lee and contact, the type sits on stratus or night that the art routes around (no blurred box under any headline), and the masterful depth frame (01) is built and read first. The two recurring texture causes are designed against: grain only through `AK.grainOn`, every gradient dithered, DEM sampled smoothly and low-passed.
