# SELECTION, 2026-10-20, No.91

## The pick
The world's open speech AI barely hears Central Alaskan Yup'ik. Whisper large-v3 (99 languages), VoxLingua107 and ESPnet OWSM v4 carry no Yupik variety at all; only Meta's largest language-ID model, with 4,017 labels, can say "Yup'ik", and on Yup'ik audio from KYUK's broadcasts it said Central Siberian Yupik more often than Yup'ik. A small head trained on about five hours of that archive lifted Yup'ik recall from 0.41 to 0.92. On Saturday, October 24th, AFN delegates take up resolution 26-20, which asks Congress for permanent, non-competitive, Tribally administered language funding whose allowed uses include technology and archives. The deck's position: the hard part of speech AI for Yup'ik is not the model, it is the labeled hours, and who controls them; that is the question 26-20 puts to Congress.

Sources: AmericasNLP 2026 paper (Brandeis, primary, July 3rd; background evidence), AFN 2026 draft resolutions (primary, September 27th) and agenda (primary, October 16th, in window), the convention itself October 22nd to 24th (forward).
