# "THE REMAINDER": Fairbanks counted so far, an UPDATE of No.76

## THESIS
Fairbanks didn't settle Seat D on October 6th. A 123-vote lead is what was left when early ballots outran Election Day, the same thing happened in Seat H, and 1,235 absentee and questioned ballots are still out until October 13th. The Assembly that asked the state for an AI data center moratorium leads all three seats, but on unofficial numbers, and the land the Pentagon wants for data centers is the land its moratorium request doesn't name.
PDF document title (copy.json): "Fairbanks So Far. A 123-Vote Lead, 1,235 Ballots Out" (53 chars).
