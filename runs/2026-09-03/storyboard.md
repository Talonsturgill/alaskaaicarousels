# STORYBOARD — Carousel No. 49 — 2026-09-03

## DECK HEADER

### Thesis
On Kodiak's isolated grid every new load is subtracted from a fixed number, and
the only figure anyone has put on the Navy's new building is one the cooperative
had to estimate for itself.

### Document title (49 chars)
`Five Turning, One Still, and a Load With No Number`

### Arc, beat by beat
1. HOOK. Five rotors turning, one locked still, above a dark town. The picture
   argues before a word is read.
2. PAY IT OFF IMMEDIATELY. Two true sentences that sit badly together. The Navy
   says the building runs off the existing grid. The cooperative says it has not
   been told how much power it will use.
3. WIDEN. What the island actually has, four places in one night. The keepable
   inventory.
4. ESCALATE. The fixed number. In March the peak hit just over 31 MW and the
   diesels nearly ran.
5. BREATHE. One word, the utility's own, and the island drawn as a closed shape.
6. THE DATA BEAT. The only number on this load, and where it came from.
7. THE SUBJECT. Spruce Cape at night, a footprint with no purpose visible.
8. THE HONESTY BEAT. What is sourced, what is one newsroom, what is unstamped.
9. CLOSE. One ask. Emotional temperature drops from slide 4 into slide 5, holds
   low through 7, and lifts on the question rather than on a resolution.

### Slide count rationale
Nine. The default band is 8 to 10 and the argument has exactly nine beats, of
which one is a declared rest. Eight would force the inventory and the ceiling
into one frame and lose the keepable slide. Ten would need a second rest beat
this story does not have.

### Continuity system
THREE devices, two carrying the argument and one carrying the swipe.

**A. THE UNLIT BAND (motif, shape state machine).** Exactly one region per frame
sits outside the lamp's reach and is drawn as genuine near black `#04070E` with a
measurable falloff edge. Its state change is always a SHAPE change, never a
brightness change. It is declared as a wordless claim against C04.

**B. THE CONDUCTOR (edge-tease).** One overhead distribution conductor, a cased
catenary with rain beads, exits the right edge of slide n and re-enters the left
of slide n+1 at the same height. On slide 09 it dead-ends at an insulator with
nothing past it.

**C. THE WALKING SHOOT (camera).** One person, one night, one walk, nine
different places. Nine distinct camera heights, nine distinct focal lengths, nine
distinct bearings, all listed below and checkable. Continuity is the constant
light and weather, never a constant frame, which is what separates it from the
forbidden STATION LINE CAMERA.
