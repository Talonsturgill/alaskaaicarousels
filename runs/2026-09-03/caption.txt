Terror Lake carries about 85 percent of the generation on the grid Kodiak Electric Association calls isolated.

Pillar Mountain supplies most of the rest. One of its six turbines is stopped. In March the peak hit just over 31 MW and the diesels nearly ran.

At Spruce Cape the Navy is building what it calls a multi-mission support building and says it will run off the existing grid. The cooperative says the Navy has not told it how much power that will take. The only number on that load is the co-op's own estimate, up to an additional two megawatts.

The cooperative's published numbers, the Navy's denial that it is a data center, and the estimate the co-op had to make itself.

CEO Dan Menth says the diesels could become a possibility as the load grows. Who owes the number on a new load, the customer adding it or the utility planning around it?
