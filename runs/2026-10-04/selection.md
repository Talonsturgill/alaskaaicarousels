# SELECTION, run 2026-10-04 (No.77)

## Pick
**Bristol Bay's escapement count and the AI drone that wants to take it over.** ADF&G's September 22nd season summary puts the 2026 inshore sockeye run at 45.7 million with 15.6 million fish counted past the escapement projects, eight of them towers where, per ADF&G's own methods document, counts are made for 10 minutes of every hour on each bank and then expanded. On the Wood River a drone in a box (UW Fisheries Research Institute, BBRSDA, BBEDC) flies a daily path and feeds a computer vision model, and the pilot's next phase tests that model against the Fish and Game tower counts. Alaska Public Media's October 2nd season wrap is the in-window hook.

The deck's position: the validation test is the decision, and its answer key is itself a sample. ADF&G states the towers' sampling CV at about 0.02 (2004 and 2005, assuming no error in the ten minutes counted), and in 2026 the Togiak River missed the floor of its escapement goal by 702 fish of 120,000. A machine that only matches the tower inherits the tower's error; the bar it must clear has to be written down before the comparison is run.

Criteria: (1) concrete Alaska impact, a $264.0 million fishery managed in-season off these counts; (2) visual potential, river from above, fish, a tower, a clock sector, a gauge; (3) tangible, a deployed machine and a published method; (4) a Bristol Bay fisherman or a Dillingham manager would forward it.
