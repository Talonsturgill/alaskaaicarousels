# STORYBOARD, Carousel No.77, run 2026-10-04
# "ONE LIT SIXTH": what the AI drone will be graded against

## THESIS
Bristol Bay's escapement towers count 10 minutes of every hour on each bank and multiply by six, and ADF&G's own published sampling CV for them, about 2 percent, is more than three times the 702 fish by which the Togiak missed its 2026 floor. The Wood River AI drone pilot plans to test its model against those tower counts next, so "matches the tower" can't be the pass mark: the margin, and who sets it, has to be written down before the comparison runs.
Document title (<= 60 chars): `Ten Minutes of Every Hour: the Count an AI Drone Must Beat`
