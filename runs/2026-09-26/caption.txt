Alaska News says its old footer told every reader an article was "reviewed by editors," whether or not it was.

Most of its articles begin as AI drafts. Its label says "reviewed by" only when an editor has read the whole piece. When none has, it says so.

In the 48 hours to September 26th, 90 articles ran. 73 were labeled reviewed by Cale Green. 15 had no full editor review on record. 2 had no AI label. No other human name was on a label.

A counter of 1,258 recorded meetings is set against one Axon police-data story that ran with no editor review on record.

The outlet's AI agent Walter says the strongest protection is "whether skeptical readers can audit the source trail." A label describes process, not accuracy. It tells a reader when the checking is theirs.

Should Alaska's human-written news carry the same line, naming who read each story?
