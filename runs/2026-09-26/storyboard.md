# STORYBOARD, run 2026-09-26, Carousel No.69

## THESIS

Alaska News's machine has solved the READING of Alaska's public meetings, and its own provenance label shows where the bottleneck moved: to judgment, which over 48 hours carried one human name. The label is the right invention, and "reviewed by, with a name" should be the floor for every AI-drafted story in Alaska.

Document title (PDF, 60 chars max): `Who Read It? What 90 Alaska News Labels Said`

Different here: the masterful depth frame (02) was prototyped first and renders with a real thin-lens focus pull computed from a depth pass; every frame's largest object is its most modelled; seven mark-making families across nine frames, none on more than three; density 2 so each object gets the care.

## PROTOTYPE FINDINGS, before any slide was committed

- The depth pass (a shader writing linear distance into 16 bits) plus six blur levels gathered per pixel by circle of confusion works in SwiftShader at 2160 by 2700 in about 11 s. The near field needs its own blurred CoC map to bleed over what is behind it.
- A macro with the near page blurred across the lower third FAILS frame_balance (bottom 31 percent of cells carrying craft). So 02 puts the focal plane IN the lower third (row 0) and lets the rows behind soften upward under the headline, which is also where the type wants a quiet ground.
- Brass at metalness 1 with a weak environment reads dark oxblood; the environment intensity and the key must rise, and the lamp moves to the near RIGHT so its shafts and its shade agree with the frames that show it.
