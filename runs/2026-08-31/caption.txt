A subsidy that has paid Alaska's rural to urban rate gap since 1997 now shares a Federal Register issue with the FCC's AI questions.

On August 31st an FCC notice asked whether AI tools should review applications, audits and appeals. Nine sentences in it name AI, all questions.

In funding year 2025, 34 of Alaska's 340 approved Telecom Program requests relied on the methods the FCC prefers. That leaves 306 that didn't. It was 32 of 317 the year before.

Those figures are in a second notice, published the same day. It never says AI. The AI notice never says Alaska.

The deck holds the FCC's AI question as written, both Alaska funding years drawn at one scale, and what neither notice says.

Comments close September 30th on both. What would a faster review need to know about Alaska that neither notice puts on the record?
