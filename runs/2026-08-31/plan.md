**THE ATTACK: VALUE STRUCTURE IS PLANNED BEFORE TECHNIQUE IS CHOSEN.**

Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 9 of the last 10 scored runs,
mean 6.65, while every other criterion sits above 8. The two defect classes
that keep shipping, busy art under text (4 of 10) and top-loaded composition
(4 of 10), are not two problems. They are one problem seen twice: detail is
being distributed evenly across the frame instead of being organised into
masses, so the type sits on noise and the bottom band ends up holding whatever
the top did not want.

Past runs attacked this at the level of the OBJECT (No.44 put the type on a
modelled surface, No.45 made the reserve a physical hole in the paper). Both
helped and both left craft weakest, because an object fixes ONE region. This
run attacks it at the level of the WHOLE FRAME, using the oldest tool there is
for the job.

Every slide is planned as a THREE-MASS NOTAN before a single technique is
named, and the dossier states the plan in field 4 with the three masses and
their approximate area fractions. Three rules, from the Phase 1 refresh:

1. **One mass predominates, over half the frame.** Not two masses at forty
   percent each.
2. **The remaining two are deliberately unequal.** An even split of the
   leftovers is what reads as clutter.
3. **The darks are WELDED into one connected shape.** Scattered dark marks
   of similar weight are the thing that makes a thumb read as six unrelated
   marks instead of one picture.

And the operative prohibition, which is the whole reason this is worth doing:
**detail is added INSIDE a mass and may never break it.** The most common
cause of a weak value structure is losing the structure while adding the
detail. That sentence describes "busy art under text" exactly. Under type, the
mass is the dominant one and it stays unbroken; the detail budget for that
region is spent on TONE and MATERIAL, not on marks.

Top-loaded composition falls out of the same rule: a dominant mass over half
the frame cannot live only in the top third, so the bottom band is composed by
construction rather than by remembering to compose it.

This is checkable, and will be checked. Phase 8 gets a posterize pass over
each render (4 levels), reporting each mass's area fraction and whether the
darks are one connected component. If that pass is worth keeping it becomes a
Phase 12 upgrade candidate rather than an assertion.

Second target, cheap and specific: **outside safe zone** and **tiny-text**
(3 and 2 of 10). Both are layout arithmetic, not taste. Every text block gets
its box declared in the dossier's field 10 with the measured 80px margins, and
`AK.fitText` does the sizing. No hand-guessed coordinates.
