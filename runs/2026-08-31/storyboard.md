### Thesis

On August 31st, 2026 the FCC published one notice asking nine times whether
artificial intelligence should help administer the Universal Service Fund, and
a second notice, in the same Federal Register issue and closing on the same
date, that names Alaska as the place where nine in ten approved rural health
requests fall off the Commission's preferred rate methods. Nothing in either
record connects the two, so the connection is Alaska's to file within thirty
days.

### Slide count rationale

Nine. Inside the 8 to 10 default. The story has exactly three irreducible
parts, what the two documents are, what Alaska's numbers say, and what the
connection between them is, and each needs a setup and a payoff. Eight would
force the mechanism (06) into the data slide (05) and make the data slide carry
two jobs, which is the failure the essayist director named in its own pitch.
Ten would add a slide with nothing new to verify.

### Continuity system

**Device A. THE ACCESSION RULE.** A brass strip engraved across the bottom
band on every slide, left-anchored, growing one tick per swipe. It carries
exactly nine ticks across the deck, one per slide, in record order.

CORRECTED AGAINST THE RENDER (Phase 12 review, this run). An earlier draft of
this section described the rule as a PAN, nine 1080px windows onto one
continuous 9720px strip. The build does not do that and never did: the strip is
left-anchored on every frame and simply grows longer, about 100px on 01 and
about 1330px on 09. It is a growth rule, not a panorama, and there is no seam
to align between frames. The description is corrected here rather than the
build changed, because the growth rule is what carries the subtractive promise
below and it carries it exactly. The ticks are also drawn bare, without the
per-tick date labels an earlier draft promised; that is logged as an unfixed
defect in this run's email rather than asserted as delivered.

**TICKS AFTER THE CURRENT SLIDE ARE NEVER DRAWN.** The rule runs on bare past
the present tick and ends in a 45 degree cut terminal. This deck is forbidden
to write the future, because the FCC asked rather than proposed (C25), and this
is that prohibition rendered as artwork rather than asserted as a caption. It
prints `SEQUENCE, NOT TO SCALE` at its left terminal on every slide, because a
true time scale would crush twenty nine years into nine tenths of the strip and
the last thirty days into a sliver.

**Device B. THE 2026 SLOT.** The gap in the shelf run where the 2026 volume is
not bound yet, and the unsewn block standing in it. It changes SHAPE and COUNT,
never brightness alone. **It never seals.** At the close it holds one gold
thread through one sewing station of five and four stations stay open, because
the record does not close until September 30th.

**Device C. EDGE TEASE**, mechanically tied to Device A's pan. Whatever the
right frame edge cuts on slide n is the first thing fully in frame on slide
n plus 1.

### THE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS DECK ATTACKS

Artwork craft, weakest in 9 of the last 10 scored runs at a mean of 6.65. The
attack is stated in plan.md and it binds every dossier below.

**Every slide is planned as a THREE-MASS NOTAN before a technique is named.**
One mass over half the frame. The other two deliberately unequal. The darks
welded into one connected shape. **Detail is added inside a mass and may never
break it**, so under type the mass stays unbroken and the detail budget there is
spent on tone and material, not on marks. Gradients stay inside their own mass.

Measured, not asserted. `python scripts/value_structure.py --render-dir
out/2026-08-31/render` posterizes each render to three masses at 432px feed
width in CIELAB L*, reports each mass's area fraction, squints the darkest mass
and counts its connected shapes, and reports how much of the bottom third the
dominant mass fills. It REPORTS and does not gate, because one deck of
calibration is not enough evidence to gate on.

### The honesty system, carried in the type

**Instrument Serif ITALIC is the FCC talking, and nothing else, ever.** Roman is
this deck's own voice. A reader learns the rule inside two slides without being
told it. Every string that is the deck's argument rather than the record's
content additionally carries a mono `ALASKA.AI READING` stamp. Forget-me-not
`#6EA5FF` has ONE meaning, the line this deck draws itself, and it never touches
an FCC element.

**Every object in the deck is stamped with a real docket number, page range or
date in mono**, so the blocks read as documents and not as books. No on-slide
string ever claims the shelf exists. The bound volume is a drawing convention,
carried entirely by the artwork and never by the copy.

### B. COMPOSITION

**4. Layout map.** 12 x 8 grid, 80px margins. Display occupies cols 1 to 8,

4a. **Lower-third treatment.** The lit brass ledge and the two standing blocks

shadow cast into that lit ground, a 6px contact core at alpha 0.42 with zero
gap plus a 34px ambient at alpha 0.14, coloured `#071019` and never black. The
paper of each block is a stack of engraved leaf edges with real tonal variation
across its depth, and the cast shadow of the right block falls across the
ledge's texture toward frame right. The mass moved here is the deck's brightest
material, so the bottom band is the lit part of the picture and the top is the
dark one, which inverts the top-loaded habit by construction.

**5. Depth plan.** z-stack, back to front. Void `#03070E` with a 2.4 percent

veil in the beam at alpha 0.05; grain. Four depth cues named. Occlusion, the
right block is partly behind the left one's cast shadow. Atmospheric
perspective, the recess behind the blocks is lerped 40 percent toward `#24505C`.
Scale gradient, the right block is drawn at 0.94 of the left. One key light with
a global shadow direction. Focal plane is the left block's fore edge.

**6. Continuity device state.** Accession rule shows its first window, tick at

### C. ART DIRECTION

undistorted and square to camera, depth at half scale at 45 degrees, lid

**8. Data-in-art mapping.** The two blocks are the two notices and nothing else.

# ENGINE NOTE, from the pre-build spike

`AKPOST.grade` takes OBJECTS, not numbers, and the key names are not the ones
two of the three treatments assumed. Verified by rendering a throwaway frame
before any dossier was written.

```js
AKPOST.grade(cx, {w: 1080, h: 1350, exposure: -0.04, saturation: 1.02,
  contrast: 1.08, filmic: true, lift: [0.010, 0.014, 0.024],
  gain: [1.015, 1.0, 0.975], vignette: 0.18,
  bloom: {threshold: 0.72, strength: 0.12, radius: 14},
  grain: {amount: 0.055, size: 2, seed: 20260831},
  aberration: 0, dither: true, sharpen: 0.32});
```

`exposure` here is STOPS and the house range is -0.15 to +0.06. It is
`contrast` and `sharpen`, never `logContrast`, `pivot` or `unsharp`. A number
where an object belongs is a render hard fail, not a warning.

`akengrave.js` throws `AKC is not defined` unless `akcolor.js` loads first, per
the instinct ledger at 0.99 confidence. Script order for every slide in this
deck is noise, akcolor, akengrave, aksnow if used, akpost, aktype.

# BUILD RECONCILIATION

Every dossier number the build actually changed, written before any pixel critic
was spawned.

**THE ENGRAVING LIBRARY WAS NOT USED, and the type reserve was built instead.**
Every dossier specified `AKENGRAVE.create` with `eng.reserve(...)` zeroing the
lay inside measured text boxes. The build wrote per slide lay with beziers and
fbm and implemented the reserve as a MEASURED even odd canvas clip applied to
every detail pass. The reason is that `eng.reserve` protects an engraved surface
and nothing else, while the first render's fourteen "label crossed by art" fails
came from leaf strokes, recess strokes and cast shadows, none of which akengrave
drew. The clip protects any drawing code on any slide. Two API errors in the
dossiers are recorded so a later run does not repeat them. akengrave takes
`region` as an ARRAY `[x,y,w,h]` and `budget` as an OBJECT `{main, cross, dots}`,
where the dossiers wrote an object and a number.

**AKPOST OPTION NAMES WERE WRONG IN THE PLAN AND ARE RIGHT IN THE BUILD.** Found
by the pre build spike, before a single slide existed. `bloom` and `grain` are
OBJECTS and a number throws a render hard fail. The keys are `contrast` and
`sharpen`, never `logContrast`, `pivot` or `unsharp`. Two of the three treatments
would have hit this.

**SLIDE 06 WAS REBUILT AFTER ITS FIRST RENDER.** The dossier's extreme close on a
gilt leaf run drew as an even barcode field with four dots on it, flat and
unreadable as paper. It is now a perspective stack receding to a vanishing point
at x 1180, each leaf carrying a lit top edge and the shadow it throws on its
neighbour, with a four leaf gilt group and five sewing stations at real size
pierced through the stack. The five stations moved from the block's lower third
to the stack itself, which is where they read.

**SLIDES 04 AND 09, THE SPINE PROFILE.** The dossier said rounded spine and the
