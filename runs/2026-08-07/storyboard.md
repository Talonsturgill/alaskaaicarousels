## THE SYNTHESIS, AND WHY

Three directors pitched (data-journalist, editorial-essayist, systems-illustrator)
and **two of the three independently diagnosed the same fatal flaw in their own
pitch, an invented register.** The essayist built a post office box wall and
volunteered that no source says these notices went to post office boxes. The
systems illustrator built a galvanised sorting line and volunteered that no such
apparatus exists, that the Division compared two databases and mailed letters,
and that this is exactly the move DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 6 warns against on a
story where four words are already banned. Both offered the same salvage, keep
the load-bearing organs and rebuild on objects the record actually contains.

That is what this storyboard does. The organs kept, and from whom:

- **THE REPLY AS KEY LIGHT** (essayist). The best single idea in the room. The
  key light is EMITTED BY THE ANSWERING MEMBERS rather than aimed at them.
  Illumination equals knowledge. Register-independent, so it survives the graft.
- **THE HOLLOW FIGURE** (systems). Quantities the state MEASURED are set solid.
  Quantities that count people who returned NO information are set outline only.
  The hardest constraint on this deck, that it must never claim the 1,900 are
  anything, is solved IN THE TYPE rather than in a caveat.
- **THE OPEN EDGE and the unwalled residual** (data-journalist and systems, who
  invented it separately). The population bleeds off frame and the unexplained
  13 to 23 people are drawn as a channel with no closing wall, so the deck is
  STRUCTURALLY INCAPABLE of drawing a closed whole.
- **THE ENLARGED SPECIMEN as repoussoir** (data-journalist). Under an
  orthographic camera the near card is not a perspective foreground, it is a
  specimen at a printed magnification, so size can never be misread as quantity.
- **TWO REGISTERS, and perspective is banned on quantities** (systems). Every
  magnitude in this deck lives in a true orthographic elevation with a printed
  scale constant.
- **THE 24x DETAIL CIRCLE** (systems). 3,048 and 3,058 differ by less than the
  drawing's own line weight, which is a true fact about the record drawn as a
  true fact about the drawing.
- **GOLD MEANS ONE THING, what a reader can still do** (data-journalist), and it
  is held out of the data entirely.

Dropped: every postal signifier, every piece of apparatus, every read head,
chase, hopper, duct and lobby. Nothing in this deck depicts a mechanism the
record does not describe.

**THE REGISTER THAT SURVIVES.** A registration record drawn as an identical
small card, one card standing for 25 registrations, with the unit printed on the
slide. That is a DIAGRAM OF A LIST, which is what the record actually contains,
and it asserts nothing about how anything was processed or delivered. There is
no table, no bench and no ground plane anywhere in the deck, which also cures
the data-journalist's own self-named weakness that its counter was a surface and
the last five decks were surfaces.

## HERO STRUCTURE

### THE ANSWERED FIELD (NEW)

A population of identical registration cards floating on an **unbounded dark
void with no visible table, bench, ground plane or edge anywhere in nine
slides**. One card stands for 25 registrations, printed on slide 02 with a
dimension call.

**The cards that answered are the only light sources in the deck.** Each
answered card is a small warm emissive plane. Unanswered cards receive only that
neighbours' spill plus a 3 percent cold ambient, and fall to near-black at
distance. The lit fraction of any frame IS the measured fraction of that frame's
population. Nothing external illuminates anything.

Camera is **strict orthographic on every frame that carries a quantity** (02, 03,
04, 06) and a shallow 18 degree tilt on the rest, and it never orbits a subject.
The deck's register changes are specimen to field to true elevation, not
viewpoint changes around an object.

**Argued against the forbidden four.** Not the CUT BLOCK (24), nothing is
sectioned, no solid has a mapped face, nothing is extruded to show an interior.
Not the HEAD SHEET (25), there is no drawing set, no sheet, no title block, and
magnification is not the organising move. Not the OPEN BLOCK (26), there is no
object at all, let alone one object under a frozen camera. Not the TRAVERSE
(27), there is no ground, no country, no station, no route and no engraved
surface.

**Argued against the deeper charge**, that five straight decks were one rigid
object or surveyed ground under a fixed or stepping camera. This deck has **no
object and no ground**. Its subject is a population, its ground is a void with
no declared extent, and its light comes from inside the subject rather than from
a lamp position. That is a different thing from all five.

## ATMOSPHERE

### THE REPLY AS KEY LIGHT (NEW)

**There is no external key light in this deck.** Every photon originates from a
card that answered.

- **Emitters.** Each answered card is a 44 by 28 px emissive plane, colour
  `#F4DCA8`, core `#FFF0CC`, intensity 2.4, with physically correct inverse
  square falloff. Spill on a neighbouring card face reads `#8A6A3C`.
- **Fill.** One cold ambient at `#8FA0B4`, intensity 0.06, no direction. This is
  the only non-emitted light and it exists so an unanswered card is a shape
  rather than a hole.
- **Measured key to fill is about 12 to 1 at an answered card's nearest
  neighbour and falls to about 1 to 1 at eight card-widths.** That falloff is the
  argument and a critic can measure it.
- **Shadows are radial from each emitter**, warm-darkened at `#12100C`, never
  black, two-part everywhere (tight contact within 5 px at alpha 0.85, wide
  ambient to 34 px at alpha 0.16).
- **Zero fog, zero haze, zero veil, zero volumetric shaft, zero horizon** in nine
  slides. The only atmospheric term is depth of field on the repoussoir.

**THE CONTACT SHADOW HAS SOMETHING TO SUBTRACT FROM BY CONSTRUCTION**, which is
the No.26 failure solved structurally rather than by strengthening a shadow.
Each emitter lights the void around itself into a warm pool first, and the
card's own body then occludes that pool. The shadow bites into lit ground
because the light and the ground are made by the same object. Every declared
contact pair sits inside an emitter's pool and none is cast onto bare void.

Not SUBMERGED WORK LIGHT (25, two external sources, one a water emitter). Not
BINDERY AIR (26, a high external pendant with paper dust). Not BURNER BLUE AT
ALTITUDE (27, an external low raking methane key over a plate).

A hollow numeral cannot be misread as a claim about those people. That is the
hardest constraint on the deck. It is solved in the type on three slides and in
the drawing on the rest, and the difference is worth stating rather than
flattening.

## RENDERED LADDER POSITION

**Rung 1, akthree GPU PBR, on slide 04 only, and argued rather than defaulted.**

An emissive-lit scene with physically correct inverse-square falloff, soft
shadow maps radiating from many small area sources, and real contact occlusion
is precisely and only what the GPU bench is for. A 2D approximation of many
overlapping inverse-square pools with correct occlusion is exactly the case
where Canvas compositing looks fake.

**HAND-ROLL THE LIGHTS. DO NOT CALL `AKT.rig`.** FIELD_NOTES 2026-08-06 records
that `AKT.rig(R, AKT.rigs.arcticNight)` plus `AKT.environment` produces mean and
variance both under 1 across 24 samples and zero lit pixels in this container,
while hand-rolled `THREE.DirectionalLight` plus `AmbientLight` renders correctly.
Use `THREE.MeshStandardMaterial` with `emissive` and `emissiveIntensity` on the
answered cards, plus one `THREE.AmbientLight` at 0.06, and no rig call at all.
`setPixelRatio` BEFORE `setSize`. `await AKT.snapshot(R)` and check `.ok`, one
retry, then the designed fallback.

**Slides 02, 03 and 06 are deliberately FLAT and argued as flat.** A true
orthographic elevation with a printed scale constant is a DRAWING, not a render,
and rendering a quantity would be a data-honesty error under DESIGN_DOCTRINE
section 4. That is a positive choice and it is stated here in those words.

**Build order, so a budget overrun degrades honestly rather than shipping a
claim the pixels do not carry.** S04 is built first. If the GPU path fails its
sentinel, S04 ships on its designed Canvas fallback (radial pool compositing at
`globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter'`, painter-sorted cards, two-part contact
shadows) and the artwork ledger records the shortfall rather than the rung.
