### The ONE standing weakness this run attacks, and how

**TOP-LOADED COMPOSITION.** It is the single most persistent defect class on the
board: present in 5 of the last 10 runs' final machine_qa and shipped again
yesterday, on 2026-08-06. It is also the mechanism behind the top repeat offender
rather than a separate problem. Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 7 of
10 runs at a mean of 5.89, and the scorer's named reason six consecutive times was
the dead lower zone. DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 1 was amended for it on 2026-07-26,
dossier field 4a was added for it, `dossier_check.py` gates the field and
`frame_balance()` measures the pixels, and it still shipped yesterday. So the
remaining gap is not a missing rule and not a missing check. It is that the fix keeps
being attempted as furniture in the bottom band, which DESIGN_DOCTRINE explicitly
calls "the same defect wearing a costume", instead of as composition.

The attack is a named classical device rather than another rule: **REPOUSSOIR.** A
dark, out-of-focus mass placed in the near foreground along the bottom and one side
edge, bleeding off frame, occluding the subject's base. It is the Baroque landscape
answer to exactly this problem, it fills the lower band with modeled tone by
construction because it is a lit solid rather than a plate, and it pays for itself
twice: DESIGN_DOCTRINE section 4's depth stack lists "a blurred foreground element
bleeding off-frame" as an instant expensive lens, and occlusion is the strongest and
cheapest depth cue there is. A hairline or a plate in the bottom band cannot do
either job. A repoussoir mass does both while it is filling the zone.

Committed to at planning altitude, so it is checkable:
- EVERY slide's dossier field 4a names a repoussoir element or declares
  `BREATHER` with `data-breather` set. No slide satisfies 4a with a plate, a
  hairline, a caption or the footer fixture.
- The repoussoir carries the deck's continuity load, so it changes state slide to
  slide and cannot be copied forward.
- `qa.py frame_balance()` must report zero top-loaded warnings on the final render.
  That is the falsifiable test and it goes in the run record either way.
