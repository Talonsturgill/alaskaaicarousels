NOAA's 16-day AI forecast now finishes in about 40 minutes. Y-K Delta airport stations have gone days or weeks with no observations.

Climatologist Rick Thoman told KYUK it has happened at every airport in the region since April.

NOAA fine-tuned that AI model on a grid of surface, balloon and satellite observations.

A year ago, global models had Halong's track and missed its shift east, Thoman said. A lack of balloon launches across Western Alaska may have played a role, KYUK reported.

NOAA's AI models went operational later, in December 2025.

A faster model can't fill in a reading nobody took.

Maps show the grid NOAA's AI model learned from, the Delta's dark stations, Halong's turn east and its saltwater on the tundra.
