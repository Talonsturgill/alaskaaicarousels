# THE SHOWRUNNER'S OWN ARC, written before the treatments land

Written deliberately BEFORE reading the three treatments, so the room is judged
against a real alternative rather than against whichever pitch arrives first.
This is a yardstick, not a decision. If a director beats it, the director wins.

## THE ARGUMENT IN ONE LINE

Southcentral's gas position is measurable, and this page measures it daily. The
order in which that gas gets taken away is written down, and nobody outside the
utility can read it.

## THE NINE BEATS, and the claims that carry each

**01 COVER.** The measured number, alone, with the thing it is a fraction of.
7.65 of 13.0 Bcf, 58.8 percent of design, read September 10th (C01 to C04).
Not an alarm. A reading. The cover's job is to say this is a measurement and
somebody is taking it every morning.
Open loop planted: a fraction of what, and who decides what happens to the rest.

**02 PAYS IMMEDIATELY.** The instrument. This page reads CINGSA's public
dashboard every morning and keeps the history CINGSA does not keep, 36 days on
record, 34 of them verified, and the two that are not are said so (C08, C09).
The honesty beat goes SECOND, not last, because it is what licenses everything
after it.
Pays 01. Plants: what does a number like that mean as time.

**03 THE CHARACTERISATION, and its hedge in the same frame.** About 3 billion
cubic feet short of ideal, as Enstar's president told state lawmakers in August
(C15). About 18 days of winter demand, if temperatures are normal or slightly
colder, as Kenai's mayor relayed it to his council on September 2nd (C16). Then
the utility's own refusal to make it a forecast, verbatim (C17).
This is the slide where the deck refuses to become a prediction, and it does it
early rather than in a footer.

**04 THE TURN. The rulebook exists.** Reductions are phased by customer class
under the utility's tariff, with facilities serving human needs and occupancy
given the highest priority (C20). The tariff is real and public and its
Interruption section is numbered 1200 (C40, primary).
Plants the deck's biggest loop: phased in what order.

**05 THE ANSWER, AND ITS PROVENANCE, WHICH IS THE POINT.** Kenai's mayor said
Enstar described a plan that would first look to large commercial and industrial
users for reductions before residential customers (C19). Attributed to him. On
the same frame, what nobody has, which is the text.
This is the deck's thesis slide and its accuracy structure has to be visible in
the drawing, not asserted in a caveat. Solid stroke for what is written down,
phantom for what is relayed.

**06 THE BREATHER, and it is a real one.** Voluntary conservation first. Modest
thermostat adjustments down to 65 degrees, where safe and practical, could help
during periods of extreme cold (C21). One number, one frame, lots of air. The
qualifiers stay.

**07 WHY THE TANK IS WHERE IT IS.** The regulatory record. The commission denied
Enstar an advance determination of prudence on July 8th, calling it an
extraordinary request not explicitly contemplated in its governing statutes or
regulations (C32, C33). Enstar filed 31 pages asking it to reconsider (C34). The
project would cost $240 million and add up to $12 a month to the average bill
(C35). Docket U-26-003 (C39, primary).
This is the keepable data slide. It is also the fair-minded one, because it shows
the regulator's reason.

**08 WHEN MORE GAS ARRIVES.** Cosmopolitan no earlier than the first quarter of
2027 (C22). A Nikiski import project reportedly not before the fourth quarter of
2029 (C23). Meanwhile gas at close to $11 per mcf against about $24 on the Japan
Korea spot market (C30, C31), and 70 percent of Southcentral's electricity made
from it (C28).
The AI beat belongs here or on 05. A data center is a large commercial and
industrial customer. Anyone bidding into this system is bidding into that order.

**09 CLOSE.** One ask. The site fixture, alaskaaihq.com, small in the mono face
near the brand mark. Sources in comments. The closing question is about the
ORDER, not about the weather.

## WHAT I WILL BE LOOKING FOR IN THE THREE TREATMENTS

1. Does the concept carry the PROVENANCE distinction in the drawing, so a reader
   can tell measured from relayed without reading a caveat? That is the hardest
   thing this deck has to do and it is where a treatment earns its place.
2. Does it put the honesty beat early rather than burying it at slide 08?
3. Is the topology genuinely not a continuous medium crawled across nine frames?
4. Does every slide name what the type sits on, with the reserve as a term in
   the generator?
5. Is there a literal anchor per abstract composition, and does the art encode a
   story number rather than illustrate a mood?
6. Does it avoid dread? A cold blue creeping darkness would argue catastrophe
   with light, which is the honesty violation wearing a better coat.
