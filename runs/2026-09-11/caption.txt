Enstar, the gas utility for almost half of Alaska's population, publishes a tariff section numbered 1200 and titled Interruption.

Cook Inlet storage read 7.65 of 13.0 Bcf on September 10th, or 58.8 percent of design. Enstar says any reductions would be phased by customer class under that tariff, with facilities serving human needs and occupancy ranked highest.

The public learns where a large commercial and industrial load sits only because Kenai Mayor Henry Knackstedt told his city council what Enstar described to Southcentral mayors on August 21st.
