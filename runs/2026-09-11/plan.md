### The ONE standing weakness this run attacks, and how

Artwork craft and genuine detail. Weakest in 7 of the last 10 and the last
reading was 6.0, the floor of the window. The three defect classes that keep
shipping are all the same argument from different angles. Busy art under text,
canvas mark near reserved text and art touching glyphs are one problem, which is
that this studio keeps building a field first and then negotiating with it for
somewhere to put the words.

The attack this run is a PLANNING move, not a repair move, and it is made in the
Phase 5 dossiers where it costs a paragraph instead of a round.

**Every slide declares its TYPE GROUND before it declares its field.** The
dossier's layout map names the rectangle each text block sits on, and the
drawing loop is written so that rectangle is a place the art is BUILT to be
quiet, not a place the art is punched out of afterwards. Concretely, for a
generative field the reserve is a term in the field's own generator (a density
falloff, a contour gap, a hachure spacing that opens), so the quiet band still
carries the field's grain and modelled tone and therefore still fills the bottom
third. The blurred-rectangle punch stays available as the SECOND line of
defence, per the 2026-09-10 note, never the first.

Paired with it, one technique deliberately chosen for tone rather than for
pattern, from today's craft refresh. Aerial perspective as an elevation-keyed
contrast gain, applied to whatever surface this deck's field turns out to be. It
raises modelled contrast where the story's focus is and compresses it everywhere
else, which is simultaneously a depth cue, a hierarchy device and a legibility
device, and it is the opposite of the mute-the-art-under-the-type habit that has
been costing the craft score.

The second-ranked offender, Deliverable completeness, reads STALE at 2 of 10 and
is a record-hygiene criterion rather than an art one. It is handled by running
`gate_status.py --sync` after EVERY round that changes an artifact, per the
0.99-confidence instinct, rather than by a dedicated intervention.

## Top instincts injected into every subagent prompt this run

1. A machine_qa PASS is never composition approval. The pixel critics judge
   composition, hierarchy and collision at full size AND at 432px thumb.
2. A generated block pasted into a run record goes stale the moment another
   round runs. Re-sync after every round.
3. A constraint you can't point at is a constraint you invented. There is no
   context budget, no token budget and no remaining-budget gate in this routine.
4. Sanity-check long serif or body copy line counts against fixed-position
   labels, bars and plates before rendering. DOM text overlaps pass machine QA
   and fail the eye.
5. Grain is a small repeating tile (AK.grainTile), never a full-frame
   feTurbulence rect.
