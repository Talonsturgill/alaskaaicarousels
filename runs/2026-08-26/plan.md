## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN IS ATTACKING, AND HOW

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 9 of the last 10 scored runs,
ten-run mean 6.65, last run 7.

Read as a design brief rather than a report, the last two scorers say the same
thing twice and it is specific, not vague. No. 40's verdict: "Slide 05's top 40
percent, slide 07's bottom quarter and slide 09's margins are still inert", and
"the DECLARED DETAIL BUDGET was asserted in the deck header and NOT executed:
no dossier declares three named texture octaves with what each encodes. Do not
log this run as having landed the detail budget."

So the defect is not that the machine cannot draw. It is that detail is
promised at deck level and never specified at slide level, which leaves whole
quadrants of frames with nothing in them, and the only reviewer who ever sees
that is the scorer at the ship gate.

This run attacks it with a rule that is per-slide, written before any code, and
checkable by reading the dossier:

1. **THE THREE-OCTAVE DECLARATION IS A REQUIRED DOSSIER FIELD THIS RUN.** Every
   slide's technique stack names exactly three texture octaves, MACRO, MESO and
   MICRO, each with its own generator, its own parameters and seed, and, in the
   same sentence, WHAT IT ENCODES from claims.json. An octave that encodes
   nothing is ornament and gets cut rather than renamed. A dossier that cannot
   name three is not finished.
2. **NO FRAME HAS A REGION WHERE THE FIELD STOPS.** The deck's field runs to all
   four edges of every slide at the amplitude it carries at the focal point.
   This is No. 40's own one-sentence fix, unspent, and it is inherited rather
   than reinvented.
3. **THE GENRE MOVES.** Four consecutive staged-object decks is why the craft
   score sits at a plateau: the same chassis produces the same inert margins
   every time, because a staged object has a background by construction. A
   field, a terrain or a real map has no background to leave empty.
4. **THE PIXEL CRITICS ARE ASKED THE QUESTION DIRECTLY.** Each critic prompt
   this run carries the three declared octaves for its slides and must answer,
   per slide, whether all three are visible at 100 percent and whether any
   quadrant is inert. That moves the finding from Phase 10, where rebuilding
   art is no longer affordable, to Phase 8, where it is.

Secondary, and cheap: No. 40 shipped a deck whose hook was a number and
declared ZERO `window.__akAssert` entries. If this deck's cover carries a
count, it declares its marks.
