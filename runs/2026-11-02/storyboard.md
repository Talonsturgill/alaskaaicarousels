Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 6 of the last 10 runs (mean 7.26). This deck routes the art around a fixed type column on every frame (x 80 to 640, y 140 to about 640 on content frames) instead of reserving under text afterwards, names every frame's largest object with its lit face, lee and contact in the CRAFT PLAN, builds the masterful depth frame (01) first, and uses no primary mark-making on more than two frames. ## HONESTY GUARDS (bind every frame)

- CIPLink's route is not published as geometry in claims. Every drawn corridor is labelled schematic, between two named shores, and the RFI says the route is subject to change (C69). No crossing is placed at an invented position on the water.
- December 2031 is AEA's TARGET (C11); its page says 2032 (C32). Always "targeted".
- AEA's funding lines are printed separately as AEA lists them and never summed (206.5 + 62.5 + 142.5 is 411.5, not 413).
- CIPLink is 100 MW with a second 100 MW pole only as an option (C07, C08). Never an unqualified 200 MW.
- The cable is not said to "have no supplier". AEA is prequalifying suppliers (C21) and plans an award at the end of 2027 (C14).
- "Co-locating with the CIPLink southern terminal" is DeepGreen's phrase (C38); the converter at Soldotna is AEA's RFI (C10). No distance is drawn or printed between DeepGreen's landing and Soldotna.
- DeepGreen's 48-month permit (C39) has no start date and is never placed on AEA's calendar.
- The docket counts are a FERC eLibrary count taken the evening of November 2nd (C52 to C57).
