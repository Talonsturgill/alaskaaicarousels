In February, DeepGreen's FERC filing for a Cook Inlet AI data center named AEA's planned undersea cable its "Strategic Partner."

On October 27th, DeepGreen conceded no binding partnership with AEA, only "conceptual engineering alignments."

The partner is still a request for information. AEA plans the award for the end of 2027 and targets service for December 2031. The comment window closed November 2nd with 262 filings counted that evening, none a competing application.

DeepGreen's filings face AEA's cable, its Soldotna converter, the docket and AEA's calendar.

AEA says the cable would end the single point of failure between Kenai and Anchorage. Kenai Peninsula Borough Mayor Peter Micciche says his complaint is "being in the dark."

Is a borrowed partner harmless in a permit that builds nothing, or should FERC hold it against DeepGreen?
