**PDF document title:** On File, Off Record. Alaska's AI Governor Race. (46 chars)

**Thesis.** Alaska's public record will tell you to the dollar who paid for this
governor's race and almost nothing about what any of the 17 candidates would do
with AI, because disclosure of money is compulsory and disclosure of policy is
voluntary, and that asymmetry, not any donation, is what a voter should be angry
about with 24 days left.

8. THE ASK SHEET. Keepable. Six proposed rules, six blanks for your candidate.
9. WHO IS TELLING YOU THIS. Sourcing and conflict, at full headline scale.
10. CLOSE. One ask.

Emotional temperature: civic alertness (1) to sharp receipts (2) to a pause on his

VISUAL_DENSITY 2 compressing to 9 would force two argued beats onto one slide.

**SHARED CAMERA MATH (all four rendered slides, computed not eyeballed).**
Slab 3.00 (x) by 1.90 (z) by 0.12 (y thickness), top face at y = 0, centered at
origin, top edges chamfered 0.012.
- Camera at (0, 2.60, 4.20) looking at the origin, up +Y.
  Pitch below horizontal `theta = atan(2.60 / 4.20) = 31.75 deg`.
  `cos(theta) = 0.8505`, `sin(theta) = 0.5262`.
- **OrthographicCamera**, half-height H = 1.10 world units. This is the load-bearing
  choice. Doctrine forbids perspective on quantities, and this deck's cut LENGTHS
  encode dollars, so a perspective camera would make the artwork lie. Orthographic
  also means screen placement is arithmetic, so DOM numerals register to the pixel
  and the Canvas fallback is geometrically identical rather than merely similar.
- Art rect 1080 x 570 px, aspect 1.8947, so ortho half-width = 1.10 x 1.8947 =
  2.084. Scale `s = 570 / 2.20 = 259.1 px per world unit`. Art centre
  `artCy = 905`.
- Projection used throughout: `screen_y = 905 - 259.1 * (y * 0.8505 - z * 0.5262)`,
  `screen_x = 540 + 259.1 * x`.
- Verified placements:
  - slab far edge (z = -0.95, y = 0) -> `905 - 259.1 * 0.4999 = 775.5`
  - slab near edge (z = +0.95, y = 0) -> `905 + 129.5 = 1034.5`
  - so the top face spans **775.5 to 1034.5 = 259 px**
  - near bottom edge (z = +0.95, y = -0.12) -> `905 + 156.0 = 1061.0`,
    so the visible side face is **26.5 px**, readable at thumb size
  - slab left and right (x = +/- 1.50) -> **151.3 to 928.7**
  - cast shadow runs from y 1061 to about 1150 at S1's light angle and to about
    1185 at S10's, which is the sundial arc made visible
- **Recess depth 0.090 in y** -> `0.090 * 0.8505 * 259.1 = 19.8 px` of visible
  groove wall, which is 7.9 px at the 432px thumb, enough to read as a lip. The
  first draft used 0.050 and was rejected at 11 px because it dies at thumb size.
- Because the camera is orthographic there is no horizonY term and none is faked.
  Stated deliberately, per the instinct against eyeballed cameras.

**THE MONEY SCALE (one number, used on S2, S4 and S7).**
`165.6 px per 1 million dollars`, equivalently `0.6392 world units per million`,
linear, left-aligned at x = -1.28 (screen_x 208). Well vertical height 0.16 in z =

# BUILD RECONCILIATION (what actually shipped, and why it differs from the plan)

The dossiers above are the plan. The deck that rendered differs in the ways
below. Recorded here so the scorer, the flow critic and any later reader are
reading the shipped deck rather than the intention. Every change tightened
either honesty or legibility; none loosened a gate.

1. **THE PROJECTION GAINED AN AZIMUTH, and this was the run's biggest craft
   correction.** The plan specified an orthographic camera at pitch 31.75 deg
   with NO azimuth. Built and rendered, that projects the slab's top face to an
   axis-aligned RECTANGLE: no second side face, no visible corner, only one
   interior wall per recess. The first contact sheet read as pale bands rather
   than a milled solid, so the deck's entire depth argument had failed. Rebuilt
   at **azimuth 14 deg**, which makes every face a parallelogram, gives the slab
   two visible side faces, and gives every recess TWO interior walls plus corner
   occlusion. Still a parallel projection, so cut lengths keep encoding dollars
   honestly. Final projection, shared by the generator and the slide code and
   committed as `geom.py`:
   `screen_x = 540 + 259.1 * (0.9703x - 0.2419z)` and
   `screen_y = 905 + 259.1 * (0.1273x + 0.5106z - 0.8505y)`.
2. **Slab dimensions grew** to 3.00 x 2.30 x 0.30 world units (from 3.00 x 1.90 x
   0.12) and the standard recess deepened to **0.16** (from 0.09). At the planned
   numbers the visible groove wall was 19.8 px, which is 7.9 px at the 432px
   thumb and died there. It is now 35 px, about 14 px at thumb.
3. **The money scale held exactly**: 165.6 px per 1 million dollars, now measured
   along the slab's axonometric x axis, which is 0.6530 world units per million.
   Every cut length in the deck derives from that one constant in `geom.py`, so
   the art and the printed figures cannot drift.
4. **Depth is distributed differently than planned.** Slide 06 is the single
   akthree GPU PBR slide (orthographic camera matching `geom.py`, plane-shell
   slab so the left bay is a genuine hole, dark bay floor, four bay walls, real
   PCFSoft shadow, `AKPOST.grade`). Slides 02 and 04 are NOT akthree as the
   dossier said; they are Canvas 2D built from the same projection with
   quad-filled faces, real interior groove walls, AO pooling at every floor
   edge, gradient chamfers and two-part warm cast shadows, plus `AK.reliefShade`
   stone tooth. The twins argument survives because 02 and 04 are identical to
   each other in camera, light and technique. Every one of the ten slides now
   carries real side faces, real groove walls and a two-part warm shadow, which
   is a better answer to the recurring flat-deck ceiling than one rendered hero
   and nine flat slides would have been.
5. **Slide 06's scored right bay is drawn in the 2D layer**, not as GL geometry,
   as a 2.4px dash-dot rule with crop-mark corner Ls. It stays vector-crisp in
   the PDF and it can never accidentally acquire an interior fill. The left bay
   deepened to 0.26 so the void reads at thumb size.
6. **Slide 02 lost its drilled point and its scale bar.** At VISUAL_DENSITY 2
   the slide was carrying four annotation systems and the labels collided. The
   100,000 dollar largest gift and the Soldotna to Berkeley fact (c5, c6) moved
   into the base strip, where they read better. The slide now carries three
   systems: the cut with its fireweed segment, the dimension call, and the
   unmeasured brace.
7. **Slide 07 dropped to six rows** (four scored blanks, two real cuts) from
   seven, and the three verbatim quote fragments became short accurate tags
   ("Gilbert , refuses AI outright", "DeVries , used AI to answer"). The
   fragments were 1153 px wide on a nowrap label and pushed the page past the
   canvas. Claim c26 (McGuire on appealable algorithmic decisions) is therefore
   NOT used on-slide. The headline also changed from "Two of them have million
   dollar hauls" to "Two of them raised about a million dollars each", because
   Crum reported nearly 940,000 and the plural claim was an overstatement. The
   caption critic independently caught the same defect in the caption.
8. **Slide 08's rule labels were shortened** to fit one line each inside their
   grooves: "AI disclosed in ads" and "Children protected" (from "AI in
   political ads disclosed" and "Protections for children from AI content").
   Substance preserved, all six still trace to c12.
9. **Type floors were raised.** Every mono label is now 24px and every chip is
   24px, up from the planned 22px and 20px, because the engine warns under 24px
   and those labels are meaningful content rather than decoration. Slide 09's
   plate bodies are 26px, the deck's one sub-32px body copy, which is the price
   of fitting the full disclosure inside a plate that must stay identical in size
   to its twin.
10. **The cover set at 126px over four lines**, not 152px over three. At 152px
    the line "THE MONEY IS ON FILE." could not fit the measure and `AK.fitText`
    would have shrunk it to about 72px, which is far worse than a deliberate
    four-line break at 126px.
11. **Vertical zones final**: kicker 88, display 170, chips 400 to 620 depending
    on headline depth, art band 703 to 1173, base strip 1192 (30px, two lines),
    footer 1288 (marked `data-decorative`, the constellation fixtures live in the
    bleed band on purpose).
12. **A real bug worth logging**: the `.display` CSS rule had no
    `position:absolute`, so every headline collapsed to 0,0 and overprinted its
    own kicker. Machine QA caught it as a text collision on six slides at once.
    The lesson is that a positioning class and a styling class must not be
    separable, and it is a Phase 12 candidate.
