864 organizations sent nearly 1,800 letters of interest in Alaska's $272,174,856 rural health program.

The Department of Health invited 403 of them to apply. On August 7th the first Friday of a weekly series funded nineteen projects, $4.5 million in all. That is less than 2 percent of the first year allocation.

Chugachmiut got $627,200 for portable X-ray machines in five rural clinics. The Nenana Volunteer Fire Department received $50,000 to plan a new mobile crisis team. Kodiak, the Yukon-Kuskokwim Delta and the North Slope have nothing in round one.

The state's own words on drones and AI-enabled tools sit next to five awards named with amounts plus fourteen that published reporting doesn't itemise.
