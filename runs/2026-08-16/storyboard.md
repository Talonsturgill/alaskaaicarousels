# DECK HEADER

## THESIS

A menu and an order are two different documents. Alaska's own page says the
$272,174,856 may buy drones, kiosks, remote dispensing equipment and AI-enabled
tools. The first Friday moved $4.5 million, which at the deck's welded scale is
fifteen pixels of a nine hundred and twenty pixel year, and with the rest still
unwritten the useful thing to notice is how little of the page has been filled in.

Nobody in this story did anything wrong. That is exactly why the difference
between eligibility and expenditure is worth drawing at full size.

## VARIETY LEDGER CHECK, and this deck's required divergence

Last four decks' hero structures, atmospheres, hooks, palettes and type pairings
were read out of `ledger/artwork.json` at wake and are listed in full in
`plan.md` section 4. The divergence, stated explicitly:

- **Hero.** No.31 made SCALE the variable across nine rungs. **This deck welds
  scale and never rescales it once, and the constancy IS the argument.** That is
  31's deliberate inversion. No.32 was iron furniture on a composing stone at a
  fixed 30 degrees under a work lamp; this has no object, no interior, no lamp
  and no specular. No.33 deformed a projection and printed a note about it; this
  has no projection on its spine at all and exactly one linear axis. No.34 had a
  camera height, a dollying room, a floor, a subject and volumetric fog; this has
  none of the five.
- **THE SAMENESS THE LEDGER DOES NOT POLICE.** Three of the last three decks are
  an object or a sheet on a work surface under a single raking light. This deck
  has no table, no work surface and no interior. That is the register break, and
  it was the explicit brief to all three directors.
- **Atmosphere.** LOW NORTH SUN ON A LONG PLAIN, key azimuth 18, elevation 22,
  key to fill 5.5 to 1, cold sky-dome fill at `#1B3348`, **zero fog terms and
  zero volumetric shafts**. The forbidden three sit at 292, 118 and a cold rake
  with fog. 18 degrees is 100 off the nearest, so every surface here is lit on a
  face the last three decks left in shadow, and the absence of fog is the direct
  denial of No.34.
- **Hook.** NEW: THE UNWRITTEN REMAINDER. Diverges from THE HONEST WITNESS (32,
  a self-incriminating quotation), THE CONTRADICTED ARTIFACT (33, a document at
  odds with itself) and THE SUBJECT'S EYE LINE (34, a camera height and a still
  subject). This cover quotes nobody, contains no contradiction and has no camera.
- **Palette.** NEW: RADIOGRAPH BONE AND STATE BLUE, below. Not type metal and
  newsprint, not plate night and regulated magenta, not carpet wool and hallway
  blue.
- **Type.** Instrument Serif + Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono. Clears both
  forbidden pairings outright with no shared display or body family.

## THE SUBSTRATE, deck-wide, and it is binding from the prototypes

Weighted stipple on every slide. Height field is always **a slow plane plus at
most two broad gaussian lobes plus exactly one fbm warp at 0.10 amplitude**,
because the lay of a smooth field is the gradient of its form and any
high-frequency term turns tone into noise (No.32's three-round lesson, carried
across the bench). Density, dot radius and alpha are **three separate functions
of the same height**, so a region physically can't be flat unless the story's
quantity is flat there. About 50,000 dots per slide, six-step ramp from
`#33475A` to `#C9DCE6`, `r = 0.9 + 1.4 h`, `alpha = 0.30 + 0.55 h`. Mass is
authored low, y 860 to 1110, so `frame_balance` cannot fire.

**THE SOFT RESERVE.** The substrate reads the DOM and suppresses itself inside
every element marked `data-reserve`, measured after `document.fonts.ready`,
padded by `PAD = max(16, fontSize * 0.6)`, with a **ramped falloff over the PAD
band**. Prototype P4 proved this is mandatory (a stipple substrate crosses glyphs
and FAILS the deck) and prototype P6 proved the falloff must be soft (a hard hole
reads as a black rectangle at feed size, which is the thing plates were supposed
to avoid). Every art layer honours the reserve, not only the substrate; P5 failed
because d3 path strokes ignored it.

Seeds are `20260816 + slideIndex`. No `Math.random()`, no `Date.now()` anywhere
including profiling lines.
