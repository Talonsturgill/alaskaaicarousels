The first federal answer to machines that talk to children is a plan and a study.

Duckworth's office says S. 5171 cleared the committee that day with Alaska's Lisa Murkowski as the Republican cosponsor.
The release describes an FTC and CPSC plan and a National Academies study. It names no rule and no ban.
The committee's page publishes no tally.
The bill text returned 403 on August 15th. No due date for the plan or the study is published.

The agenda with every result row blank, both senators in their own words, and the record as sheets stamped READ or 403.
