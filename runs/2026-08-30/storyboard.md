# DECK HEADER

## THESIS

**Alaska's $181.9 million rural health award list bought eight machines for about $12 million,
one dollar in fifteen, and every one of them hands the decision back to a person except the
one that would act alone, which got a planning year instead of an aircraft.**

Document title, 24 characters: **`Eight Machines, One Sheet`**

## THE ROOM'S DECISION

Three treatments came in and all three independently chose a high key paper white register
after nine consecutive dark decks. That convergence is treated as a finding rather than a
coincidence and the deck is built on it.

**WINNER, the CARTOGRAPHER's THE CUT PLATE**, because it is the only one of the three whose
hero structure is a physical object that carries the argument without a caption. One sheet of
cotton rag on a table. Printed on it, an engraved plate of Alaska with all 29 boroughs and
census areas ruled in graphite. The twelve boroughs named in a machine award's service area
have been **cut out of the sheet and lifted**, each riding above the plate, throwing a
two part shadow, its exposed cut edge showing gold. The seventeen untouched lie flat, printed,
part of the paper. Water is bare unprinted paper, which is where every block of copy sits.

**STRENGTHENED WITH THE ESSAYIST'S THESIS.** The cartographer's deck was a survey of where.
The essayist's was an argument about what kind. The argument wins the spine and the map wins
the body, so the deck reads as ASSISTS versus ACTS ALONE, resolved on the plate. Its empty
third column, the one headed ACTS ALONE with nothing in it, is the single best idea produced
in the room and it becomes slide 06.

**STRENGTHENED WITH THE HISTORIAN'S TWO FACTS.** First, the four Friday rounds drawn at widths
proportional to their dollars, so the shape of August is a drawn quantity. Second, and this is
the sharpest fact in the whole run, **none of the eight are in the August 7th round**, which is
exactly the round carousel No.35 covered on August 16th. No.35 reported that the first Friday
bought X ray machines, a hydroponic farm and a scheduling system, and said fourteen of nineteen
were unreadable. This deck can now say why there was no machine in it. That is the UPDATE, and
it is drawn rather than asserted.

**REJECTED, with reasons.** The essayist's cast plaster sill loses Alaska almost entirely and
its own self critique said so. The historian's fanfold panorama puts three consecutive slides
inside one August 21st panel and its own self critique said so. Both critiques were correct
and both organs above were taken instead.

## SLIDE COUNT RATIONALE

Nine. The default band is 8 to 10. Nine gives one cover, one payoff, three place beats, one
keepable reference, one honesty beat, one turn and one close, with no slide doing two jobs.

thinnest frame in its pitch.

## CONTINUITY SYSTEM

**Device A, the physical semantic system, NEW: CUT IS A PLACE, SCREEN IS EVERYWHERE, PHANTOM
IS A PLAN.** Deliberately a PHYSICAL distinction rather than a colour one, which is how it
diverges from No.41's GOLD IS MEASURED, BLUE IS MODELLED and No.42's INK IS RECORDED, VOID IS
MODELED. Three states, each a different fabrication of the same sheet:

- **CUT AND LIFTED, gold edge.** A place specific award. The borough is excised and raised.
- **SCREENED, misregistered blue.** A statewide award. It has no address, so it can only be a
  second press pass over the whole sheet.
- **PHANTOM, outlined and flat.** A planning award. It has bought nothing yet, so nothing rises.

**Device B, the constant bar.** A scale bar of fixed physical length, 260px, at x 80 in the
bottom margin of all nine slides. Its printed distance changes as the camera zooms, so one
unchanging object makes every zoom honest. Its left terminal is a 4px gold tick, guaranteeing
gold presence at negligible area on every slide. Its right terminal carries the `NN / 09`
counter, so it is also the progress indicator.

**Device C, edge tease.** A cut element crosses the right frame edge on 03 and 06 and completes
at the left edge of the next slide. Planned for 01 as well and dropped in the build; the reason
is in BUILD RECONCILIATION.

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, AND HOW EVERY DOSSIER ANSWERS IT

Artwork craft has been the weakest criterion in 8 of 10 runs at a mean of 6.75, and the defect
table names busy art under text and top loaded composition at 4 of 10 each. Four rules bind
every slide below, and each was tested on a real render before this storyboard was written
(see `proto_findings.md`).

1. **THE THREE SCALE RULE.** Each dossier names what the 432px thumb carries, what the 1080
   render adds, and what only appears on zoom. Proven on a real render at all three scales.
2. **FORM COMES FROM INK, NOT FROM LIGHT.** Measured. On this ground `AK.reliefShade` gives a
   luminance stddev of 16.8 and never gets below 170 of 255, while `AKENGRAVE.surface` gives
   62.1 and reaches 30. Every modeled surface in this deck is engraved or hatched, never
   relit. **Load `akcolor.js` BEFORE `akengrave.js` or the render hard fails on `AKC is not
   defined`.**
3. **TYPE RESERVES ARE PHYSICAL AND DRAWN FIRST.** Every copy block in this deck sits on bare
   unprinted water, on the sheet's margin, or inside a knockout window on a printed table.
   A reader can name why that region is blank in every case.
4. **THE LOWER THIRD CARRIES MODELED TONE.** All three prototypes defaulted to a dead bottom
   band, so this is not assumed. Every dossier below names the object that occupies it.

offset DX = 7.7, DY = minus 14. Key azimuth 315, elevation 32.

**8. Data in art.** Which tiles lift is binary and driven by the twelve boroughs in the eight
awards' service areas (C17, C22, C26, C31, C32, C29). Lift height is CONSTANT at 14px and
