## THE TRAP IN THIS TABLE

**Four point three percent and six point six percent are both true and they are not the same
statement.** Eight of 185 awards is 4.3 percent BY COUNT. Twelve million of $182 million is
6.6 percent BY MONEY. A slide that prints one number and names the other quantity is wrong,
and no gate will catch it, because both figures are individually correct and both trace to
real claims. If the deck says "one dollar in fifteen" it is talking about money. If it says
"eight of 185" it is talking about count. Never let one sentence carry both.

This is precisely the aggregate defect class from 2026-08-02, where a slide printed "FIVE
STATE POSTINGS" by counting a federal item as a state one and every machine gate returned
PASS.
