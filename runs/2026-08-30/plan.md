## THE ONE STANDING WEAKNESS THIS RUN ATTACKS, AND HOW

**Artwork craft and genuine detail.** Weakest in 8 of the last 10 runs, mean 6.75, and
6.5 as recently as yesterday. Read as a prediction rather than a report, this says today's
deck scores about 6.5 on artwork unless something structural changes.

The diagnosis, from the defect table above and from what the last four decks actually did:
recent runs have answered "genuine detail" by adding MORE, and the machine has been telling
them so. Busy art under text warned in 4 of 10. Top-loaded composition warned in 4 of 10.
Those two warnings together describe one deck: a crowded upper field with type laid over it.
No.41 is the written case, five critics and a mean of 6.1, and its own retro says several
defects were regressions introduced while fixing other defects.

So this run inverts the answer. **Detail earns its score by being STRUCTURED, not by being
dense.** The attack has three parts and each one is checkable before a critic ever sees it.

1. **THE THREE-SCALE RULE, declared per slide in the dossier.** Every slide names what a
   reader sees at feed scale (432px thumb), what appears on stopping at full 1080, and what
   only appears on zoom. Three named things, not three adjectives. A frame that cannot name
   its zoom-scale payload does not have genuine detail, it has noise, and the dossier is
   where that gets caught for free. This is the cheapest possible place to fix the criterion
   that has cost this deck eight of ten runs at the ship gate.

2. **TYPE RESERVES ARE PHYSICAL, AND THEY ARE DESIGNED BEFORE THE ART.** Every block of
   copy sits on a region that exists in the depicted world for a reason a viewer can name:
   a planed flat, a snow-filled recess, a lit panel, a cleared apron. Never a plate dropped
   on afterwards, and never a gradient scrim. The reserve is drawn FIRST and the art is
   composed around it, which is the only ordering that makes "busy art under text"
   structurally impossible rather than repeatedly repaired.

3. **DENSITY 2 WITH A DRAWN-SHARE FLOOR.** Fewer marks, and every one of them a real
   drawing operation rather than a rectangle. bespoke_check's 45 percent drawn-share floor
   is the machine's version of the maintainer's "blocky, like a kid drag and dropping
   shapes"; this run treats 45 as the floor and aims well above it, with the technique
   bench (hachure, stipple, contour, relief, flow) doing the work that fillRect has been
   doing.

And the composition rule that follows from the same table, since top-loaded composition is
4 of 10 and the lower third is a criterion the scorer has named six consecutive times:
**the lower third carries modelled tone with its own light, on every slide, named in field
4a of every dossier.** dossier_check enforces the field; this plan sets the standard for
what a passing answer looks like.
