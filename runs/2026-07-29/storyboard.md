# STORYBOARD - Alaska.Ai Carousel No. 19 - 2026-07-29

## DECK HEADER

**THESIS.** The robot on a Cook Inlet salmon boat does not make a better
fish. It manufactures time, about 53 extra hours out of 6.5 seconds of work
on an Alaska deck, and every asset built out of those hours (the brand, the
plant, the freshness dataset, the company) is held outside Alaska.

**PDF DOCUMENT TITLE (43 chars).** `The Machine Makes Time. Who Owns the Clock?`

**ARC (9 slides).**
1. HOOK. The correction. It is not a better fish, it is time. (temperature: cold curiosity)
2. PAYOFF, immediately. The four physical moves and the 6.5 seconds.
3. HERO + KEEPABLE. The hour column. Seven hours of steel, fifty-three of brass.
4. THE HINGE. Every one of those numbers is the company's. Provenance taught.
5. BREATHER (declared). The fleet at 2 a.m. Scale and honesty about scale.
6. THE DEAL. The machine is free, the split is not disclosed. (temperature turns: the reader stops expecting a villain)
7. THE TURN. The fish stays Alaskan, the asset does not. (temperature peak)
8. THE LIMIT. None of it is on a shelf yet.
9. CLOSE. One ask.

**SLIDE COUNT RATIONALE.** Nine sits in the 8 to 10 default band. The deck
has exactly nine things to say and the directors room independently flagged
that its ten-slide variants contained two foldable pairs. Nine keeps every
slide load-bearing, keeps the swipe under the completion cliff, and leaves
the two keepable artifacts (S03 the column, S07 the ownership list) far
enough apart to both get read.

**CONTINUITY SYSTEM (three devices).**

- **(A) THE HOUR MARKER.** A slim vertical hour scale in the left margin of
  every slide. Hour 0 sits on the deck plane at y=1150, hour 60 at y=310, a

  across the deck and doubles as the progress read, paired with the mono
  counter. Vertical and marginal by design, structurally unlike deck 18's
  horizontal span states and deck 17's cut register.
- **(B) THE PROVENANCE CHIP.** A 3-form source-class tag introduced large on

- **(C) PALETTE ARC.** Sodium warmth is the Alaska register and it drains out

## WHAT CHANGED FROM THE DOSSIERS

### Deck-wide

1. **EVERY mono label is 24px, not 20 or 22.** The dossiers specified 20 to
   22px for chips, labels and counters. qa.py's mobile floor is 24px and it
   warned on roughly forty labels. All meaningful mono rose to 24px, which
   widened every chip and forced most of the reflows listed below. The
   coordinates footer stays at 20px and is marked `data-decorative`, which is
   what that attribute is for.
2. **The counter moved from the bottom right to the bottom LEFT margin on
   slides 01 to 08**, directly under the hour rail it pairs with, and it now
   rides a knockout plate. Planned as bottom-right. It collided with the
   column on the cover and sat on bare art everywhere else. Slide 09 keeps it
   bottom-right because that slide's left column carries the brand block.
3. **Body copy, pull quotes, the counter and the coordinates all ride
   knockout plates** at `rgba(10,8,5,0.86)`. The dossiers only required plates
   for labels in the art band. qa.py's glyph-ink probe failed light body copy
   over the deck's sodium stipple on four slides. This is the declared defence
   the SKILL contract asks for, and the doctrine permits a scrim where the
   composition cannot route a quiet zone, which is exactly the case here
   because the lower band is deliberately dense by design.
4. **The grain is a DOM overlay div carrying `AK.grainTile`'s data URL**, not
   a canvas pattern. `AK.grainTile` returns a data URL, not a canvas, so
   `createPattern` threw on the first render. This is the documented usage.
