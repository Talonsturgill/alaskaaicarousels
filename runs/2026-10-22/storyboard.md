# STORYBOARD, No.93, 2026-10-22, THE COMPOSING STONE

## THESIS
DeepGreen's FERC application for an underwater AI data center in Cook Inlet named the Alaska Energy Authority's CIPLink as a "Strategic Partner" and ORPC's turbines as its interface. Both have now told FERC in writing that they never spoke with DeepGreen, AEA filed to intervene with no position after its chief said it would oppose, the two Alaska Native organizations the filing says it coordinates with have filed nothing, and 117 members of the public have. Position taken: a FERC filing can name a partner for free, and the record should require that partner's word. PDF title: "Named in Writing, Answered in Writing" (copy.json carries the final one).

UPDATE of No.65 (2026-09-21, "So far, the Cook Inlet data center is a document.") and No.68 (2026-09-25, one of six sites). Material new since No.68: AEA's motion (October 8th), the City of Kenai's motion (October 13th), AEA's filed no-position against its chief's "oppose", the public record doubling (59 to 117), DeepGreen's footprint retreat with no new size. The cover says UPDATE.

## THE STANDING WEAKNESS, and what is different here
Artwork craft was the weakest criterion in 6 of the last 10 runs. Here the largest object on every frame is a physical object built first and modelled under one key (the stick, the proofs, the forme, the clipping, the plate, the galley, the docket sheet, the slug galley, the machine), each with its own mark-making; text sits on modelled paper or on reserved stone; gold is drawn after the grade or held with AKPOST.holdInk; every foot sits on a lit pool with a measured contact.
