## Directors room synthesis
Three treatments (out/2026-10-22/treatments/): the cinematographer's THE LANDING TRIPOD (one station on the Nikiski bluff, nine headings at nautical dusk), the cartographer's THE DRAINED NARROWS (a Berann drained inlet with lettered tie lines), the editorial essayist's SET IN TYPE / THE COMPOSING STONE (the record as brass type, proofs, a clipping, a galley).
Winner: the essayist. Its thesis is the sharpest because the story IS what parties put in writing, and its ink law (gold for DeepGreen's own words, press ink for everyone else's filed words, newsprint for things said off the record, a phantom for nothing filed) makes every frame carry the argument without a word. Its object list gives every frame a different largest object and a different mark-making, which is the standing weakness.
Organs taken from the others: the cinematographer's close (a rule, not a person: "Should a filing name a partner without the partner's signature?") and its honesty about ORPC (never called a partner); the cartographer's real geography for the depth frame, here as the essayist's copper plate engraved from the committed Cook Inlet DEM with the four filed corners in gold, and its insistence that gold marks the filed points and never a route.
Cut for feasibility and risk: the cinematographer's 360 degree bearing layer (insinuation risk about the silent names), the cartographer's lettered rotated type (thumb and collision risk), the essayist's newsprint-on-bond triple paper frames reduced to two paper frames (02 and 04) plus the docket sheet (07), each modelled differently.
