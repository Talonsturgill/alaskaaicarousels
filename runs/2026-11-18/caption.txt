Flights within Alaska kept flying on November 13th while every flight leaving the state was grounded.

So said an Anchorage airport official. The FAA's nationwide stop ran 11:54 a.m. to 4:15 p.m. Alaska time.

By mid-afternoon Cirium counted 670 flights sent back and 157 redirected nationwide. Three days earlier SMART, the FAA's AI traffic advisor, went national. The FAA blamed a router overloading older equipment. SMART's maker says the stop wasn't a result of its system.
